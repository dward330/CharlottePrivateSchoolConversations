# Charlotte Catholic High School — College Support (School Profile 2026-2027)

**Provenance**

- **Compiled by:** Claude Code planning pass (`/plan catholicprofile`).
- **Date compiled:** 2026-11-04
- **Method:** Direct download of the official **2026-2027 CCHS School Profile** PDF (4 pages;
  PDF metadata: Canva, created 2026-08-27), converted with `pdftotext -layout` and
  transcribed. The PDF carries a clean text layer. The matriculation list was extracted by
  fixed-column parsing of its three-column layout (continuation lines such as
  `(Downtown Phoenix)` and `Sciences` rejoined to their parent), then counted: **168**
  names, which matches the profile's own prose claim of "168 different colleges and
  universities" exactly.
- **Supersedes, does not replace:**
  [`Charlotte Catholic - College Support - School Profile 2025-2026.md`](Charlotte%20Catholic%20-%20College%20Support%20-%20School%20Profile%202025-2026.md).
  That file is the record for the **Classes of 2023–2025** list and every 2025-26 figure, and
  it stays. The 2025-26 PDF is a **separate** Finalsite asset and still served HTTP 200 on
  2026-11-04, so it was **not** overwritten (unlike Charlotte Christian and Davidson Day).
  It has no Wayback capture (`archive.org/wayback/available` returned no snapshot on
  2026-11-04), so the committed 2025-26 research file is the fallback citation.
- **Figures are tagged to their year and never averaged across years.** Testing and the decile
  chart are **Class of 2027** (this year's seniors). AP figures are the **2026** exam session.
  Outcomes, scholarships and National Merit are **Class of 2026**. The matriculation list is
  **Classes of 2024–2026** combined.

## Curriculum and rigor (verbatim, with what changed)

> 10th grade. Placement in all Charlotte Catholic core courses is determined by previous
> coursework and grades, standardized testing results, and teacher feedback. Students are
> afforded the opportunity to enroll in AP classes starting in 11th grade. Two AP classes in
> the junior year and four AP classes in the senior year is considered a most demanding
> course load.
>
> Students may enroll in additional AP classes above the two in junior year and the four in
> senior year. Admission to AP courses is competitive, and students must submit an
> application for each course. Placement in all AP courses is determined by previous
> coursework and grades, standardized testing results (PSAT), and teacher feedback.

> The Class of 2027 has the opportunity to participate in Dual Enrollment courses during
> their senior year through Central Piedmont Community College. Students are only able to
> take one DE course each semester and courses are taken asynchronously.

  asynchronous).
