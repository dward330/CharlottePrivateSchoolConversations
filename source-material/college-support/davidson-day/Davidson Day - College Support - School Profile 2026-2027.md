  age 2 through grade 12; 750 Jetton Street, Davidson, NC, a town of 15,000 about 25

**Planning-time AP count (derived, same rule as the 2025–26 derivation in
`Davidson Day - College Support - Redesign Research 2026.md`):** the AP column prints

- "The Class of 2026 included 8 NCAA student-athletes and 20 NHS members."

**Class of 2027 GPA Distribution** (verbatim): Highest: **4.67** · Median: **3.93** ·
Lowest: **2.58**.

**Footnote under the Academic Information column, verbatim:** "*GPAs for transfer
students are not included in the distribution. Figures calculated from the 9/1/26 roster
export (47 of 50 students with a GPA on file); confirm against final records and
transfer-student exclusions before printing."
→ The second sentence reads as an internal drafting instruction that was printed. As
published, the GPA figures are self-described as provisional and cover 47 of 50 students.

**Academic Information** (verbatim bullets): Honors and AP courses are weighted (.5 and 1
point respectively); Calculus III is also weighted 1 point · GPAs reflect only courses
taken at Davidson Day · not factored into the GPA: transfer courses, Upper School courses
taken during Middle School, Independent Studies, and online courses not sponsored by the
school · Davidson Day does not rank its students · a transfer student's previous
transcript is enclosed · .5 credit per season for school athletics or approved off-campus
fitness activity.

**Minimum Graduation Requirements — 21 Credits** (was 22.5 in 2025–26): English 4 ·
Math 4 (minimum sequence Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra II, and 1 credit beyond Algebra II)
· Science 3 credits of lab science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) · History 3 · World
Language **2 credits (in the same language)** (was 3) · Fine Arts 1 · PE and Health 1 ·
Electives 3.
Note, verbatim: "During the Class of 2027's Upper School enrollment, the school revised its
graduation requirements. Composition (0.5 credit) and Level III of a World Language are
no longer required. Because many students had already completed these courses before the
requirements changed, transcripts for members of the Class of 2027 may reflect coursework
completed under the previous requirements."

## Page 3 — programs, athletics, fast facts

- **Global Studies Diploma Program:** "Over a quarter of Davidson Day School students
  participate in international research." Requirements: all minimum graduation
  requirements; specific coursework with a global perspective, **including the same
  world language in all four years of upper school**; a minimum of two weeks of
  international field research; a capstone paper. "An additional transcript and profile
  will be sent for students who are receiving this diploma."
- **International Research Projects:** AFAR in Belize since 2009, Spain since 2014,
  Greece since 2017, Portugal since 2018.
- **Varsity Athletic Teams:** Baseball (B)**, Basketball (B, G), Cheerleading (G), Crew
  (B,G), Cross Country (B, G), Football (B)**, Golf (B, G), Lacrosse (G), Soccer (B, G),
  Swim (B, G), Tennis (B, G), Track & Field (B, G), Volleyball (G). "**In partnership with
  SouthLake Christian Academy."
- **Honor Code** (verbatim, unchanged): "I will not lie, cheat, steal, or plagiarize, nor
  will I tolerate the dishonorable behavior of others."
- **Visual and Performing Arts:** Improv Comedy Showcase; Musical Theatre Review; Student
  Written and Directed Short Play Showcase; Contemporary Ensemble; Performance Ensemble;
  theatre productions (Shakespeare, Greek, Musical, Contemporary); recent Blumey

- **Community Engagement:** Learn-Serve-Reflect; "Service is encouraged, but not required."
- **2025-2026 PATRIOTS Review** sidebar: **1** NCISAA State Champions — Varsity Boys
