### ⬇⬇⬇ PASTE FROM HERE ⬇⬇⬇

You are producing a two-host Audio Overview for a parent-facing podcast episode: "Inside Davidson Day College Support." Warm, candid, specific. Cover ALL SEVEN segments below as SEPARATE, clearly delivered beats. Honesty rule stated aloud early and kept throughout: when the school does not publish a number, say so plainly and call it a transparency gap, NOT a weakness. Report only what the school publishes and what research surfaced.

STRUCTURE GUARANTEE: Seven segments plus a closing = eight beats. Segments 5 and 7 are the most often shortchanged; give EACH at least two concrete specifics. If you run long, TRIM THE OPENING BANTER AND EARLY ANALOGY, never the later segments. One analogy for the WHOLE episode, near the top; after that, no new analogies or invented anecdotes.

Open with a plain-language agenda: name that this walks through seven parts of how Davidson Day supports college-bound students, then go.

1) THE COUNSELING ENGINE: a two-person office, individualized counseling starting in 9th grade, roughly ninety-nine students per counselor. The Director is a former university admissions officer. Work is run through Scoir.

2) THE ACADEMIC CASE: no class rank by design; instead the profile publishes the Class of 2026 GPA spread. Say these three figures exactly: highest four point six-oh, median three point nine six, lowest two point four nine (that is 2.49, NOT 2.44). Honors weighted plus 0.5, A.P. plus 1.0, plus a post-A.P. Calculus 3. GAPS: the school withholds SAT/ACT profiles, does not publish A.P. pass rates, and has no published National Merit standing. Do NOT confuse Davidson Day (North Carolina) with the similarly named Davidson Academy in Nevada.

3) INSTITUTIONAL LEVERAGE: admissions reps come to campus; the page advertises "eighty-plus" reps, while recent reporting documents about seventy-one, nearly eighty percent from out of state. A spring Case Studies Program and college fair are co-hosted with a neighboring independent school. The School Profile frames each student for colleges.

4) APPLICATION SUPPORT: help with the list, the essay, and financial aid, but students own their applications; the Class of 2025 was offered over seven point four million dollars in merit. GAPS: no published promise that every application is reviewed, no published Early Decision doctrine, and college FAFSA/C.S.S. Profile navigation is undocumented. Note "Clarity" is the school's OWN tuition-aid tool, not college aid.

5) STANDING OUT (the signature strength; at least two specifics): AFAR, real international archaeological field research since 2009 in Belize (also Spain, Greece, Portugal), founded 2006 at Cahal Pech; participation is cited as "hundreds" or "more than two hundred since 2010" depending on source. And the Diploma Distinctions: Global Studies (over a quarter of students; a separate transcript to colleges) plus STEMM and Business tracks with a hundred-hour practicum and a Scholars Research Conference.

6) OUTCOMES: Class of 2025 had fifty-three graduates, one hundred percent to four-year colleges, thirty-five different colleges selected. On the FIVE-YEAR acceptance list (2021 to 2025, read as list breadth, NOT a placement rate): three of eight Ivies, eight of seventeen Ivy Plus, about forty-eight Top Seventy-Five National Universities, about twenty Top Seventy-Five Liberal Arts Colleges, and about forty-nine Power Four schools. GAPS: no college-specific yield and no post-enrollment persistence data.

7) FIT AND THE WHOLE CLASS (at least two specifics): a no-rank, best-fit philosophy meant to serve every student, not just the top; and the Learning Enrichment Center, which offers one-on-one tutoring, executive-function coaching, speech-language and occupational therapy from age two through grade twelve. GAP: the LEC's role during the college-application stage is not documented.

CLOSING (its own separate beat, NOT a tail of segment 7). Start with a clear pivot line such as "To pull this together." Give three one-line takeaways spanning the whole episode: (a) the edge is a high-touch office plus a genuine research hook, not published test metrics; (b) the honest gaps are disclosure choices, not proof of weakness; (c) read every outcome number as breadth of acceptances, not a placement rate. Then end with this as the LITERAL FINAL SENTENCE, with nothing after it: "We reported only what Davidson Day publishes and what research surfaced, and where a number is unpublished, that is a transparency gap, not a verdict on quality."

PRONUNCIATION: Scoir is said "score" (do NOT spell S-C-O-I-R). AFAR is "AY-far" (not "a far"). Cahal Pech is "kuh-HAL PETCH." Division I/II/III as "One/Two/Three." Power 4 as "Power Four." Top 75 as "Top Seventy-Five." FAFSA as "FAF-suh." C.S.S. Profile said "C-S-S Profile." Gloss "yield" and "matriculation" on first use.
