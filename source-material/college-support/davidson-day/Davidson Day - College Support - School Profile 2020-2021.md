## Why this is the latest published SAT/ACT data

Every later profile that could be read withholds testing, in the school's own words:

- **2022–23** (Wayback capture above), "Class of 2023 Profile": "Due to limited testing
  opportunities since March 2020, we are not providing the testing profiles for the Class
  of 2022 or the Class of 2023. Many of our students are taking advantage of test-optional
  policies."
- **2023–24**, "Class of 2024 Testing": "Due to limited testing opportunities since March
  2020, we are not providing the testing profiles for the Class of 2024 or the Class of
  2023. Many of our students are taking advantage of test-optional policies."
- **2024–25**, "Testing at Davidson Day": "Due to the popularity of test-optional
  policies, we will not be providing the testing profiles for the Class of 2025."
- **2025–26** and **2026–27**: the same sentence, for the Classes of 2026 and 2027.

The only class not covered by a later withholding sentence is the 2021–22 edition's own
cohort, and that edition cannot be read. The Class of 2022 is explicitly withheld in
2022–23. So the **Class of 2021** figures below are the most recent scores the school has
published that we can find.

**Weighted GPA Distribution, Class of 2021 (does not include transfer students):**
Highest 4.54 · Median 3.93 · Lowest 2.59.

**Class of 2020 Highlights:** the 53 members were offered more than $5.1 million in
scholarships; 98% enrolled in four-year institutions; 100% of the students who applied
were admitted to college; the Class of 2020 included 2 National Merit Scholarship
Finalists, and the Classes of 2015–2018 included 3 National Merit Scholarship Finalists,
1 National Hispanic Scholar and 1 National Merit Commended Scholar.

**Academic Information:** Honors and AP weighted .5 and 1 point; Calculus III also
weighted 1 point; transfer courses, Upper School courses taken in Middle School,
Independent Studies and online courses (NCVPS, Aventa Learning, Visnet) are not in the
GPA; "Davidson Day does not rank its students"; GPAs reflect only Davidson Day courses.

## Notes for the app

- The Middle 50% columns are printed with a hyphen (`1060 - 1230`, `18-26`). The app writes
  ranges with an en dash (`1060–1230`, `18–26`), the same normalisation every other school's
  Compare cell uses. The digits are unchanged.
- The Class of 2021 SAT figures come from only **12 of 53** seniors, a sample the school
  itself attributes to COVID-19. Any surface showing them should say so.
