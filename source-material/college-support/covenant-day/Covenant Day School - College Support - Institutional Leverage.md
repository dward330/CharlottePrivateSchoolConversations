### The relationships the school can spend

- **Covenant College dual enrollment** — starred courses in the profile's own
  matrix (Honors Calculus, Honors Statistics, Honors Literature & Philosophy) carry
  Covenant College dual-enrollment credit, a standing institutional pipe to the
  PCA's denominational college. Covenant College is also a steady matriculation
  destination (bold on the acceptance list).
- **NACCAP standing** — the school is a member of the North American Coalition for
  Christian Admissions Professionals, and director Heather Mills was named a NACCAP
  K-12 Professional of the Year — name recognition inside the Christian-college
  admissions circuit specifically.
- **NACAC membership** (National Association for College Admission Counseling) —
  the mainstream counterpart, listed in the profile's accreditation block.
- **Accreditation stack**: Christian Schools International (CSI), SACS/AdvancED
  (Cognia), North Carolina Association of Independent Schools (NCAIS), National
  Council for Private School Accreditation (NCPSA), NASSP.
- **A 30-year director** (Mills, at CDS since 2003) — institutional memory with
  college admissions offices is a personal asset the school's bios lead with.
- **SevenStar Academy** online partnership formally noted on the transcript,
  extending the catalog beyond the 37-teacher faculty.

### Ceiling check

Covenant Day's leverage is strongest in the NC publics and the Christian-college
network, real but thinner at national-elite level: the 2023-2026 window shows
Dartmouth, Penn and Duke matriculations, while Stanford/MIT/Princeton appearances
belong to earlier windows (2018-2023 highlights). No named college-partnership
programs (e.g. flagship research pipelines) beyond Covenant College are published.
