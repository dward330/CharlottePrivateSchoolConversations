# Covenant Day School — College Support — Outcomes

> **Provenance:** Compiled by Claude Code on 2026-08-15. Acceptance lists extracted
> from the school's own High School Profile PDFs with `pdftotext -layout`;
> **matriculation (bold) markings recovered with `pdfplumber`** by font name, since
> plain text extraction drops bold. The school publishes two overlapping multi-year
> lists — classes of 2021-2024 and classes of 2023-2026 — with bold indicating an
> institution where a student from those classes actually matriculated.

### Headline outcomes

- **85 seniors** in the Class of 2027; **225 institutions** on the published
  2023-2026 acceptance list (exact count from a full line-level extraction of the
  profile PDF; 94 of them bold/matriculated).
- Acceptances in the current window include **Dartmouth, Penn, Duke, Notre Dame,
  Emory, Georgia Tech, Michigan, UVA, UNC-Chapel Hill, Wake Forest, Case Western,
  William & Mary, NYU, Villanova, Vanderbilt-tier absent** (no Vanderbilt in

  highlights) and **Princeton, Brown, Rice, Washington & Lee, Berkeley-tier UCs**

### Scholarships

No scholarship dollar totals are published — checked across all three profile
editions, the news feed, and targeted searches. Qualitative claims only ("identifying
scholarship opportunities"). This is a confirmed absence, not a research gap.

### Yield note

Because both lists mark matriculation in bold and overlap on the classes of
2023-2024, acceptance *and* enrollment can be cross-validated across editions —
a disclosure most peer schools do not offer.
