### Caseload

**85 seniors in the Class of 2027** (2026-2027 profile) against **one dedicated
Guidance & College Counseling Director** — a **~85:1** peak-season ratio, cushioned
by the second guidance counselor for non-college guidance load. This is the honest
like-for-like figure against peer schools' dedicated-college-counselor counts.

### What the office does

The guidance page states the aim: "match each student with the college or university
that best suits his or her interests and talents," with academic advising on course
selection, student counseling, and college admissions support; the office "meets
with juniors and seniors throughout their high school careers." The high-school
academics page adds: "Each student receives one-on-one support through the college
process, from course planning and building a strong application to identifying
scholarship opportunities."

### Not published

A formal grade-by-grade (9th–12th) counseling timeline is **not published** — only
the junior/senior meeting cadence above. No college-counseling platform (Naviance /
SCOIR) is named on the public site. A College Representatives page exists for
visiting admissions officers.
