### The mechanics the office owns

- **One-on-one support "through the college process, from course planning and
  building a strong application to identifying scholarship opportunities"**
  (high-school academics page).
- Academic advising on course selections tied to college goals — placement into
  Honors/DE/AP is by previous coursework, grades, and teacher feedback, so course
  planning runs through the same office that writes the school's transcript story.
- The office "meets with juniors and seniors throughout their high school careers"
  (guidance page).
- A **College Representatives** page manages visiting college admissions officers.
- The **High School Profile** itself is the office's application artifact: CEEB code
  342532, grading scale, rank policy, AP gating, and the acceptance list in one
  two-page PDF refreshed annually — three editions verified.
- Transcript policy: grades/GPAs on the CDS transcript reflect courses taken at CDS
  or the online partner SevenStar Academy, as noted; transcripts from other schools
  attended are attached as separate documents.

### The rank story an application rides on

The profile states the no-rank policy in full: "Due to the small class size and the
college-bound nature of Covenant Day's population, we believe that class rank is not
a strong discriminator from which to predict college performance. Therefore, we do
not rank." Colleges instead get the 4.33/+0.5/+1.0 weighting key and the AP-gating
rigor statement, letting readers reconstruct rigor-in-context.

### Not published

No essay-workshop calendar, application-boot-camp, or interview-prep programming is
named publicly; no application platform (Common App workflow, Naviance/SCOIR) is
named. The support model as published is relational — a 30-year director, small
class, junior/senior meeting cadence — rather than a productized curriculum.
