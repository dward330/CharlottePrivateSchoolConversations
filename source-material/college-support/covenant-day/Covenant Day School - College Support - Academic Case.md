### The AP record — the school's headline academic claim

From the 2026-2027 profile (Class of 2027 cover year, 2026 exam administration):

- **179 students took 401 AP exams** in 2026. All students enrolled in AP courses are
  required to take the AP exams.
- **96% of all AP exam scores were 3 or higher**; **78% were a 4 or 5**.
- Five-year AP 3+ series (2022 → 2026): **84% · 93% · 92% · 96% · 96%** — a rising
  line the school prints as a chart.
- 2026 AP Scholar tally: **38 AP Scholars with Distinction, 27 AP Scholars with
  Honor, 33 AP Scholars**.
- Prior year for comparison (2024-2025 profile, Class of 2024): 168 students took
  344 exams; 35 Scholars with Distinction, 13 with Honor, 29 Scholars.
- 2023-24 profile (Class of 2023): 161 AP students, 324 exams, **93% scored 3+**.

### National Merit

Since 2006 — the year of the school's first graduating class — Covenant Day has had
**28 National Merit Finalists and 70 National Merit Commended students** (2026-2027
profile). The 2024-2025 edition said 26 and 61; the 2023-24 edition said 25 and 59,
so the ledger is still accruing.

### Rigor architecture

- Grading scale: A+ = 97-100 at **4.33 unweighted**; **Honors +0.5; Dual Enrollment
  and AP +1.0** quality point. GPA computed on all subjects taken in grades 9-12.
- **25 Carnegie Units to graduate**: 4 English, 4 Math, 3 Science, 3 Social Studies,
  3 Bible (a Bible course every year), 1 Fine Art, 0.5 Health, 1 PE, 5.5 electives
  (2026-2027 profile; the 2024-2025 edition showed 3.5 Bible / 5 electives).
- **AP pacing is deliberately gated**: no AP courses for freshmen; sophomores may
  take a maximum of one AP. "Two AP courses in the junior year and four AP courses
  senior year is a very demanding course load" (profile's own words). Placement in
  Honors/DE/AP is determined by previous coursework, grades, and teacher feedback.
- Dual enrollment through **Covenant College**; online partner **SevenStar Academy**
  (transcript notes courses taken there).
- **80% of full-time high school faculty hold advanced degrees** (2026-2027 profile;
  81% in 2024-2025; the live academics page says "over 83%").
- 37 teaching faculty for ~350 high school students (2026-2027 profile).

### Distinctive department

**Restoration & Sustainability** is a named high-school department with its own
director (Dr. Rebeca Burnett) — courses include Augustine Literacy Project,
Intercultural Practicum, Introduction to Engineering, and Restore525. The profile
gives it a stand-alone descriptive block, the only department treated that way.
