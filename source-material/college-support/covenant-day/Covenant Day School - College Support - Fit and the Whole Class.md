### Fit philosophy

The office's stated aim is fit, in so many words: "match each student with the
college or university that best suits his or her interests and talents." The
matriculation footprint bears it out — the bold (enrolled) institutions on the
2023-2026 list run from Dartmouth and Duke through Covenant College, Cedarville,
and community colleges, with the deepest concentrations at NC publics (Chapel
Hill, NC State, UNC Charlotte, App State, ECU) and Christian colleges.

### What the whole-class picture shows — and what it withholds

The school publishes strong **aggregate** tables and **no distributions**:

- Five-year AP 3+ series: 84 → 93 → 92 → 96 → 96% (2022-2026).
- Class SAT/ACT averages per class (1263/26 for 2026; 1243/27 for 2024; 1268/26
  for 2023) and five-year averages (1261/26).
- AP Scholar tiers per class (38 Distinction / 27 Honor / 33 Scholars in 2026).
- National Merit cumulative ledger (28 Finalists, 70 Commended since 2006).

**No GPA distribution, no score quartiles/quintiles, no per-class matriculation
rates** appear in any of the three profile editions. This follows directly from the
school's stated no-rank policy ("we do not rank") — a school that declines to rank
does not publish distribution tables either. Confirmed consistent across all three
editions; a deeper pass will not recover it.

### Support for the middle and below

- 5 Learning Specialists and 1 Academic Resources Director on the high school
  staff table (2026-2027 profile; the 2024-2025 edition listed 5 Academic Resources
  Specialists + 1 Academic Resources Director + 1 Library Media Specialist).
- A Clinical Counselor (Emily Lee, LMFT) distinct from the college office.
- The tuition terms note tutoring/special testing beyond classroom help is
  available at extra cost — a disclosed, priced support channel.
- AP gating (none for freshmen, one for sophomores) is itself a whole-class
  guardrail: rigor is sequenced rather than open-enrollment.
