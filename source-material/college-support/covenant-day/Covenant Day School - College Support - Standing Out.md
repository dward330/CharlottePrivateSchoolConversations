### The differentiators an application can carry

- **Restoration & Sustainability** — a named high-school department (unique among
  the schools in this project) with its own director and course ladder (Augustine
  Literacy Project, Intercultural Practicum, Introduction to Engineering,
  Restore525). The profile gives it a stand-alone block: students "dive deeply into
  facets of our community, society, and world in a manner that develops
  problem-solving skills, leadership, and practical abilities, through a biblical
  worldview." A transcript line no other Charlotte-area applicant carries.
- **Senior Capstone Project** — listed among the high school's "meaningful
  milestones."
- **ContainIt** — high-school students converting shipping containers into housing
  for the Pine Ridge Reservation (South Dakota), aiming to house "more than 30
  teens" — a concrete, multi-year service build with the students' own trades work
  in it.
- **Dual-enrollment + AP layering** — Covenant College DE credit on Honors
  Calculus/Statistics/Literature & Philosophy lets a transcript carry college credit
  beyond the 15-course AP matrix.
- **The rigor narrative** — AP gating plus the required-exam policy (every AP
  student sits the exam; 96% score 3+) makes the school's AP grades legible to
  admissions readers in a way open-enrollment AP programs are not.
- **Mock Trial + courtroom-artist results** — Cornelia Knight (Covenant Day School
  Blue team) won the NC Mock Trial courtroom-artist regional (Hickory) and was 2025
  **state runner-up** — see the Student Clubs research for sourcing.

### The honest scope

These are differentiation-by-depth stories (service builds, a bespoke department,
denominational college credit) rather than national-competition credentials; the
school publishes no research-program, Intel/Regeneron, or Olympiad results.
