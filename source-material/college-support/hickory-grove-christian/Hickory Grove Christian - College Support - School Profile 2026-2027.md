are as follows: **65% attend 4-year colleges, 25% attend 2-year colleges, 10% transition to the
workforce, military, or a gap year experience.**"

### Advanced Placement Scores (verbatim)

"**85** HGCS students took a total of **176** AP exams in **May 2026**. **14** students earned a 5
on one or more AP exams; **38** students earned a 4 on one or more AP Exams; **50** students
earned a 3 on one or more AP exams."

These are overlapping students-scoring-X counts, as in earlier editions — still no per-exam
1–5 distribution and no pass rate. (May 2025: 82 / 176; 29 / 59 / 53.)

### Class of 2026 ACT Scores (n=79), verbatim

"Mean ACT: Composite– **20.5**; English– 21.1; Mathematics– 18.7; Reading– 21.7; Science– 21;
Composite Range: 12-31"

### Class of 2026 SAT Scores (n=45), verbatim

"**Mean** SAT: Composite– **1066**; ERW– 551; Mathematics– 515. Composite Range: 760-1400"

- **Note the statistic changed.** The 2025–26 edition printed a Class of 2026 SAT **median**
  (1090, ERW 560, Math 525, range 780-1500, n=20). This edition prints a **mean** for the same
  class, on more testers (n=45). Its ACT figures for the same class also moved (n 85 → 79;
  English 19.7 → 21.1, Math 19.7 → 18.7, Reading 21.3 → 21.7, Science 20.9 → 21, range
  12-34 → 12-31) while the composite stayed 20.5. The newer edition is the class's final
  figure; the older one was mid-class.
