## S1 — the 2026–27 School Profile, page by page

### Page 1 — cover

> 2026-2027 School Profile. At Charlotte Country Day School, education is not simply the
> acquisition of knowledge – it is a purposeful journey of intellectual curiosity, personal
> growth and meaningful engagement with the world. From our earliest learners in Junior
> Kindergarten to students undertaking the rigor of Advanced Placement and the International
> Baccalaureate Diploma Programs, we foster a dynamic learning environment …

**Program Highlights**

- **Our Farm** — "a 330-acre living classroom … produce, livestock, beekeeping, mushroom
  farming, and a ten-mile trail system, offering integrated programming in sustainable
  agriculture, ecology, biology, environmental science, and entrepreneurship."
- **International Baccalaureate Diploma Program** — "Charlotte Country Day was the first
  school in North Carolina to offer the International Baccalaureate Diploma Program.
  **Twenty-six (26) seniors in the Class of 2027 are enrolled in the full IB Diploma
  Program.** Over the last five years, our IB students have had an average diploma pass rate
  of **88%**."
- **International Studies and Experiences** — Office of International Studies; "This year,
  they have options to explore the Silk Road in China or see firsthand how the country of
  Rwanda has rebuilt and reconciled following the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi, among
  other trips."
- **Mecklenburg County Special Olympics Spring Games** — "Every April since 1984, the entire
  Upper School takes a two-day break from classes to host …"
- **Summer Science Symposium** — "For the past 15 years, motivated students have had
  opportunities to serve as researchers at college and university labs across the country,
  then present their posters to our school community at a special event in the fall."
- **Student Leadership** — "Significant leadership opportunities are available for students
  across **50+ different clubs and organizations**, including Honor Council, Student
  Government, Affinity Groups, Athletics, Theater Arts, Big Brothers Big Sisters, and more. We
  offer membership to Cum Laude Society to eligible members of the graduating class in spring
  of their senior year."

**Upper School Faculty at a Glance** — **83** administrative and teaching staff · **82%**
hold advanced degrees · **100%** engage in professional development each year · "AP exam
readers, Klingenstein Fellows, National Board graders, National Board Certified, Arts and
Science grant winners, World Affairs Council grant winners".

**Our School's Bold and Visionary Plan** — three strategic-plan paragraphs (students known as
"healthy, happy, high-achievers who are 'good humans'"; educators "best in class"; the school
"an exemplar of healthy striving"). Not card material.

**Contact & School Information** — **CB Code #340666 · IB Code #0667** · Dr. Tara Christie

  for AP Physics C. To qualify for AP Physics 1, students must earn a qualifying grade in

- "AP Art History was not available in 2025-26 but is available this year."
