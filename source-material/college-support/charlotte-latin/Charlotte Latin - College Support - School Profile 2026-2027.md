# Charlotte Latin School — 2026–27 Upper School Profile

Charlotte Latin's **2026–27 Upper School Profile** is the four-page document the school sends to colleges with every application. This edition publishes far more than the 2025–26 one did. It now includes AP results by subject, SAT and ACT means, junior- and sophomore-year grade distributions, the full grading scale and a no-rank statement, alongside a new **College Admission Summary 2024–26**.

Verbatim from the program description:

- "A traditional college-preparatory curriculum is offered on a semester system. Six courses are required each year, of which four must be academic. The recommended and typical course load is five academic courses and one enrichment class."
- "Twenty-one credits are required for graduation, including English (4 credits); math (4 credits); science (3 credits); history (3 credits including World History I, World History II, and U.S. History); world language (3 credits in the same language); and physical education (1 credit, of which ½ may be waived based on athletic participation)."
- "Students must meet certain benchmarks to be placed by faculty in Advanced, Honors, or AP courses, with the exception of AP Business with Personal Finance, AP Computer Science Principles, AP Cybersecurity, AP Networking Fundamentals, AP Economics, AP Environmental Science, and AP Psychology." That makes **seven** open-enrollment APs.
- "Students in AP classes are required to take the AP exam. Advanced Placement courses are offered in 24 subjects, while Honors courses are available in English, mathematics, certain sciences, history semester courses, and level 3+ in world language."
- "High school classes completed in Middle School are indicated on the transcript but are not included in any GPA."

**Note regarding math and science curricula (verbatim):** "Charlotte Latin math courses are taught at a high level across standard, advanced, and honors/AP coursework. Standard courses include Algebra I/1B, Geometry, Algebra II, and Precalculus (and Advanced Functions w/Trig prior to 2026-2027). Advanced courses include Geometry (Adv), Algebra II/Trig (Adv), Precalculus (Adv), and Calculus (Adv), while honors courses are available beginning with Geometry and Honors Precalculus typically leads to an AP Calculus course. By contrast, Precalculus (Adv) does not lead directly to AP Calculus; the AP Calculus curriculum begins at the end of Honors Precalculus. As our Honors math curriculum is unusually rigorous and fast-paced, many fine math students are placed in standard and advanced courses.

In science, the first Upper School course typically taken is a Conceptual Physics course. There is no honors version, but Advanced Conceptual Physics demands a higher level of mathematical thinking and addresses topics at a greater level of complexity.

All Advanced courses in math and science are weighted accordingly (+0.5). The decision to weight Advanced math and science courses was made in the spring of 2026 but was applied both prospectively and retroactively for the Class of 2027 forward. From 2026-2027 forward, no class name will include the word Advanced unless the course has that extra GPA weight."

## Method

- **Document:** "2026–27 Charlotte Latin Upper School Profile", 4 pages. The PDF was created 2026-09-03 in Adobe InDesign 21.2 and has a text layer.
- **Source URL (supplied by the user, 2026-11-03):** https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1793645005/charlottelatin/izzo9suck4uojytpwadd/2026_27UpperSchoolProfileforCC1.pdf
- **Transcribed 2026-11-03** by Claude Code during `/plan latinprofile`. Page text came from `pdftotext -layout`. The acceptance list was cross-checked against a second `pdftotext -raw` extraction, and both give 292 names with 132 asterisks. The AP and grade-distribution tables are **images**, so they were read from 300-dpi renders of page 3.
- **Arithmetic checks:** the AP table sums to exactly 833 exams, with 91.96% scoring 3+ and a mean of 4.10, all matching the printed summary. Six grade-distribution rows sum to one or two below their printed "#" column: junior Algebra II/Trig (Adv) 31 of 33, Algebra II/Trig (H) 3 of 4, Biology 28 of 29 and Spanish III 16 of 17; sophomore Geometry (Adv) 34 of 35 and Chemistry 63 of 64. That is consistent with the printed note that grades below C- are not shown, so these are not transcription errors.
- **The previous edition is still live at its own URL.** The 2025–26 profile at https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1760637579/charlottelatin/qzxflgjmybf7ydg71xab/USProfile-2.pdf still serves the 2025–26 file (created 2025-09-08) on 2026-11-03. It is a different asset id, so unlike Cannon's profile the old citations were not overwritten.
- **The school's live Acceptances page lags this profile.** https://www.charlottelatin.org/academics/college-counseling/acceptances still reads "Acceptances by the Classes of 2023-25" on 2026-11-03.
- **Absent from this edition:** a CEEB code, a four-year matriculation percentage, National Merit Finalists, the "Honor Above All" disciplinary-reporting block, the named-scholarship history, Cum Laude, and the Learning Resources program. The unremoved internal editorial note in the 2025–26 edition ("We'll need to somehow check to make sure all these students are still here, yes?") is **gone**.
