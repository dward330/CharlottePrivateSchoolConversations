### Academic information

- **GPA (verbatim):** "Our Advanced Topics (AT) and Advanced Placement (AP) courses are
  weighted with a 1.0 quality point. Honors accelerated courses are weighted with a 0.5
  quality point. Honors courses are not weighted. The cumulative weighted and unweighted
  GPAs for each student are calculated using final grades from courses taken at Cannon
  School."
  > **Change:** the 2025–26 edition said Honors **and Honors Accelerated** were not weighted.
  > The 2026–27 Course Catalog dates the +0.5 to July 1, 2026 (see
  > `source-material/course-offerings/cannon/Cannon - Course Offerings - Curriculum Guide.md`).
- **Calendar:** "Cannon School operates on a semester system. (First semester ends in
  December. Second semester and final grades end in May.)"
- **Course load:** "Students carry an academic load of six courses unless extenuating
  circumstances dictate otherwise. Currently, all classes are taught as full-year courses."
- **Rank and reporting policy:** "Cannon School does not rank students. The College
  Counseling office reports out-of-school suspensions and expulsions through time of
  graduation to any colleges and universities that request this information."

### Program highlights

- **Zero Hour:** "These classes meet Monday, Wednesday, and Friday before the school day
  starts." Varsity Chorus *offered Pass/Fail* · Varsity Orchestra *offered Pass/Fail*.
- **Summer Term:** Geometry Acceleration *offered Pass/Fail* · Honors Biology *offered for a
  grade* · Honors Chemistry *offered for a grade*. "Courses taken in the Summer Term will be
  designated as such on the transcript." (page 3 footnote)

### Signature learning experiences

"Student-driven choice and inquiry define Cannon's signature learning experiences. Students
collaborate with peers and teachers to define a research topic, problem or project, which
they publicly defend in their respective fields."

- **Portfolio Defense:** in Advanced Topics: American Language and Composition, students
  design and present digital portfolios featuring multiple drafts of projects.
- **Symposium:** in Advanced Topics: U.S. History, students choose, trace, and develop an
  original argument about a historical topic.
- **BIOREX:** Biological Research Experience projects in Honors Biology: hypothesis, test,
  statistical analysis, presentation "to a committee of faculty, science professionals, and
  local business leaders. (Not offered during Honors Biology Summer Term.)"
- **Independent Study:** work alongside a faculty member and produce an end-of-year
  artifact.
- **Senior Capstones:** service projects on a philanthropic cause; culminating
  presentations to faculty, staff, peers and members of the community.

**Other notable learning opportunities:** The Mill (and additional Makerspaces), Esports,
Global Education Certificate, Jazz Combo, Technical Theater, and Recording Studio.

and students in AT/AP classes have the option of sitting for the appropriate AP exam. One
year of physical education or athletic participation is required."

Precalculus, AP Statistics, AP Biology, AP Chemistry, AP Physics C Mechanics, AP Physics 1,
AP Psychology, AP Chinese Language and Culture, AP French Language and Culture, AP Spanish

History II; AT: U.S. History; AT: Microeconomics: Politics and Policy; AT: U.S. Government;

> It titles the business course "Advanced Topics: Business and Entrepreneurship with
