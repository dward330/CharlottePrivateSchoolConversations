# Charlotte Christian School — College Support — School Profile 2026-2027

**Provenance:** Transcribed by Claude on 2026-11-03 from the school's own **2026-27 Academic &
Student Profile** (4 pages, Adobe InDesign; PDF creation date 2026-10-27), during the
`christianprofile` plan. The user supplied the URL. The PDF has a text layer: the figures were
read with `pdftotext -layout` and checked against the rendered pages at 110 dpi.
**School:** Charlotte Christian School, 7301 Sardis Road, Charlotte, NC 28270. CEEB 340667.

**Source URL (cite this for every figure below):**
https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1725038322/charlotte/b12msqpqbmeqjqgszan1/2024_USprofile_admiss.pdf

**The URL is misleading. It still says `2024_USprofile`, but it now serves the 2026-27
edition.** The school overwrote the 2024-25 edition at the same Finalsite asset, which is
the same pattern as Cannon's profile on 2026-09-11. Every app citation that labels this URL
"2024-25 Academic & Student Profile" therefore opens a document that contradicts the 2024-25
figures cited next to it. The 2024-25 figures themselves stay on record in
`Charlotte Christian - College Support - Redesign Research 2026.md`.

### Academic Conservatory Program
Students can graduate with distinction in six areas: I. Math and Science; II. Fine Arts;
III. Bible, Philosophy & Religion; IV. English; V. Social Studies; VI. World Languages.
Conservatory achievement is noted on the transcript in the fall and spring of senior year.
**"From the Class of 2026, 20% of graduates received one or more conservatory distinctions."**

### Seal of Biliteracy
Implemented in 2019, based on a proficiency exam in listening, speaking, reading and writing
in two languages. **"From the Class of 2026, 7% of graduates earned this distinction."**

"Charlotte Christian School complies with the NACAC Statement of Principles of Good Practice.
