### ⚠️ Advanced arts coursework is "1 AP + 12 Honors", never an AP-only count

CCHS offers exactly **one** AP arts course — **AP Studio Art** (`358`). It offers **no** AP
Music Theory and **no** AP Art History; this was confirmed by a full sweep of all 195
courses in the guide, not inferred from absence on a web page. Reporting "1 AP course"
alone would make a 39-course department with twelve Honors ensembles and studios read as
thin. The honest framing, used in the app, is **1 AP + 12 Honors**.

The twelve Honors arts courses: Honors Portfolio Prep · Honors Art IV · Honors Women's
Chamber Ensemble · Honors Men's Chamber Ensemble · Honors Concert Choir · Honors Costume
Design and Production · Honors Theatre: Design and Tech · Honors Acting · Honors TV, Film,
and Production · Honors Dance III · Honors Wind Ensemble · Honors Percussion.

### ⚠️ CCHS won NO Blumey award in 2026

Checked directly against the winners release (S7). The 2026 winners in the five individual
performance categories went to North Lincoln, Carmel Christian, Northwest School of the
Arts (×2) and Weddington. **A "wins" claim for CCHS would be factually wrong**, and none is
made anywhere in the app.

### Other recognition

- Students submit to the **Charlotte-Mecklenburg Scholastic Art Awards**, earning Gold,
  Silver and Honorable Mention (S2).
- **The band performs quadrennially in the St. Patrick's Day Parade in Dublin, Ireland**;
  band and choral programs have performed in **New York** and **Chicago** (S8).
- **Partnership with the Charlotte Symphony** — orchestra members provide sectionals and
  private lessons to CCHS students (S2).
- **Three Fine Arts Festivals** plus the annual school musical each year (S2).
- The choral program tours and undertakes service projects across the Diocese of Charlotte.
