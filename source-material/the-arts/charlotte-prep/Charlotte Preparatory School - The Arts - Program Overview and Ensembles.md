# Charlotte Preparatory School — The Arts — Program Overview and Ensembles

Charlotte Preparatory School (PreK–8, 212 Boyce Road, Charlotte) teaches music, drama and visual art to every student from Kindergarten through 4th grade, then lets students narrow their arts choices in Middle School (grades 5–8). The school says students take part in "20+ Arts performances and events per year" (Fast Facts, https://charlotteprep.org/about/fast-facts, and the Fall 2025 Admissions Packet, which words it "20+ art performances and events per year"). No count of how those events break down is published.

### The arts ladder, grade by grade

| Grades | What the school publishes |
|---|---|
| Early School (age 2 to 5) | "All early school students participate in music classes. Montessori Kindergarten students have weekly art, P.E., Spanish, and Tech classes." (Fall 2025 Admissions Packet) |
| K–4 | "Students in Kindergarten through 4th Grade participate routinely in a 6-day rotation of specialized classes ... Students engage in Fine Arts classes (music, drama, and art), Technology and STEM, Spanish, and Physical Education." (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/lower-school) |
| K–2 | "Our youngest Lower School students (Kindergarten-2nd Grade) shine on stage annually in age-appropriate, curricular-themed costumed productions" (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/fine-arts) |
| 4–8 | Band. The Fine Arts page lists "recorders, Orff instruments and band for grades 4-8". The school's band teacher is titled "Middle School Band and 4th Grade Music Teacher". |
| 5–6 | "Students may choose between Chorus and Band as 5th graders, while they continue to take both Drama and Art." (Fall 2025 Admissions Packet) |
| 7–8 | "Once they are in 7th grade they may choose between Drama and Art." (Fall 2025 Admissions Packet) |
| 5–8 | "Middle School students further investigate dramatic arts in year-long electives, introducing students to every facet of theater including performance and stage production opportunities." (Fine Arts page) |
| 3–8 | "our after-school theater arts program features student performances where 3rd-8th Grade artists come together to produce and perform on the stage for the school community." (Fine Arts page) |

How often each art meets inside the 6-day rotation is not published. The Admissions Packet carries sample daily schedules for the Early School only, and no Lower School or Middle School timetable is posted anywhere on the site.

The packet does not say whether 7th and 8th graders keep a music ensemble alongside their Drama-or-Art choice. The school's own concert recordings show a Middle School chorus and band performing together (see below), and a 2022 story names "the middle school 7/8 choir", so a 7th–8th grade choir has existed.

### Where the music is heard

- **Two showcases a year.** A Winter Fine Arts Showcase in December and a Spring Fine Arts Showcase in May, both recorded by the school: Spring 2023 (https://vimeo.com/823744458), Winter 2023 (December 14, 2023, https://vimeo.com/894640249), Spring 2024 (https://vimeo.com/947518792), Winter 2024 (title card December 19, 2024, https://vimeo.com/1041163811), Spring 2025 (https://vimeo.com/1088013904), Winter 2025 (https://vimeo.com/1147741958) and Spring 2026 (https://vimeo.com/1194125604). The recordings show the band and chorus on the stage at the end of the gymnasium.
- **Grandparents & Special Friends' Day performance**, recorded in April 2023, April 2024 and November 21, 2025 (https://vimeo.com/820557101, https://vimeo.com/938294345, https://vimeo.com/1139741166).
- **School assemblies**: the Veterans Day program and the International Day of Peace (both above).
- **Off campus**, in the school's own words: "Charlotte Prep's choral groups have performed at the Biltmore Estate, Charlotte Knights and Hornets games, and with Distinguished Concerts International New York at Carnegie Hall." (Fine Arts page). No dates are given for any of these.

### The Carnegie Hall claim is the school's own and is undated

The Carnegie Hall sentence has been on the Fine Arts page since at least December 2021, and the 2022 viewbook repeats it ("everywhere from local venues to Carnegie Hall", https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/char_intro_singles_fin). No year, concert or repertoire is published. No school news story describes the trip, and no Distinguished Concerts International New York (DCINY) page or program naming Charlotte Prep could be found. The 2015 music page does not mention it. Treat it as a school-reported performance with no published date.

A separate Carnegie Hall mention should not be confused with it. In October 2024 the school hosted a benefit concert by the Sphinx Virtuosi, a professional chamber orchestra, whose "next stop on their tour is Carnegie Hall". That was a visiting ensemble, not Charlotte Prep students.

### Visual arts

From the Fine Arts page, verbatim: "Art is taught as a spiral curriculum woven around artistic principles, concepts, and habits, growing in complexity from year to year. Students participate in the traditional fine art mediums of drawing, painting, printmaking, and sculpture, as well as collage, weaving, and digital photography. The curriculum is shaped in part by current events, students' interests, and past art experiences."

The school's stated goal: "that a Prep graduate manifests a continuing interest and enthusiasm for the arts and art career options, whether expressed through active participation or informed appreciation."

The arts curriculum has been the subject of teacher summer grants: an "Art curriculum scope and sequence grant for grades K-8" and a "4th-grade music curriculum alignment grant" (2022-23 Prep Fund, https://charlotteprep.org/support/the-prep-fund), and a "Lower School Immersive Art Exhibition Development" project in summer 2026 (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/building-the-foundations-for-student-learning). No description of that exhibition has been published yet.

### Arts in the after-school studios

After School Studios run each fall and spring, last about 12 weeks, and carry "a fee, separate from Charlotte Prep's annual tuition" (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/after-school). The page labels these as "Sample Studio offerings", and they include these arts options:

| Studio | Grades | Notes as published |
|---|---|---|
| Storybook Theater | K–2 | ends in "an informal virtual Reader's Theatre performance" |
| One-Act Play | 3–8 | students audition for and perform a one-act play |
| Ukulele | 3–5 | taught by Ms. Moscati |
| Beginner Print Making | 3–5 | taught by Ms. Mould, in the Art Studio, 3:15–4:00 |
| Intermediate Print Making | 6–8 | taught by Ms. Mould, 3:15–4:00 |
| Primary Chorus | 1–2 | invited to sing at Knights Stadium and Bojangles Coliseum |
| Beginner Chorus | 3–6 | open to "3rd and 4th grade students; 5th or 6th Grade Band students"; meets in the Music Cottage before school, 7:15–8am |

These are labelled samples, and the page carries no date. Two of the instructors it names, a drama teacher "Mrs. Stafford" and a chorus director "Ms. Stefanini", do not appear in the current faculty directory. Treat this list as an example of what has been offered rather than the current term's catalog. Studio prices are not published.

### Arts spaces

- **The stage in the gymnasium.** Productions and showcases are staged in "the Charlotte Prep gymnasium". It has a raised proscenium stage with curtains. In the 2023-24 Prep Fund year the school paid for stage lighting costing $180,000, funded by "$65,000 Auction Proceeds 2024", a "$35,000 2021 Parent Fundraiser" and "$80,000 Prep Fund" (2024-2025 Calendar and Annual Report, https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/2024-2025_calendar_and_annual_report). The 2024-25 Prep Fund then bought "Brand new theater sets including 4 periaktos", "6 fabricated wall panels with removable top" and "Brand new digital lighting for our stage and proscenium". Separately, new LED gym lights "installed last year" were a gift from the parents of the Class of 2023 (https://vimeo.com/1026616876, November 5, 2024).
- **The Music Cottage** is where the before-school chorus studio meets (After School page). No description of it is published.
- **The Art Studio** is where the printmaking studio meets (After School page). No description of it is published.
- An earlier 2018-2019 Fine Arts Campaign paid for "the new sound system", "the band instruments", "art room upgrades and new stage lights" (2018-2019 Impact Report, https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/2018-2019_annual_report).

Since 2024-25, fine arts fees are included in tuition: "We have included the fees for athletics, fine arts, and technology in tuition for the 2024-25 school year and will continue to do so moving forward." (https://charlotteprep.org/about/strategic-plan-2022-2027)

## Method

Retrieved 2026-10-19 for the Charlotte Prep research pass.

How the data was gathered:
- The live sitemap (https://charlotteprep.org/sitemap.xml, which points to /cache/sitemap.xml.gz) lists 178 URLs, 171 of them unique, including 113 news stories. Every page was fetched with curl as raw HTML and converted to text locally, and quotes were copied from that text. The site's own search was not used.
- The Fall 2025 Admissions Packet PDF was extracted with pdftotext -layout. It has 6 pages, was created August 19, 2024, and is titled "Fall 2025 Admissions Packet".
- Issuu documents were read by fetching each page image listed in reader3.isu.pub/charlottepreparatoryschool/<doc>/reader3_4.json. The image host returned 403 to a short user-agent and 200 to a full browser user-agent string with Referer https://issuu.com/. The pages were OCR'd with the macOS Vision framework. The documents were the 2022 viewbook (char_intro_singles_fin), the 2018-2019 Impact Report, the 2023-24 calendar and 2022-23 annual report, the 2024-2025 Calendar and Annual Report, and the 2024-2025 Annual Report.
- Vimeo, https://vimeo.com/charlotteprep. The public API https://vimeo.com/api/v2/charlotteprep/videos.json?page=1..3 returned the newest 60 videos (page 4 onward returned 403). The full channel list of 137 videos came from a headed Chrome session paging https://vimeo.com/charlotteprep/videos/page:N/sort:date. Upload dates and descriptions came from https://vimeo.com/api/oembed.json. Performance dates were read off each video's title-card thumbnail.
- Wayback Machine snapshots: Fine Arts page 20211206171942 (https://www.charlotteprep.org/student-life/fine-arts), music page 20150806224433, visual arts page 20150806224540 and theater page 20160331073126.
- Faculty profiles came from https://charlotteprep.org/about/faculty-staff-directory?id=455382 (Davis), ?id=455423 (Moscati), ?id=493619 (Miller), ?id=455424 (Mould) and ?id=493938 (Sweer).

History, for context only and not current:
- The 2015 music page said "Music is available to all students, with Band and Chorus offered in Middle School". The current pages extend band down to grade 4.
- The 2015 visual arts page mapped the Four Pillars onto art-making ("Perseverance: in art making; reflecting and revising, trying one's best").

Conflicts and how they were recorded:
- Where band starts. The 2015 page said Middle School; the current Fine Arts page says grades 4–8. The current page is followed, and the old wording is kept here as history only.
- Student count. Fast Facts says 440, while the Fall 2025 Admissions Packet says 425. This is outside this area and is noted for the admissions file.
- The Admissions Packet's "100% of graduates admitted to one or both of their top two choice high schools in 2024" is outside this area.
- The after-school studio list is labelled "Sample" and names two instructors who are not in the current directory. It is presented as an example list, not the current offering.
- Brooklyn Miller is listed under Lower School and Middle School but not under the Fine Arts category, unlike the other four arts teachers. This is recorded as published.

Corrections to the indicative sweep:
- The sweep's "per-grade frequency unknown; check the packet's sample schedules" is now resolved. The packet has sample schedules only for the Early School (Pre-Primary and Primary), so the frequency is confirmed not published for K–8.
- The sweep had faculty "in the staff directory". There are five named arts staff with credentials, as tabled above, including a long-term substitute band teacher in 2026.
- New, not in the sweep: the $180,000 stage-lighting project and its funding split, the 2024-25 set and lighting purchases, the NCMEA job posting, and the Early School and Kindergarten music performances.

Confirmed not published (searched across all 171 sitemap pages, the 6-page packet, the viewbook and the five Issuu reports):
- How many times per 6-day rotation each art meets in K–4.
- Whether 7th–8th graders keep a music ensemble alongside Drama or Art. This is not stated, though a 7/8 choir is evidenced.
- Any strings or orchestra program. "Orchestra" and "strings" appear only in the Sphinx Virtuosi story and the ukulele studio.
- A date, concert or program for the Carnegie Hall performance.
- Descriptions of the Music Cottage or the Art Studio.
- Studio fees.

Not found (searched less exhaustively):
- Any DCINY concert program naming Charlotte Prep. A web search of dciny.org found none, but DCINY's full participant archive was not enumerated.
