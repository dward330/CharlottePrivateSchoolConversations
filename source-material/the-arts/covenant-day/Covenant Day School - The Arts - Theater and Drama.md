### The season — three productions a year plus showcases

The program's annual performance slate, per the school's own pages:

- **Middle School Musical** (October run, e.g. Oct 15-17).
- **High School Musical** (spring; calendar shows March 8).
- **Spring Drama** (April; calendar shows April 25-27).
- **Middle School Theater Night** and **High School Theater Night** showcases.
- **High School Shakespeare Competition** (fed the 2025 National Shakespeare
  Competition finalist, Caroline Garbarino).
- **North Carolina Theater Conference** participation.

The school lists production *categories*, not titles; the one recoverable named
production is ***Curtains*** (the 2019 Blumey Best Actress nomination for Evan
Bertram as Georgia Hendricks).

### The venue

**Lions Theater** — a 200-seat house with LED lighting, a costume room, and set
storage (from the school's own pages via site search).

### Courses and co-curriculars

- Middle School: Drama (grades 6-8).
- High School: **Theater I-II**; **Auditioned Theater** with **Performance or
  Technical track** (Honors-weighted per the profile matrix); **Theatre Tech** as
  its own CP course.
- Co-curricular: **International Thespian Society** chapter.

### Mission statement

"Give students the opportunity to explore their creative abilities through
theater, inspiring them to use their gifts to be the salt and light of this world."
