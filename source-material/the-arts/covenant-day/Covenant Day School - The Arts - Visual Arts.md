### The course path — Intro to AP

High school sequence, per the school's own page: "Intro to Art, Art 2/3 (2D Art and
3D Art), Honors Studio Art, AP Studio Art, Digital Photography, Graphic Design,
Ceramics, Yearbook." The profile matrix confirms the weighting: Intro to Art / Art
2D/3D / Ceramics / Digital Photography / Graphic Design / Yearbook at CP, Studio
Art at Honors, **AP Studio Art** as the terminal course. Art is integrated JK-K and
sequential from grade 1.

### The exhibition circuit

- **ArtWalk** — the all-school art exhibit.
- **Art Talk** — the AP Art exhibit (AP students present their portfolios).
- **Pineville Art Show**, **Youth Art Month (YAM)**, **Mint Hill Arts
  Competition**, **Scholastic Art & Writing Awards**.

### Recognition

"Multiple Scholastic Art Award Winners"; **7 YAM honorees in 2023**; some students
"have even published their work."

### Faculty

**Katie Spata — High School Art Teacher.** The program aims to "cultivate personal
development and a sense of joy and confidence in students as they create and share
their artwork," integrating art history and design principles with "God-given
creativity."
