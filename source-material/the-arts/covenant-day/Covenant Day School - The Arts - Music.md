### The ensemble ladder — 9 named groups plus an honor society

- **Lower School:** General Music (JK-4) → Introduction to Choir and Band (grade 5);
  **CDS Children's Choir (grades 3-5)** as the first named ensemble.
- **Middle School:** MS Band (6-8), MS Choir (6-8), **MS Worship Band**.
- **High School:** Symphonic Band, HS Choir, **HS Worship Band**, **HS A Cappella**.
- **Co-curricular:** **Tri-M Music Honor Society** chapter.

Worship bands at both divisions are the distinctive rung — course-credited worship
leadership exists too (Worship Leadership is a CP Fine Arts course in the profile
matrix).

### Adjudication record (Music in the Parks)

- 2021, 2022, 2023 — MS Choir **Superior** rating, three consecutive years.
- 2021-2023 — MS Band **Excellent** rating and 3rd placement.
- 2023 — HS Band Excellent rating.
- 2025 — HS Band **Excellent rating and 2nd placement**.

### Where they play

Performances include Biltmore Estates, all-school events, Christmas programs,
sporting events, and spring concerts. **Director: Zach Thompson, Worship and Choir
Teacher.**

### Not published

No NCMEA All-State/All-District honors appear on the music page (confirmed
absence), and no marching band exists — the band program is concert/symphonic.
