### The program's shape

- **Span: JK/TK–12.** Art is integrated in JK-K and becomes sequential from grade 1;
  general music runs JK-4 with grade-5 introduction to choir and band; dedicated
  ensembles begin in grade 6.
- Organized as three areas — **Visual Arts, Music, Theater** — with a support
  organization, the **Covenant Arts Association** (four patron tiers, $100-$1,000,
  funding and volunteering across all arts areas; contact Sherie-Beth Wilson).
- **Fine Arts Director: Sherie-Beth Wilson** (also on the HS profile's
  administration block). One **Fine Arts graduation unit is required**; the school
  states arts are "a crucial part of the curriculum, through which students
  acknowledge God and take responsibility in the creative production, expression,
  understanding, evaluation, and response to art," built on four components:
  "foundations, creative expression, historical and cultural relevance, and
  critical evaluation and response."
- High school Fine Arts course matrix (2026-2027): CP — Choir, Symphonic Band,
  Theatre, Theatre Tech, Worship Leadership, Intro to Art, Art 2D/3D, Ceramics,
  Digital Photography, Graphic Design, Yearbook; Honors — Choir, Auditioned
  Theatre, Symphonic Band, Studio Art, Yearbook; AP — Studio Art.
- ~9-10 named ensembles across divisions (see the Music file for the roster).
