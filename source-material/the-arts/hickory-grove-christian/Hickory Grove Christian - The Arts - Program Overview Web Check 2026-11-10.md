## Arts overview (A1)

- Motto "Soli Deo Gloria: To the Glory of God Alone"; a Fine Arts program from the Early
  Education Center through 12th grade.
- "Band, choir, theatre, dance and worship leadership give students a stage to grow in
  confidence".
- **Throughout the year, students:** perform in chapel and end-of-semester concerts; take
  part in "At least one adjudicated band festival each year, along with auditions for NCBA
  honor bands"; stage **two drama productions: a fall show and a spring musical**; sing in
  "Choir performances, tours, and community events"; show artwork around campus and at
  school events, in the Silent Auction and in competitions; produce "The annual Exodus
  yearbook and a weekly school broadcast produced by students".

## Performing arts (A2)

- **Overview:** each year students perform in chapel, end-of-semester concerts, band
  festivals, two drama productions and a spring musical.
- **Elementary:** students explore music in Music class, and "Elementary Choir offers more
  chances to sing and perform." (More elementary details "coming soon".)
- **Middle School** ("every course ends with a chance to perform"):
  - Beginner Band (6th): "Students learn the basics of an instrument: proper technique,
    music reading, rhythm and tone." No experience needed.
  - Intermediate Band (7th–8th): "Students build on band fundamentals through rehearsals
    and performances, growing in technique, discipline and teamwork."
  - Praise Team (7th–8th): "For students who want to use their musical gifts to lead others
    in worship."
  - Theatre (7th–8th): improv, pantomime, acting, an introduction to tech roles; each
    quarter ends with a one-act play for invited guests.
  - Musical Theatre (7th–8th): rotate through dance, singing and acting; perform one or two
    songs at semester's end.
  - Beginning Dance (7th–8th): basic dance principles, modern dance; no experience needed.
- **High School** (choir, theatre and musical theatre classes each combine all levels):
  - High School Choir: "One choir for singers at every level." Two- to four-part singing,
    music reading; performs several times each semester.
  - Concert Band: requires two years of middle school band or an audition; each spring
    performs at the ACSI Festival; students can audition for NCBA All-District, All-State
    and Solo & Ensemble.
  - Chapel Band: "Students audition, rehearse and help our worship pastors lead student-led
    chapel."
  - Theatre; Musical Theatre ("jazz, tap, singing, and acting"; the spring musical "takes
    part in the Blumey Awards each year"); Technical Theatre & Management; Introduction to
    Public Speaking.
  - International Thespian Society: "Eligible theatre students can be inducted into the
    International Thespian Society".
- **Not on the page:** Tri-M, Jazz Band, orchestra, a cappella, tours (A1 mentions choir
  tours).

## Visual arts (A3)

- Preschool through 12th grade; artwork displayed across campus, featured in the Silent
  Auction, entered in competitions.
- **Elementary:** sketching and drawing first, then painting and other media; elements of
  art and principles of design; artists from many cultures and movements.
- **Middle School:** Bible Sketch (6th grade); Sewing (7th–8th grade).
- **High School:** Art I–IV and AP 2D Studio Art (a 15-piece body of work driven by the
  student's own essential question); Ceramics I and II; Fiber Crafts I and II; Art
  Appreciation ("open to all students regardless of art background").
- **Not on the page:** AP score statements, any honor society, a named gallery or exhibit.

## Digital arts (A4)

High school pathway: Yearbook (the *Exodus* TK–12 yearbook; editors; a Senior Magazine each
spring) · Introduction to Photography and Design · Digital Media I (10th–12th) and II
(11th–12th), which produce *HG360*, the weekly student broadcast · Communications
Internship (by application).

## Course Selection Catalog 2026–27 (school document)

Lists Concert Band (I–IV), Choir/Ensemble I–IV, Advanced Placement 2D Studio Art, Ceramics
I–II, Fiber Crafts, Yearbook I, Yearbook II and Yearbook III - Honors. **No Jazz Band
course.**
