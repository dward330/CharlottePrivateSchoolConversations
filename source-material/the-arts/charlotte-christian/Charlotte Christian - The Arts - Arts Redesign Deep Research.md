### Performance venue — Center for Worship and Performing Arts

**[VERIFIED]** The performance venue on the Sardis Road campus is the **Center for Worship and Performing Arts**. Friends of the Arts confirms "productions take place at the Center for Worship and Performing Arts."
Source: https://www.charlottechristian.com/fine-arts/friends-of-the-arts

**[VERIFIED]** Building specs: a **two-story** facility completed **Fall 2017** as **Phase 1 of the Master Campus Plan**, built on an **11-month** construction timeline, housing an auditorium capable of full-scale theatrical productions plus weekly chapels, assemblies, and band/choir/orchestra concerts. Published square footage differs slightly by source: **20,515 sq ft** (school/architect) vs **20,775 sq ft** (builder Rodgers).
Sources: https://www.charlottechristian.com/about/master-campus-plan, https://www.ls3p.com/portfolio/charlotte-christian-school/, https://www.rodgersbuilders.com/projects/charlotte-christian-school-2/

**IMPORTANT — the facility contains more than the main auditorium. [VERIFIED]** The general contractor's project page documents three features the school's own fine-arts pages never mention, and they materially upgrade the assessment of this program:

- **A black box theater** for smaller productions — a genuine second performance space
- **A scene shop** for set construction — dedicated tech/build space, not a converted classroom
- **Dressing rooms**

Source: https://www.rodgersbuilders.com/projects/charlotte-christian-school-2/

**Why this matters:** the earlier concern that "the theatre is really a chapel" is **substantially mitigated**. A black box plus a scene shop means the program has a rehearsal/studio-scale venue that does *not* compete with the chapel calendar, and a real place to build sets. This is stronger infrastructure than the school markets — and it also supplies the physical basis for the technical-directing and stage-management electives. **Confirm both spaces on a tour**, since the school itself does not advertise them.

**Seat count — 518 seats. [VERIFIED]** The architect of record, **LS3P**, states the Worship and Performing Arts Center seats **518**, on its own project page for this school. As the designer's published figure, this is the most authoritative source available.
Source: https://www.ls3p.com/portfolio/charlotte-christian-school/

Other published figures, for transparency:
- **513 seats** — appears in the builder/architect source family
- **520 seats** — an earlier school announcement, https://www.charlottechristian.com/cf_news/view.cfm?newsid=1205 (likely a pre-construction projection)

**Recommended app figure: 518 seats (architect LS3P).** If a single hedge is preferred, "~515–520." Do not use 520 as the primary number.

**Design note:** the venue is explicitly dual-purpose (worship + performance). For parents comparing against a dedicated 500+ seat proscenium theater at a peer school, this is a real distinction — the room serves chapel first and theatre second, though it is purpose-built to handle full-scale productions.

## 1e. Verdict

### Concrete reasons the program holds up

1. **A top-prize Blumey win, plus proof the program is still competitive.** The 2013 *Oklahoma!* **Best Musical** win is confirmed directly by Blumenthal Arts and came as a **four-award sweep** — Best Ensemble/Chorus, Best Choreography Execution, and **Best Lighting Execution** — signaling technical capability, not just strong singing, against a 17-school field. And in **2024**, *Tarzan* earned **finalist slots in all five performance categories** (Best Actor, Actress, Supporting Actor, Supporting Actress, Featured Performer) — a whole-cast result, in a field that had grown to 50. **[VERIFIED]** https://www.blumenthalarts.org/news/detail/announcing-2013-blumey-award-winners and https://www.blumenthalarts.org/assets/doc/2024-Blumey-All-Nominees-and-Finalists-c0f5a7ba22.pdf

2. **A purpose-built 2017 arts facility with TWO performance spaces and a scene shop.** A **518-seat** (LS3P) Center for Worship and Performing Arts, ~20,500–20,800 sq ft, Phase 1 of the master plan — and per the general contractor it also contains a **black box theater**, a **scene shop** for set construction, and **dressing rooms**. Backed by a real ticketing operation (online sales, PDF tickets, HomeTown Fan app) and a themed subscription season. That is infrastructure, not aspiration — and notably, better than the school itself markets. **[VERIFIED]** https://www.rodgersbuilders.com/projects/charlotte-christian-school-2/

3. **A transcript-level arts credential and three honor societies.** Students can graduate **"with distinction" in Fine Arts** via the Academic Conservatory Program, and all three branches have honor societies (**International Thespian Society, National Art Honor Society, Tri-M**). Few peer schools formalize arts achievement on the transcript this way. **[VERIFIED]**

4. **Digital Arts is genuinely resourced, with professional software.** Nine electives on **Logic Pro X, Ableton, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Lightroom** — industry tools, in a dedicated Digital Media Studio, inside an Apple Distinguished School environment. This is the most concretely evidenced branch and the one where the school's tech identity and arts program reinforce each other. **[VERIFIED]**

5. **Breadth of entry and a named curated gallery.** Mainstage musicals cast **grades 4–12** (elementary students on the mainstage), instrumental music starts in **Grade 5** with brass/woodwind/strings tracks, and the **Huge Gallery's "Blue Ribbon Collection"** curates work from all three divisions year-round. Wide on-ramps plus year-round visibility. **[VERIFIED]**

6. **A funded parent arts organization with real line items.** Friends of the Arts has seven giving tiers up to $7,500 and demonstrably funds **commissioned compositions, guest artists, instrument inventory, and faculty PD** — not just concession stands. **[VERIFIED]**

### Honest treatment of faith integration

The faith integration here is **structural, not decorative** — and that cuts both ways for a comparing parent.

**Where it is a genuine asset [VERIFIED]:** **uKnight Worship** is a real credit-bearing Upper School worship ensemble. The **CITA** circuit gives students a competitive arena where the school's identity is native rather than incidental, and it is the venue where the program has its most recent verified competitive breadth (six placements in 2016 across ensemble, solo, duo, monologue, and costume design). The main venue is literally named the Center for **Worship** and Performing Arts and hosts weekly chapel.

**Where a parent should probe:** the main auditorium is dual-purpose — a chapel that also does theatre, on a shared calendar. (The **black box theater** meaningfully softens this, since it gives the program a second space that does not compete with chapel.) More substantively, repertoire runs through a faith lens: the verified show list (*Oklahoma!*, *Shrek*, *Footloose*, *Beauty and the Beast*, *Joseph*, *Into the Woods*, *Tarzan*, *Freaky Friday*, plus a 2025-26 "Pure Imagination" season) is mainstream family musical theatre with **none** of the edgier contemporary canon (*Spring Awakening*, *Rent*, *Cabaret*). For a student who wants that range, this is a real constraint — and it is **[NOT FOUND]** whether any formal repertoire policy exists or whether it is simply convention.
