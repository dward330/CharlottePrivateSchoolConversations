# Gaston Day School — Student Clubs (Club Catalog and Overview)

**Provenance**

- **Compiled by:** Claude Code deep research pass (`/implement add-gaston-day`).
- **Date compiled:** 2026-08-18
- **Method:** The Upper School page and the Academic Profile were fetched directly for the
  school's own statements (club policy, service awards, Honor Code, Capstone, robotics).
  **The enumerated club roster is reconstructed from third-party listings** — Private School
  Review and Niche — because the school publishes no dated, complete club list of its own;
  its Upper School page names only exemplar clubs ("3-D Printing Club to Green Team to
  International Council") and states that students may start new ones. iGEM participation
  confirmed from the archived iGEM team wikis.
- **School:** Gaston Day School, Gastonia, NC.

> **VERIFY FLAG.** The club roster below is **third-party-sourced and undated**. It is not a
> school-published list, club membership changes year to year, and a student-founded club
> can appear or lapse without any listing updating. Counts derived from it carry a `verify`
> flag in the app. This is a deliberate provenance caveat, not a gap to be closed by
> guessing.

## Additional clubs (S2, S3 — third-party or exemplar mentions)

Interact · Mock Trial · Quiz Bowl · FCA (Fellowship of Christian Athletes) · Art Club ·
Spirit Club · Junior Heart Board · International Club · **3-D Printing Club** ·
**Green Team** · **International Council**

The last three are named by the school itself (S3); the rest are third-party listings
carrying the verify caveat. Student Government operates at **both Middle School and Upper
School** level.

**Club formation policy (S3):** students can **start a new club by securing a faculty
sponsor** — so the roster is intentionally open-ended and any enumeration is a snapshot.
