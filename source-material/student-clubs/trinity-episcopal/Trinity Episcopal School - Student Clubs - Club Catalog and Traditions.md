### Girls on the Run — the one LS club with real substance

Trinity was the organization's **second independent-school chapter**, it "continues to be
a popular program for students in **grades 3-5**", and founder **Molly Barker** is a
Trinity alumni parent.

### Chess Club is a paid enrichment, not a club

From the TED page, verbatim:
> "TED also offers enrichment programs, **for an additional fee**, several days a week…
> Examples of enrichments include **chess club, music lessons, karate, cheer club** and
> more."

Note "Examples of… and more" — **this is not an exhaustive enrichment list**, and none of
them are priced anywhere.

## Signature traditions

These are the school's own named rituals, and several are structural rather than
club-like.

### Koinonia — the Middle School advisory system

From MS Enrichments, verbatim:
> "each student belongs to a **Koinonia with 10-12 other Middle School students and one
> adult leader**. Koinonia is a Greek word for 'fellowship' or 'communion' often appearing
> in the New Testament… Koinonia groups gather **every morning for check-in and twice a
> week for activities** that include team-building games; tasks that help develop group
> identity; lessons around diversity, belonging and identity; intramural sports,
> challenges between Koinonias, and discussions specific to the needs of our Middle School
> community."

Its stated purpose:
> "ensures that each student has at least one adult in the school community s/he knows
> well…"

### 12 Tribes of Trinity — the Lower School analogue

From LS Enrichments (fuller than the affinity-groups version), verbatim:
> "Based on the Biblical 12 Tribes of Israel, Trinity Tribes are **co-ed groups of
> students in grades 3-5** that provide the opportunity for students from different grade
> levels to get to know one another, engage in meaningful activities together, and build
> relationships. **Each Tribe is led by 2 adult staculty members.**"

### Science Buddies — 8th grade ↔ Kindergarten

From `/academics/middle-school.cfm`, verbatim:
> "Our **8th Graders cherish the time they spend with our kindergartners as Science
> Buddies** and look forward to their class trip to Washington, D. C., where they meet with
> lobbyists and legislators to advocate for a social issue they've studied and researched
> all year in their Social Issues Seminar class."

### RISE — 5th grade transition programme

From LS Enrichments, verbatim:
> "In the last trimester of **5th Grade**, students split into **gender-based small groups
> for the RISE program** to develop personal leadership skills that build confidence for
> the transition to Middle School."

### Greet the Week — Monday mornings

From `/spirituality/chapel.cfm`, verbatim:
> "All staculty and students gather in **Jamie's Courtyard** each Monday morning to start
> the week with scripture, prayer, songs, the Pledge of Allegiance, our Honor Code and
> general student-led school announcements for that week."

The profile PDF's shorter version:
> "Monday mornings, the school gathers as a community to begin the week with songs, Bible
> verses, the Lord's Prayer and announcements."

**Chapel rhythm:** bi-weekly K–2 and 3–5 chapels, weekly 6–8 chapel, and a **K–8 chapel
every Friday in the Gym**.

## ⚠️ "Staculty"

The school's own portmanteau for staff + faculty, used throughout its site and in blog
post titles. It is **not a typo** — preserve it inside quotations.
