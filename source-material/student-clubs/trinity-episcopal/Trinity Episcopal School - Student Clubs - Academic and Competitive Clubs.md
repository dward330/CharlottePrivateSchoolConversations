## ⭐ Mock Trial — three consecutive state championships

**The strongest competitive achievement Trinity has, and it is academic rather than
athletic.** It appears in **neither** School Profile PDF and was found only by
enumerating the blog.

- Competition: the **North Carolina Bar Foundation**'s annual Mock Trial competition
- Level: **Middle School**
- Team size: **15 students**
- Coach: **Jennifer Bader**
- **Champions in 2023, 2024 and 2025 — three consecutive years**

The second-year championship was competed at the **Mecklenburg County Courthouse on
Dec. 8**, against **five other middle school teams**. Individual student recognitions
were published; per the rule above they are counted, not named.

Coach **Jennifer Bader**, verbatim:
> "I think one thing that sets our team apart is how well our attorneys can argue the
> rules of evidence and handle objections."

> "I just love when the light bulb comes on for kids. It's really challenging material,
> but they really get into it."

## Speech & Debate — also a state champion, and also a class

From "Celebrating TES Staculty Achievements", verbatim:

> "The **speech and debate and mock trial teams both won their respective state
> championships this year.**"

**Speech & Debate is simultaneously a 7th-grade weekly enrichment** (per the profile
PDF's enrichment list) **and a competitive team** — i.e. it is both a class and a club.
Worth stating explicitly, because listing it only as an enrichment understates it.

**Jennifer Bader** is **Speech and Debate and Mock Trial Team Coach**, and has served as
**Forensics Committee Chair** and a board member of the **North Carolina Association for
Scholastic Activities**.

## Honor Council — confirmed

From `/spirituality/honor-code.cfm`, verbatim:

> "A group of honor council members who are **elected by their grade level peers in
> Middle School** are responsible for encouraging integrity, for creating a community of
> trust that is core to our academic community and in life, and for holding fellow
> students to the expectations set out in the pledge."

**The Honor Code was created by students in 2002.** The Middle School **Honor Pledge**
was adopted in **2012**, verbatim:

> "As a child of God, I pledge that I have not cheated by giving or receiving help on
> this work, including plagiarizing. Moreover, I am unaware of any violation of this
> pledge by other students."

The Honor Code itself carries six pledges: responsibility, doing one's best, respecting
others' uniqueness, kindness, celebrating joy and beauty, and refraining from dishonesty
or theft.

General leadership framing from `/admission/k-8-advantage.cfm`, verbatim:
> "eighth graders often find themselves as the captains of sports teams, **leading honor
> councils**, and assuming the leading roles in school productions."

## The full club list — the profile PDF is the ONLY source for most of it

Verbatim from the 2026-27 School Profile:

> "**Student Clubs & Groups: Lower School-** Chess Club, Girls on the Run & Let Me Run,
> Young Children of Color, **Middle School-** Odyssey of the Mind, Young People of Color,
> Young People for Social Justice Group, Gender Sexuality Alliance Group, Radagast
> Rabies, Creative Writing, Climate Justice, Koinonia Representatives, Honor Council,
> Invisible Differences, Spirituality and Chapel Music, Monday Math Mania"

**Only 4 of the 14 are corroborated anywhere on the website.** Zero web hits for:
Odyssey of the Mind, Radagast Rabies, Climate Justice, Monday Math Mania, Let Me Run,
Spirituality and Chapel Music, Creative Writing (which appears only as a teacher's
conference topic, not a club).

**Two are corroborated but in a different character than "club":**
- **Chess Club** — corroborated only as a **paid TED after-school enrichment**
  ("for an additional fee"), not as a student club.
- **Girls on the Run** — corroborated: Trinity was the organization's **second
  independent-school chapter**, it "continues to be a popular program for students in
  **grades 3-5**", and founder **Molly Barker** is a Trinity alumni parent.

**Invisible Differences is a PARENT group, not a student club** — see the affinity-groups
file. The profile PDF lists it under student clubs, which conflicts with the school's own
affinity-groups page.

**Consequence:** cite the profile PDF as the authority for the club roster, and expect
**no per-club descriptions to exist** for most of them — none are published.
