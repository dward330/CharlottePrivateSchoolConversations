**Provenance.** Compiled 2026-07-26 by Claude Code for the Student Clubs sub-section
redesign (cards 1a Affinity & Identity Groups, 1b Service & Civic Engagement,
1c Honor Societies). Method: live fetches of providenceday.org plus Wayback Machine

**Confidence key.** VERIFIED = read from a live page. ARCHIVED = read from a dated Wayback
capture because the live page 404s. NOT FOUND = explicitly searched and absent.

below therefore comes from dated Wayback captures, and the live page's silence is itself the

### Flags — 1a

**GAP (not a count discrepancy).** There is **no advertised "N affinity groups" figure** to
conflict with the roster. The live EIB page, the "Why Providence Day" admissions page, Get
Involved, and the archived reports were all checked: the live EIB page presents affinity
groups as **four image tiles** (Lower School / Middle School / Upper School / Families,
Faculty, and Staff) with no names and no count in the HTML. So the honest flag here is that
the school's own live site no longer enumerates the groups it runs — the roster survives only
in archive.

**One genuine internal inconsistency worth flagging:** the archived Upper School list
contains a **duplicate Black Student Union entry with two different years — "Black Student

above).

## 1b. Service & Civic Engagement

### Program 1 — Providence Day Freedom School — scale: 55 scholars; founded 2012; a national first

**"The first independent school in the country to host a CDF Freedom School"** and "the only
independent school in the nation to host a Freedom School program each summer." A summer
literacy program for K–8 Charlotte scholars, run in partnership with Freedom School
Partners. The community collects supplies for all **18 Charlotte Freedom School sites**.

- Sources: https://www.providenceday.org/about/freedom-school ·
  https://web.archive.org/web/20250407090326/https://www.providenceday.org/about/social-responsibility

### Program 2 — Community Engagement / President's Volunteer Service Award — scale: 5,247 hours in one year

**5,247 service hours in a single school year; 20,642 hours since 2017; 48 students earned
the award in 2024** (24 Gold, 5 Silver, 9 Bronze). Led by Jennifer Bratyanski, Upper School
Community Engagement Coordinator. Named partners: Wayfinders, RecycleBalls, Charlotte
Mecklenburg Library, Mecklenburg County Teen Court, Augustine Literacy Project, and National
Charity League.

- Source: https://www.providenceday.org/about/pd-communications/news-media/post/~board/news-media/post/students-earn-national-honor-while-serving-their-community

### Program 3 — Round Square — scale: joined 2016; the only member school in North Carolina

A network of **250+ schools in 50 countries** built on the IDEALS framework (International
understanding, Democracy, Environmental stewardship, Adventure, Leadership, **Service**),
including international service projects ("Big Builds") and exchanges.

- Source: https://www.providenceday.org/the-pd-difference/global-education

### Additional scale figure — VERIFIED

"Over **30 student-run service clubs**." →
https://www.providenceday.org/admissions/why-providence-day

### "Individual activity, not a club" — ARCHIVED

Curricular or divisional initiatives and individual recognition, **not** standalone clubs
(all from the archived Community Engagement page above):

- 9th-grade **Charger Impact Challenge** — a grade-level initiative
- Grade-level partnerships: 6th → Movement School; 7th → Winterfield Elementary;
  8th → Wilson STEM Academy
- Lower School service exposure: Levine Children's Hospital, The Lunch Project, Fifth Grade
  Safety Patrol, Collateral Repair Project, The Relatives, Ronald McDonald House, Crisis

- **US Community Engagement Portal** — an opportunity-listing tool
- **Student-led Foundation Board** — described as a student-led body, not explicitly a club
- The **President's Volunteer Service Award** itself — an *individual* recognition of hours

**UNCONFIRMED, excluded:** voter-education internships appeared in a search snippet but could
not be verified on any live PD page.

## 1c. Honor Societies

### The roster — VERIFIED (five, all Upper School)

| Society | Division | What it recognizes | Feeds from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cum Laude Society** | Upper School | "Superior scholastic achievement in secondary schools"; motto excellence, justice, honor | The classroom |
| **National Honor Society** | Upper School | Scholarship, leadership, service, character | Classroom + service |
| **National Art Honor Society** | Upper School | Visual-arts achievement and service | The Arts — visual |
| **Tri-M Music Honor Society** | Upper School | Music achievement and leadership; a NAfME program | The Arts — music |
| **Société Honoraire de Français** | Upper School | French achievement; "the high school equivalent of Pi Delta Phi" | World languages |

Sources (each society is evidenced by its own news post):
- Cum Laude: https://www.providenceday.org/about/pd-communications/news-media/post/~board/news-media/post/pd-celebrates-34-new-members-of-the-cum-laude-society
- NHS: https://www.providenceday.org/cf_news/view.cfm?newsid=2101
- Tri-M: https://www.providenceday.org/about/pd-communications/news-media/post/~board/news-media/post/eleven-upper-school-students-inducted-into-tri-m-music-honor-society

### Induction criteria — only NHS publishes them

**National Honor Society:** "Selection is based on four criteria: scholarship, leadership,
service and character," plus attendance at PDS for a minimum of one year and a **cumulative
GPA of 3.6 or higher**; selection by a faculty committee.

### Published induction counts — news posts only, not a standing page

- **NHS: 89 inducted** (8 seniors, 81 juniors), Nov 8 2017, joining 81 existing members
- **Cum Laude: 34** (2022), **31**, and **29** (2018) in separate years
- **National Art Honor Society: nearly 50** in May; the chapter was added "in the last five
  years" per the admissions page; service projects at Wing Haven and Daniel Stowe Botanical
  Garden
- **Tri-M: 11 inducted**, Nov 2019; chapter established **2016**; advisor Sarah Russell

### GAP FLAG — 1c

- **No standing honor-societies page exists** on providenceday.org. Every fact above comes
  from one-off news posts, so the counts are historical rather than current.
- **Induction criteria are not published for Cum Laude, NAHS, Tri-M, or Société Honoraire de
  Français** — only for NHS.
- **Annual induction counts are not published systematically.**

### NOT FOUND — explicitly searched

**National Junior Honor Society, Quill & Scroll, Rho Kappa, Mu Alpha Theta, Science NHS**,
and any **Spanish / Latin / Chinese** honor society. Targeted CDX archive searches for
`rho-kappa`, `mu-alpha`, `quill`, `junior-honor`, and `spanish-honor` across the full
providenceday.org URL history returned **zero hits**. Notably **no Middle School honor
society** could be confirmed at all — and despite PD offering five world languages, only the
French society is evidenced.
