### Filtering rule for an Upper School organizations count

The 27-item roster is **not** usable as-is: it mixes divisions (MS Board Game
Club, Junior First Lego League, After School Children's Choir gr. 3-5), non-clubs
(Piano and Guitar Lessons, Voice Lessons, Painting — paid enrichment classes;
Band/Choir/Theater Program — curricular; Men's Volleyball — a club sport), and
double-lists Thespian Society. After filtering to Upper-School student
organizations and honor societies, the documented set is:

Mock Trial · Robotics (First Lego League) · Latin (JCL) Club · French Club · Book
Club · Sustainability Engineering · Beta Club · Covenant Crazies (student spirit
section) · Student Council · NHS · National Art Honor Society · Spanish Honor
Society · International Thespian Society · Tri-M Music Honor Society (from the
music page) · Restore525 (also a course — counted once as a program)

**≥14 documented Upper School organizations** — a documented floor, not a school
total, because the school publishes no roster of its own. This is the
`us-organizations` counting rule.

### Participation

**No club-participation figure is published** anywhere — school site or
aggregators (confirmed absence; PrivateSchoolReview carries none). The school's
published participation figure is athletic (82%), which belongs to Sports.
