### Lower School

- **Goodness Gorillas** (4th grade service, ~20 community sites).
- **Kanuga** fifth-grade overnight retreat; **Field Day**.
- **CDS Children's Choir** (grades 3-5) and the **After School Children's Choir**.
- **Junior First Lego League** robotics.
- Extended Day enrichment classes via LionsWeb registration: music, art, sports,
  robotics, and outdoor experiences (JK-8).
- Missions Week with missionary speakers and cultural education.

### Middle School

- **Doulos Day** — twice-yearly all-MS service days.
- **MS Board Game Club** (third-party roster).
- **First Lego League** robotics; MS ensembles (Band, Choir, Worship Band) and MS
  Drama (grades 6-8) sit curricular-side.
- **Windy Gap** three-day retreat (MS + HS).

The lower/middle activity slate is program- and enrichment-based rather than a
chartered club roster — consistent with the school publishing no club directory.
