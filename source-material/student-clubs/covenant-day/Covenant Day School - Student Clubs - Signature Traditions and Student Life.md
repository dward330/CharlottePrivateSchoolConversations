# Covenant Day School — Student Clubs — Signature Traditions and Student Life

### All-school traditions

- **Color War** — all-school team challenges.
- **Homecoming** — "a week of dress-up days and fun activities culminates with our
  homecoming game" (played at Warner Park).
- **Spirit Week** + all-school pep rally; **Spirit Fridays**; the **Covenant
  Crazies** student spirit section.
- **Field Day** — lower and middle school.

### Retreats

- **Windy Gap** — middle and high school students, "a three-day retreat filled
  with chapels, team-building."
- **Kanuga** — the fifth-grade overnight ("fifth graders enjoy their own overnight
  adventure at Kanuga").

### The discipleship structure (the school's grouping model)

Chapel weekly, **separated by age** ("praise songs, scripture reading, and a
message from guest speakers, principals, or teachers"); **HS Advisory Groups**
led by teachers, weekly, together all year; **daily Homeroom Devotions** with
shared prayer requests; **Missions** trips to Spain, Haiti, Belize, and the Pine
Ridge Reservation, with a Lower School Missions Week. This faith-and-structure
grouping model is the school's answer to belonging — it publishes **no
identity-based affinity groups** (see the app's omitted affinity card; confirmed
against /campus-life/student-life and /campus-life/discipleship).

### Signature academic events

- **Senior Capstone Project** — a named high-school milestone.
- **McKnight Oratory** — the school's signature speaking event.
- Educational field trips from JK up; "student clubs and extracurricular classes"
  are the stated vehicle for leadership, service, spirit, and new passions.
