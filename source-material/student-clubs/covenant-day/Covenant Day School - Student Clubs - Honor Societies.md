# Covenant Day School — Student Clubs — Honor Societies

> **Provenance:** Compiled by Claude Code on 2026-08-15. Society membership is
> documented across the third-party roster and school arts pages; **the school
> publishes no honor-society page of its own**, so per-society division and
> induction criteria are largely not stated.

### Sources

- https://www.privateschoolreview.com/covenant-day-school-profile (NHS, NAHS,
  Spanish Honor Society, Thespian Society, Beta Club)
- https://www.covenantday.org/arts/music (Tri-M)
- https://www.covenantday.org/arts/theater (International Thespian Society)

### The documented societies (5, plus Beta Club)

| Society | Evidence | What it recognizes (national charter) |
|---|---|---|
| National Honor Society | third-party roster | scholarship, service, leadership, character |
| National Art Honor Society | third-party roster | visual arts achievement |
| Spanish Honor Society | third-party roster | Spanish-language achievement |
| International Thespian Society | school theater page | theatre participation/achievement |
| Tri-M Music Honor Society | school music page | music achievement + service |
| Beta Club | third-party roster | academic achievement + service (national org) |

### What is not published

Which divisions each society serves, induction criteria, member counts, and
faculty sponsors are **not published** for any society. The two school-confirmed
chapters (Thespians, Tri-M) are arts societies documented on their program pages;
the academic societies rest on the third-party roster only and carry that
confidence level in the app.
