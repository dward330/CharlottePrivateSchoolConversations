# Cannon School — Student Clubs Redesign Deep Research

**Provenance.** Compiled 2026-07-26 by Claude Code for the Student Clubs sub-section
redesign (cards 1a Affinity & Identity Groups, 1b Service & Civic Engagement,
1c Honor Societies). Method: live fetches of cannonschool.org pages plus the 2024-25 and
2025-26 Student Profile PDFs.

**Confidence key.** VERIFIED = read directly from a live page or PDF. NOT PUBLISHED =
explicitly searched and absent. SUPERSEDED / REFUTED = corrected against a better source.

**Headline.** Two prior claims were corrected by this pass. The **~15,000 service-hours
figure is outdated** (the current published figure is ~10,000, and the newest profile drops
the stat entirely), and the **"up to 20% of seniors" Cum Laude criterion is not published by
Cannon** — it is the national society's own cap. Card 1a is largely a gap: Cannon confirms
affinity groups exist but never names one.

---

## 1a. Affinity & Identity Groups — CONFIRMED TO EXIST, NEVER ENUMERATED

### The only affinity fact Cannon publishes — VERIFIED

A single passing mention on Upper School Student Life: "We have a rich club life, ranging
from the significant such as our **Affinity Groups**, to fun and adventurous clubs like the
Star Wars Club, and service-based clubs such as the Habitat Club." Clubs are described as
**student-led, faculty-supported**, meeting during and after school.
→ https://www.cannonschool.org/cannon-life/student-life/us-student-life

That is the whole of it. Cannon **confirms affinity groups exist in the Upper School but
never enumerates a single one by name.**

### Gaps — nothing published anywhere

- **No enumerated roster.** No affinity group names, descriptors, or counts.
- **No count discrepancy exists to flag** — Cannon publishes no count on any page, so
  there are no two numbers to reconcile.
- **No umbrella or coordinating organization**, and no founding year.
- **No division breakout.** Middle School Student Life lists only academic competition teams
  (Battle of the Books, Elementary Battle of the Books, Science Olympiad, Model UN,
  Envirothon) plus Student Council — no affinity groups at MS or LS.
- **No parent affinity groups.** "Parents at Cannon" is a general volunteer network
  (Connect, Create, Cultivate, Champion) with no identity-based subgroups. →
  https://www.cannonschool.org/cannon-life/parents-at-cannon
- **No student diversity leadership arm**; no SDLC or PoCC attendance found in any source.

### Structural evidence for the gap — VERIFIED

Cannon's site has **no standalone DEI page**. "Diversity, Belongingness, and Engagement" is
an anchor section on the About page containing only commitment statements ("Many
Individuals, Many Perspectives; One Mission, One Set of Core Values; Knowing We Belong
Here"), six Individual Agreements, and six Community Agreements — **no groups, no programs,
no names**. → https://www.cannonschool.org/about#dbeanchor

### REFUTED — do not ingest

The **"Infinity Group"** (described as a group for students of color) appears **only in
third-party review-site paraphrase** (Niche). It appears **nowhere** on cannonschool.org.
Treated as unverified and excluded.

## 1b. Service & Civic Engagement

### CORRECTION — the 15,000-hour figure is outdated; use ~10,000

Both the origin of the 15,000 number and its replacement were located:

| Figure | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| "around **15,000** service hours each year" | 2024-25 Student Profile PDF, "ENGAGEMENT" section | **Superseded** |
| "almost **10,000** service hours each year" | Upper School page (live) | **CURRENT — use this** |

Current wording: "Although service is **not a graduation requirement** at Cannon, students'
deep sense of commitment and personal responsibility is evidenced by the collective
completion of almost 10,000 service hours each year through school-wide projects and
individual efforts." → https://www.cannonschool.org/academics/upper-school

Superseded 15,000 wording:
https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1727105974/cannonschoolorg/q6vdhpxfxelejegmx4n8/2024-2025_StudentProfile_87212e_webemail.pdf

**Two further cautions.** (1) The **2025-26 Profile dropped the service-hours stat
entirely** — the ENGAGEMENT section no longer exists →
https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1757349467/cannonschoolorg/i5lqf0krxcb4uejss6ul/Cannon_2526_Profile.pdf
(2) The 10,000 figure sits on the **Upper School** page, so it is most defensibly labeled
**"Upper School"**-scoped rather than school-wide.

### Program 1 — Senior Capstone — scale: 111 seniors (Class of 2025; universal to seniors)

Service projects letting seniors immerse in a philanthropic cause they are passionate about;
culminating presentations to faculty, staff, peers, and community members allow time for
reflection and questions. Listed as a Signature Learning Experience.
→ https://www.cannonschool.org/academics/upper-school/curriculum (program) ·
2025-26 Profile (senior count)

### Program 2 — Winterm — scale: 1 week, 4 experience tracks, grades 9–11 for trips

A one-week experiential learning program after winter break that "challenges students to
think critically and practically, **to serve others**." Four tracks: First & Second Year
Experience (local community), Junior Experience (professional skills / job shadowing), and
Trips & Certifications (service learning, certifications, cultural immersion). Documented
service offerings include the **Sea Island Habitat for Humanity Service Trip** (Johns
Island, SC) and "Food and Photos" (serving at a local food bank, discussing food insecurity
and food waste). → https://www.cannonschool.org/academics/upper-school/winterm

### Program 3 — Middle School Service Learning & Community Outreach — scale: 8+ partner organizations

A grade-level service-learning model where students "investigate topics that interest them,
work in teams to identify specific needs, and develop action plans." Hosted guest speakers
from **more than eight organizations**, including **Special Olympics**, the **Society of St.
Andrew Gleaning Network**, and the **Cabarrus County Soil & Water Conservation District**.
Off-campus work: Share the Harvest Community Garden (picking and cleaning cabbage, sweet
potatoes, green beans), Salvation Army canned food drives, and the Cabarrus County Bilingual
Preschool. → https://www.cannonschool.org/cannon-life/student-life/middle-school-student-life

### "Individual activity, not a club" — VERIFIED

- **Senior Capstone** — a graduation-track Signature Learning Experience (individual
  project), not a club.
- **Winterm** — a curricular one-week program, not a club.
- **The ~10,000 service hours** — explicitly include "individual efforts," and service is
  **not a graduation requirement**, so the figure reflects individual activity rather than
  club membership.
- **Habitat Club** is the exception — named on the Upper School page as a service-based
  club, and the only named service club.

### Cum Laude — split verdict

- **CONFIRMED — chapter since 2005.** "Awarded a Cum Laude chapter in 2005." Appears in
  **both** profile PDFs (2024-25 and 2025-26), so it is current and stable. Wikipedia's
  chapter list shows Cannon as "Active" but with no charter year, so the school's own PDF is
  authoritative.
- **REFUTED — "up to 20% of seniors."** Cannon publishes this **nowhere**. The 20% figure is
  the **Cum Laude Society's own national maximum**, not a Cannon statistic. **Not attributed
  to Cannon.**

### NOT FOUND — explicitly searched

**National Honor Society (NHS)**, **National Junior Honor Society (NJHS)**, and **Science
National Honor Society** appear nowhere on Cannon's site or in any source. Cannon having
**no NHS chapter** is unusual for this set and worth flagging; Cum Laude appears to serve
that role.
