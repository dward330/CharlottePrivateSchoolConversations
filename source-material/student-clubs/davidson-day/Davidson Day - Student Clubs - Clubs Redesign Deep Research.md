# Davidson Day School — Student Clubs Redesign Deep Research

**Provenance.** Compiled 2026-07-26 by Claude Code for the Student Clubs sub-section
redesign (cards 1a Affinity & Identity Groups, 1b Service & Civic Engagement,
1c Honor Societies). Method: full sitemap sweep (`/site-map`), all Student Life /
Academics / Get Involved pages, the news and blog archives, the Issuu publication
library, and third-party profiles.

**Confidence key.** VERIFIED = read directly from a live page. NOT PUBLISHED = explicitly
searched across the whole site and absent. UNCONFIRMED = third-party only, not corroborated
by the school.

**Headline.** Davidson Day is the smallest school in this comparison set and publishes far
less than its larger peers. Two of the three cards have genuinely nothing to render:
**1a is councils rather than affinity groups, and 1c has no published record at all.**
Per the redesign's omit-never-placeholder rule, **cards 1a and 1c are omitted for this
school**; only 1b renders. That absence is the honest finding, not a research shortfall.

---

## 1a. Affinity & Identity Groups — NO ENUMERATED ROSTER (card omitted)

Davidson Day publishes **no named affinity/identity groups** — no Black Student Union,
Asian Student Alliance, GSA, Latine affinity group, or equivalent. There is no DEI nav
item; the school's framing is "**Belonging**," not affinity.

### What does exist — VERIFIED

All from https://www.davidsonday.org/student-life/belonging. These three bodies are
**councils and committees, not affinity groups**:

| Body | Type | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Student Belonging Council** | Student-led | "Developed by students… started hosting monthly belonging forums in **2020**. Advances conversations about diversity, community, and belonging at Davidson Day and beyond." |
| **Upper School Belonging Council** | Student, Upper School | Leadership plus faculty sponsor **Steve McGill**; featured on the Davidson Day Community Podcast with Head of School Andrew Bishop. |
| **Belonging Committee** | Adult (administrators, faculty, staff) | Meets **monthly**; implements belonging initiatives, addresses strategic-plan goals, organizes professional development, guided development of the Statement of Belonging. |

**Forum topics** — event themes, **not** standing groups: The African-American Student
Experience · The LGBTQ+ Experience · Disability Awareness Forum · The Immigrant Experience ·
Mental Health Forum.

**Statement of Belonging** (verbatim): "Davidson Day School is committed to maintaining a
welcoming, supportive learning environment that values belonging. We respect individual
differences in ethnicity, gender, national origin, race, color, religion, sexual
orientation, ability, and socioeconomic status."

### Why the card is omitted

- **No enumerated affinity-group roster by name** anywhere on the site. The 1a card is an
  ecosystem map built from a named roster; with no roster there are no cells to draw.
- **No division breakdown** — only Upper School is named; no Lower/Middle School groups.
- **No parent affinity groups.** The only two parent orgs are the **Patriot Parent
  Association (PPA)** and the **Patriot Athletic Club (PAC)** — social and athletic, neither
  identity-based. → https://www.davidsonday.org/get-involved/parent-organizations
- **No student diversity leadership arm**; no NAIS SDLC attendance published anywhere.
- **No count discrepancy to flag** — no page publishes any count at all.

## 1b. Service & Civic Engagement — THREE PROGRAMS CONFIRMED (card renders)

This is the one area with real, citable substance.

### Program 1 — Diploma Distinctions Practicum — scale: minimum 100 hours

Every Diploma Distinction requires an "Immersive Practicum (Minimum 100 Hours)" —
internships, civic engagement, nonprofit leadership, medical shadowing, or pre-collegiate
programs. Students apply at the end of sophomore year, and the track adds quarterly
**Ethics Labs** and a capstone at the **Davidson Day Scholars Research Conference**. The
**Business and Civic Engagement Diploma** specifies "At least 100 hours of an approved
internship, volunteer opportunity, or pre-collegiate program."

**Important qualifier:** this is an **opt-in diploma track**, *not* a school-wide graduation
service requirement.

- Sources: https://www.davidsonday.org/academics/diploma-distinctions ·
  https://www.davidsonday.org/academics/diploma-distinctions/business-civic-engagement

### Program 2 — Davidson College Deliberative Citizenship Initiative — scale: 13 students; first high school ever to partner

Davidson Day is "the first high school to collaborate with this program." Thirteen Upper
School students completed two deliberation modules — "Balancing Liberty and General
Welfare: The Case for Social Safety Nets" and "Justice and the Rule of Law: The Case for
Constitutional Rights" — attending expert panels and two-hour small-group deliberations
centered on civil discourse rather than debate-to-win.

- Source: https://www.davidsonday.org/p/~board/all-blogs/post/learning-to-disagree-better
- **"Not a club" label:** this is a structured partnership/program, not a standalone club.

### Program 3 — Middle School Service Learning — scale: 100% of Middle School students

"Every Middle School student takes part in our hands-on service-learning program,
partnering with local organizations." Service Days: "every student actively participates in
dedicated days of service throughout the year." No hour count, partner count, or founding
year is published.

- Sources: https://www.davidsonday.org/academics/middle-school ·
  https://www.davidsonday.org/student-life/community-and-culture

### Also confirmed — Upper School Community Engagement Council

Described as demonstrating "a deep commitment to service and leadership by creating
meaningful opportunities for students to make a positive impact both locally and beyond."
**No scale number, roster, or founding year published**, and its "Learn More" link is dead.
→ https://www.davidsonday.org/student-life/community-and-culture

### Community partnerships — 8 named — VERIFIED

Champions House of Care · The Kindness Closet · Food for Days · Beds for Kids · American
Foreign Academic Research (AFAR) · Davidson Public Library · Davidson College Deliberative
Citizenship Initiative
→ https://www.davidsonday.org/student-life/community-and-culture

### "Individual activity, not a club" — VERIFIED

- **Longevity LKN** — a student-**founded**, student-run **501(c)(3) nonprofit**, not a
  school club. Free blood-pressure screenings across Lake Norman; founder Adam Rezai
  (junior), co-presidents Alexander Ajjan and Piper Pritchard. **Over 100 community members
  screened**, more than half showing high readings. Partnered with FeedNC. →
  https://www.davidsonday.org/p/~board/all-blogs/post/making-a-difference-one-conversation-at-a-time
- **AFAR archaeology** — **13 students** excavated the Adam Spach Rock House (Nov 2024)
  alongside five archaeologists from the Environmental Research Group; "first high school
  students to work on an archaeological site with the North Carolina State Historic
  Preservation Office." Parent liaisons Jim and Christy Pritchard. Whether this is a club or
  a program is **not defined** on the page. →
  https://www.davidsonday.org/p/~board/all-blogs/post/making-history-uncovering-history-davidson-day-afar-and-the-adam-spach-rock-house
- **Patriot Pals** — a peer-mentorship tradition, not a service club.

### Gap — 1b

No school-wide service-hours graduation requirement is published anywhere; the Upper School
page carries no service requirement.

## 1c. Honor Societies — NOTHING PUBLISHED (card omitted)

**Davidson Day publishes no honor-society information of any kind.** Zero references to
National Honor Society, National Junior Honor Society, Cum Laude Society, National Art
Honor Society, Tri-M, Mu Alpha Theta, Beta Club, or any world-language honor society.

Pages checked with negative results:

- https://www.davidsonday.org/academics/upper-school
- https://www.davidsonday.org/academics/middle-school
- https://www.davidsonday.org/academics/college-counseling
- https://www.davidsonday.org/student-life/student-life-at-davidson-day
- https://www.davidsonday.org/student-life/community-and-culture
- https://www.davidsonday.org/about/news-media/news and `/blog` — no induction stories at
  all; the only induction coverage on the whole site is the **Patriot Athletic Hall of
  Fame**, which is athletics rather than academic honors.

An independent web search for `"Davidson Day School" "National Honor Society" OR "Cum
Laude" induction` (2026-07-26) returned **no results for this school** — only other schools'
ceremonies and Davidson *College* (a different institution). The absence is therefore
corroborated off-site, not merely a fetching artifact.

### UNCONFIRMED third-party signal — deliberately not used

https://www.privateschoolreview.com/davidson-day-school-profile lists 17 extracurriculars
(Art Club, Battle of the Books, Chess Club, Community Service Club, Creative Writing Club,
Debate Club, Environmental Club, Film Club, Guitar Club, **Honor Council**, Math Olympiad,
Outdoors Club, Photography Club, Science Club, Student Ambassador Program, Student
Government Association) and enrollment of 572, PK–12. **"Honor Council" is a
disciplinary/integrity body, not an honor society.** None of this is corroborated on
davidsonday.org and the list appears stale, so it is **not ingested as club data**.

## Methodology limits (honest caveats)

- Issuu hosts the **2026-2027 Upper School Course Catalog**, the **2025-2026 catalogue**,
  and the **25-26 School Profile** — the documents most likely to name honor societies.
  Issuu's text-layer APIs return 403 AccessDenied and the HTML shell carries no extractable
  text, so their contents could **not** be verified. Confirmed again on 2026-07-26: no
  profile PDF is served from davidsonday.org itself (only images), so no local extraction
  was possible. A profile PDF obtained through a browser session remains the single
  highest-value follow-up for 1c.
- The site's search endpoint renders client-side only; no server-reachable JSON API exists.
- Five of the six "Learn More" cards on the Community and Culture page (Field Trips, Middle
  School Service Days, Patriot Pals, Social-Emotional Learning, Upper School Community
  Engagement Council) are **unlinked** — only Belonging has a live detail page. The card
  summaries quoted above are the full extent of published detail.
