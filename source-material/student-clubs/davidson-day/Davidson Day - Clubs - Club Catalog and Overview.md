# Davidson Day — Clubs — Club Catalog & Overview

Research Area — Scope, honest gaps, and the clubs confirmable from public sources.

**Provenance.** Compiled 2026-07-26 by Claude Code via live web research (web search
+ page fetches). Prior Davidson Day dossiers organized student life thematically
(AFAR, Academic Recognition, Service, Traditions) with no catalog/roster section, so
this file adds the missing catalog view. Nothing here is inferred or invented.

**Honesty caveat (important).** Davidson Day does NOT publish a public, enumerated
roster of named student clubs on its own site (davidsonday.org). Every division and
student-life page frames activities through programs, councils, and traditions — the
Community Engagement Councils and Patriot Week service framework, the Student Diversity
Council, the Middle School House System, Patriot Pals, the AFAR archaeology
field-research program, and the Global Studies Diploma — rather than an interest-club
list. A real activities list likely lives behind the login-gated Patriot Connect /
parent portal, which could not be accessed.

**Verdict:** PARTIAL — no roster on the school's own site; one named list exists only
on the third-party aggregator PrivateSchoolReview (single-source, undated, no division
breakdown, and uncorroborated against the school).

**Named clubs (single source: PrivateSchoolReview, unverified by the school).**
PrivateSchoolReview lists 17 extracurriculars with no division breakdown. Applying the
series scope (non-athletic, non-fine-arts student clubs), the confirmable interest and
academic clubs are: Chess Club, Debate Club, Science Club, Math Olympiad, Environmental
Club, Outdoor Club, Creative Writing Club, Film Club, and Community Service Club.

**Excluded.** Art, Guitar, and Photography clubs are excluded as fine-arts; Student
Government Association, Honor Council, and the Student Ambassador Program are governance
bodies or programs, not interest clubs; Rowing is athletic; Battle of the Books is the
Middle School reading-competition team covered in the Academic Recognition & Competition
dossier.

**Not clubs — documented on davidsonday.org, correctly excluded.** Community Engagement
Council (CEC), Middle School Service Days, service-learning (service framework); Patriot
Pals (mentorship tradition); House System / House Cup (Middle School tradition); Student
Diversity Council (council); AFAR archaeology and the Global Studies Diploma (academic
programs); Revolution yearbook, theatre, and show choir (arts / publications).

**Gaps / login-gated.** No school-hosted club roster found; candidate URLs 404. A real
activities list likely lives in the login-gated Patriot Connect / Veracross-style
parent-student portal, which was not fetchable. PrivateSchoolReview names are
uncorroborated. Niche returned HTTP 403; U.S. News timed out — neither confirmed.

**Honest position:** no official club count is asserted. The clubs above are reported as
aggregator-listed and unverified; the school's own documented structures are programs
and councils, not a club roster.

Source: PrivateSchoolReview — Davidson Day School profile (privateschoolreview.com/davidson-day-school-profile; single-source, unverified). Davidson Day School — Upper School, Middle School, and Community & Culture pages (davidsonday.org). Wikipedia — Davidson Day School.
