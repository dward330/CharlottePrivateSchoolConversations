# Charlotte Preparatory School — Student Clubs — Club Catalog and Traditions

Charlotte Prep does not publish a clubs list. Its Life at Prep page promises "room enough for everyone at the playing field, on the stage, or in the club" (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/life-at-prep), but names no clubs. The catalog below is built from the clubs and activities that actually appear in the school's news stories and reports, each with the date of its most recent evidence. All are Middle School (grades 5–8) unless noted.

**Student Council in practice.** The council organized the October 2024 Hurricane Helene supply drive. It "led" the middle school assemblies in 2024-25, which "facilitated students sharing presentations about their passions and wonderings" (strategic plan). It recorded a New Family Welcome video in 2022 (https://vimeo.com/708790980). Council members also take part in the International Day of Peace each September. Students run for president, and the presidents named in 2024 and 2025 were both 8th graders.

**MOSAIC** is the school's only student diversity club on record. Its members have attended the ACT (Affirming Community Together) Middle School Diversity Conference: "Ten MS students attended the ACT Diversity Conference at Charlotte Latin" in 2023-24, and "10 MS students attended the ACT Diversity Conference at Providence Day School" in 2024-25. No description, meeting pattern or advisor for MOSAIC is published. It is a general diversity club, not an identity-based affinity group, and **the school publishes no student affinity groups**. The Diversity, Equity, Inclusion, & Belonging page (https://charlotteprep.org/about/diversity-equity-inclusion-belonging) names none. It is led by Danielle S. Ferguson, Director of Equity, Belonging, & Engagement, and describes school-wide celebrations of "cultures and faiths from around the world" and 77 countries represented.

**After School Studios** add fee-based options each fall and spring, such as STEM, Hindi, chorus, ukulele, printmaking and theatre. They are covered in the after-school file.

### Joy Term, a January mini-course week

**Joy Term** is a K–8 program of short elective courses held each January since 2025.

- **January 2025, the first Joy Term:** "With over 20 'mini-courses' available ranging from Juggling to International Cooking and Maker Space Engineering to Prep Poetry Jam, each student enrolled in 3 to 4 based on their interests." (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/prep-s-inaugural-joy-term, January 31, 2025)
- **January 2026:** "Joy Term returned for our K-8 students with over 20 courses available, ranging from 'What Pet Should I Get' to 'Show Choir' and 'Adventures in Aviation' to 'Surviving the Zombie Apocalypse.'" (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/joy-term-returns, January 13, 2026)

The full course list is not published for either year, so only the seven courses named above are on record. Its length in days is not published either. Faculty design the courses using the school's Understanding by Design curriculum framework.

### Signature traditions

| Tradition | What it is |
|---|---|
| **BUDEE mentorship** | "Be Understanding, Dedicated, Empathetic, and Engaged". It pairs TK students with 3rd graders, and each other Lower School student with a Middle School buddy, for service, character education and teamwork. The school calls it "Our Signature Leadership Program" (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/leadership). |
| **Honor Code and the Four Pillars** | "Each individual is responsible for his or her conduct, and should actively promote honesty and integrity. Students at Charlotte Preparatory School respect the personal and property rights of others and the school learning environment. They demonstrate honor by always acting honestly, responsibly, and ethically." The Four Pillars of Leadership are "Respect, Integrity, Perseverance, and Courage" (Leadership page). |
| **8th-grade Ignite presentations** | In the 8th-grade Leadership class, students give talks on topics of their choice in a format the school calls the "Prep 5x30": "five slides displayed for 30 seconds each" (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/8th-grade-ignite-presentations, April 30, 2026). |
| **Pep Rally and spirit week** | An "annual Pep Rally" closes a week of dress themes before the December break, including a faculty-versus-students basketball game (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/school-spirit-on-full-display, December 11, 2025). |
| **Grandparents & Special Friends' Day** | An annual visit with a student musical performance and classroom visits. In 2023 it was held on campus for the first time since 2019, in April. It was held in April 2024, then moved to just before Thanksgiving in 2025, with "a record number of guests" (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/grandparents-special-friends-day-2023; Vimeo recordings April 2023, April 2024, November 21, 2025). |
| **International Day of Peace** | Every September 21 the whole school gathers around the flagpole (2022 story). |
| **High School Fair** | Each year "local and regional independent day and boarding schools visit our campus to meet with our 8th-Grade class", the culmination of the Middle School interview-skills curriculum (Leadership page). |
| **Winter and Spring Fine Arts Showcases** | See the arts files. |

### Publications

There is no student newspaper, literary magazine or yearbook club on record. First graders write a monthly class newspaper as a classroom project ("Hot off the Press", https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/hot-off-the-press-first-grade-news). The school's financial reports list "yearbooks" among auxiliary-program income and costs, so a yearbook exists, but who produces it is not published.
