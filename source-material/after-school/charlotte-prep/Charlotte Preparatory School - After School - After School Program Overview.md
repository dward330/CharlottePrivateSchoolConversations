# Charlotte Preparatory School — After School — After School Program Overview

Charlotte Prep runs an After School Program for Kindergarten through 8th grade every school day, from dismissal until 5:30 p.m. The Montessori Early School has its own Extended Day add-on, also to 5:30 p.m. Families can enroll for the year or drop in by the hour. There is no paid before-school care. The school's own wording is "No additional before-school care is available at this time." Its After School page (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/after-school) and Tuition & Fees page (https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/tuition-fees) were both read on October 19, 2026.

### Coverage: when care is available

| Who | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| Montessori Early School (Pre-Primary, Primary, TK) | Drop-off from 8:00 a.m. | Extended Day add-on to 5:30 p.m. |
| Montessori Kindergarten | Drop-off from 8:00 a.m. | Hourly drop-in only is listed for 2026–27 (see Cost) |
| Kindergarten–4th grade (Lower School) | Drop-off from 7:45 a.m. | After School Program, dismissal to 5:30 p.m. |
| 5th–8th grade (Middle School) | Drop-off from 7:45 a.m. | After School Program, dismissal to 5:30 p.m. |

In the school's words:
- "Our daily After School Program operates from the student dismissal time until 5:30 p.m."
- "You may drop off Early School students as early as 8:00 a.m., and lower and middle school students as early as 7:45 a.m. No additional before-school care is available at this time."
- "We provide complimentary care for younger students who have siblings with a later dismissal time." This covers a younger child who would otherwise wait for an older sibling, at no charge.

Children are grouped by age: "kindergarteners, first and second graders, third and fourth graders, and then our middle school students."

The school does not publish any of these:
- whether the program runs on teacher workdays, school holidays or school breaks
- what happens on early-dismissal days
- a late-pickup fee or policy
- current dismissal times for each division

A current job listing for program teachers mentions "full-care days" and "certain longer full-time days". That suggests some full-day coverage exists, but the school does not say when or what it costs.

### Cost, 2026–27

| Option | Who | Price |
|---|---|---|
| After School Program, annual | Kindergarten - 8th Grade (3:00 p.m.- 5:30 p.m.) | $4,500 |
| Drop-in After School | Montessori Kindergarten through 8th Grade | $20/hour |
| Pre-Primary/Primary Extended Day | 12:00 p.m. - 5:30 p.m. | $5,900 |
| Transitional Kindergarten Extended Day | 2:30 p.m. - 5:30 p.m. | $2,700 |
| Pre-Primary/Primary School Day* | 12:00 p.m. - 2:30/3:00 p.m. | $3,400 |
| After School Studios | Per studio | Not published |

*Only "for students with siblings enrolled at Prep in TK-8th grade."

What the annual price works out to (our calculation, not a school figure): about $25 per afternoon over a 180-day school year. Drop-in costs $20 an hour, so a full 2½-hour afternoon on drop-in costs about $50. A family using the program more than two or three afternoons a week would pay less on the annual plan. The school publishes neither its number of program days nor a monthly or per-semester rate. The $4,500 is the only annual figure it gives.

How the price has moved: the annual K–8 program cost $3,200 in 2017–18, ran to 6:00 p.m. and came with free 7:30 a.m. before-school care. It cost $4,000 in 2023–24, $4,200 in 2024–25 and $4,500 in 2026–27, all to 5:30 p.m. and with no morning care. Drop-in went from $17.00 an hour in 2015–16 to $20 an hour from 2023–24. See the Tuition History file.

Other notes:
- The sibling discount ("We offer a 3% discount for younger siblings of current students") is described on the tuition page. The school does not say whether it applies to the After School Program.
- Families add or drop after-school care for the next year through the school's Change to Enrollment form (https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/change-to-enrollment). The live form still carries 2021–2022 wording and a January 28 – February 5 window. Treat that window as out of date.

### A day inside: what happens after school

The school describes the program this way: "exciting and fun-filled, offering hands-on activities, homework time, and learning opportunities that coincide with monthly themes and the school-day curriculum. We also provide an afternoon snack."

It adds: "Classes are led by warm and enthusiastic teachers and include a healthy mix of age-appropriate activities, homework time, and free time." It also says the teachers "set these routines at the beginning of the school year and reinforce expectations throughout the year."

In the school's own words:
- afternoon snack
- homework time
- monthly themes
- hands-on activities
- free time
- "healthy routines"
- "a loving and nurturing environment"

The school's job listing for program teachers describes a typical afternoon: "planning and prep of group activities, supervision of recess and snack, homework assistance as needed, leading group activities, organizing free play materials, and clean-up." The school publishes no timed daily schedule.

**Who runs it.** Justen Kirchner is Director of Auxiliary Programs. The school's strategic plan says this post "will now oversee all after-school, summer camps, extended day, and studio programs". The staff directory lists 14 After School Program Teachers, plus six Early School Extended Day Teachers, an Extended Day Teacher Assistant, and an Early School Extended Day Coordinator, Tonja Giles (https://charlotteprep.org/about/faculty-staff-directory, October 2026). The program teachers work part-time, generally 2:00–6:00 p.m., per the school's job listing.

### After School Studios: classes for an extra fee

Studios are short courses that run on top of after-school care. The school's wording: "A new selection of studio choices is typically offered each fall and spring, and each session lasts approximately 12 weeks. Students are escorted to the studio location by their teacher after the school day ends. Studios usually last 45 minutes to 1 hour. There is a fee, separate from Charlotte Prep's annual tuition, associated with each class."

The school lists these as "Sample Studio offerings". They are examples, not the current term's schedule.

Two of the named leaders, Mrs. Stafford and Ms. Stefanini, do not appear in the current staff directory. Ask which studios run this term, and what they cost.

### Verdict: what holds up and what to ask

**What holds up**
- **One clear annual price.** $4,500 covers K–8 afternoons to 5:30 p.m., with a $20 hourly drop-in for occasional use.
- **Free care for younger siblings.** A younger child is cared for at no charge while waiting for an older sibling's later dismissal.
- **Age groups and homework time.** Children are grouped in four age bands, and homework time, a snack and themed activities are built in.
- **Studios add enrichment.** Twelve-week studios in language, drama, music, art and STEM are offered each fall and spring.
- **A dedicated director.** A Director of Auxiliary Programs runs after-school, extended day, studios and summer camp.

**What to watch**
- **No morning care.** Drop-off starts at 7:45 a.m. (8:00 for the Early School), and there is nothing earlier. This used to be free from 7:30 a.m., through 2021–22.
- **Pickup by 5:30 p.m.** The program ended at 6:00 p.m. through 2021–22. It now ends at 5:30.
- **Holiday and workday coverage is not published.** Neither is a late-pickup policy.
- **Studio prices are not published.** The studio list is labelled a sample, and parts of it look out of date.
- **Montessori Kindergarten after-school care is unclear for 2026–27.** Its after-school slot appears only as hourly drop-in on the tuition page. In 2024–25 it had an annual price.

**Questions to ask on a visit**
- Is after-school care offered on teacher workdays, holidays and early-dismissal days, and at what cost?
- What is the late-pickup policy and fee?
- Which studios run this term, on which days, and what does each cost?
- Can a Montessori Kindergartner enroll in the annual After School Program, or only drop in?
- Does the 3% sibling discount apply to after-school fees?
- How do I register, and by when?
