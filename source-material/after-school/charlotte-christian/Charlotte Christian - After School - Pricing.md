After School Pricing at Charlotte Christian School
Charlotte Christian School (JK-12, Christian) - Charlotte, NC - Extended Day Pricing (2024-25 packet, most recent published)
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school after-school programs. Data gathered via deep web research on July 22, 2026; figures below were re-verified 3-0 against the school's published Extended Day PDF packet by independent verification agents. Hours, ages, and fees change yearly - verify with the school before relying on them.
Pricing by Grade & Days per Week
The Lower School Extended Day program serves grades JK-4, from dismissal (2:20 p.m. for JK-K, 2:40 p.m. for grades 1-4) until 6:00 p.m. Families are billed monthly based on the number of days attended per week. Rates from the most recent published packet (2024-25 school year):

| Grade band (monthly, 2024-25) | 1 day/wk | 2 days/wk | 3 days/wk | 4 days/wk | 5 days/wk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| JK-Grade 4 | $95/mo | $115/mo | $165/mo | $215/mo | $240/mo |

Fees & Fine Print
• Late pick-up: $25 per quarter hour beginning at 6:01 p.m.
• Billing is monthly, based on days attended per week.
