## Card — The Coverage Map

Dismissal times, confirmed identically across three independent pages (Admissions FAQs,
Parents' Hub, Back-to-School):

| Division | Arrival | Dismissal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| JK & Kindergarten | 8:00 am | 2:20 pm |
| Lower School, Grades 1–4 | 7:45 am | 2:40 pm |
| Middle School, Grades 5–8 | 7:45 am | 3:00 pm |
| Upper School, Grades 9–12 | 8:00 am | 3:20 pm |

The existing file's "2:20 JK-K / 2:40 grades 1-4" is confirmed. The Extended Day page
separately references a 3:15 pm middle-school figure, which is the carpool-clearance
deadline rather than the bell; the MS packet reconciles these — dismissal 3:00, carpool
pickup by 3:15, auto-billed to Extended Day at 3:20.

Programs by division
- JK–Grade 4 — Lower School Extended Day, dismissal to 6:00 pm. "All Extended Day
  services and care end promptly at 6 p.m." JK-K gather in the Early Education Building;
  grades 1–4 go to the playground or dining hall; all students consolidate in the main
  dining hall after 4:00 pm.
- Grades 5–8 — Middle School Extended Day, 3:00–5:00 pm only, a full hour earlier than
  Lower School. Students report to the dining hall at 3:00.
- Grades 9–12 — NO after-school care program. Upper School has athletics, arts and
  activities but no supervised extended-day equivalent. Edge case: MS students "waiting
  to be picked up by their upper school sibling will be charged a pro-rated extended day
  fee beginning at 3:20."

Pickup tiers (Lower School) — beyond the full-day-to-6:00 option, the school sells two
cheap Bridge Care tiers that cover only the gap between an early dismissal and the
grades 1–4 bell:
- JK-K Bridge Care, 2:20–3:15 pm — $125/month
- Grades 1–4 Bridge Care, 2:40–3:35 pm — $125/month

Notes & gaps
- Before-school care: NOT PUBLISHED as a paid program. The FAQs state only that "Students
  are welcomed on campus at 7:15 a.m." — free early access roughly 30–45 minutes before
  bells, not a fee-based program. No before-school rate appears in the 2026-27 packet.
- Half-day early dismissal: $25/day, billed in addition to the monthly fee. So coverage
  exists on half days but carries a surcharge.
- Teacher workdays and holiday breaks: NOT PUBLISHED. No holiday-camp or workday-camp
  offering appears in the packet or on the Extended Day page. The packet references an
  "extended day calendar…available on the Extended Day webpage" which did not resolve to
  a posted file.
- Summer: Charlotte Christian School Summer Camps — six weeks, June 1 – July 17, 2026,
  JK through grade 12. Early Care 8–9 am and After Care 4–5 pm, each $50/week or $10/day.
  Late fee after 5 pm $25 per 15 minutes. Lunch/Bridge Care is free for campers enrolled
  in both a morning and an afternoon camp. Registration $35/camper before May 17, $45
  after; opens January 5. Non-refundable within two weeks of camp start; $25 cancellation
  fee. Director: Kaitlin Pleshko.

## Card — The Cost Planner

Lower School Extended Day (JK–Grade 4), monthly, 2026-27:

| Days/week | 2026-27 | 2024-25 (superseded) | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 day | $125 | $95 | +$30 (+32%) |
| 2 days | $175 | $115 | +$60 (+52%) |
| 3 days | $245 | $165 | +$80 (+48%) |
| 4 days | $295 | $215 | +$80 (+37%) |
| 5 days | $325 | $240 | +$85 (+35%) |

Other Lower School rates and fees
- JK-K Bridge Care (2:20–3:15): $125/month
- Grades 1–4 Bridge Care (2:40–3:35): $125/month
- Daily drop-in: $50/day
- Late fee: $25 per quarter hour beginning at 6:01 pm (the existing file's figure,
  re-verified)
- Half-day early dismissal: $25/day, on top of the monthly fee
- Auto-enrollment rule: "Any lower school student not picked up by 3:05 p.m. will be
  brought to Extended Day and billed as a daily drop-in" — $50 for being 25 minutes late
  off the 2:40 bell.
- Families without a completed enrollment form on file are billed the daily drop-in rate
  until the form is submitted.

Middle School Extended Day (Grades 5–8) — newly published, closes the prior gap:
- $8 per hour, billed monthly based on hourly attendance
- Late fee $25 per quarter hour beginning at 5:01 pm
- Students not picked up by 3:20 pm are brought to extended day "and billed accordingly"
- Siblings waiting on an Upper School student are charged a pro-rated fee from 3:20
- All middle school students are automatically registered — no enrollment form, no
  minimum: "All middle school students are automatically registered for extended day and
  are welcome to attend any afternoon."

At $8/hr for the full 3:00–5:00 window, five days a week runs roughly $80/week.

Registration fee, sibling discount, billing
- Registration fee: NOT PUBLISHED and apparently none. The packet requires an enrollment
  form by the first day of attendance but names no fee for it. (By contrast, summer camp
  charges $35/$45.)
- Sibling discount: "There is a 25 percent family fellowship discount for the third
  student. This applies to the lowest rate." Note this is third-child-only, not a
  second-child discount, and applies only to the cheapest of the three rates.
- Billing: monthly, based on days attended per week (Lower School) or hourly attendance
  (Middle School). All changes must be in writing via the Extended Day Change Form or
  email.
- Emergency care available ad hoc: "Your family may take advantage of our services in
  family emergency situations that require special care provisions."

## Card — Verdict & Visit Checklist

Strengths
- Coverage to 6:00 pm for JK–4 is among the later end times in the Charlotte private
  market, and it is a hard, clearly stated cutoff.
- Bridge Care is a real cost innovation: $125/month to bridge JK-K's 2:20 bell to the
  grades 1–4 bell at 3:15 solves the staggered-sibling problem cheaply, rather than
  forcing a family onto a full $325 plan.
- A published, structured daily rhythm with a protected 45-minute homework/reading block
  in both divisions — many programs publish nothing at all.
- Middle School is pay-as-you-go at $8/hr with automatic registration: no commitment, no
  enrollment form, no minimum. For occasional users this is far friendlier than a monthly
  tier.
- The MS program is cohort-differentiated (grades 5–6 versus 7–8) — a real developmental
  consideration, since 8th and 5th graders in one room is a common failure mode.
- Deep summer bench: roughly 100 distinct camps across six weeks, JK–12, including
  sports, STEM, arts and academic intervention.
- Explicit, enforced pickup-authorization and ID-check policy.

Watch-outs
- Rates jumped 32–52% in two years. The 3-day tier rose 48% and the 2-day tier 52% —
  budget forward, not off the old numbers, and note the increases were steepest on the
  mid-tier plans.
- The 3:05 pm auto-billing rule is expensive: miss the 2:40 carpool by 25 minutes and
  you are billed a $50 drop-in. There is no grace tier between "on time" and "full
  drop-in day."
- No Upper School after-school care at all — grades 9–12 dismiss at 3:20 with athletics,
  arts, or nothing.
- Middle School ends at 5:00 pm, a full hour before Lower School's 6:00. A family with a
  3rd grader and a 6th grader has two different hard deadlines, and the MS late fee
  starts at 5:01.
- Snack is not provided in either program — a daily packing obligation, and MS students
  face a purchase option that quietly costs money.
- The sibling discount is thin: 25% for the third student only, applied to the lowest
  rate. Two-child families get nothing.
- Club fees are entirely invisible pre-enrollment. You cannot price the "additional fee"
  for the 8-week club sessions, or Junior Knights basketball, without being a current
  parent receiving the Focus email.
- Half days cost an extra $25/day, so the monthly figure is a floor, not a ceiling.
- No published teacher-workday or holiday-break camp — on breaks, families are likely on
  their own.
- Both published schedules are hedged "subject to change as needed."

Visit checklist
- What do the 8-week after-school clubs actually cost, and can I see the current
  session's catalog with days of the week and grade ranges?
- Is there any care on teacher workdays and during holiday breaks, or should I plan for
  full coverage gaps?
- What is the staff-to-student ratio in Extended Day, and are staff CCS teachers or
  outside hires?
- What is the actual enrollment capacity of Extended Day — is there ever a waitlist?
- Is the 3:05 auto-drop-in rule enforced strictly, and is there any grace period?
- Can I switch tiers mid-year, and is there proration when we do?
- Is there any before-school care beyond free 7:15 am campus access?
- How much homework actually gets finished in the 45-minute block — is there active help
  or just supervision?
- For Middle School: how are the 5–6 and 7–8 cohorts physically separated, and what
  happens between 5:00 pm and a later pickup?
- Does Extended Day run on early-dismissal days automatically, or must we opt in for the
  $25?
