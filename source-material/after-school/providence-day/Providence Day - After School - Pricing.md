Pricing by Grade & Days per Week
Extended Day serves Lower School students (TK-Grade 5), open 1-6 p.m. on regular school days (Activity Days 7:45 a.m.-6 p.m.). Families register for a weekly schedule of 1-5 days per week; the fee is charged monthly via Blackbaud Tuition Management. There is explicitly no drop-in option for Extended Day. Middle School Clubhouse
Clubhouse, the Middle School after-school program, is drop-in: Monday-Friday, 3:10-6:00 p.m., starting the first day of school. Cost is a $100 annual registration fee per student; no per-day or hourly rates are published.
Fees & Fine Print
• Extended Day annual registration fee: $80.
• Late pick-up: $1.00 per minute after 6:00 p.m., charged to the monthly bill.
• Schedule-change adjustment rate: $30/hour (unexpected circumstances; the school distinguishes this from drop-in pricing). Schedule changes must be emailed by the 1st of the month.
• Semester enrichment classes carry a separate, unpublished fee (pro-rated only if a student withdraws after one class).
