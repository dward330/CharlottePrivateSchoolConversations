Notes & gaps
- Upper School (grades 9–12): NOT PUBLISHED / no program exists. The official After
  School Options page lists exactly two programs, Extended Day and Clubhouse.
- Before-school care: NOT PUBLISHED at any division. Extended Day runs 1–6 pm on
  regular days; morning coverage appears only on Activity Days. The 7:30–7:50 am
  arrival window is unsupervised drop-off, not a care program.
- Activity Days (teacher workdays, parent-teacher conferences, most school holidays):
  Extended Day operates 7:45 am–6 pm, opening at 7 am on parent-teacher conference
  days, with themed activities. Sign-up is mandatory even for enrolled families.
  Activity Day fee: NOT PUBLISHED.
- Discrepancy to flag: grades 1–5 dismiss at 2:50 pm but the fee matrix bills them from
  3:00 pm. The 2:50–3:00 gap is not explained on any published page.
- Summer: "Summer at Providence Day School", https://summer.providenceday.org/ — nine
  sessions, June through early August. Extended Day explicitly does not run in summer
  ("opens on the first day of school and closes on the last day of school"). Summer
  rates, grade ranges and hours: NOT PUBLISHED on the dates-rates page.

Range: $100/mo (K or grades 1–5, short tier, 1 day) to $750/mo (TK, full tier, 5 days).
A full-time working family in grades 1–5 (3–6 pm, 5 days) pays $470/month.

Fees & fine print
- Extended Day annual registration: $80.
- Clubhouse annual registration (Middle School): $100/student.
- Late pickup: $1.00 per minute after 6:00 pm, charged to the monthly bill.
- Schedule-change / adjustment rate: $30/hour, for unexpected circumstances. The school
  explicitly distinguishes this from drop-in pricing.
- Drop-in for Extended Day: none — "No drop-in option" is stated explicitly.
- Enrichment withdrawal: no fee if withdrawn before the first class; $25 administrative
  fee after one class; full semester fee after more than one class.
- Schedule changes must be emailed by the 1st of the month to
  pds.extendedday@providenceday.org.
- Billing: Extended Day fees are charged monthly via Blackbaud Tuition Management.
  Enrichment fees also appear in the Blackbaud account.

Notes & gaps
- Number of Extended Day billing months: NOT PUBLISHED. The fee document gives monthly
  rates but never states how many months are billed.
- Sibling discounts: NOT PUBLISHED, for either tuition or Extended Day.
- Billing-platform ambiguity: the fee doc names Blackbaud, while the tuition/aid pages
  reference Clarity and an archived FAQ references Smart Tuition.
- Financial aid: the Tuition & Financial Assistance page states additional fees apply
  for Extended Day or Clubhouse and that "Financial assistance may be available for
  some of these additional costs" — an explicit, if hedged, statement that aid can
  reach after-school fees. About 21% of families receive some assistance.
- 2026-27 tuition context: TK $25,510 | K $28,010 | Grades 1–5 $32,960 | Grades 6–12
  $36,325.

## Card — A Day Inside + Enrichment

The enrichment day, in the school's own words (Fall 2026 Enrichment packet, "Enrichment
Day Schedule"). This is a published, division-specific rhythm — not an estimate:

- TK/K — picked up directly from their classrooms by an Extended Day teacher; escorted
  to the Dining Hall; school-provided nut-free snack; bathroom break and water bottle
  refill; walked to the enrichment class by an Extended Day teacher; supervised
  throughout the class; escorted to their dismissal location after class.
  Dismissal options: bus, carpool, Extended Day.
- Grades 1–2 — brought to the Dining Hall at dismissal and check in with Extended Day
  staff; nut-free snack; bathroom break; escorted to enrichment; supervised throughout;
  escorted to dismissal after class.
  Dismissal options: carpool, parent pickup from Extended Day, Extended Day.
- Grades 3–5 — walk independently to the Dining Hall and check in with Extended Day
  staff; from there the schedule is the same.
  Dismissal options: carpool, parent pickup from Extended Day, Extended Day.

Snack: students do not need to bring one — Extended Day provides a nut-free snack daily.

Study hall: grades 3–5, Monday–Thursday, 3:15–4:00 pm — "a quiet space for independent
study hall". Explicitly independent study only, with no one-on-one academic assistance.

The program in the school's own words
- "Teachers actively promote each child's social, emotional, and physical development
  through nurturing relationships, flexible scheduling, and choices that reflect each
  child's individual needs, interests, and abilities."
- The school describes Extended Day as nationally recognized and as providing "an
  enriching experience".
- Director Lynn Mayhew (via Scoop Charlotte, 2016): "It's literally a home away from
  home." / "This truly is their house when they can't be at home."

Middle School Clubhouse, in the school's own words
- "Clubhouse is open to all Middle School students on a drop in basis during after
  school hours."
- "open to all Middle School students to drop in to catch up with friends, study,
  unwind, play and eat"
- Emphasis on "a safe, fun, and inclusive space". Daily nutritious snacks provided.
  Students may self-register and sign themselves in and out for sports practices, games,
  music lessons, theater rehearsals and other approved campus events.

Health and safety (Extended Day)
- All staff trained in CPR, AED and First Aid; registered nurse on duty daily; access to
  student health records via the Magnus system.
- Discipline philosophy: "discipline with dignity".
- Allergy protocol: no peanut or tree nut products served; nurse reviews food content
  for other allergies; home snacks containing nuts prohibited.
- Illness: sick children cared for in a "Get Well Room"; must be fever-free without
  medication for 24 hours before returning.
- Medication: written parental permission required; stored in the locked nurse's office
  with a daily confidential log.

## Card — Verdict & Visit Checklist

Strengths
- Best-in-class price transparency: a complete published 40-cell (grade × tier × days)
  matrix in an official document, so a family can compute an exact annual cost before
  applying.
- Three-tier flexibility for the youngest students, explicitly designed around
  sibling-pickup logistics — the 1:00 pm TK dismissal is a real burden and the school
  priced an answer to it.
- 1–5 days/week at every tier, so part-time families are not forced to buy five days.
- A published, division-specific enrichment day rhythm with escorted transitions and a
  school-provided nut-free snack — unusually concrete operational detail.
- Serious health and safety infrastructure: RN on duty daily, Magnus records
  integration, full CPR/AED/First Aid staff training, nut-free protocol, Get Well Room,
  locked medication log.
- A real 15-class Fall 2026 enrichment catalog with published fees, days, grade bands
  and named outside professional providers.
- Financial assistance may extend to Extended Day and Clubhouse — written on the
  tuition page, and uncommon among peers.
- Strong Activity Day coverage: 7:45 am–6 pm on teacher workdays and most holidays,
  opening at 7 am on conference days — a full working-parent day on exactly the days
  that usually break childcare.
- Clubhouse is cheap and frictionless: $100/year flat, unlimited drop-in, with
  self-sign-out to practices and rehearsals.

Watch-outs
- No Upper School coverage at all — grades 9–12 dismiss at 3:10 pm with nothing published.
- No before-school care at any division on regular days, even though Activity Days show
  the capability exists (7:45 am, or 7 am on conference days).
- No drop-in for Lower School, stated explicitly. Occasional-need families are pushed to
  the $30/hour adjustment rate, which the school says is not drop-in pricing.
- Enrichment is gated behind Extended Day enrollment, so an enrichment class always sits
  on top of a monthly Extended Day charge.
- Activity Day fee is unpublished — an unbudgetable line item on days families most need
  coverage.
- Schedule changes are rigid: email required by the 1st of the month.
- Study hall is thin and narrow — grades 3–5 only, Mon–Thu only, 45 minutes, explicitly
  independent study with no one-on-one help. Grades 1–2 get none.
- Cost stacks on already-high tuition: grades 1–5 full-time is $470/mo on top of
  $32,960; TK full-time is $750/mo on top of $25,510.
- The 2:50 vs 3:00 gap for grades 1–5 (dismissal vs billed Extended Day start) is
  unexplained.
- Late pickup is $1.00/minute and unbounded.
- Billing-platform ambiguity: Blackbaud vs Clarity vs Smart Tuition across sources.
- No summer continuity — Extended Day closes on the last day of school and summer is a
  separate paid program with unpublished rates.
- No published Clubhouse daily structure, staff ratio, or homework-support commitment.

Visit checklist
- What is the Activity Day fee, and is it per-day or bundled for monthly-enrolled families?
- How many months is Extended Day billed — 9, 10, or 12? This changes the annual total
  by hundreds of dollars.
- Is there any Upper School after-school supervision — library, study hall, a supervised
  space — even if not a formal program?
- Is there truly no before-school option? If Activity Days can open at 7:00–7:45 am, why
  not regular days, and is there a demand list?
- What exactly happens between 2:50 and 3:00 pm for grades 1–5, and is it billed?
- How does financial assistance actually apply to Extended Day and Clubhouse — same
  award rate as tuition, and does it reach enrichment fees?
- Which platform actually bills Extended Day — Blackbaud, Clarity, or Smart Tuition?
- Are there sibling discounts on Extended Day or Clubhouse? Two children at $470/mo is
  $940/mo.
- What is the staff-to-child ratio by age group, and what is staff retention?
- Can you change tiers mid-year, and does the $30/hour adjustment rate apply?
- Clubhouse specifics: ratio, homework support, and is the $100 really the only cost?
- Is Extended Day capacity-constrained at any tier, especially 3–6 pm five-day?
- How do enrichment classes interact with the pickup tier — if a class runs to 4:45,
  must you buy the 6 pm tier?
