### The enrichment layer

Beyond the Extended Day sessions, after-school **extracurricular classes register
separately on LionsWeb** (the school's registration portal), in five stated
categories: **music, art, sports, robotics, and outdoor experiences**.

Named class-level offerings documented third-party: **Piano and Guitar Lessons,
Voice Lessons, Painting, After School Children's Choir (grades 3-5)**, plus
robotics (Junior First Lego League / First Lego League feed from the same
after-school layer).

### What is not published

A current class catalog with per-class fees, days, and grade bands is **not
published on the public site** — LionsWeb registration sits behind a login. The
five category names and the third-party class names above are the documented
extent. No published pricing for enrichment classes.
