# Covenant Day School — After School — Extended Day

> **Provenance:** Compiled by Claude Code on 2026-08-15 from the school's extended
> day page; every figure below is the school's own published rate.

### Sources

- https://www.covenantday.org/campus-life/extended-day
- https://www.covenantday.org/admissions/faqs (dismissals, free before-care)

### Sessions

Extended Day runs in **1.5-hour sessions**, grades JK-8:

| Session | Who |
|---|---|
| 1:30 – 3:00 p.m. | JK/K only (they dismiss at 1:30) |
| 3:00 – 4:30 p.m. | all JK-8 |
| 4:30 – 6:00 p.m. | all JK-8 — latest pickup **6:00 p.m.** |

Free before-care from 7:30 a.m. school-wide.

### Monthly pricing, per 1.5-hour session

| Days/week | Monthly cost |
|---|---|
| Drop-In | $20 |
| One day | $58 |
| Two days | $112 |
| Three days | $162 |
| Four days | $206 |
| Five days | $248 |

"August and December … are exactly half of the listed monthly rate."

### Annual pricing ("for 10 Months")

| Days/week | 1 session | 2 sessions | 3 sessions |
|---|---|---|---|
| One day | $522 | $1,044 | $1,566 |
| Two days | $1,008 | $2,016 | $3,024 |
| Three days | $1,458 | $2,916 | $4,374 |
| Four days | $1,854 | $3,708 | $5,562 |
| Five days | $2,232 | $4,464 | $6,696 |

### The most-expensive-arrangement figure for the app

A JK/K child needing coverage from 1:30 dismissal to 6:00 p.m. five days a week
buys **all three sessions**: 3 × $248 = **$744/month**, or **$6,696 "for 10
Months"** on the annual table (the school's own top-of-table figure). A Lower/
Middle School child (3:00/3:10 dismissal) to 6:00 p.m. five days is 2 sessions =
$496/month / $4,464 annual. The single-session five-day rate is **$248/month**.

Note the annual table is *cheaper* than 10 × monthly (e.g. $2,232 vs. $2,480 for
one session five days) — the annual figures bake in the half-rate August and
December months. Both are the school's own published numbers.
