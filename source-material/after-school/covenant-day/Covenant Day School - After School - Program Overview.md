# Covenant Day School — After School — Program Overview

> **Provenance:** Compiled by Claude Code on 2026-08-15 from the school's extended
> day page and admissions FAQs.

### Sources

- https://www.covenantday.org/campus-life/extended-day
- https://www.covenantday.org/admissions/faqs (bell schedule, free before-care)

### The coverage picture

- **School day:** JK & K 8:15 a.m.–1:30 p.m. · Lower School 8:15–3:00 · Middle
  School 8:15–3:10 · High School 8:15–3:15. **On Wednesdays all grade levels begin
  at 8:50 a.m.** (late start).
- **Free before-school care from 7:30 a.m.** until the start of school — no charge,
  per the admissions FAQ.
- **Extended Day Program, grades JK-8**, in 1.5-hour sessions after dismissal:
  1:30–3:00 p.m. (JK/K only) → 3:00–4:30 p.m. → 4:30–**6:00 p.m.** Latest pickup
  is 6:00 p.m.
- The program provides "a nurturing, Christ-centered environment after the regular
  school day" with homework assistance and recreational time.
- Separate **enrichment classes** (music, art, sports, robotics, outdoor
  experiences) register via LionsWeb — see the Enrichment file.

### Who it serves

JK through 8th grade. High schoolers (dismissed 3:15) have no formal aftercare —
their after-school programming is athletics (82% participation) and activities.
