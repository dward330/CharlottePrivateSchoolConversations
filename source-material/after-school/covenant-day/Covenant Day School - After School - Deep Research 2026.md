Free before-care 7:30 a.m. school-wide; Wednesdays start 8:50 a.m. (late start —
before-care absorbs the difference at no charge).

### The cost architecture — session-based, uniquely here

Covenant Day prices **per 1.5-hour session** rather than per grade band: $248/month
buys one session five days a week; full JK/K coverage (1:30→6:00) is three
sessions, $744/month by the monthly table or **$6,696 "for 10 Months"** by the
school's own annual table. The annual table is deliberately cheaper than
10 × monthly because "August and December … are exactly half of the listed monthly
rate." Drop-in exists at $20/session.

Comparable-arrangement figure (highest-priced band × latest pickup × 5 days):
**$744/month (JK/K, three sessions) · $6,696 published annual**. The single-session
five-day rate is $248/month — the figure a Lower/Middle family adds per session.

### Verdict-level observations

- The 6:00 p.m. ceiling matches four of the six existing schools; free before-care
  from 7:30 is the differentiator (peers charge for morning care).
- Session-stacking makes JK/K full coverage look expensive per month yet the
  $6,696 annual is mid-pack against Country Day ($9,000-est) and Latin ($9,300).
- No aftercare for grades 9-12, and no published enrichment-class catalog
  (LionsWeb login-gated) — the two disclosure gaps.
