SUPERSEDED (July 27, 2026) - the central claim below is REFUTED. Cannon DOES publish a full After School Program rate card, a drop-in hourly rate, a $25 registration fee and a $1/minute late-pickup fee. They live on a surviving orphaned backup page the July 22 pass never reached: https://www.cannonschool.org/backup-copy-back-to-school-center - inside a collapsed accordion invisible to a plain page fetch. See "Cannon - After School - Redesign Research 2026.md" in this folder for the full table. Note the published rates are 2025-26, not 2026-27.

Why the earlier 3-0 verification was not careless: the live Back-to-School Center was emptied for 2026-27 and now points families to the My Cannon Portal, and the program page and tuition page genuinely do carry zero dollar figures. The earlier result was correct for the pages it checked; it simply checked the wrong two pages.

ORIGINAL (July 22, 2026) FINDING, now known to be wrong: Cannon does not publish after-school pricing. The After School Program serves grades JrK-8 with care until 6:00 p.m., but no day-per-week rates, drop-in rates, or late-pickup fees appear on the public program page or the tuition-and-fees page, and no third-party source publishes Cannon's rates. Families are directed to afterschool@cannonschool.org or the Family Portal (Veracross) for cost details.
