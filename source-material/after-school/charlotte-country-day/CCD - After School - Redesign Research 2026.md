## Card — The Coverage Map

Dismissal times, all divisions (Quick Reference). All divisions start at 8:00 am:

| Junior Kindergarten (JK) | 1:15 pm |
| Kindergarten | 2:00 pm |

The unusually early JK dismissal (1:15 pm) — nearly two hours before Middle School —
drives the JK fee structure, and JK is the only band with a 2:00 pm pickup tier.
Delayed opening: classes start at 10:00 am, JK–12.

Programs by division
- Extended Day — grades JK–8, the umbrella care program. Home is the Brock Center for
  Extended Day.
- Enrichment Classes — "more than 25 after-school enrichment classes." The FAQ scopes
  these to Lower and Middle School, but the enrichment time-slot table published for
  program directors includes a grades 9–12 slot.
- After School Sports — JK–8.
- Swim Programs — Klein Aquatic Center, ages 2–12, open to the public. Run by Athletics,
  not by After School.

Pickup tiers — four, plus a JK-only fifth: 2:00 pm (JK only), 3:00 pm (carpool siblings
only), 3:30 pm, 4:30 pm, 6:00 pm. Entry price (1 day/week, monthly). NOTE: the
app's Coverage Map surfaces the **5 day/week** column from the full 13-row matrix
below, not this 1-day entry column, because full-time is the common case and a
1-day figure reads as "the price" when it is the cheapest possible one:

| Band | 2:00 pm | 3:00 pm | 3:30 pm | 4:30 pm | 6:00 pm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| JK | $60 | $115 | $140 | $200 | $255 |
| K | — | $60 | $90 | $140 | $208 |
| Grades 1–4 | — | — | — | $90 | $175 |
| Middle School | — | — | — | $60 | $140 |

Notes & gaps
- Upper School (grades 9–12): NO Extended Day coverage. Extended Day is explicitly JK–8
  on every page. Upper School students may access enrichment classes (a 3:45–4:45 pm
  slot exists) but there is no supervised-care program for them.
- Before-school care during the school year: NOT PUBLISHED. Nothing on the Quick
  Reference guide, the FAQ, or anywhere else. Before-care exists only for summer camps
  (7:30–9:00 am).
- Half-day / conference / professional days: "The program operates on a full-day basis
  during parent conferences and faculty professional days at a separate cost," with
  special activities planned. The separate cost is NOT PUBLISHED. School holiday and
  break coverage is NOT PUBLISHED.
- Program dates: August 19, 2026 – May 25, 2027. Registration deadline for 2026-27 was
  June 5, 2026 — already passed as of July 2026, so late registrants likely route
  through drop-in. Post-deadline availability is NOT PUBLISHED.
- Summer: "Country Day's Summer Programs", https://www.charlottecountryday.org/summer —
  ages 4–18, June 8 – July 24, 2026, 150+ camps. $15 annual registration per camper;
  before-camp care (7:30–9 am) $105/wk; afternoon care (4–5 pm) $85/wk; lunch
  supervision $30/wk; Super Care (all three) $160/wk. A separate Summer Programs
  financial aid application exists.

Structural observations (arithmetic on published values, not new facts)
- Max exposure: JK, 6:00 pm, 5 days/week = $900/month.
- Cheapest full-week option: $255/mo (JK 2:00 pm, K 3:00 pm, or MS 4:30 pm).
- The K 6:00 pm row is the only one with non-round values ($208, $489, $615) — every
  other row uses round numbers. Worth confirming this row is not a typo.
- Middle School is the cheapest band at equivalent tiers, because MS dismisses at
  3:10 pm and therefore consumes less care time.

Fees & fine print
- Contract registration fee: $80, due at enrollment.
- Drop-in: $19/hour, one-hour minimum, plus a one-time $40 registration fee (separate
  from the $80 contract fee). Drop-in requires 24-hour advance notice via form.
- Late pick-up after 6:00 pm: $10 per 10-minute interval or fraction thereof.
- Billing: families are billed monthly by the Business Office. Extended Day is an
  incidental charge alongside dining hall, Campus Store, tutoring, PE uniforms and bus
  fees; payable online or by check.
- Contract changes: maximum one change per semester; additional changes incur a $50 fee;
  no contract changes permitted in August or December.
- Contracts: 1–5 days/week, monthly or annual.

Notes & gaps
- Sibling discount: NOT PUBLISHED. (The 3:00 pm JK/K tier is restricted to "carpool
  siblings only," which is a scheduling accommodation, not a discount.)
- Financial-aid applicability to Extended Day: NOT PUBLISHED. Tuition "covers the core
  educational experience including technology"; Extended Day is classified as an
  incidental charge billed separately, which places it outside the tuition-assistance
  envelope. About 30% of K–12 students receive need-based tuition assistance. A separate
  Summer Programs financial aid application does exist, so the school-year asymmetry is
  conspicuous.
- 2026-27 tuition context: JK $24,515 · K $25,725 · Grades 1–4 $30,375 · Grades 5–8
  $32,700 · Grades 9–12 $34,075.

## Card — A Day Inside + Enrichment

The program in the school's own words
- "Our Extended Day program provides a warm, structured environment for students from JK
  through grade 8. Whether attending daily or dropping in occasionally, students enjoy a
  mix of homework time, outdoor play, games, enrichment, and a healthy snack."
- "A safe, engaging space for students in grades JK–8 to learn, play, and grow after the
  school day ends."
- "a safe, engaging place to unwind, explore, and connect after the school day ends"
- "structured care and supervised play until 6 pm"
- From the school-news feature: "a vibrant extension of the school day and our Mission
  and Affirmation of Community"; a balance of "academic support and extracurricular
  fun"; "a home away from home filled with connection, creativity, and exploration";
  "every day offers something new".

Published hour-by-hour daily schedule: NOT PUBLISHED. The school publishes the
components — homework time, outdoor play, games, enrichment, healthy snack — but never
sequences them with clock times. The only time anchors published are the pickup tiers
and the enrichment slot times below.

Observed activities (from the school-news feature): collaborative art projects;
basketball; competitive board games; Lego building; playground time with the school dog,
Peaches; cross-grade activities (Upper School students helping JK students make Lunar New
Year paper snake crafts); winter scavenger hunts and snowball target practice. The
cross-grade detail is notable — Upper School students, who have no Extended Day of their
own, appear in the program as helpers.

Middle School: NOT PUBLISHED as a distinct program. Grades 5–8 sit under the same JK–8
Extended Day umbrella with their own fee rows (4:30 and 6:00 pm only — MS has no 3:00 or
3:30 tier). Whether MS students are grouped separately from Lower School students, and
what programming differentiates them, is NOT PUBLISHED.

Snack: "a healthy snack" is included in the program description. Whether it is included
in the fee or billed separately is NOT PUBLISHED.

## Card — Verdict & Visit Checklist

Strengths
- The most granular pricing ladder in this set: 13 published rows across five pickup
  times and five day-counts, so a family pays close to only what it uses. A JK family
  needing 2:00 pm coverage twice a week pays $115/month, not a flat care fee.
- Full published rate card, publicly accessible — no "call for pricing." Every fee
  (contract, drop-in, late, contract-change) is on the open web.
- Coverage to 6:00 pm, late enough for a standard commute, and the school explicitly
  frames the program around dual-income families: "More than 50 percent of our
  two-parent families are dual income, and they can count on After School programs to
  meet their children's needs and interests."
- Enrichment starts at the dismissal bell for every division, with published per-grade
  ratios — no dead gap between school and activity.
- Four enrichment cycles a year with a low-commitment 3-week mini option at $225.
- Real breadth: 25+ enrichment classes plus six sports tracks, several through credible
  outside partners.
- On-site aquatic center with private/semi-private instruction, open to the public.
- Drop-in is a real option, not a token one — $19/hour with a modest $40 one-time fee.
- JK-appropriate design: the 1:15 pm JK dismissal is backstopped by five separate pickup
  tiers, and JK enrichment runs a developmentally sensible 30 minutes at 10:1.

Watch-outs
- No Upper School coverage — Extended Day stops at grade 8.
- Cost compounds steeply: JK at 6:00 pm five days is $900/month, roughly $8,100/year on
  top of $24,515 tuition — about a 33% increase over sticker. The 6:00 pm tier costs
  roughly 3.5× the 2:00 pm tier for the same child.
- Extended Day appears to sit outside financial aid: billed as an incidental charge with
  no published aid policy, even though ~30% of students receive tuition assistance.
  Summer programs, by contrast, have a dedicated aid application.
- Contract rigidity: one change per semester, $50 for any additional change, and no
  changes at all in August or December — the two months a family is most likely to
  discover its schedule was wrong.
- The June 5, 2026 registration deadline has already passed; a newly enrolling family may
  be routed to $19/hour drop-in or a waitlist.
- No published daily schedule — components are listed, but no times and no homework-help
  staffing model.
- The enrichment catalog is invisible to prospective families, behind a portal currently
  showing 0 results.
- Conflicting ratio figures: 12:1 on the enrichment hub versus up to 20:1 in the
  per-grade table. Both are published by the school.
- No Middle School differentiation published.
- Before-school care is entirely NOT PUBLISHED for the school year despite an 8:00 am
  start across all divisions.
- Fragmented systems: Extended Day registers via Veracross, enrichment and summer via
  UltraCamp, swim via Jackrabbit — three portals, three logins, three billing rhythms.
- Outside providers mean outside terms — fees, refunds and vetting sit with the vendor.
- Half-day and conference-day care costs "a separate cost" that is never stated.
- Enrichment refunds are unforgiving: nothing back once instruction begins.

Visit checklist
- What actually happens between 3:15 and 6:00 pm, hour by hour? How much is homework
  support versus free play?
- Is homework help staffed by teachers or by supervision-only staff?
- Are Middle School students programmed separately from Lower School?
- Does financial aid ever apply to Extended Day? If not, what do aided families do?
- Is there any before-school or early drop-off option during the school year?
- Registration closed June 5 — what are the options now for 2026-27?
- What is the separate cost for full-day care on conference and professional days, and
  how many such days are there?
- What happens on school holidays, snow days, and breaks?
- Which ratio is real: 12:1, or the per-grade table up to 20:1?
- Is the K 6:00 pm row ($208 / $489 / $615) correct, or a typo? Every other row is round.
- Is snack included in the monthly fee, and what are the allergy protocols?
- Is there any sibling discount?
- What is the actual Fall 2026 enrichment class list — names, days, grade fits?
- If my child does a 4:30 pm enrichment class, does Extended Day pick them up afterward,
  and does that change my contracted tier or my bill?
- How are outside vendors vetted and background-checked, and who is liable?
- What is the staff-to-child ratio in Extended Day itself, as opposed to enrichment?
- Can the pickup tier be changed mid-year given the one-change-per-semester and $50 rule?
- Is there a cap on Extended Day enrollment — can a contract be denied at the tier I need?
