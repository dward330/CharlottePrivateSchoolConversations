**Provenance.** Collected 2026-10-20 by Claude (Claude Code), fetching Hickory Grove
Christian School's **own** pages directly (`curl`, raw HTML) and the PDFs those pages link
to. Per the user's instruction for this area, **only official school sources** are used:
hgchristian.org and the Finalsite-hosted PDFs it links. No third-party listing, district
page or state program page is used as evidence for a school fact.

`5da24575-40bf-4787-8934-0fadfc56059f`, etag `1792544338475945`). The design was drawn from
an earlier research pass; **the live site has changed since**, and the live site wins (see
"Corrections to the design" at the end).

## S2 — Educational Support Services (verbatim)

> Educational Support Services (ESS) is the umbrella under which students receive various
> individualized academic supports. ESS teachers and academic coaches/tutors have
> specialized training to help students realize their God-given potential. Services may
> include academic coaching, tutoring, learning accommodations, consultation with teachers
> and parents, and specialized support for students with identified learning differences.
>
> ESS works collaboratively with families, teachers, and students to promote academic
> success, self-advocacy, organizational skills, personal responsibility, and other
> executive functioning skills. Educational Support Services also assists with the
> implementation and monitoring of accommodation plans and provides guidance regarding
> available academic resources.
>
> As parents begin the admissions process, they are encouraged to provide any documents
> that describe their students' learning needs or diagnoses that may impact learning. All
> students who enroll with a Section 504 Plan, an Individual Education Plan (IEP), or an
> academic accommodations plan from a previous school may be enrolled under the umbrella of
> Educational Support Services. We provide a range of fee-based services to address the
> needs of our learners.
>
> Currently, our specialization does not extend to providing emotional and behavioral
> interventions, as our staff is best equipped to provide academic interventions. We assist
> families in connecting with educational testing providers, as needed. Additionally, we
> provide direction for parents in navigating the public school district to request
> educational evaluation services that may be available to their students as authorized by
> the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA).

## S3 — 2026-2027 Educational Support Services Fees (transcribed; "Revised 1/22/26")

> All services require an annual contract/financial agreement.

**K5-12th Case Management Only** (Pull-out testing services are not included.) — **$300/year**
- Development and maintenance of an HGCS Accommodations Plan based on educational testing
  and/or 504/IEP from previous school
- Facilitation of annual review with teachers and parents; student attends when appropriate
- Learning and testing accommodations occur in primary classroom

**K5-12th Case Management and Approved Pull-Out/Push-In Testing Services Only** — Standard Testing

|---|---|
| 1 subject | $370/year |
| 2 subjects | $760/year |
| 3+ subjects | $1200/year |

|---|---|

**9th–12th Grade: All include Case Management, and 1:1/Small Group Instruction**

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| 1-2 Academic Flex Sessions — Up to 60 min. weekly | $1806/year* · $903/semester |
| Academic Coaching — Up to 3 hours weekly | $2227/year* · $1113/semester |
| HS Instructional Support Class — 5 class periods weekly | $4455/year* · **$2227/year** (sic) |

**K5-12th Grade: Applied Interventions/Specially Designed Instruction** — **$1500-$2000/year**
> Annual Fee Depends on Individual Student Need. This addition to a student's service plan
> is developed based on professional diagnosis, teacher feedback, specialist observations,
> student performance, and parent/student goals.

> \*Academic Flex & Coaching sessions may be scheduled and billed per semester based on each
> student's needs.
> \*\*HS Instructional Support Class is scheduled per semester.

**Reading note.** The HS Instructional Support Class's second figure reads "$2227/year" on
the PDF, where the two rows above it give a per-semester figure in that position, and the
2024-25 schedule (S4) printed "$2227/semester" for the same class. It is very likely a
per-semester price misprinted; the app shows only **$4455/year** and turns the discrepancy
into a tour question rather than guessing.

**Change from 2024-25 (S4):** testing rose $357/$715/$1155 → $370/$760/$1200; K5-8 and
9-12 instruction fees are unchanged; the HS class changed "80 mins./day Monday-Friday" →
"5 class periods weekly"; **Applied Interventions/Specially Designed Instruction
($1500-$2000) is new** for 2026-27.
