## S1 — Learning Support Program (verbatim)

> Engaging Fully in the Learning Process
>
> Cannon School's Learning Support Program helps students with diagnosed learning
> differences become successful learners and confident self-advocates. We are committed to
> ensuring that all programs and services are fully accessible, providing reasonable and
> effective accommodations that promote student success and foster independence.
>
> **Learning Support Program's Mission** — Our program is strengths-based and
> growth-oriented, focused on the process of developing self-awareness and self-advocacy.
> We aim to maximize the academic achievement and personal success of students with
> learning differences at Cannon School.
>
> View Cannon's Learning Support Program Policy and Enrollment Form here. *(→ S2)*

**Lower School — Junior Kindergarten–Second Grade (JK–2)**

> In Junior Kindergarten through Second Grade, students experience significant
> developmental growth. Learning support at this stage focuses on early identification and
> classroom-based interventions. The Learning Specialist collaborates closely with teachers
> to assess student needs, differentiate instruction, and implement targeted strategies
> within the classroom. Regular communication among parents, teachers, counselors, and the
> Learning Specialist ensures a team approach to supporting students who may face
> challenges or show signs of a possible learning difference.

**Lower School — Third–Fourth Grade (3–4)**

> In Third and Fourth Grades, learning support begins to foster students' self-awareness
> as learners. Students are encouraged to explore how they learn, study, and interact with
> others, laying the foundation for self-advocacy. A collaborative team—including teachers,
> counselors, and the Learning Specialist—works together to implement classroom
> interventions while encouraging students to actively understand and express their
> learning needs.
>
> In Lower School, we are committed to supporting every child's unique learning journey.
> When students need additional reading support beyond the classroom, our Reading
> Interventionist may provide small-group instruction designed to close specific skill gaps
> and build confidence in young readers. For students who demonstrate readiness to go above
> and beyond, our Reading Interventionist may offer targeted academic enrichment to further
> support their reading development. Additionally, our Learning Specialist works closely
> with families to provide guidance for families to secure external resources, such as
> speech therapists or tutors, ensuring a well-rounded support system that complements the
> school's efforts.

**Middle School**

> Middle School learning support focuses on fostering self-awareness and helping students
> understand how they learn, study, and interact with others. Led by the Middle School
> Learning Specialist, a collaborative team—including teachers, advisors, deans, and
> counselors—works together to empower students to use their accommodations and develop
> strong self-advocacy skills.
>
> Students are introduced to their learning plans through one of three approaches: parents
> may share the plan with their child, the Learning Specialist may present the plan, or the
> student and parents may meet with the Learning Specialist. The chosen approach depends on
> parental preference, student readiness, and developmental needs.
>
> One-on-one academic coaching with the Middle School Learning Specialist helps students
> strengthen executive functioning skills, such as time management, organization, and
> self-advocacy. These skills are further reinforced in all classes, while subject-specific
> support is available through help sessions with teachers.
>
> The program emphasizes a relational approach, recognizing meaningful support requires
> time and individualized attention. Students are encouraged to embrace their learning
> differences and appreciate that everyone learns in their own unique way.

**Upper School**

> Upper School learning support builds on the foundation established in Middle School,
> guiding students to enhance their self-awareness, explore their learning styles, and
> develop essential skills in time management, organization, communication, and
> self-advocacy. Students actively participate in creating and implementing personalized
> learning plans that include specific learning and testing accommodations. They are
> encouraged to take ownership of their educational journey by advocating for their needs
> and making effective use of their accommodations.
>
> Led by the Upper School Learning Specialists, the support team includes the student,
> parents, teachers, the Upper School Assistant Head for Academics, the Student Dean, the
> Upper School Counselor, and the student's advisor. Together, they ensure that the
> student's voice is heard and their individual learning needs are addressed.
>
> Students have access to one-on-one academic coaching sessions with Upper School Learning
> Specialists to strengthen key executive functioning skills. Subject-specific support is
> available through The Academic Resource Center (The ARC) and Academic Labs, providing
> targeted help in areas like math, writing, science and world languages (Spanish, French,
> Chinese).
>
> The program prioritizes relationship-building and developmentally appropriate goals,
> ensuring students with learning differences receive personalized, effective support. For
> juniors and seniors, additional guidance is offered to help navigate the process of
> requesting testing accommodations from the College Board and ACT.

## S2 — Learning Support Program Policy and Enrollment Form (full transcription)

Retrieved 2026-10-24 via the S1 link, which 302s to
`https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1744298191/cannonschoolorg/rgdxn8w18phggi7m2irf/LearningSupportPolicyandEnrollmentFormEdited2025.pdf`.
Two pages; plain prose plus a blank form, so the text layer is reliable.

**Page 1 — CANNON STUDENT LEARNING SUPPORT PROGRAM / ENGAGING FULLY IN THE LEARNING PROCESS**

> The purpose of Student Learning Support is to assist students with diagnosed learning
> differences in achieving success within Cannon's challenging academic setting. Learning
> Support provides students with strategies, organizational skills, and study techniques to
> develop the self-awareness and self-advocacy necessary for them to become effective
> lifelong learners. Course instruction incorporates best teaching practices that promote
> techniques and study skills to help all students maximize their individual strengths,
> engage fully in the learning process, and apply their individual strengths to enhance
> academic performance. Cannon School is dedicated to ensuring that all programs and
> services of the school are accessible by providing reasonable and effective
> accommodations which promote success and independence in each student.
>
> **Eligibility:** To be considered for Student Learning Support, Learning Plan, and
> Accommodations
> - Student must be a current student enrolled at Cannon School
> - Parents must complete a request for Learning Support Services
> - Cannon must be able to accommodate the student's needs with present resources.
> - Student must have demonstrated a history of academic difficulty
> - Student must have a specific learning difference that has been diagnosed and documented
>   by a certified professional in a recent (last 3 years) formal psycho-educational
>   evaluation. The testing must have been done by a licensed clinical psychologist; include
>   comprehensive cognitive and academic assessment; and provide specific information
>   regarding the impact the disability has on the academic performance of the student.
>   Acceptable comprehensive cognitive and academic assessments are individually
>   administered, nationally normed, and administered under standardized conditions. A copy
>   of the complete evaluation must be provided and must include a summary of the assessment
>   procedures and evaluation instruments used to make the diagnosis; a narrative summary of
>   the evaluation results; test results with subtest scores (standard or scaled scores);
>   and specific recommendations for accommodations as well as why each accommodation is
>   recommended.
>
> **Accommodations:** Accommodations and services are provided for students with documented
> disabilities on a case-by-case basis. The following are examples of the accommodations
> that may be coordinated when appropriate: preferential seating, note-taking assistance,
> extended time for tests, alternate assessment location, alternate forms of class
> handouts, or large print testing. To be eligible for accommodations, students must
> provide documentation from a licensed clinician that states the specific diagnosis.
> Recommendations by the clinician regarding the impact of the disability on academic tasks
> as well as appropriate accommodations will be taken into consideration. No accommodation
> will be provided unless a disclosure with appropriate documentation has been made to the
> school.
>
> **Learning Plans:** When a student is eligible for Learning Support as verified by the
> documentation described above, the Learning Specialist will create a Learning Plan for
> the student that will detail the learning difference and diagnosis, list applicable
> classroom accommodations, and list applicable testing accommodations. The Learning Plan
> will be shared with the student, parents, and student's teachers to ensure that all
> accommodations are appropriate for the student's learning need while maintaining the
> integrity of our school's curriculum.
>
> **College Board and ACT Accommodations Request:** When a student has been eligible for
> Learning Support and has had a Learning Plan in place for at least four months, official
> accommodation requests may be submitted to College Board and/or ACT. The Learning
> Specialist may assist families with submission of this request. Confidentiality release
> forms must be signed allowing Cannon to disclose information regarding the student's
> learning diagnosis and learning plan.
>
> **Not Otherwise Specified Learning Impairments:** When a student has a learning impairment
> not documented by a complete psychological-educational evaluation such as a hearing
> impairment, speech/language impairment, vision, etc. reasonable accommodations will be
> considered on a case-by-case basis. Any request for accommodations must be accompanied by
> documentation from an appropriate physician and/or evaluator. The Learning Specialist will
> assess the request, decide which accommodations are appropriate and reasonable, and
> communicate those accommodations to the student's teacher(s). No official Learning Plan
> will be constructed without a complete psychological-educational evaluation.
>
> **Acceptance of a student into Cannon School does not imply that the school will assume
> the responsibility for all of the special learning needs of the child, especially when
> such needs may require a modification/alteration of the school's curriculum or when the
> child's needs are beyond Cannon's present resources.

## S3 — Upper School Academic Resource Center (verbatim)

> The Academic Resource Center at Cannon School creates opportunities for students to learn
> how to learn. Imperative to a student's success in the Academic Resource Center program
> is a willingness to engage fully with all available resources and learning opportunities,
> including academic coaching and academic labs. This model encourages the individual
> student to drive aspects of their educational experience and empowers them to take
> ownership of their learning.
>
> **Academic Coaching** — Our Academic Coaches work in a relational 1:1 environment with
> students during each coaching session to build and strengthen executive functioning (EF)
> skills. These include planning, organization, task initiation, flexibility, attention,
> self-control, metacognition, and more. Our academic coaching sessions are customized based
> on identified areas of greatest need for each student, following an initial conversation
> between coach and student.
>
> **Academic Labs** — The Academic Lab Program at Cannon School offers faculty-facilitated,
> peer-led tutoring in writing, math, and science to Upper School students. The goal of the
> Academic Lab Program is to provide students with additional support outside of the
> classroom, in addition to meeting with their teachers for extra help. Available at
> consistent times and locations during the school day, our trained Academic Lab Fellows
> use friendly curiosity to engage their peers in the process of learning.

Sub-labs described: Writing Lab ("Writing Lab Fellows act as thought partners and
inquisitive guides rather than scribes or editors"), World Languages Lab, Science Lab
(physics, chemistry, biology), Math Lab ("Tutors and teachers partner with learners to break
down challenging concepts, explore multiple approaches, and develop strategies…"). Lab
Fellows are Upper School students selected each spring by application and interview.
