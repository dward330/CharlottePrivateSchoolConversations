# Charlotte Preparatory School — Course Offerings — Curriculum by Division

Charlotte Prep is a PreK–8 school in three divisions: a Montessori Early School, a Lower School for Kindergarten to 4th grade, and a Middle School for 5th to 8th grade. The school does not publish a course catalog, a program of studies or a current curriculum guide. What follows is assembled from its division pages, its fall 2025 admissions packet, its testing overview and its news stories. Each fact carries its date.

### The Three Divisions at a Glance

| Division | Grades or ages | How teaching is organised |
|---|---|---|
| Montessori Early School | Ages 2 to 6: Pre-Primary, Primary, Transitional Kindergarten (TK), Montessori Kindergarten (MK) | Montessori method, uninterrupted work cycles |
| Lower School | Kindergarten to 4th grade | Workshop model, plus a 6-day rotation of specials |
| Middle School | 5th to 8th grade | Rotation schedule, Spanish as a core class, leadership curriculum |

Families choose between two kindergarten options: Montessori Kindergarten in the Early School, or Kindergarten in the Lower School. Both are listed at $25,800 for 2026-27 (https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/tuition-fees).

Fifth grade sits in the Middle School by design: "As a PreK-8th Grade school, having our 5th-grade students as part of our Middle School makes sense. They are developmentally ready for more responsibility and learn at an early age how a rotation schedule works" (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/middle-school).

### Why the School Stops at 8th Grade

The school sets out its reasoning on its PreK-8 model page (https://charlotteprep.org/about/the-prek-8-model): "We believe, and research shows, the Kindergarten-8th grade model is the best for our students." Without a high school on campus, it says, students "stay a step removed from the presence and pressures of high school". The Middle School students become "the true student-leaders of our school".

### Montessori Early School

The Early School follows the Montessori method. "Our teachers are degree-educated and Montessori certified. We are members of the American Montessori Society and follow its guidelines as a basis for our program" (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/montessori-early-school). The school is also an American Montessori Society member on its Fast Facts list.

The school describes three age bands:

- **Younger children (ages 2-3)** "focus on gaining independence, large motor control, and early language and communication skills."
- **Primary students (ages 3-4)** "build concentration, coordination, organization, and independence through repeated, sequential practical life activities in preparation for future academic work."
- **Older students (ages 5-6)** "begin to make connections between concrete and abstract concepts through the use of specialized didactic materials and meaningful learning experiences."

Specials in the Early School, per the fall 2025 admissions packet: "All early school students participate in music classes. Montessori Kindergarten students have weekly art, P.E., Spanish, and Tech classes."

### The Montessori Three-Year Cycle

The school's 2025 guide to the Primary cycle (https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/montessori_3_year_cycle) sets out what a child typically works on in each year:

| Year | Age | Typical milestones (school's wording, abridged) |
|---|---|---|
| First Year | 3 | Beginning phonemic awareness; "Developing the concepts of grading and 1:1 correspondence, and working on numeral recognition 0-10" |
| Second Year | 4 | "Connecting phonetic sounds to their alphabet symbols"; "Connecting the numerals and quantities for numbers 0-20 and beyond"; a shorter rest time and an extra work cycle |
| Third Year | 5 | Writing with the movable alphabet and pencil; "Beginning to blend sounds to decode (read) words"; "Extending mathematical concepts, such as the decimal system and operations"; teaching younger peers |

The guide adds that "these characteristics are general; they may occur at different ages or times within the 3-year cycle." It also says that "Children do not need 'skill and drill' (sight words, flashcards, etc.) outside of school."

### Lower School, Kindergarten to Grade 4

The Lower School teaches through a **workshop model**: "With a workshop framework as the core of our instructional model, students can set and achieve personal goals for growth, while continuing to build developmentally-appropriate cognitive skills alongside peers" (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/lower-school). Concepts are "taught over time, first at a simplified level and eventually at a complex level."

- **Reading and writing, K–2:** "foundational language skills–in the areas of reading, writing, speaking, and listening—are the focus of daily instruction, including decoding knowledge, word attack strategies, fluency, literal and inferential comprehension skills, and essential skills in the writing process."
- **Reading and writing, grades 3–4:** students "support cross-discipline learning, develop interpretive and analytic comprehension skills, and establish their voices as writers."
- **Math:** "grounded in a global philosophy. A balanced approach is used to build and apply computational, conceptual, and problem-solving knowledge."

**Named programmes.** These come from the school's Prep Fund page (https://charlotteprep.org/support/the-prep-fund):

- **Collaborative Classroom**, a reading curriculum "based on the science of reading", for grades K-3, introduced with 2022-23 Prep Fund support and listed again as "Launched" in 2023-24.
- **SIPPS** reading intervention, used by learning-support specialists in Lower and Middle School.
- **Daily Language Instruction**, a grammar programme for 3rd and 4th grade.
- **IXL**, an online practice platform for math, used in grades 3-8 since 2023.

### The Six-Day Specials Rotation

"Students in Kindergarten through 4th Grade participate routinely in a 6-day rotation of specialized classes" (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/lower-school). The rotation covers:

- **Fine Arts:** music, drama and art
- **Technology and STEM**
- **Spanish**
- **Physical Education**

The school does not publish how many times each special meets in a rotation.

### Middle School, Grades 5 to 8

The Middle School page (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/middle-school) describes "a four-year sequence" that "introduces students to developmentally appropriate concepts and then reinforces and builds on that knowledge over time." Its published curriculum highlights:

- **Technology:** students "become creative and responsible users of emerging technologies, leveraging tools like coding and robotics."
- **Spanish** as a core class (see below).
- **Leadership and character:** "tangible opportunities to learn characteristics of leadership and exemplify good character."
- **Public speaking:** "Students learn how to present themselves, speak confidently in public, and thrive in an increasingly complex, global society."
- **Arts and athletics:** "Our robust arts and athletics programs provide a well-rounded experience for all students."

The fall 2025 admissions packet adds: "Reading and writing are emphasized throughout the curriculum" and "Our integrated STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math) program reaches every classroom."

### Math: A Year Ahead

The fall 2025 admissions packet states: "Our standard math curriculum is a full year ahead of learning at most other schools." The school does not say which schools it is comparing itself with.

**Algebra and Geometry in 8th grade.** The school's April 2026 testing overview lists, for grade 8, a "CTP-5 Algebra Sub-Test (For students in Geometry or Algebra only)" (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/testing-at-charlotte-prep). So some 8th graders are taking Algebra or Geometry. The school does not publish:

- its current Middle School math course names
- how students are placed
- whether either course carries high-school credit

### Spanish from Kindergarten to Grade 8

"We offer Spanish as our foreign language for grades K-8. Middle School students take Spanish as a core class, developing the confidence and skills they will need to succeed in high school Spanish" (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/middle-school).

- Spanish is the **only** world language offered.
- Spanish appears in the Lower School specials rotation, and weekly for Montessori Kindergarten.
- Middle School Spanish uses the **Somos** curriculum, which "uses comprehension-based and proficiency-oriented methods" (Prep Fund page, 2022-23).
- Students in grades 5-8 have used IXL in Spanish for listening and reading since 2023.
- 8th graders sit the **National Spanish Exam**.
- Spanish is under a full curriculum review in 2026-27 (see below).

The school does not say that its Spanish earns high-school credit. It says only that students are prepared "to succeed in high school Spanish".

### Leadership Class and Public Speaking

The Middle School has a leadership curriculum: "students learn about presenting themselves, including interview skills such as shaking hands properly, appropriate dress, eye contact, and asking thoughtful questions" (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/leadership).

- **8th-grade Ignite talks:** in the leadership class, 8th graders give "Prep 5x30" talks, "five slides displayed for 30 seconds each", on a topic of their choice (April 30, 2026: https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/8th-grade-ignite-presentations).
- **6th grade:** a persuasive speech project (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/academics-overview).
- **8th-grade Capstone Project:** policy proposals built around the 8th-grade trip to Washington, D.C. The school's strategic plan names it as a signature programme launching in 2024-25.
- **Model United Nations:** a Middle School club that attends interschool conferences (February 4, 2026: https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/sparking-debate-to-grow-critical-thinking).

### Arts and PE in Middle School

From the fall 2025 admissions packet: "Students may choose between Chorus and Band as 5th graders, while they continue to take both Drama and Art. Once they are in 7th grade they may choose between Drama and Art. In addition, students have Physical Education twice a week."

| Grade | Music | Drama and Art | PE |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5th–6th | Chorus or Band | Both Drama and Art | Twice a week |
| 7th–8th | The packet does not say | Choose Drama or Art | Twice a week |

### Joy Term

Each January, students in kindergarten to 8th grade set aside the regular timetable for Joy Term, a week of mini-courses chosen by interest.

- **2025 (inaugural):** "over 20 'mini-courses'", from Juggling to Maker Space Engineering. Each student enrolled in 3 to 4 (January 31, 2025: https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/prep-s-inaugural-joy-term).
- **2026:** "over 20 courses", from "Show Choir" to "Adventures in Aviation" (January 13, 2026: https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/joy-term-returns).

### How Charlotte Prep Tests

The school publishes this testing schedule (April 9, 2026: https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/testing-at-charlotte-prep):

| Grade | Test | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Grades 3-6 | CTP-5 (Comprehensive Testing Program, Fifth Edition) | ERB (Education Records Bureau) |
| Grade 7 | Independent School Entrance Exam (ISEE), for practice | ERB |
| Grade 8 | Independent School Entrance Exam (ISEE), for high school placement | ERB |
| Grade 8 | National Spanish Exam | American Association of Teachers of Spanish and Portuguese |
| Grade 8 | CTP-5 Algebra Sub-Test (for students in Geometry or Algebra only) | ERB |
| Grade 8 | Core Class Final Exams | Charlotte Preparatory School |

The school's stance: "In our classrooms, we do not 'teach to the test'". It calls the results "one snapshot of student learning—not the full picture." Its strategic plan records that the school "streamlined its use of the ERB standardized test" to reading and mathematics.

### How the Curriculum Is Being Rebuilt

The school is partway through a multi-year curriculum review, described in its September 24, 2026 story (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/building-the-foundations-for-student-learning):

- **2021:** a summer grant programme for teacher-led curriculum work began. It has grown from 8 teachers to a cohort of 24.
- **2023-2024:** the school "formally adopted Understanding by Design (UbD)", a backward-planning framework that starts from what students should know and be able to do.
- **2024 to present:** improvements in "phonics and reading, math, health, the arts, responsive classroom approaches, scheduling, field trips, and Joy Term".
- **2025 to present:** two subjects get a deep-dive review each year. These were Math and Writing last year, Spanish and Social Studies this year (2026-27), and STEM and ELA in 2027-2028.

The 2026 summer grant projects include:

- a K-2 phonics and sight-word scope and sequence
- hands-on math work in Kindergarten, 3rd and 4th grade
- a PK-4 Spanish "Cuaderno de Español"
- design teams reviewing Social Studies and Spanish

The strategic plan also records a move to "longer instructional blocks in 2025-2026".

## Method

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-19.

**How.** The pages were fetched with curl (raw HTML) and the text extracted locally, so every quotation is verbatim. The PDFs were run through pdftotext -layout. The Issuu documents were fetched as page images from image.isu.pub with the header Referer: https://issuu.com/; their reader3_4.json manifests return AccessDenied. No OCR tool was installed, so the images were read visually. The Issuu document ids were:

- montessori_3_year_cycle = 250506190340-9841aeb1ecbc500d33f0b4c4d2a37db7 (5 pages, May 2025)
- ms_curriculum_overview-2018 = 180914161114-89cde402c4d163ce9c52775bfd62c7e4 (5 pages)
- curriculum_overview_2018-revised = 180911191527-a39124fbd084bf4f7ef8493feb21742d (5 pages; the Lower School math page uses an I/D/M "Introduce / Develop / Master" key)
- welcome_to_middle_school (15 pages, 2021) was downloaded but not used.

**Inventory searched.** All 178 URLs in https://charlotteprep.org/cache/sitemap.xml.gz were mirrored (171 distinct pages after de-duplication) and grepped for every linked PDF and Issuu document. The PDFs linked anywhere on the current site are admissions forms, tuition-assistance guides, the DEIB statement and initiatives, the board and Parents' Association documents, the philosophy of education, the portrait of a graduate (two versions), the parent partnership pledge, an alumni proposal, a New York Times reprint, and the fall 2024 (Spanish) and fall 2025 admissions packets. None is a curriculum guide. The linked Issuu documents are annual reports, calendars, the high-school placement brochure, a viewbook and an intro reel. Keyword sweep counts across the mirror: "curriculum guide" appears once, on the unpublished template /academics/sample-page as placeholder text; "curriculum overview" 0; "program of studies" 0; "algebra" 2 (IXL story, testing story); "geometry" 1 (testing story); "robotics" 1 (Middle School page).
