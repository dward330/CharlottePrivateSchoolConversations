### Counting rules (decisions, recorded per the plan)

- **`us-courses` = 80** — level-qualified course entries in the 2026-2027 matrix
  (a title offered at CP, Honors and AP counts once per level, matching how the
  other schools' catalogs list courses). Distinct titles ≈ 65.
- **`advanced-courses` = 15 AP** — the 2026-2027 matrix's AP column. The school's
  own earlier editions ran higher (17 explicit APs in 2023-24; 18 in the 2024-2025
  matrix, adding Calculus BC, Human Geography, French); third-party counts
  (15-21) are superseded by the profile. The current-year figure is used.
- **`us-departments` = 9** — the 8 matrix disciplines (English, Mathematics,
  Science, Social Studies, World Language, Fine Arts, Restoration &
  Sustainability, PE) **plus Bible**, which has no matrix row but is a named
  subject area on the academics page and a 3-unit graduation requirement with a
  course every year.

**English** — CP: Language & Literature · American Literature · Composition &

**Mathematics** — CP: Algebra 1 · Algebra 2 · Geometry · Pre-Calculus ·

**Fine Arts** — CP: Choir · Symphonic Band · Theatre · Theatre Tech · Worship

\* = available for **Dual Enrollment with Covenant College** (Honors Calculus,
Honors Statistics, Honors Literature & Philosophy).

### Structure and policy

- **25 Carnegie Units to graduate**: 4 English · 4 Math · 3 Science · 3 Social
  Studies · 3 Bible (a Bible course each year) · 1 Fine Art · 0.5 Health · 1 PE
  (earnable via two school sport seasons) · 5.5 electives.
- Weighted scale: 4.33 A+ base · Honors +0.5 · DE/AP +1.0. No class rank.
- AP gating: none for freshmen; sophomores max one; "two AP courses in the junior
  year and four AP courses senior year is a very demanding course load."
- Online partner: SevenStar Academy (transcript-noted).
- **Edition drift note:** the 2024-2025 matrix additionally carried AP Calculus BC
  ("course offering may be impacted by student enrollment"), AP Human Geography,
  AP French, CP Liberal Arts Math, CP Intro to Engineering (Science), CP Digital
  Storytelling, and ContainIt (R&S) — evidence the catalog flexes year to year
  around enrollment.
