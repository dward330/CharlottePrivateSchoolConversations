                                              Enrichment Courses

       At Hickory Grove Christian School, our Middle School Enrichment Program is designed to help students
     discover new interests, develop practical skills, and grow in ways that support our Expected Student Outcomes
     of being Committed, Connected, and Wise. These courses provide opportunities for students to explore areas
     beyond the core curriculum while encouraging creativity, leadership, service, collaboration, and problem-
     solving.
       We encourage students to thoughtfully select courses that align with both their interests and their
     willingness to learn something new. While students may naturally gravitate toward familiar activities, some of
     the greatest growth occurs when they step outside their comfort zones and embrace new experiences.
       Because these classes are an important part of the school day, students are expected to participate fully in
     every course to which they are assigned. This includes:
       • attending class prepared,
       • engaging in activities,
       • contributing positively to the learning environment, and
       • completing all required assignments.
       Enrichment classes receive grades and should be approached with the same commitment and responsibility
     as core academic courses.
       Our prayer is that every student will use these experiences to discover God-given gifts, develop new skills,
     and grow as individuals who are Committed to God, Connected to others through service and collaboration,
     and Wise in applying biblical truth to everyday life.

                                          6th Grade Enrichment Courses
       All 6th grade students will rotate through each enrichment class during the school year. This rotation allows
     students to experience a variety of courses and discover new interests and skills. The rotation includes Art,
     STEAM/Tech, Life Skills, Faith and Leadership. Students will not need to choose any courses. They will be
     placed in an enrichment class based on the rotation schedule.
       Students enrolled in Band will participate every day for the course of the school year during the enrichment
     period. Because of this, Band students will attend an additional enrichment course each Friday for the quarter.
     Students will rotate through each of the rotations during the school year by quarter.

                                      7th & 8th Grade Enrichment Courses
       Students in 7th and 8th grade will select their top 4 enrichment courses and 2 alternate enrichment courses.
     All 7th and 8th Grade students will need one Life Skills course each year. When choosing courses, students
     may only take two courses from the same category in one year.
       These courses will be part of the student’s schedule for the school year. Students will rotate to a new course
     each quarter (Every nine weeks students will rotate to their new courses)
       Students enrolled in Band will participate in Band for the full year during the enrichment period. Band
     students will also be placed in one course of their choice one day each week for the quarter.

                                                                 6th Grade

     Course Title: Physical Education

     Grade Level(s): 6

     Course Description: Through our course in Physical Education, we will cover various individual and team sports that focus on
     the five aspects of physical fitness (muscular strength, muscular endurance, cardiorespiratory endurance, body composition,
     and flexibility) as well as the skill related components of physical fitness (coordination, speed, agility, reaction time, balance, and
     power). Some of the sports we will cover include football, soccer, baseball, volleyball, basketball, and jump rope. Students will
     be tested on their knowledge of the rules of each sport and/or their skill level of each sport.

     Course Title: Bible Sketch

     Grade Level(s): 6

     Course Description: This course invites students into the grand story of Scripture through the combined practices of Bible
     study and visual art. Students will explore the biblical themes of Creation, Fall, Redemption, and Restoration while engaging
     creatively with God’s Word through sketching and watercolor techniques. Each lesson pairs a key Bible passage with a specific
     artistic skill, encouraging students to respond to Scripture not only intellectually, but also creatively through hands-on artistic
     expression. Students will develop foundational drawing and watercolor skills as they illustrate scenes, symbols, and themes from
     the Bible’s redemptive story.

     Course Title: Innovation Lab

     Grade Level(s): 6

     Course Description: STEM Exploration is a hands-on course designed to spark curiosity and creativity while introducing
     students to the exciting world of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM). Through engaging experiments,
     design challenges, problem-solving activities, and collaborative projects, students will explore scientific concepts and
     learn how innovation impacts the world around them. This course encourages critical thinking, teamwork, and creativity as
     students investigate real-world problems and develop solutions through inquiry and discovery. This course emphasizes active
     participation, collaboration, and hands-on learning experiences.

     Course Title: Navigating Middle School with Character and Faith

     Grade Level(s): 6

     Course Description: (This course will be NON Graded) This course will be paired with Computer. The transition from
     elementary to middle school is one of the most significant passages in a young person’s life. New classrooms, new expectations,
     new friendships, and new pressures arrive all at once — and most students arrive without the tools to navigate them well. This
     course exists to change that. Navigating Middle School with Character & Faith is designed specifically for 6th grade students
     entering middle school for the first time. Over nine units, students explore the real challenges of adolescent life — conflict, peer
     pressure, identity, honesty, failure, and friendship — through the lens of biblical truth and the wisdom of God’s Word.
