## Academic policy (S1)

- **GPA:** 4.0 scale, calculated on GDS coursework only. Outside coursework appears on the
  transcript but is not calculated into the GPA.
- **Weighting:** Honors **+0.50**; Advanced Placement **+1.0**.
- **GPA requirement:** grades 10-12 must maintain a cumulative **2.0** using core academic
  courses only.
- **AP limits:** **one** AP in grade 10; **three** in each of grades 11 and 12 — stated as
  the most rigorous course of study available.
- **Dual enrollment:** available from fall of grade 11; students may replace **up to two
  elective credits** with DE credits in each of grades 11 and 12. DE coursework is **not**
  calculated into the GPA.
- **Online coursework** from outside providers is not listed on transcripts until the course
  is complete and a final grade received.
- **World language exception:** "International students for whom English is a second
  language are not required to take an additional world language."
- **Class rank:** the school **does not rank** students.

**S7 (Private School Review) says 9** and omits **Chemistry** from its list while including

taught by Gaston Day faculty; the DE policy above allows up to two elective credits per year
in grades 11-12 more broadly.

## STEM and facilities (S2)

STEM options include **Engineering, Research Methods, Calculus and Computer Science**,
taught in "state-of-the-art science facilities and a newly renovated **makerspace** with
access to **3-D printers, laser cutters, and CNC machines**."

## Signature curricular structures (S2)

- **Advisory:** every Upper School student is assigned a faculty advisor matched to
  "individual interests and needs," who stays with them through graduation.
- **Soft-skills curriculum:** **22 skills** integrated across courses through collaborative
  assignments and project-based learning.
- **Leadership workshop:** one day, grades 9-10.
- **Testing:** **PSAT required in grades 9-11**; **ACT required of all grade 11 students**.

- Occupational Therapy

## Early/Lower School, PS-4 (S4)

Language arts and mathematics form the curricular foundation, with "specific blocks of
uninterrupted time devoted for instruction and mastery in these areas." Fine arts are
integrated — "lessons in music, art, and drama are coordinated with units of study in the
classroom." Experiential learning via "frequent field trips, special guest speakers, and
virtual tours." A deliberate **character education** programme. "Discovery and challenge are
the hallmarks of the pre-school and lower school programs."

## International Program (S6)

Folded into curriculum rather than treated as a separate topic, per the plan.

- **Director of International Programs: Carolyn Senter** (ext. 225) — also Head of Upper
  School.
- **Admissions** run through **ABC Education Group**, the school's **exclusive partner
  agency**, with a streamlined process and an online inquiry form.
- **Homestay:** ABC Education Group manages placements with background checks, in-person
  home visits, host-family training before arrival, "precise host family matching to
  maximize personality fit," a landing orientation within two weeks of arrival, and monthly
  check-ins.
- **GDS Global Classroom Initiative:** Gaston Day partners with international schools to
  offer collaborative courses in **English, mathematics, science and social studies for
  grades 10-12**, with students interacting with peers at partner schools. A partnership
  with a Chinese school is confirmed (the school also maintains a `/shangde/` page).
- **Cohort size:** a third-party listing reports **~14% international students** against the
  510 enrolment. **Not confirmed by a school publication** — carried with that caveat.
- The profile's **world-language exception** for ESL international students (S1)
  independently corroborates a real international cohort.

**Not published:** countries represented, a named ESL/English-language support course
sequence, international-specific fees, or the number of partner schools.
