## Financial aid process (S2)

- Administered through **FACTS** (Grant & Aid; third-party). Financial Aid Committee determines
  awards on verified need. Payment managed through FACTS with automatic monthly draft options.
- **Financial Aid Application Deadline: February 5, 2027** (for 2027-2028 year).
- **NC Opportunity Scholarship:** income caps removed, eligibility expanded to currently-enrolled
  private-school students; new CCS applicants wait until February when the application opens.
  Priority window referenced as roughly February 1 – March 1 (confirm with NCSEAA).
- Aid stats (% on aid, total awarded, average award): **NOT PUBLISHED by the school.**

## Form 990

- **Carmel Christian School itself files NO Form 990** — it is a ministry of Carmel Baptist Church
  and is church-exempt. Confirmed via ProPublica search (S7): "Nonprofits (0)" for the school name.
- **RELATED entity — Carmel Educational Foundation, EIN 56-2107877** (Matthews, NC; 501(c)(3),
  tax-exempt since Feb 2000; "Single Organization Support" / educational). ProPublica summary (S6):

  | Fiscal Year (end Jul 31) | Total Revenue | Total Expenses | Net Assets |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | July 2025 | $1,068,745 | $618,023 | $1,894,088 |
  | July 2024 | $1,049,257 | $532,350 | $1,530,281 |
  | July 2023 | $806,345 | $329,090 | $1,013,374 |

  Most recent (FY ending July 31, 2025): Contributions ≈ $1,000,001 (~93.6% of revenue).
  No separate grants/scholarships line itemized in the ProPublica summary table.

- The Foundation-to-school connection is **probable but not explicitly stated** in the fetched
  sources, so the 990 does **NOT** yield a school-level aid figure.
