# Covenant Day School — Financial Aid and Tuition — Tuition History

> **Provenance:** Compiled by Claude Code on 2026-08-15. Two tuition years are
> live simultaneously — the website table shows 2026-27 while the linked payment
> terms PDF shows 2025-26 — so both are recorded with their sources, and the app
> pins its figure to one year explicitly.

### Tuition by band and year

| Grade band | 2025-26 (terms PDF) | 2026-27 (website) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| JK and K | $14,290 | $15,490 | +$1,200 (+8.4%) |
| Grades 1-5 | $16,590 | $17,790 | +$1,200 (+7.2%) |
| Grades 6-8 | $19,290 | $20,490 | +$1,200 (+6.2%) |
| Grades 9-12 | $21,590 | $22,790 | +$1,200 (+5.6%) |

A flat-dollar (not percentage) increase across bands.

### The year-pinning decision

**The app uses 2026-27 ($22,790, grades 9-12)** for `top-tuition` and every
tuition figure in prose, matching the 2026-27 basis used for the six existing
schools. The 2025-26 figures above exist so a reader reconciling against the
payment-terms PDF understands the two live years; no app figure mixes years.
