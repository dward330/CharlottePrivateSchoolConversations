Commonly Asked Questions about Financial Aid".

### Scholarships

Source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1utWb3PNI0U_PR6NowrtZOzifRjMQNry0/edit

> Latin has a long history of endowed scholarships for students who qualify. Several of our
> named scholarships including the Malone, the Sunny, the Thrive, the Wall, and the Kearney
> cover additional school expenses beyond tuition. These Middle and Upper School scholarships
> are awarded at the time of acceptance; no additional application beyond the financial aid
> application is required. All accepted Middle and Upper School students with demonstrated
> financial need are eligible for a named scholarship.

### Commonly Asked Questions about Financial Aid

Source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1t1sFQuFUKNG3-39FrDofWJDkbe_MOtRs/edit

> **What can you expect after you apply for financial aid?**
> A need-blind decision · Financial aid based on need · Regular communication about deadlines ·
> Scheduled optional webinars throughout the season · Direct support from Clarity, the software
> system used to determine financial aid eligibility · An aid award designated on your
> enrollment contract if offered admission
>
> **What is a need-blind decision?** The Office of Admission and the Office of Financial Aid run
> a parallel but separate process to ensure the confidentiality of the applicant. Once students
> are recommended for admission by the Office of Admissions, a list of accepted students is
> communicated to the Office of Financial Aid. At that time, any accepted student eligible for
> need will be notified the award amount through the student contract. The contract is
> published at the time of acceptance.
>
> **How is financial aid determined?** Financial awards are based on the eligible need as
> determined through a consistent and equitable process. The Clarity application enables all
> applicants to submit relevant materials for review by the Financial Aid committee.
>
> **Are any expenses beyond tuition covered?** For students with the highest level of financial
> need, or students that have earned a named endowed scholarship at admission, additional costs
> related to school may/will be covered.
>
> **How will I know if I receive support for additional school-related expenses?** At
> acceptance, you will be notified by our stewardship and scholarship administrator if you are
> eligible for additional financial aid, as well as what expenses are covered.
