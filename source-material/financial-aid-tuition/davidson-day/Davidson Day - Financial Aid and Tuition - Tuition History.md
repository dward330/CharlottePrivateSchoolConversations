# Davidson Day — Financial Aid and Tuition — Tuition History

## Provenance

- **Retrieved by:** Claude (Claude Code agent), on behalf of Derrick.
- **Retrieval date:** 2026-07-23
- **Method:** Internet Archive Wayback Machine. Davidson Day overwrites its tuition page
  each year, and additionally **moved the page** between years (see "URL change" below).
  Snapshots were fetched with the `id_` (raw, un-rewritten) Wayback modifier and the
  tuition table was read directly out of the archived HTML.
- **Live page (current):** https://www.davidsonday.org/admission/tuition-and-affordability
- **Prior URL (through 2025–26):** https://www.davidsonday.org/admission/tuition-and-financial-aid
- **CDX queries used:**
  - `https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=davidsonday.org/admission/tuition-and-financial-aid&output=json&from=2021&to=2026&collapse=digest`
  - `https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=davidsonday.org/admission/tuition-and-affordability&output=json&from=2021&to=2026&collapse=digest`

**Important:** every dollar figure below was read verbatim from a retrieved archived
document. None are derived, interpolated, or back-computed.

### URL change

Through the 2025–26 year the tuition table lived at `/admission/tuition-and-financial-aid`.
For 2026–27 the school moved it to `/admission/tuition-and-affordability`. Both paths were
enumerated and fetched. This is why a single-URL Wayback query does not show the full
history.

### Band-structure change (read this before charting)

Davidson Day **restructured its early-childhood pricing** over this period, so the
early-childhood row is *not* a like-for-like series across all years:

- **2022–23 and 2023–24** — early childhood was split into several priced options:
  "Toddler (3 half days)", "Toddler (3 full days)", "Toddler (5 half days)",
  "Toddler (5 full days)", "Eaglets", and "Transitional Kindergarten". The last three of
  these shared a single full-time price.
- **2024–25** — labels changed to "Early Preschool-5 half days", "Early Preschool-5 full
  days", "Preschool (3s and 4s)", "Junior Kindergarten (4s and 5s)". The last three
  shared a single full-time price.
- **2025–26** — the half-day option disappeared from the table; "Early Preschool (2s and
  3s)", "Preschool (3s and 4s)", and "Junior Kindergarten (4s and 5s)" all shared one
  price.
- **2026–27** — collapsed into a single row: "Early Childhood (Two year olds - Junior
  Kindergarten)".

For a year-over-year chart, the **full-time early-childhood / Junior Kindergarten price**
is the correct comparable to the 2026–27 "Early Childhood" figure. That is the series
given in the main table below. The half-day and reduced-day options are preserved
separately underneath so nothing is lost.

## Financial aid detail captured in the same snapshots

- **Maximum award floor** — verbatim from `20240412152106`: "No financial aid award covers
  the full cost of tuition at Davidson Day School. Families receiving the 'maximum' award
  at Davidson Day are required to pay $3,000 per year, per student for tuition." The same
  $3,000 floor still appears in the 2025–26 snapshot `20250806061814`.
- **Application deadline** — February 15 each year; reapplication required annually.
- **Acclaim Scholars Program** — introduced for the class of 2030 to mark the school's 25th
  anniversary; covers 90% of tuition through graduation contingent on good standing.
  Source: 2026–27 snapshot `20260415013011`.
