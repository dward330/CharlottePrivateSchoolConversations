# Charlotte Preparatory School — Financial Aid and Tuition — Tuition and Fees

Everything below is the school's "Tuition and fees for 2026-2027" page (https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/tuition-fees), copied line for line on October 19, 2026. Prices are for the 2026–27 school year.

**Middle School**

| Grade | Tuition |
|---|---|

Charlotte Prep prices by band, not by grade. One price covers Grades 1–4 and one covers Grades 5–8. Montessori Kindergarten and traditional Kindergarten cost the same, $25,800.

### Longer days for the Early School (add-ons)

| Add-on | Hours | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Primary/Primary School Day* | 12:00 p.m. - 2:30/3:00 p.m. | $3,400 |
| Pre-Primary/Primary Extended Day | 12:00 p.m. - 5:30 p.m. | $5,900 |
| Transitional Kindergarten Extended Day | 2:30 p.m. - 5:30 p.m. | $2,700 |

*The school's footnote: "Pre-Primary/Primary School Day option is only available for students with siblings enrolled at Prep in TK-8th grade."

So a 2- or 3-year-old with no older sibling at Prep can attend either half days or the full Extended Day, which runs to 5:30. There is no middle option.

The school says drop-in "is available for $20/hour for Montessori Kindergarten through 8th Grade students." The Lower and Middle School sections of the page also list the program as "After School Program (Pickup by 5:30 p.m.) - $4,500".

### Fees and other costs, word for word

| Item | Amount | School's wording |
|---|---|---|
| Annual Down Payment | $2,000 | "Upon acceptance to Charlotte Preparatory School, a non-refundable down payment of $2,000, accompanied by a signed contract, secures an academic placement for an eligible student. This deposit is then credited to the overall tuition." |
| Parents' Association Fund | $150 per student, every year | "A fee of $150 per student is charged annually to fund the activities of our Parents' Association" |
| One-time New Family Commitment Fee | $1,000, once | "We require new families to submit this one-time payment of $1,000 upon signing their first tuition contract with Charlotte Preparatory School." |
| Tuition Refund Plan (TRP) | "approximately 1.45% of tuition cost" | "required unless the full annual fees are paid by May 1, in which case the plan is elective." |
| Hot lunch | Not published | Ordered and paid directly through FLIK Independent School Dining |
| Field trips | Not published | "Field trip fees may be assessed if needed." |

The school gives the TRP only as "approximately 1.45%" of tuition, with no dollar amount. At that rate, the TRP on $28,800 tuition would be about $418. That is our own estimate, not a school figure.

### Discounts and free care

- "We offer a 3% discount for younger siblings of current students."
- "We provide complimentary care for younger students who have siblings with a later dismissal time."
- Early drop-off: "You may drop off Early School students as early as 8:00 a.m., and lower and middle school students as early as 7:45 a.m. No additional before-school care is available at this time."

### What tuition now includes

The school's strategic plan says three former add-on fees are now part of tuition: "We have included the fees for athletics, fine arts, and technology in tuition for the 2024-25 school year and will continue to do so moving forward. Other fees, which are mainly for optional activities, will still be billed separately." (https://charlotteprep.org/about/strategic-plan-2022-2027)

The same plan says the board compares tuition with nearby peer schools every year "to keep tuition at or below the median of peer schools while still investing in impactful programs and school needs."

### How to pay

The 2026–27 page does not say how payments are processed. Pages archived through 2021–22 named FACTS Management as the processor for full or monthly payment. The 2021–22 page added: "FACTS account fee is $20 for full payment and incidentals processing, or $50 for monthly payment and incidentals processing." The current page carries neither line.

**TRP pamphlet (A.W.G. Dewar, Inc.; "Academic year: 2026-2027"; Form G14220; "©2025 Dewar-15 (7.2025)").** It is the insurer's generic leaflet, not a Charlotte Prep document. What it covers:
- Medical withdrawal, or absence of "31 or more consecutive calendar days": the plan pays "75% of the unused yearly insured fees". This includes mental health conditions under DSM V.
- Withdrawal for other than medical reasons, after more than seven consecutive calendar days of attendance: 60%.
- Dismissal: 75%.
- Death of a parent or guardian (enrollment continuation): 75%.
- Claims must be reported within 30 days of separation.
- The leaflet says preschool coverage ends if the school moves to remote instruction.

The leaflet does not state the premium. It says, "The cost of the Tuition Refund Plan is detailed in your enrollment materials." The approximately 1.45% figure is from the school's page.
