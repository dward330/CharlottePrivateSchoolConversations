# Charlotte Preparatory School — Financial Aid and Tuition — Financial Support

Charlotte Prep calls its financial aid "tuition assistance". It is offered for Kindergarten through 8th grade and is awarded on need, merit and fit. The school states two different application deadlines on the same page. This file sets out what the school publishes, and what it does not, as of October 19, 2026. The source page is https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/tuition-fees.

### Who can get it, and on what basis

- **Grades:** "Tuition assistance is offered in grades K-8th". The school does not offer it for its Montessori programs below Kindergarten (Pre-Primary, Primary and Transitional Kindergarten).
- **Basis:** "Tuition assistance is awarded on the basis of financial need, academic merit, and the student's fit with our Charlotte Prep missions and vision."
- **How need is measured:** "Tuition assistance is determined through analysis of a family's financial resources, including income and assets. To determine fair and equitable assistance awards, Charlotte Preparatory School subscribes to the principles and needs analysis system of the Clarity application."
- **Every year:** "Each family applying for financial assistance must complete all application procedures annually by the renewal deadlines and continue to demonstrate comparable financial eligibility to receive assistance."
- **Confidential:** "Yes, all applications for tuition assistance are held in the strictest confidence."
- **Families abroad can apply:** through Clarity, with the Non-US Tax Filer Verification upload. Canadian families upload their T1, T4 and Notice of Assessment.

### How to apply

The school lists these steps:
1. "Make a formal request in writing or via email to the attention of the Director of Enrollment." That is Seher Khanna, skhanna@charlotteprep.org.
2. Complete the Clarity application at https://auth.clarityapp.com/en/signup/.
3. Use Clarity's guides, which the school links: a Quick Reference Guide, a Family Application Guide, and Spanish versions of each.

**Clarity's own fee.** Clarity's quick-reference guide says "The app fee is $60 per household and covers all the Clarity schools to which you are applying." This is Clarity's figure, from its 2025/2026 guide. Charlotte Prep's page does not state it.

### The deadline: the page gives two different dates

Both statements are on the same live page:

| Where on the page | What it says |
|---|---|
| Main text, "Tuition Assistance" | "We recommend you apply by January 2; applications received after the deadline will still be considered as long as funds are available." The steps repeat it: "Apply by January 2 prior to the enrollment year." |
| FAQ, "When do I need to apply?" | "You must submit your full completed application by January 15." |

Both come from https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/tuition-fees, retrieved October 19, 2026.

The likely reason: in November 2024 the main text and the FAQ both said January 15. The main text has since moved to January 2, which is the same date as the Early School and Kindergarten application deadline. The FAQ was not updated. Until the school resolves it, applying by January 2 meets both statements. Either way, the school says late applications are still considered "as long as funds are available."

### Other out-of-date details on the aid section

- The FAQ still says Clarity "removes the need to upload your 2022 W2 and 1040 by transferring them directly from the IRS". The 2022 tax year is several years old.
- The guides the school links are labelled for 2025/2026. The Spanish Family Application Guide is the 2023–24 edition. None is the 2027–28 edition.

### How much aid, and how many families

**The school's own figure.** In its 2024–2025 Prep Fund results, Charlotte Prep says: "10% of Charlotte Prep families received tuition assistance."
- Sources: the "See The Impact of the 2024-2025 Prep Fund" section of https://charlotteprep.org/support/the-prep-fund, and the 2024–2025 Annual Report on Issuu (https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/2024-2025_annual_report).
- This counts families, not students. The number of families is not published.

**The IRS figure.** The school's federal tax returns report each year's aid as "Financial Aid", described as a "Reduction in tuition base" (Form 990, Schedule I). These are tax-return figures, not numbers the school publishes on its website.

| Fiscal year (July–June) | Total aid | Students aided |
|---|---|---|
| 2024–25 | $476,679 | 49 |
| 2023–24 | $457,393 | 50 |
| 2022–23 | $368,575 | 47 |
| 2021–22 | $363,160 | 48 |

The full eight-year series is in the Form 990 file. The return explains: "These funds are applied to the tuition balance and are not available for other uses."

The school does not publish an average award, the largest award, or a total aid budget.

### Merit scholarships

- **Charlotte Prep's current page:** aid is awarded partly on "academic merit", but no stand-alone merit scholarship is offered.
- **The Edward Ritter Scholarship, a past program.** The school's archived tuition pages from 2023 described it. Its guidelines document is still online at https://charlotteprep.org/uploads/files/Admissions/Ritter_Scholarship_Award_External_Guidelines.pdf.pdf, dated June 2022. It says: "Annually, one student new to Charlotte Preparatory School will be awarded free tuition and up to $1,000 toward school related expenses or fees (i.e., computer fees, books, class trips)." The award was renewable through 8th grade and required the family to qualify for aid.
- The scholarship is no longer mentioned on the tuition page; it was gone by November 2024. Its guidelines still refer to the school's previous aid system (SSS). Treat it as discontinued unless the school says otherwise.

### NC Opportunity Scholarship (state voucher)

Charlotte Prep's website does not mention the NC Opportunity Scholarship anywhere, and the school appears in none of the state's recent recipient lists.

The state publishes an annual list of every private school with Opportunity Scholarship recipients. Charlotte Preparatory School is not on any of the last four:

| School year | State report | Charlotte Prep listed? |
|---|---|---|
| 2024–25 | https://webservices.ncleg.gov/ViewDocSiteFile/102407 | No |
| 2023–24 | https://webservices.ncleg.gov/ViewDocSiteFile/91108 | No |
| 2022–23 | https://webservices.ncleg.gov/ViewDocSiteFile/81618 | No |
| 2021–22 | https://webservices.ncleg.gov/ViewDocSiteFile/72170 | No |

For comparison, the 2024–25 report lists Trinity Episcopal School (210 recipients) and Charlotte Christian School (608).

Being absent means no family used a voucher at Charlotte Prep in those years. It does not prove the school would refuse one. A family relying on the voucher should ask the school directly.

### Other ways families save

- **Sibling discount:** "We offer a 3% discount for younger siblings of current students."
- **Free sibling care:** "We provide complimentary care for younger students who have siblings with a later dismissal time."
- **Staff families:** "All full-time employees are eligible for a 50% tuition remission for their children (up to two)." (https://charlotteprep.org/about/employment)
- **Tuition Refund Plan:** required unless tuition is paid in full by May 1. See the Tuition and Fees file.

**How the deadline conflict arose.** The archived page of November 24, 2024 (capture 20241124043626) read "We recommend you apply by January 15" and "Apply by January 15 prior to the enrollment year", and its FAQ said "by January 15". Today the main text says January 2 and the FAQ still says January 15. The body records both, never one alone.

**Aid-platform history.** Through the 2023–24 page, the school used SSS ("Apply to SSS by February 1 prior to the enrollment year. Our school link number is #4919.") and awarded through "The Financial Aid Committee". By the 2024–25 page it used Clarity. The 2023–24 wording described aid as cultural and diversity-driven and did not mention merit. "Need, academic merit, and fit" first appears on the 2024–25 page.

**Annual report financial summary.** The 2024–2025 Annual Report also prints an unaudited pie chart, captioned "Unaudited financial information":
- Revenue: "90% Tuition & Fees", "7% Auxiliary Programs", "3% Gross Fundraising".

- It reports $357,744 in Prep Fund dollars raised in 2024–25.

The 2023–2024 annual report (https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/2024-2025_calendar_and_annual_report) shows:

- No aid share.
