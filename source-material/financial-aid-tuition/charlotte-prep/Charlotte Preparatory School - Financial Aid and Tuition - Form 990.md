# Charlotte Preparatory School — Financial Aid and Tuition — Form 990

As a nonprofit, Charlotte Prep files an annual federal tax return (IRS Form 990) under the name "Charlotte Preparatory School Inc", EIN 45-2588411, 212 Boyce Rd, Charlotte. The returns show how much tuition the school collects, how much aid it gives, and what it pays its leaders. **Every figure on this page comes from those tax returns, not from the school's website.** The school's fiscal year runs July 1 to June 30. The most recent return covers the year ending June 30, 2025, and the IRS released it in 2026.

### Financial aid, year by year

Schedule I of the 990 reports aid to students as "Financial Aid". The value is described as a "Reduction in tuition base", and the return adds: "These funds are applied to the tuition balance and are not available for other uses."

| Fiscal year ending June | Aid total | Students aided | Average per student (our calculation) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | $476,679 | 49 | $9,728 |
| 2024 | $457,393 | 50 | $9,148 |
| 2023 | $368,575 | 47 | $7,842 |
| 2022 | $363,160 | 48 | $7,566 |
| 2021 | $516,135 | 58 | $8,899 |
| 2020 | $502,105 | 68 | $7,384 |
| 2019 | $448,646 | 61 | $7,355 |
| 2018 | $374,330 | 51 | $7,340 |

The average is the aid total divided by the number of students. The school does not publish it.

The number of students aided peaked at 68 in the year ending June 2020 and has held at 47–50 since 2022. Meanwhile the average award has grown, to $9,728 in the latest year. That is about a third of the 2026–27 Grades 1–4 tuition of $28,800.

Measured against tuition revenue, aid has run between 4% and 7% over these years (our calculation). The school's own statistic is that 10% of families received tuition assistance in 2024–25; see the Financial Support file.

"Tuition and fees" is the return's only program-service revenue line. It is labelled "Tuition and Fees" and carries the IRS business code 611600.

Revenue jumped in the two most recent years for reasons beyond tuition. The 2024 return reports $4,961,877 of "Other" revenue that it does not explain further. The 2025 return reports $1,894,014 of "Insurance Proceeds" and $979,886 of "Other". Contributions were $1,187,398 in 2024 and $1,669,188 in 2025. For context, the school's 2024–2025 annual report says that "In the summer of 2023, … the lower school building was destroyed by a fire". It also says the lower school classrooms "will begin the 2025 school year in a beautiful new building". The tax returns do not connect the insurance proceeds or the "Other" revenue to the fire. The returns also show the school financed construction with tax-exempt bonds through the North Carolina Capital Facilities Finance Agency. This was the period when the new Lower School building went up. Year-end liabilities were $6,157,451 in 2024 and $14,344,547 in 2025.

How to read this:
- The returns title Chris Marblo "Ex-Officio", meaning his board seat. The school's own 2023–2024 annual report signs him as "Head of School".
- In the year ending June 2021, Eddie Mensah and Chris Marblo each received partial-year pay, consistent with a mid-year change of head. The two figures are not a pay cut.
- The year ending June 2018 also shows a change. N Blair Fisher is listed as Head of School, and Eddie Mensah is listed as Head of Middle at $120,000.
- The school's current head is **Todd Ballaban**, per the staff directory, the 2024–2025 annual report and his September 2025 letters to families. No return filed so far covers a year in which he was head.
- The school's 2024–2025 annual report says Chris Marblo "has served as Head of School at Charlotte Prep for the past five years" and "arrived in 2020". It wishes him "endless joy and adventure in retirement".
- The 2025 figure ($346,133 plus $15,431) covers his final year and is much higher than the year before. The return does not explain the increase, and this page does not guess at it.

The returns say head-of-school pay is set using "Data from comparable sized and types of schools (NAIS Benchmarks)".

### Staff size, as reported

The returns report total employees of 159 (2025), 146 (2024), 146 (2023), 137 (2022), 126 (2021), 128 (2020), 134 (2019) and 134 (2018). This counts everyone paid during the year, including part-time after-school and summer staff. It is not a teacher count.

## Method

Retrieved 2026-10-19.

**Filing list.** The ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API (https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/api/v2/organizations/452588411.json) supplied the summary figures for fiscal years ending June 2012–2017, and the list of filings. ProPublica identifies the organization as "Charlotte Preparatory School Inc", 212 BOYCE RD, ruling date 2012-03-01, NTEE B24.

**Full returns.** Each full return was read from the IRS's own e-file XML (TEOS). Each year's index_<year>.csv at https://apps.irs.gov/pub/epostcard/990/xml/<year>/ gives the object id. That single member was then pulled from the IRS zip with HTTP range requests, reading the zip's central directory, without downloading whole archives of 300–520 MB. The newer zips use Deflate64, which Python's zlib cannot read, so each member was re-wrapped and opened with the system unzip.

| June 30, 2023 | 202421369349310162 | 2024_TEOS_XML_05A.zip | 2024-05-15 |

**Fields read from the XML:**
- CYTotalRevenueAmt, CYProgramServiceRevenueAmt (ProgramServiceRevenueGrp "Tuition and Fees"), CYTotalExpensesAmt, NetAssetsOrFundBalancesEOYAmt, TotalLiabilitiesEOYAmt, TotalEmployeeCnt
- Schedule I GrantsOtherAsstToIndivInUSGrp (GrantTypeTxt "Financial Aid", RecipientCnt, CashGrantAmt, ValuationMethodUsedDesc "Reduction in tuition base"; the valuation text is present from FY2021 on)
- Part VII Section A (PersonNm, TitleTxt, ReportableCompFromOrgAmt, OtherCompensationAmt)
- OtherRevenueMiscGrp
- The Schedule O supplemental text, including "Part I, Issuer name: North Carolina Capital Facilities Finance Agency"

- Average aid per student: Schedule I aid ÷ recipients, rounded to the dollar.
- Aid as a share of tuition revenue: Schedule I aid ÷ program-service revenue. Each year: 2018 5%, 2019 6%, 2020 7%, 2021 7% (7.46%), 2022 4%, 2023 4%, 2024 4%, 2025 4%.
