### How fast tuition has risen (our calculation)

| Change | Kindergarten | Grades 1–4 | Grades 5–8 |

| 2016–17 → 2017–18 | 0% | +2% | +3% |

| 2018–19 → 2021–22 (three years) | +10% | +10% | +10% |
| 2021–22 → 2023–24 (two years) | +12% | +12% | +12% |
| 2023–24 → 2024–25 | +5% | +5% | +5% |
| 2024–25 → 2026–27 (two years) | +15% | +15% | +15% |
| 2015–16 → 2026–27 (eleven years) | +53% | +62% | +65% |

These percentages are our own arithmetic on the school's published prices, not figures the school publishes.

Before 2019, Charlotte Prep raised tuition about 2–4% a year. From 2021–22 on the pace picked up to roughly 5–7% a year. The two years from 2024–25 to 2026–27 added 15%, about 7% a year.

Two things changed alongside the prices. From 2024–25 the school folded its athletics, fine arts and technology fees into tuition, so part of that year's rise replaced fees families used to pay separately. Before-school care from 7:30 a.m. was free through 2021–22; by 2023–24 the page said "No additional before school care is available at this time."

## Method

Retrieved 2026-10-19. Every price above comes from the school's own tuition page (https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/tuition-fees, earlier https://www.charlotteprep.org/admissions/tuition.cfm). Earlier years were read from Internet Archive captures fetched raw with the id_ modifier, with text extracted locally. Gzipped captures were decompressed first. Every figure is copied character for character.

**Captures used, one per price set:**
- 20150804004312 (/admissions/tuition.cfm). The same prices appear in every capture through 20160114125740. No year label is printed. The school year is inferred from the date the page was in force (August 2015 to January 2016).
- 20160215042100 (/admissions/tuition.cfm). The same prices appear through 20170112194701. No year label. The new prices were posted in February 2016, so they are assigned to 2016–17.
- 20170510020954 and 20170713083413 (/admissions/tuition.cfm), labelled "2017-2018".
- 20180204144733 (/admissions/tuition.cfm), labelled "2018-2019". The same prices appear through 20180811044130.
- 20211206170346 (/admissions/tuition-fees), labelled "2021-2022 School Year". Identical at 20211226100953.
- 20230130003405 (/admissions/tuition-fees), labelled "Tuition and fees for 2023-2024". Identical at 20230308163414.
- 20241124043626 (/admissions/tuition-fees), labelled "Tuition and fees for 2024-2025".
- Live page, 2026-10-19, labelled "Tuition and fees for 2026-2027".

**The gaps, and why they are real:**
- **2019–20 and 2020–21.** The CDX listing for the whole charlotteprep.org domain shows no tuition URL captured between 20180811044130 and 20211206170346. The 2019–2021 site's /admissions/ and /admissions/apply captures (20200808084032, 20201025105027, 20210125071433) carry no prices.
- **2022–23.** The only capture in the window, 20220327073222, is an empty 0-byte response (CDX length 719, application/octet-stream), and its 20220608095813 revisit points to the same empty body.
- **2025–26.** Every tuition-fees capture from 20250910232509 through 20260824104926 (14 captures) is an archived HTTP 520 error from the school's server. These are stored failures, not rate limiting, so retrying will not help.

The fall-2025 admissions packet (https://charlotteprep.org/uploads/files/Admissions/fall-2025-admissions-packet-web.pdf) carries no tuition.

**Inconsistencies within the school's own pages, recorded rather than resolved:**
- The 2021–22 page prints the K–8 After School Care price twice. It is "$3,600" under the Montessori Kindergarten and Lower School blocks and "$3,500" under Other Fees and Services. The table carries $3,500 and notes the $3,600.
- The 2023–24 and 2024–25 pages listed Montessori Kindergarten after-school as "(3:00 p.m.-5:45 p.m.)" at the same price as K–8 to 5:30.

**Other details from the archived pages:**
- Application fee: $90 through 2021–22, then $100 from 2023–24.
- Tuition down payment: unstated in 2015–16, $1,700 from 2017–18 through 2021–22, and $2,000 by 2024–25.
- TRP: "approximately 2.1 percent" through 2018–19, "approximately 2.2%" in 2021–22, 1.85% in 2023–24, 1.7% in 2024–25 and 1.45% in 2026–27.
- Annual fees: a Room Parent Activity Fee of $100 (2015–2018) and $115 (2021–22), then a Parent Association Fund of $150 from 2023–24. A Technology Fee of $150 applied to grades 4–8 through 2021–22 and to grades 3–8 in 2023–24.
- Early School extended-day prices, for reference:
  - 2015–16: Pre-Primary/Primary School Year Extended Day $14,850 and Year-Round $16,500. TK $16,300 and $17,850.
  - 2023–24: Pre-Primary $19,750 and $22,050; Primary $20,250 and $22,600; TK $21,450 and $23,550.
  - 2024–25: Pre-Primary $20,750 and $23,150; Primary $21,300 and $23,750; TK $22,550 and $24,750.
  - Extended day ran to 6:00 p.m. through 2021–22 and to 5:30 p.m. from 2023–24.
