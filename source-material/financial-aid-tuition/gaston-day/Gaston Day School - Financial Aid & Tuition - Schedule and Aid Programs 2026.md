**Note on the published "total".** Gaston Day publishes tuition and fees as separate columns
plus an explicit combined total. The Upper School sticker figure is therefore **$22,390**
(tuition $20,215 + fees $2,175) — that combined figure is what third-party sites quote as
the school's tuition, and it is the like-for-like number against other schools' all-in
figures.

**2026-27 tuition (S2):** **$20,300 – $23,310** depending on grade. Published as a range on
the cost page; the per-grade 2026-27 schedule PDF was not posted at the time of this pass.

**Rate conflict with the after-school web page — recorded, not silently resolved.** The
`/afterschoolprogram/` page states hours of **3:15 PM – 5:30 PM** and prices of
**$100/month** (Lower School) and **$60/month** (Middle School), with drop-in at $30/day and
$15/day. The tuition schedule (S1) states **3:00–6:00** / **4:00–6:00** at **$95/week** and
**$55/week**, with the same $30/day and $15/day drop-in rates.

The drop-in rates agree exactly across both sources. The weekly-vs-monthly labels do not:
$100/**month** for daily aftercare would be far below $30/day drop-in economics and below the
schedule's own $95/week, so the page's "/month" reads as a label error for a weekly rate.
Both sources are retained above; the app uses the **official tuition schedule (S1)** as the
rate authority and the **web page (S2/after-school file)** for the current operating window,
which is the more likely thing to have been updated in-year. See the after-school file for
the full page transcription.

### Financial aid

- **Platform: Clarity** (not FACTS or SSS). Five steps: sign up at the Clarity app; add
  Gaston Day School to the applicant entry; complete the application and pay the **$65**
  fee; complete tax verification (IRS transfer or Clarity's option); submit and monitor the
  dashboard for document requests.
- **Deadline: rolling** — "The financial aid deadline is rolling, but funds may become
  limited or unavailable towards the beginning of the new school year (August)."
- **Three assistance channels:** need-based Financial Aid, Merit Scholarships, and the
  **North Carolina Opportunity Scholarship**.
- **Discount ceiling: up to 80%** of tuition (Kindergarten–Grade 12).
- **Contact:** Kevin Napp, Director of Admissions, 704.864.7744 ext. 414.

**Not published** on any page found in this pass: the total aid budget in dollars, the
average award, the number of families receiving aid, sibling discounts, tuition-insurance
or refund-plan terms, and the payment-plan tiers (S1 states only that "Gaston Day offers a
variety of payment plans" and that all plans assume the tuition deposit and fees have been
paid). These are recorded as confirmed absences, not as unresearched gaps.

### Aid share

**26% of the student body receives tuition assistance** (2025-26 Academic Profile).

S5 (Private School Review) reports **45%**. The school's own profile is used. The two are
also not measuring the same quantity: the profile's "tuition assistance" is need-based aid,
while a third-party "percent on financial aid" figure commonly folds in merit awards and
state Opportunity Scholarship recipients — which at Gaston Day are three separate channels
(S2). The gap is therefore plausibly definitional rather than either figure being wrong.
