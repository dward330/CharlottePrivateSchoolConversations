# Charlotte Christian School - Read Reviews - Review Site Links

Charlotte Christian School's own pages on three independent review sites. The app links to them from the
Read Reviews section. It copies no rating, grade, review text or reviewer name from any of
them.

- GreatSchools: https://www.greatschools.org/north-carolina/charlotte/2170-Charlotte-Christian-School/reviews/
- Niche: https://www.niche.com/k12/charlotte-christian-school-charlotte-nc/reviews/
- SchoolDigger: https://www.schooldigger.com/go/NC/schools/9999924895/school.aspx

Map links (the decorative map under the boxes):

- Open in Google Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Charlotte%20Christian%20School%2C%207301%20Sardis%20Road%2C%20Charlotte%2C%20NC%2028270
- Open in Apple Maps: https://maps.apple.com/?address=7301%20Sardis%20Road%2C%20Charlotte%2C%20NC%2028270&q=Charlotte%20Christian%20School
