## What this list is

The profile's own heading: **"HIGH SCHOOL CHOICES (TES Classes of 2004-2026)"** — a
**cumulative** list across 23 graduating classes and **1,060 graduates**, not a
single-year destination list.

**99 entries as published.** It is a list of *names only* — the profile publishes **no
counts, no per-school student numbers and no per-year attribution**.

## ⚠️ The asterisk is UNEXPLAINED — do not interpret it

**41 of the 99 entries carry a trailing asterisk.** The full extracted text of the PDF was
searched for a legend — any "indicates", "denotes", or footnote — and **none exists
anywhere in the document**.

It plausibly marks multiple attendees or current attendance, **but that is not stated**.
**Publish no interpretation of it.** Either strip the marker or omit the list; do not
invent a meaning. (The same is true of the **71 asterisked** college entries.)

One further unexplained marker: **"Woodberry Forest School, VA (7)"** carries a
parenthetical `(7)` with no explanation.
