# Charlotte Preparatory School — High School Placement — Where They Land

Charlotte Prep publishes two kinds of destination record. One is a cumulative list of every high school its graduates have attended, sorted into four categories of the school's own. The other is a named list for each graduating class since 2023, giving where each graduate is heading. This file transcribes the first and counts the second by school. Student names are not carried here.

### Notes on the list itself

- **The list has 64 entries, but fewer distinct high schools.** The South Carolina Governor's School for the Arts appears twice, once under Boarding and once under Other cities. "University in Madrid" is a university, not a high school. "Queen's Grant High School Union Academy" is two schools run together on one line, Queen's Grant High School and Union Academy, and Union Academy is also listed separately just below it.
- **Some entries are filed under a category that does not describe them.** Charlotte Lab School is a tuition-free public charter, but the page lists it under independent schools. The school's own brochure lists it under public schools. The University of North Carolina School of the Arts and the South Carolina Governor's School for the Arts and Humanities are public residential arts schools listed under boarding.
- **American Hebrew Academy closed in June 2019.** It remains on the list because the list is cumulative.
- **Washington-Lee High School** in Arlington, Virginia, is now named Washington-Liberty High School.
- **Olympic Community of Schools** is today Olympic High School, part of Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools.

### Independent, public or boarding: our count by class

**The school does not publish a sector split.** The table below is our own count from the class lists, sorting each destination by what the school actually is rather than by any label on the list.

| Class | Charlotte-area independent | Public, magnet or charter | Boarding | Private day school outside Charlotte | Total on list |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | 25 | 12 | 1 | 1 | 39 |

| 2024 | 15 | 10 | 4 | 0 | 29 |
| 2023 | 13 | 6 | 1 | 0 | 20 |
| All four | 73 | 34 | 7 | 1 | 115 |

Classification choices that affect these counts:

- **York Prep Academy** (2026) is counted as public. It is taken to be York Preparatory Academy in Rock Hill, South Carolina, a tuition-free public charter school. The list gives no city.
- **Carolina Day School** (2026) is a private day school in Asheville, so it is counted separately from Charlotte-area independents.
- **Northwestern High School** (2023) is counted as public. It is taken to be the Rock Hill, South Carolina public high school, which also appears on the school's brochure list. The list gives no city.
- **Central Academy of Arts & Sciences IB Program** (2023) is counted as public. It is taken to be the Central Academy of Technology and Arts in Monroe, a Union County public magnet school that has offered the IB Diploma since 2022-23. No school by the exact name on the list was found.
- **St. Andrew's School** (2024) is counted as boarding. Several boarding schools share that name, and the school does not say which one is meant.

## Method

Retrieved 2026-10-19.

**How the lists were captured**

- The page list was extracted from the raw HTML of https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/beyond-charlotte-prep (curl, Python HTML parser) and is transcribed character for character, including "Walnut Hill School For The Arts", "South Carolina Governor's School for Arts" in the boarding list against "South Carolina Governors School for the Arts" in the other list, "Christian-von-Dohm Gymnasium" (no second hyphen), and the run-together "Queen's Grant High School Union Academy". The page uses a curly apostrophe in "St. Andrew's" and "Queen's".
- The brochure list was read from Issuu page images, revision 260924170805, with the Referer: https://issuu.com/ header and macOS Vision OCR checked visually. The 2022 revision, 221206214505, was read too. Diffing the two lists shows the 2026 revision added Episcopal High School under boarding. Its College Destinations column also changed: it added Howard University, McGill University, New York University, Spelman College and University of St. Andrews - Scotland; dropped l'Escola Massana of Barcelona - Spain; and renamed "Georgia Tech" to "Georgia Institute of Technology" and "Washington & Lee University" to "Washington and Lee University".

The parser asserted that every name line matched the first-name-plus-initial pattern, that the name and school counts were equal (39/39, 27/27, 29/29, 20/20), and that no name repeated within a class. Names were then discarded. In 2026 one entry sits out of alphabetical order; this is harmless.

**Corrections to the indicative sweep**

**Confirmed not published:** per-school counts on the cumulative list; any year attribution on it; named class lists before 2023. The site sweep covered all 178 sitemap URLs.
