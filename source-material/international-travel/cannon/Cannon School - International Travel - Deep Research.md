## Summary (PDF opening paragraph)

Cannon School has a real global-education program, but it is small and mostly in the Upper
School. The program is called "International Programs." It includes a senior-year "Global
Studies Certificate," a teacher "Global Education Badge" and a mix of trips: Winterm trips,
summer trips and service builds. The school does not publish trip prices, a current trip
list, a selection process, safety rules or any participation numbers. It is not in Round
Square. The only travel-specific money is a named endowment whose terms are not shown. If
global travel matters to you, ask the school about it directly. The website will not answer
most of your questions.

  https://www.cannonschool.org/news-and-stories/in-the-news-details/~board/faculty-fridays/post/meet-jady-arriaga

  course, Winterm trips and outside events (World Affairs Council of Charlotte, World Quest,
  Model UN). MS and LS global learning comes through the language program, social studies
  units and occasional trips. No JrK–12 structure published.
