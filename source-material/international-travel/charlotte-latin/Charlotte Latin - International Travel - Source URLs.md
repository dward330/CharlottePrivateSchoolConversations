**Provenance.** Assembled 2026-10-13 during `/global-exposure` (mode B) by fetching each
page with `curl` (browser user agent) and reading its text, plus the Wayback Machine CDX
index for pages whose older content is gone. It checks the AI-generated

deep-research file is a lead sheet; **this file is the citation record.** A fact reaches the
app only if a row here verifies it (or `/implement` adds a row after verifying it itself).

**Verification levels**
- **fetched** — page text retrieved 2026-10-13 and the claim read in it.
- **Wayback** — gone from the live page; present in the named snapshot.
- **PDF only** — in the deep-research PDF, not yet checked against a page. **Not sufficient
  to render.** `/implement` fetches it, or drops the claim.
- **404** — the URL no longer resolves.

**The school runs two sites, and both are official.** `charlottelatin.org` (Finalsite)
carries the program overview; the **Global Studies Google Site**
(`sites.google.com/charlottelatin.org/globalstudies`, a school-run site under the school's
own Google domain, staffed and signed by the Director of Community, Belonging and Global
Education) carries every trip page, the application, FAQs and the calendar. The PDF
reported the Google Site as blocked; it loads with plain `curl`.
