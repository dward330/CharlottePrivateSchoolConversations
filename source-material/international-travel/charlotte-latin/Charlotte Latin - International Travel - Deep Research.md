## Headline summary (PDF p. 1)

Charlotte Latin School has a real but lightly documented global program. It has seven
sister schools founded between 1989 and 2008, 13 named travel programs for Middle and Upper
School, and Spanish from TK. The public website does not publish the trip prices, deadlines,
selection rules, or participation counts a parent would need, and it offers no global
diploma or certificate. *(Planning note: the Google Site publishes prices, deadlines and

## TL;DR (PDF p. 1)

- **What exists [Official]:** A "Global Studies" / "Global Education" program with seven
  sister schools in Germany, France, Argentina, Italy, China and South Africa (×2). It lists
  nine Upper School and four Middle School travel programs
  (https://www.charlottelatin.org/about/community-belonging-global-education/global-education).
  The page says "Middle and Upper School students have the chance to explore the world
  through transformative travel experiences." No Lower School student travel is listed.
  Lower School global learning is on campus (Spanish TK–5, Global Partners, Passport
  Program).
- **What's missing:** The public site gives no trip prices, deposits, dates, application
  deadlines, selection method, vendor, or safety/passport guidance. There is no global
  diploma or certificate, and no Round Square membership is published. The detailed trip
  logistics appear to sit on a Global Studies Google Site
  (https://sites.google.com/charlottelatin.org/globalstudies/sister-schools/host-family-faqs).
  That site could not be loaded in this research (tool permission block), so its content is
  unverified, not absent. *(Planning note: it loads; see above.)*
- **Scale:** The only participation figure is secondary. A February 2026 recruiting
  brochure by Carney, Sandoe & Associates says 73 students take part in a domestic or
  international travel program each year
  (https://www.carneysandoe.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Director-of-Community-Belonging-and-Global-Education.pdf, p. 2).
  Against 1,552 enrolled TK–12, that is a small share (PDF's calculation: 73 ÷ 1,552 ≈ 4.7%).
- **The money [Official]:** No trip-specific endowment or named global fund is published.
  The closest is the Plus Fund, financed by the Alumni Endowment (stated value $3.1
  million). Per the school, it pays for costs beyond tuition, explicitly including "travel
  experiences," for families on substantial aid
  (https://www.charlottelatin.org/support/alumni/alumni-giving). Tuition bills include "an
  advance payment of up to $710 for class trips" for grades 5–8 and 12
  (https://www.charlottelatin.org/admissions/tuition-financial-assistance).
- **Bottom line for parents:** Latin is a good fit if you want sister-school exchanges with
  homestays and reciprocal hosting. It is a weaker fit if you want a structured credential
  like Providence Day's Global Studies Diploma or broad, guaranteed participation. Before
  committing, ask for the current trip list with prices and the aid policy in writing.
