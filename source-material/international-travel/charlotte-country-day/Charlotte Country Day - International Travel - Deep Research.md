## Headline summary (PDF p. 1)

Charlotte Country Day School (CCDS) runs a long-established international program through
its Office of International Studies, which the school dates to 1988. International travel
is open from sixth grade. Four Upper School trips are listed for 2027 (Rwanda, Greece,
China, Argentina), and their registration window is open now (October 2–16, 2026). The
school offers no global diploma or certificate. Trip funding comes mainly from a 1977 named
endowment and a trip-specific financial aid form. The public website does not show 2027
prices, Middle School trip details or safety policies.
