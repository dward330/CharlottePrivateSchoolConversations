## TL;DR

- **How international the community is:** "The PD community has 72 different countries of
  origin represented and 38% students of color" [Official]
  https://www.providenceday.org/admissions/why-providence-day. No count of
  international/F-1 students, no list of family languages and no ESL program. The only
  official hint is a 2015 magazine reference to "PDS's international students" and an
  "English language learner curriculum map" [Official]
  https://www.providenceday.org/uploaded/About_PDS/Magazine/PDMag15-SpringFINAL.pdf (printed p. 10).
- **Headline first:** "In 2007, Providence Day School was the first in the nation to
  establish the Global Studies Diploma (GSD) for Upper School students" [Official]
  https://www.providenceday.org/the-pd-difference/global-education. The GSD Google Site
  instead says "2005 had the first graduating class of 5 GSD students" [Official]
  https://sites.google.com/providenceday.org/gsd-homepage/general-information/faq.
  **Conflict unresolved.**
- **Round Square:** Fall 2015 magazine: "PDS recently became an official member school of
  Round Square International" and "was one of only three schools in North America accepted"
  [Official] https://www.providenceday.org/uploaded/About_PDS/Magazine/PDMag15-Fall.pdf
  (printed p. 2). An earlier (search-indexed) Global Education page said "became a Global
  Member of Round Square in 2016". The current page says "For over a decade". **No single
  official year is consistent.**
- **Program origins:** "Since 2004, PDS has been offering signature programs for students,
  faculty and parents to foster global mindedness" [Official] Spring 2015 magazine (printed
  p. 9). The Global Educator Certificate has run "Since 2006" [Official]
  https://www.providenceday.org/the-pd-difference/global-education. Founding year of the
  Global Education office or director role: **NOT PUBLISHED**.
- **Community life:** a Parents' Association "Cultural Connections committee" hosts Diwali,
  Hanukkah, Lunar New Year and Eid al-Fitr events [Official] (2022 EIB report). An earlier
  parent group, FOCUS, "was created in 2006" [Official] Fall 2015 magazine (printed p. 5).
  The Upper School holds a faculty-danced Diwali celebration [Official].
