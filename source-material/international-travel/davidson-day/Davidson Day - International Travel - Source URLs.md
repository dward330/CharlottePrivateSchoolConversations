# Davidson Day School — International Travel — Source URLs

**Provenance.** Assembled 2026-10-18 during `/global-exposure` mode B. Each page was
fetched with `curl` (browser user agent) and its text read; pages gone from the live site
were read from the Wayback Machine, located through the CDX index. It checks the
AI-generated `Davidson Day - International Travel - Deep Research.md` against live pages.
That deep-research file is a lead sheet; **this file is the citation record.** A fact
reaches the app only if a row here verifies it (or `/implement` adds a row after verifying
it itself).

**Verification levels**
- **fetched** — page text retrieved 2026-10-18 and the claim read in it.
- **Wayback** — gone from the live page; present in the named snapshot.
- **PDF only** — in the deep-research PDF, not yet checked against a page. **Not sufficient
  to render.** `/implement` fetches it, or drops the claim.
- **404** — the URL no longer resolves.
- **third-party** — fetched, but from a site other than the school's. Renders only under
  the event/network exception and the user's decision recorded in the plan.
