## Opening summary

Davidson Day's global program is built around two things: a Global Studies Diploma
Distinction for the Upper School, and summer international travel. Upper School travel
runs through AFAR, an archaeology nonprofit founded by the school's Director of Global
Studies, which digs in Spain, Portugal and Belize. Middle School has a summer
language-and-culture trip to Spain and France. The school's own website publishes very
little about eligibility rules, dates, prices, aid or participation. Most hard numbers
come from AFAR's site (goafar.org) and are [Secondary].

## TL;DR

- **What exists [Official]:** "Davidson Day offers summer international trips for our
  Middle and Upper School students" (https://www.davidsonday.org/student-life/international-travel).
  Upper School travel is the AFAR archaeology field program in "Spain, Portugal, and
  Belize". Middle School has "a summer language and cultural immersion trip to Spain and
  France". The credential is the Global Studies Diploma Distinction, which has "existed at
  Davidson Day School since 2021" (https://www.davidsonday.org/academics/diploma-distinctions/global-studies).
- **What is missing:** trip dates, prices, deposits, group sizes, selection, the Middle
  School vendor, safety/passport guidance, exchange partners and graduate counts are NOT
  PUBLISHED on the school site. No Round Square, sister-school or homestay program found.
- **The money [Secondary]:** AFAR lists 2026–27 fees of "$6850" (Spain), "$7750"
  (Portugal) and "$6779" (Belize), including round-trip airfare from Charlotte; AFAR offers
  a "maximum of 20% in assistance for the 2026 field season" (https://www.goafar.org/scholarships).
  The school's aid FAQ [Official] is silent on trips; it lists a "Field Experience (4th -
  12th Grade)" fee of "$500 - $1,000" (https://www.davidsonday.org/admission/tuition-and-affordability).
  No travel-specific endowment or named fund found.
- **Bottom line:** the archaeology research pathway is unusual and substantive (students
  present at a university conference; ten went to Calgary in 2024), but the program is
  narrow, depends on one person, and is thinly documented.

  Research" and "Middle School International Travel". The credential is one of three

  Day is pioneering full-scale international archaeological field research and cultural

  Research"; the 2024 blog post says "American Foreign Archaeological Research"; AFAR's own

- **Who runs it [Official]:** the Director of Global Studies (also an Upper School history
  teacher), who founded and leads AFAR.
- **AFAR structure [Secondary]:** a separate 501(c)(3) "based in Davidson, North Carolina";
  "incorporated as a 501(c)3 non-profit in 2007" after a first Belize season in 2006
  (https://www.goafar.org/history).
- **When it started:** credential "since 2021" [Official]. AFAR dates from 2006, before the
  director joined Davidson Day (Charlotte Magazine [Secondary]: moved here in 2010). The
  school does not publish when Davidson Day first sent students on AFAR.
  ▶ The school's own archived International Research page does: "Since inception in 2006",
  and the archived program page says the director "has been at Davidson Day School since
  2010".
- **Across divisions:** travel in Middle and Upper School only; no Lower School or Early
