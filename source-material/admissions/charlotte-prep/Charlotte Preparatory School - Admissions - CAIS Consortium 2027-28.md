# Charlotte Preparatory School — Admissions — CAIS Consortium 2027-28

Charlotte Prep is one of seven Charlotte private schools in the Charlotte Area Independent Schools consortium (CAIS). The members share one testing system for young applicants. A child applying to one or several of them is tested once, by one psychologist from a shared list, and the results go to every school the family names. Charlotte Prep links this year's brochure from its application page: the 2027–2028 edition (https://charlotteprep.org/uploads/files/Admissions/2027-2028-Forms/cais-2027-2028-upload.pdf).

### What the consortium covers

The brochure's title is "A Consortium for Applicant Assessment for Pre-Kindergarten–Grade 4". Its two tests are, word for word: "the Wechsler Preschool and Primary Scale of Intelligence–Fourth Edition (WPPSI-IV) for Pre-K, Kindergarten, and Grade 1 applicants; and the Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children–Fifth Edition (WISC-V) for rising Grades 2-4."

At Charlotte Prep that means:

| Applying for | Test | Given by |
|---|---|---|
| Montessori Kindergarten, Kindergarten, Grade 1 | WPPSI-IV | A CAIS psychologist |
| Grades 2–4 | WISC-V | A CAIS psychologist |
| Grades 5–8 | ISEE | Not CAIS. Registered separately. |

Charlotte Prep does not test children below Montessori Kindergarten, even though the consortium's test starts at Pre-K.

### The seven member schools

Charlotte Christian School, Charlotte Country Day School, Charlotte Latin School, **Charlotte Preparatory School**, Covenant Day School, Providence Day School and Trinity Episcopal School.

The brochure lists Charlotte Prep's contact as **Seher Khanna, Director of Enrollment**, 212 Boyce Road, Charlotte, NC 28211, 704-366-5994 ext. 1127, skhanna@charlotteprep.org.

### Shared 2027 dates

| | TK/JK/K | Grades 1–4 |
|---|---|---|
| Application deadline to CAIS schools | January 2, 2027 | January 15, 2027 |
| CAIS testing complete by | February 1, 2027 | February 26, 2027 |
| Notification date | February 26, 2027 | April 9, 2027 |
| Contracts returned by | March 5, 2027 | April 16, 2027 |

The rule that protects families, word for word: "No contract for a CAIS school will be required prior to this deadline." A family can hear back from every member school before committing to any of them.

Charlotte Prep's own Grades 1–8 track uses these same Grades 1–4 dates for every grade from 1 to 8. That includes Grades 5–8, whose testing (the ISEE) sits outside CAIS. The school's page also adds decision and contract times that the brochure does not give: 4:00 p.m. for decisions and 12:00 p.m. for contracts.

### Fees and booking

- **Testing fee:** "$300 for WPPSI-IV" and "$300 for WISC-V". The family pays the psychologist directly.
- **Booking:** "Appointments can be made the day after Labor Day." Parents book directly with a psychologist: "All appointments for testing are made by the parents with an individual psychologist-not by the schools."
- **Payment:** By credit card or Zelle within 24 hours of confirmation. Checks have a one-week grace period if arranged in advance. "Payments must be made in full for appointments made after January 1, 2027 and are non-refundable."
- **Cancellation:** "Once payment is made, a nonrefundable $100 fee applies to all cancellations." Cancellations within 72 hours of the appointment get no refund. For Monday appointments, cancel by the preceding Wednesday.
- **Book only once:** "Book an appointment with one psychologist ONLY."

### Rules that can end an application

- **Coaching or re-testing.** "Psychologists will report to the schools any indication that a child has been coached, tutored, or re-tested. Any indication of the aforementioned will invalidate your application to any CAIS school." Parents sign a Test Validity Verification Form at the psychologist's office. The penalty applies at every member school, not only one.
- **Age and frequency.** "children must be at least four years of age at the time of testing. Testing can be administered only once in a twelve month period."
- **The interview.** "In addition to the assessment, CAIS schools require an interview with each child." The schools arrange the interview or observation, "typically held November–February".

### Results and help for families

- Results go to parents and to the schools the family ticks on the registration form.
- A more detailed feedback meeting with the psychologist is optional and billed at that psychologist's hourly rate.
- For children learning English, the brochure says "an additional Nonverbal Index score will be provided", and families should pick a psychologist marked as offering ESL testing. Two psychologists on the 2027–28 list are marked "ESL Testing Available".
- It also says "for some ESL applicants a later test date may be beneficial to allow for greater language acquisition."
- The brochure lists 15 psychologists. Some give both tests and some give only the WPPSI-IV. The brochure marks which by shading.

**Weekday error in the brochure.** It prints "Notification date is Thursday, April 9, 2027" and "Contracts must be returned by Thursday, April 16, 2027". April 9, 2027 and April 16, 2027 are both Fridays. Its TK/JK/K weekdays are correct: Friday, February 26, 2027 and Friday, March 5, 2027. The body above leaves the weekday off the April dates. Do not print "Thursday" with them. The earlier sweep copied the error ("Thu Apr 9 and Thu Apr 16, 2027"), and this file corrects it.

**What changed from the 2024–25 edition.** The 2024–25 edition, as recorded in the Trinity research, listed $275 per test, a no-refund window of 48 hours and the same seven members. The 2027–28 edition lists $300 per test, a 72-hour no-refund window and a new $100 non-refundable cancellation fee. The earlier Trinity file says Charlotte Prep "does not" have a dossier.
