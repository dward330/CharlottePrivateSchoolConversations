## The instrument, by grade — VERBATIM

> "Standardized evaluations are the Wechsler Preschool and Primary Scale of
> Intelligence–Fourth Edition (WPPSI-IV) for Pre-K, Kindergarten, and Grade 1 applicants;
> and the Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children–Fifth Edition (WISC-V) for rising
> Grades 2-4."

This resolves the item CCDS's own readable pages leave unstated. Note it **cuts across the
school's own band structure**: CCDS's JK–K band and its Grades 1–4 band are one CAIS
assessment population, but the instrument changes at **Grade 1 → Grade 2**, not at
Kindergarten → Grade 1.

| CCDS band | Grades | CAIS instrument |
|---|---|---|
| JK–K | JK, K | WPPSI-IV |
| Grades 1–4 | Grade 1 | **WPPSI-IV** |
| Grades 1–4 | Grades 2–4 | **WISC-V** |
| Grades 5–12 | 5–12 | Not CAIS — ISEE |

## Scope of the consortium

The brochure covers **Pre-Kindergarten–Grade 4** only. One evaluation serves every member
school: "your child will be evaluated only one time by a licensed psychologist designated
by CAIS."

Participating CAIS schools (2027–28), verbatim list:

- Charlotte Christian School
- Charlotte Country Day School
- Charlotte Latin School
- Charlotte Preparatory School
- Covenant Day School
- Providence Day School
- Trinity Episcopal School

**Four of these seven are schools this app already covers** (Country Day, Latin, Christian,
Covenant Day) and a fifth (Providence Day) already ships an Admissions card.

## Registration rules — VERBATIM

1. "Complete an application to the school(s) to which you are applying for admission. No
   school will consider an evaluation until an application for admission to that particular
   school has been received." — the application-before-testing sequencing rule.
2. "Appointments can be made the day after Labor Day. All appointments for testing are made
   by the parents with an individual psychologist-not by the schools."
3. "Since your child will be compared to other children with the same chronological age,
   late testing provides no advantages. However, for some ESL applicants a later test date
   may be beneficial to allow for greater language acquisition."
4. "Psychologists will not schedule appointments until after Labor Day. Allow up to 48 hours
   for a response. Ample slots are available for all applicants. Once confirmed, payment via
   credit card or Zelle is due within 24 hours."
5. **Age + retest rule:** "To ensure test reliability and validity, children must be at
   least four years of age at the time of testing. Testing can be administered only once in
   a twelve month period."
6. **Coaching rule:** "Psychologists will report to the schools any indication that a child
   has been coached, tutored, or re-tested. Any indication of the aforementioned will
   invalidate your application to any CAIS school. Parents will be asked to sign a Test
   Validity Verification Form at the psychologist's office."
7. Results go to parents and designated schools; an optional feedback meeting is billed at
   the psychologist's hourly rate.
8. **Interview:** "In addition to the assessment, CAIS schools require an interview with
   each child. The respective schools will contact you to make an appointment for the
   interview/observation sessions that are typically held November–February."
9. "Book an appointment with one psychologist ONLY."

## Testing fees — VERBATIM

> "Testing Fees: $300 for WPPSI-IV / $300 for WISC-V"

Paid to the psychologist, **not to the school**, and separate from CCDS's own $100
application fee. "Payments must be made in full for appointments made after January 1, 2027
and are non-refundable."

**Cancellation:** "Once payment is made, a nonrefundable $100 fee applies to all
cancellations. Per CAIS guidelines, cancellations within 72 hours of your scheduled
appointment are not eligible for a refund. For Monday appointments, cancellations must be
made by the preceding Wednesday."
