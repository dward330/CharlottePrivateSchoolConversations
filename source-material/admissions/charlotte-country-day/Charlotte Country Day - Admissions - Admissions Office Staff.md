# Charlotte Country Day — Admissions — Admissions Office Staff

## Provenance

- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-06, during `/admissions-episode charlotte-country-day`.
- **How:** The command's step-3 freshness check compared the app's `contacts.people` against
  the school's own "Meet Our Team" admissions page. The check found the office's most senior
  person — **Eric Brunt, Director of Enrollment Management** — present on the school's page
  and **absent from the app**, which began its list at the Associate Director.
- **Corroboration:** Independently confirmed by the 2027–28 CAIS consortium brochure, which
  names Brunt as Charlotte Country Day's contact in its participating-schools table. Two
  independent school-published sources agree, so this is a **gap in the app's original
  transcription**, not a recent staff change.
- **Classification:** `NEW` — the site publishes a person the app did not carry.

Note the brochure lists the **campus main line** (704-943-4500) against Brunt's name, while

more useful figure for a parent and is what the app carries.

## Why this matters

No date, fee or deadline is affected — nothing the admissions episode speaks as fact changes.
The defect is one of completeness: the app's Contacts panel reads as the whole office and was
not. A parent with an escalation, an unusual situation, or a question no assistant director
can settle was being pointed one rung too low.

## Not published

Individual staff members' areas of responsibility — which director handles which grade band —
are **not published** on the pages reviewed. The app does not assert any, and should not.
