(raw HTML stripped to text), the documents those pages link (`pdftotext -layout`), the

from their page images; everything taken from them is marked LEAD.

**The school.** Covenant Day School, Matthews, NC. Confirmed by the site footer, "800 Fullwood
Lane Matthews North Carolina 28105", and by /about-us: "Covenant Day is a JK-12 Christ-centered
college preparatory school located in Matthews, NC. Covenant Day is a ministry of Christ
Covenant Church (PCA) and serves approximately 1,030 students."

**Names.** No student is named in this file. Sources that name a student: /about-us/our-stories
(alumni video tiles: names a student, withheld); three news posts (the June 22, 2026 senior
project post, whose headline and address both carry a student's name; the March 11, 2026
"Sharing the Court" post; the March 2, 2026 Calling and Career Day post, which names alumni):
withheld; the Classic Magazine 2025-2026 (student art gallery, athlete feature, grandparent
spotlight, yearbook and Clay Shoot features: names students, withheld); and the 2025-2026 Annual
Report photos. Adults in adult roles (staff, trustees, principals) are named as the school

retreat dates are on the school's calendar and its Key Dates PDF). Personal staff detail: the
Head of School's leadership bio and the high school counseling bios on /academics/college-guidance
carry family and church details; none is copied here.

## Method

Pages were fetched with `curl -sL` and a desktop Chrome user agent, stripped to text, and the
shared navigation and footer lines removed for reading. Finalsite tiles were read through their
pop-up endpoints (element and post ids from each page's `fsArticle_<element>_<post>` markers).
The directory list (element 34652) holds all 197 people on one page; emails come from the
profile pop-ups' `FS.util.insertEmail` arguments, reversed. The calendar was read month by month
(August 2026 to May 2027) from five grid elements; the all-school grid carries every division's
entries. Event pop-ups returned dates and categories, and one time. The site's search page renders
in the browser, so it returned nothing through curl. The Who We Are diversity statement is a
positioned text layer split into spans; its quotes above were re-extracted with the span tags
joined, which is how the page displays. The two Issuu documents were read from their page images
(28 and 16 pages); no OCR was available, so every fact from them is a LEAD and is paraphrased
rather than quoted, except short headings.
