# Covenant Day School — Faith & Spiritual Life — Second Pass (Gap Research)

**Provenance.** Claude Code /faith-life second pass, 2026-11-27. This is a targeted pass after
`Covenant Day School - Faith and Spiritual Life - Official Site Research.md` in this folder. It
tried to close that file's "Not found" and "Could not read" gaps and its INFERENCE lines from
PUBLIC official pages only. Those are covenantday.org, documents the site links to as its own,
the Finalsite calendar, directory and post pop-ups, and the school's public communications
archive. It also walked every Finalsite page id from 2030 to 2420 (`/fs/pages/<n>`). School
confirmed as Covenant Day School, "800 Fullwood Lane, Matthews, NC 28105" (site footer and the
International High School Profile). Quotes are char-for-char from the raw downloads.

**Names.** No student is named in this file. New sources that name a student: the 2026-27
Viewbook (two student quotes signed with a first name: withheld); the High School email of
October 5, 2026 (names several students and an alumnus who was a student chaplain: withheld);
other weekly emails (athletes and award winners: withheld); and the fall 2024 Classic Magazine
contents page (student art gallery: withheld). Adults in adult roles are named as the school
publishes them. Two "Get to Know" news features carry a staff member's family or health
details; none is recorded.

**Kept out on purpose.** Trip prices and exact travel dates (season and year only), including
the Windy Gap and Doulos Day dates printed in the weekly emails. Booster Club membership
amounts on the page listing a prayer benefit are not copied.

**LEADs.** Anything read by eye from an image, found on an outdated edition, or found only on a
preview host is marked LEAD and supports no parent-facing fact.

**The biggest find is the school's communications archive.** It lives at
`/lions-web-pages-clone/comms-archive`, a "LionsWeb" resource page that the site serves
publicly with no login, though no menu links it. The page-id walk found it. It links 63 emails
for 2026-27: "Lions Weekly" (all school), weekly High School, Middle School and Lower School
principal letters, and "Anchored" (the Head of School's letter). Those emails publish the
chapel day and times for all three divisions. They also cover Moms in Prayer, the student
chaplain team, Windy Gap, Doulos Days and student Bible studies. The second find is the
**2026-27 Admissions Viewbook as a text PDF**, reached from a public download page that the
first pass took to be form-gated. The third is the **campus map page**, which names the church
buildings used for chapel.

### Gap 1. Chapel: day, time, place, frequency, attendance

- **All three divisions meet on Wednesdays, at different times.** "Please join us in praying for lower school (9:15 a.m.), middle school (10:20 a.m.), and high school (11:00 a.m.) chapels on Wednesdays." — Lions Weekly, Oct 2, 2026 (Weekly Prayer Point)
- Middle school: "Chapel each Wednesday" — 2026-27 Viewbook, middle school "DAILY SCHEDULE".
- Lower school, time and place: "I love Wednesday mornings, especially at 9:15 a.m. This is when chapel begins in the lower school." "Joining together with 450+ students, faculty, and staff is a wonderful way to start the day." "If you ever have a Wednesday morning available, you are welcome to join us in the Worship Center." — Lower School email, Aug 24, 2026
- Dress: "Thank you for dressing your student in their chapel uniform on Wednesday." — Lower School email, Aug 24, 2026. The Lions Weekly of Nov 20, 2026 describes "Chapel uniforms" as the lower school's "special occasion uniforms". INFERENCE: lower school students wear a chapel uniform on Wednesdays.
- Wednesday late start: "*On Wednesdays, all grade levels begin at 8:50 a.m." — Viewbook and /admissions/faqs.
- Places: the campus map groups two buildings under "Christ Covenant Church Facilities". "The Worship Center provides a large space for the Covenant Day community to join together for worship events and celebrations, including all-school chapels, Convocation, Baccalaureate, and Graduation." "Commonly known as the CLC, the Community Life Center features large meeting rooms, a small chapel, and a youth space. Covenant Day has the opportunity to use these spaces for chapels and special events throughout the school year." — /about-us/campusmap
- All-school chapels: Convocation is "our first all-school chapel of the year". It was held "on Wednesday, September 2, at 10:00 a.m. in the Worship Center of Christ Covenant Church", as a professional dress day. — High School email, Aug 31, 2026. The All-School Christmas Chapel is "in the Christ Covenant Church Worship Center". — Lions Weekly, Nov 20, 2026
- What chapel is for (high school): "In our first chapel of the year, William Hunter (HS Bible Teacher and Chaplain) spoke to us about the purpose of chapel." — High School email, Aug 31, 2026
- Middle school chapel theme: "This year, much of our chapel teaching will focus on the Apostles’ Creed." — Middle School email, Sep 8, 2026. The Head of School adds that middle schoolers "are studying The Apostles’ Creed by Al Mohler this year. Each week, chapel speakers focus on a portion of the creed" — Anchored, Nov 12, 2026. "Students follow up on chapel messages with discussion questions in their homerooms the following morning." — Middle School email, Oct 5, 2026
- Lower school chapel teaching: "We cover one letter every other week in chapel to help our students grasp each letter's formation goal." (the new L.I.O.N.S. framework; "I" is integrity and "O" is "Own Your Choices") — Lower School emails, Sep 14 and Sep 28, 2026
- Who speaks: the Spiritual Formation Director, the Head of School and the HS Chaplain all speak in the emails above, and a "Mrs. Schmidt" speaks at LS chapel (INFERENCE: the directory's LS Counselor has that surname). A pastor spoke at the all-school Veterans Day assembly, which replaced chapel one week (Middle School email, Nov 16, 2026).
- Attendance rule: no email or page states one. INFERENCE: chapel sits inside the school day for every division.
