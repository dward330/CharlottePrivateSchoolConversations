those pages link (`pdftotext`), the 2026-27 school calendar (August 2026 to May 2027) and the

documents it links to as its own. No third-party page is used. Quotes are char-for-char.

(statement of faith, church count, S.L.A.M., Bible credits, senior Bible course,

### 2. Bible and faith classes

- "The study of the Bible is a required part of the curriculum beginning in junior kindergarten and continuing through grade 12. The goal of the curriculum is for students to “grow in wisdom, stature and in favor with God” (Luke 2:40). In the pursuit of wisdom, emphasis is placed on practical application of the Bible, recognizing God calls us to not just be hearers of the Word, but doers as well (James 1:23). The English Standard Version of the Bible is used in all classes." — /about/what-we-believe (Biblical Worldview)
- "True Christian education is not simply a curriculum, which contains a Bible class. It is a manner of teaching in which students are led to make sense of the universe and their place in it by filtering their learning through God’s Word." — /about/what-we-believe
- Lower school: "Students are immersed in the Bible from the beginning of their time at Charlotte Christian. Along with reading God’s word, our curriculum establishes a base of biblical knowledge as it works through both the Old and New Testament. To bring the Bible to life, teachers lead class discussions, take time to pray with students and daily model walking with Christ. As the capstone of biblical learning in lower school, fourth grade students take an introductory course to Christian apologetics as they work through the young reader’s versions of Case for Christ." — /academics/curriculum
- Lower school by grade (table on /academics/curriculum): Junior Kindergarten "Heroes of the Faith, God's Love for All, the Truth of God's Word"; Kindergarten "Survey of the Bible"; Grade 1 "Creation through the Promised Land"; Grade 2 "The Gospels"; Grade 3 "Israel"; Grade 4 "Acts and the Early Church". - Middle school: "Through Bible classes, daily scripture reading (Daily Dose of the Word), ongoing student discipleship opportunities and service learning, creative weekly chapels, and our annual spiritual life retreat, our students deepen their understanding of the unique calling that God has placed on each of their lives." — /academics/curriculum
