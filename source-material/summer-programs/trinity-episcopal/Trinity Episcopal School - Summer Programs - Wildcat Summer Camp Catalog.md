## Structure

Verbatim:
> "We offer **six weeks** of full- and half-day camps at our campus in the First Ward of
> uptown Charlotte, NC, from the week of **June 15, 2026**, to the week of **July 20,
> 2026**."

> "Wildcat Summer Camp is **open to any child in the community in rising grades K-8**.
> You don't have to be a Trinity student to be a part of the summer experience!"

> ⚠️ **"Open to the public" needs a qualifier.** The **TES Academy strand is
> Trinity-students-only**: Summer LEAP is "offered by invitation only, and students must be
> enrolled in Trinity for the 2026-27 school year"; Fun with Numbers is "for current or
> incoming 2026-27 TES students only"; Summer Play Days is "Rising TES K only". The
> public-open claim holds for the camp overall, **not for every camp in it**.

**Hours**, verbatim:
> "Half-day camps: **9 a.m. - 12 p.m.**, and **1–4 p.m.** / Full-day camps: **9 a.m. - 4 p.m.**"

Drop-off begins **8:45 a.m.** per the camp page — ⚠️ though the logistics doc says **8:40**
and the FAQ says **8:40am**. A minor internal inconsistency in the school's own materials.
