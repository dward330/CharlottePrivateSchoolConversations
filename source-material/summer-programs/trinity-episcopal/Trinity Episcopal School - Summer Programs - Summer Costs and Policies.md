## Before- and after-camp care

Verbatim (the fees doc states this twice):
> "Before-camp care is available from **7:45am-9am** at **$85/week**. After-camp care is
> provided from **4:00pm-5:30pm** at **$90/week**. Before-camp and after-camp care costs
> **$175/week**."

**No after-care for half-day morning campers**, verbatim:
> "PLEASE BE REMINDED: If your camp session ends at noon (i.e. you did not register for an
> afternoon session), please pick up your child(ren) promptly as **there is no after-care
> for half-day students**."

> ⚠️ A subtlety worth getting right: the logistics doc adds that after-care "is offered for
> **afternoon or full-day campers only**". So a **morning-only** camper cannot buy
> after-care, but an **afternoon** half-day camper can.

> **Note the contrast with the school year:** camp sells morning care at $85/week, while
> **TED offers no before-school care at all**. See the After School file — this is what
> makes that null a confident one rather than an oversight.

## Payment

Verbatim:
> "A **$50 nonrefundable deposit/camp** towards your camp tuition will be due upon
> registration. A **$25 administrative fee** is assessed at the time of registration as
> well. Note: **All TES Academy camps require payment in full at the time of
> registration.** Registrations become final on **May 1**. The balance is due by and
> withdrawn from your account on file on that day. If you are registering on/after May 1,
> you must pay in full."

Payment by ACH/e-check is encouraged; debit/credit accepted; checks case-by-case.

> "Online registration **closes a week prior** to the start of each camp session. A **$25
> administration fee** may be assessed for any late registrations **or if a camper requests
> to switch camps**."

## The multi-camp discount

Verbatim:
> "An individual camper who registers for **four or more weeks of full-day camps** can use
> the discount code **`4ORMORE`** to receive **$25 off** the cost of each camp. If a camper
> registers for **8 half-day camps**, the discount will be applied to the total cost of
> both camps for the week. **This discount does not apply to registrations for less than 4
> weeks of single/half-day camps or registrations for multiple campers from one family.**"

> ⚠️ Two restrictions that are easy to miss: it is **per individual camper, not per
> family**, and there is an **8-half-day equivalence** rule.

## Financial aid — and its three exclusions

Verbatim:
> "Financial Aid applies to all camps **excluding CAMMP, TES Academy or Black Rocket
> (coding, etc.) camps**."

> "Financial assistance is available on a **first come, first served** basis. Decisions
> about aid will not be made until your **two most recent income tax statements**
> (non-Trinity families only) have been received."

**The exclusions are material**: CAMMP, TES Academy and Black Rocket together account for
a large share of the catalog — including most of the coding/game-design camps and the
entire academic strand. A family relying on aid has a meaningfully smaller menu.

## Late pick-up — monetary AND removal

Verbatim:
> "There is a **five-minute grace period** at pick-up time. After **12:05** (half-day),
> **4:05** (full-day) and **5:35** (after-camp care), there will be a **$5/minute charge**.
> **Two or more instances of late pick up in one week could result in non-refunded removal
> from the program.**"

> ⚠️ **This is the CAMP's rule — two instances, not three — and it belongs only to the
> camp.** No equivalent TED policy is published. Do not transfer it.

## Refunds and cancellation

Verbatim:
> "Refunds are generally **not issued after May 1** unless there is an extreme and/or
> unexpected circumstance. If registration for a camp is canceled, the **$50 deposit and
> registration fee can be transferred** to a new/another camp but is otherwise
> nonrefundable. Parents will be notified a minimum of **2-4 weeks** prior to the start of
> a camp in the unlikely event that a camp has been canceled. In this case, the entire
> registration fee will be refunded, **less the administrative fee**. **All camps are
> subject to a minimum enrollment.** Trinity Episcopal School (TES) reserves the right to
> cancel any class with insufficient enrollment."

## Lunch and daily logistics

**Lunch is BYO** — verbatim:
> "Campers are responsible for bringing their own lunch from home each day if staying for
> a full day."
> "**Lunch:** If your child(ren) is/are registered for a morning camp then an afternoon
> camp or a full-day camp, please send a bag lunch each day."

> ⚠️ **There is no FLIK lunch option at camp** — a real contrast with the school year,
> where FLIK is $11.50/meal.

**Two-half-day campers bridge lunch on campus**, verbatim:
> "Children participating in two separate camps (a morning camp and then an afternoon
> camp) will **stay on campus for lunch and recess** before joining their afternoon camp"

They are sent to the afternoon session at 1:00. **This is what makes stacking two half-day
camps into a full day workable**, and it is a genuinely useful planning fact.

**Other daily rules:** the camp "strives to be **nut-free**"; campers bring a labeled
reusable water bottle (the camp is cup-free); snacks are required — **1 for half-day, 2 for
full-day**, in labeled Ziploc bags.
