# Charlotte Preparatory School — Summer Programs — Summer 2026 Costs

Every price on this page is for **summer 2026** (seven weeks, June 8 – July 31, 2026). No summer 2027 prices had been published as of October 19, 2026.

The school published its 2026 prices in two places, and they do not always match:

- the **brochure**, posted in December 2025 (https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/summer_adventures_2026)
- the **registration form**, as preserved on February 26, 2026 (https://web.archive.org/web/20260226113337/https://charlotteprep.org/community/summer-camp-registration). Its header reads: "Early Bird Discount — Take advantage of $25 savings per week now through January 15, 2026. Discounted prices reflected below."

Both figures are shown wherever they differ. Neither has been averaged or chosen over the other.

### Early-Bird Pricing

The registration form offered "$25 savings per week" through January 15, 2026, and said the prices it showed were the discounted ones.

**It is not clear which published prices are early-bird and which are regular.**

- For Traditional Camp in weeks 3–7, the form's prices are exactly $25 below the brochure's ($500 against $525 full day, $250 against $275 half day). That fits the early-bird note.
- In other rows, the form's price matches the brochure, or is even higher.
- The form was captured on February 26, 2026, six weeks after the early-bird deadline, but it still displayed the early-bird header.

The safest reading for planning is that the **brochure figures are the regular 2026 prices**.

### What Is Included in the Price

- **Lunch:** "Lunch is provided for all full-day campers in our all-new, state-of-the-art cafeteria, 'the Lair.'" There is no separate lunch charge.
- **Snacks:** "We will also offer a morning and afternoon snack for all campers."
- **Field trips:** both Young Adventurers and Traditional Camp describe weekly field trips. No separate field-trip fee is published.
- **No registration fee or deposit** is published. The FAQ heading asks "Is lunch included in the registration fee?", but no fee amount appears anywhere.

### Payment, Refunds and Deadlines

From the brochure FAQ, verbatim: "Payment is due at the time of registration. Refunds are available up to two weeks before camp starts. A refund fee of $150 will apply per camper."

- **Payment:** in full at registration, by credit or debit card. The form shows a single "Credit Card" method.
- **Refund deadline:** two weeks before camp starts.
- **Refund fee:** $150 per camper.
- **Early-bird deadline:** January 15, 2026 ($25 off per week).
- **Registration closed:** Friday, May 29, 2026.
- **Discount codes:** the form has a "Discount Code" box, but no code or code-based discount is published.

### Planning a Whole Summer

Two worked examples, using the brochure's regular prices:

- **Seven weeks of full-day Traditional Camp** comes to 6 × $525 + $420 = **$3,570**. Adding Before and After Care every week adds $110 × 7 = $770 (at the brochure's flat weekly rate), for **$4,340**.
- **Seven weeks of full-day Young Adventurers** comes to 6 × $500 + $400 = **$3,400**.

These totals are calculated here and were not published by the school.

### Not Published for 2026

## Method

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-19. Prices were transcribed from the brochure page images (read visually; no OCR was available) and from the registration form's option labels in the archived HTML. Each price was checked cell by cell against both sources. Brochure page 10 ("Pricing for All Camps") agrees with the per-camp pricing tables on pages 4–9 in every cell.

**Early-bird question: unresolved, recorded as such.** The plan asked whether the listed prices are early-bird or regular. The evidence:

1. The form says "Discounted prices reflected below".
2. Traditional Camp weeks 3–7 on the form are exactly brochure minus $25.
3. Week 1 Traditional, all of Young Adventurers (except Week 3), the Specialty camps (except Broadway Bound) and the Athletic camps show no $25 reduction.
4. Two form cells sit above the brochure: Traditional Week 2 half day ($225 against $175) and Young Adventurers Week 3 full day ($525 against $500).
5. The capture postdates the January 15, 2026 deadline.

Conclusion: the brochure is the more internally consistent regular price list, and the form is a partly discounted, partly inconsistent list. Both are recorded. For a planner, use the brochure figures, flag that the form differs, and say that a $25/week early-bird applied through January 15, 2026.
