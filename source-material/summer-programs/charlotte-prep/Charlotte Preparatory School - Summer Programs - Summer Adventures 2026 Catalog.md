# Charlotte Preparatory School — Summer Programs — Summer Adventures 2026 Catalog

Everything on this page describes **summer 2026**. Charlotte Prep's camp, "Summer Adventures at Prep", ran seven weeks from June 8 to July 31, 2026 on the Boyce Road campus. The school's summer pages have since been taken down, and no summer 2027 camp had been announced as of October 19, 2026. The record below comes from the school's own 2026 camp brochure and its 2026 registration form, both preserved online.

### Who the 2026 Camp Was For

The school's FAQ says camp was "Open to all students PK-8" — not only Charlotte Prep families. The registration form asked "Does your child currently attend Charlotte Prep?" and, if not, which school the child attends.

The camp was organised into four families of camps, plus a teen helper programme:

- **Young Adventurers** — rising Primary and Transitional Kindergarten (the youngest campers, roughly ages 3 to 5)
- **Traditional Camp** — classic day camp, full or half day
- **Specialty Camps** — one week-long themed camp each week, full day only
- **Athletic Adventurers** — one sports camp each week, full day only (except the Nike Soccer Camp, which is run separately)
- **Junior Counselor Program** — rising or graduated 8th graders

The brochure called Young Adventurers and Athletic Adventurers new for 2026: "This year, we are proud to offer our Young Adventurers camps for our youngest campers and our Athletic Adventurers camps for those campers seeking a sporty summer experience."

### Camp Hours

From the brochure FAQ, verbatim:

- **Full Day:** 9:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.
- **Half Day (morning):** 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.
- **Half Day (afternoon):** 1:00–4:00 p.m.

Half-day families chose AM or PM on the registration form. Optional Before Care ran 8:00–9:00 a.m. and After Care 4:00–5:00 p.m., at extra cost (see the Summer 2026 Costs page).

### Young Adventurers (Rising Primary and TK)

"Designed for campers to grow in curiosity through play, imagination, and hands-on exploration in a warm, nurturing environment."

- "Each week features exciting themed activities, including water days, nature walks, creative arts and crafts, and fun-filled field trips that spark curiosity and wonder."
- "A quiet nap time is offered to help our youngest adventurers recharge."
- Full-day and half-day options; full-day camps include lunch.
- The registration form labels it "Young Adventurers (ES P/TK)" — Early School Primary and Transitional Kindergarten.
- The school held a Young Adventurers Camp Information Meeting on January 22 (2026) at 5:30 p.m. in the Lower School cafeteria, "The Lair."

### Traditional Camp

"Campers will experience the classic summer camp vibe with games, crafts, and outdoor adventures packed with variety, excitement, and memorable adventures!"

- "Each week features engaging activities and off-campus field trips, along with special surprises such as karaoke parties, ice cream and snow cone pop-ups, picnic days, and more."
- Full-day and half-day options.
- Traditional campers could add the optional Midsummer Tune-Up tutoring sessions (see below).

**Grade band — two school sources disagree.** The brochure says "Rising MK-8 Students" and the FAQ says "All rising MK-8 students are eligible to sign up for traditional summer camps." The registration form lists every Traditional Camp week as "Traditional Camp (K-7)". Rising 8th graders were offered the Junior Counselor Program instead on that form. Both are recorded here; the form is the later document.

### Nike Soccer Is a Separate Camp

The Nike Soccer Camp used Charlotte Prep's campus but was **not** part of Summer Adventures registration. It was run by US Sports Camps, and families registered on that company's website: https://www.ussportscamps.com/soccer/nike/nike-soccer-camp-charlotte-prep-school

Per the provider's page (2026 season):

- Dates: June 22-26, 2026 and July 27-31, 2026 (Mon-Fri)
- Ages 6-13, coed
- Half-Day Hours: 9:00am-12:00pm; Day Camp Hours: 9:00am-3:00pm
- "Lunch is NOT included"
- Held at Founders Field, with check-in in the Charlotte Preparatory School parking lot
- Camp Director: Prince Ojok

Prices for this camp are on the Summer 2026 Costs page.

### Junior Counselor Program

"Rising or graduated 8th graders can participate in The Junior Counselor Program. This program offers our older campers leadership opportunities and hands-on experience, while they assist counselors with the younger campers in our Traditional Camps."

"Please note that there are limited spots available and these positions may require an interview."

### Lunch and Snacks

"All full-time campers will enjoy free lunch daily in our new state-of-the-art cafeteria, 'The Lair.' Snack will also be provided to all campers in the morning and the afternoon." Half-day campers get the snacks but not lunch.

### Registration for 2026

- Registration was an online form on the school's website, paid by credit or debit card.
- "Registration ends on Friday, May 29, 2026."
- Children in the school-year Extended-Day Program were **not** automatically enrolled: "the Extended-Day Program no longer includes registration for Summer Adventures at Prep. All current Extended-Day students MUST be registered for camp through the registration website."
- Questions went to camp contact Mickey Stone, mstone@charlotteprep.org.
