# Charlotte Country Day School — Summer Programs Research (2026)

Provenance header
- Compiled: August 11, 2026, for the Summer Programs research-area build (two cards:
  The Camp Catalog, The Summer Cost Planner).
- Method: deep web research against the school's live summer pages and its official
  priced 2026 catalog PDF, reached via the school's own resource-manager link. Every
  figure is copied char-for-char from a Country Day source.
- Where the school publishes nothing, this file says NOT PUBLISHED rather than
  estimating. Two internal contradictions in the school's own materials are recorded
  as TO VERIFY rather than silently resolved.

**Marketed season:** the summer landing page advertises "June 8–July 24, 2026" — i.e.
7 weeks, excluding the June 1–5 "Pre-Camp" week, which nevertheless carries 9 priced
camps in the listing.

Hours are not printed per camp in the listing; they come from the official Camp Ready
page and apply by camp type:
- Morning: 9 am–12 pm
- Afternoon: 1–4 pm
- Full Day: 9 am–4 pm

**Wrap-around care: earliest drop-off 7:30 am (Morning Care) to latest pickup 5 pm**
(Afternoon Care ends 4–5 pm), i.e. a 7:30 am–5 pm envelope; $1/minute after 5 pm.
Full-day swim camp pickup is 5:00 pm.

Facilities:
- **Cannon Campus, 1440 Carmel Road, Charlotte, North Carolina 28226** — primary camp
  site. Nurse and certified athletic trainer on Cannon Campus during all camp sessions.
- **The Farm** — dedicated "Full Day - Farm" tier (Farm Camp, Outdoor Adventure Camp,
  Youth Fly Fishing, La Finca Farm Camp); the school markets "Bucky's Farm Camp,
  Fishing Camp".
- **Klein Aquatic Center** — swim camps and lessons.
- **Bissell Campus** exists as a second campus, but NOT PUBLISHED whether 2026 camps
  use it. (In 2020 an inter-campus shuttle ran at $30/week; no 2026 bus/shuttle is
  published — NOT PUBLISHED for 2026.)

Camp inclusions: every camper receives a T-shirt on their first day (one per summer).
Campers should bring a refillable water bottle, sunscreen, closed-toe sneakers. Campers
stay indoors during lightning. Carpool sign required; no exiting vehicles in the
carpool lane.

Contact: Summer Programs Office (704) 943-4702; Elise Ernette, Director of After School
and Summer Programs; Sarah Fontaine, Assistant Director.

## Rates & Add-ons

Weekly rates by tier (from the listing PDF, corroborated by the official Camp Leaders
page which states "Half-Day (3 hours): $280", "Full-Day (6 hours): $560", "4-Day Week:
$230 (half) / $450 (full)"):

| Tier | 5-day week | 4-day week (June 15-18, June 29-July 2) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Half-day (morning or afternoon, 3 hours) | $ 280.00 | $ 230.00 |
| Full day (6 hours) | $ 560.00 | $ 460.00 (Camp Buccaneer) |
| Full Day - Farm (premium) | $ 600.00 | $ 480.00 (Outdoor Adventure Camp) |
| Premium specialty half-day (Writing College Essays, Gr 12) | $ 410.00 | not offered |

**TO VERIFY — a discrepancy in the school's own materials:** the Camp Leaders page
advertises a 4-day full-day rate of $450, while the actual published 2026 listing
charges $ 460.00 (Camp Buccaneer) and $ 480.00 (Outdoor Adventure Camp). The listing is
treated as the operative price here.

Extended care add-ons — verbatim from the summer page:
- Morning Care: 7:30–9 am | $105/week
- Lunch Care: 12–1 pm — $30/week (supervised lunch – bring your own lunch)
- Afternoon Care: 4–5 pm | $85/week
- Super Care: includes morning, lunch, and afternoon care when registered for both
  morning and afternoon camps | $160/week
- Late Pick-Up Fee: $1/minute (payable at pick-up after 5 pm)

Care is supervised by Country Day employees. If a child is in a half-day camp and will
eat lunch — brought from home or provided — a lunch option **must** be registered.

**TO VERIFY — a second internal conflict:** Lunch Care at $30/week is described on the
summer page as supervision only, campers bringing their own lunch; the Camp Ready page
notes dining-hall lunch is provided for Lunch Care enrollees. These two official pages
conflict on whether food is included.

One-time registration fee: "A $15 annual registration fee is required for each camper
and is due at the time of registration."

**Sibling / multi-week discounts: NOT PUBLISHED** for 2026. (Historical note,
non-binding: the 2020 brochure offered "a 10% discount on both camp sessions" when
registering morning + afternoon + Super Care. No such discount appears in any 2026
source.)

Deposit policy: no deposit — "Full tuition is required at the time of registration for
all camps."

## Registration & Policy

- **Registration opens: "Registration begins 1/20/26"** (listing PDF). Swim camp
  registration also "opens January 20."
- Registration closes: "Registration closes two weeks prior to the camp start date."
- Payment: full tuition due at registration; $15 annual per-camper registration fee.
- Refunds: "Withdrawals made before June 5 are eligible for a full refund." Nothing is
  published about partial refunds after June 5 — NOT PUBLISHED.
- Swim camp cancellation (different policy): $50 fee for cancellations before May 4;
  cancellations after May 4 are not accepted.
- Switching camps: "Camp switches are allowed; however, switching is not permitted
  after registration closes."
- School-side cancellation: all camps subject to a minimum enrollment; the school
  reserves the right to cancel under-enrolled camps, with families notified "at least
  two weeks in advance".
- **Open to non-Country-Day families: YES.** Official: "Our Summer Programs are open to
  both current families and the broader Charlotte community, offering hundreds of camps
  for children ages 3–18."
- **Sell-out patterns: NOT PUBLISHED.** The swim page says only "availability is
  limited." No waitlist mechanism is documented. Registration is via an UltraCamp
  portal (idCamp=507, campcode=ccd) with 24/7 account access.
- Eligibility: all campers must be fully potty trained; the school is not equipped to
  change diapers or pull-ups.
- Note an inconsistency in the school's own age framing: the summer page says "ages
  4–18", the After School page says "ages 3–18". The listing's lowest published band is
  Age 4.
