## Corrections to the prior dossier

1. **2025–26 state titles: 4, not 2** — football, girls swimming, girls lacrosse AND girls
   soccer. PD led all of NCISAA 4A that year.
2. **2026 girls soccer final: beat Carmel Christian 2–1, finishing 18–4–1** — not
   "Charlotte Latin 1–0 OT". Third straight title; Grayson Dudley scored both goals.
3. **Program size: 66 teams / 27 varsity sports** per the school's own directory — not
   Wikipedia's older 68/26 figure.
4. **Boys basketball resolved:** Jonathan McIntyre promoted to permanent head coach in
   April 2026 after 19 years as an assistant (took over during Ron Johnson's personal leave).
5. **NIL now has a named-athlete ledger** — David Sanders Jr.'s WME Sports representation,
   merchandise line and OrthoCarolina work, where the prior pass concluded none existed.

## 1. Sports Offered

- **66 teams across 27 varsity sports**; 950+ athletic events annually; historically over
  80% student participation; grades **7–12** eligible.
  https://www.pdschargers.com/more/about-pds-athletics/
- State association **NCISAA**, competing in **4A / Division I** (top tier). PD is private
  and does not compete in the public-school NCHSAA.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Providence_Day_School
  https://www.highschoolot.com/story/the-2025-26-ncisaa-team-state-champions-in-every-sport/22322092/
- Upper School conference **CISAA**. Football moved to the new NCISAA **"Big South"** for
  2025–26 with Charlotte Latin, Charlotte Christian and Charlotte Country Day; PD went 3–0,
  first place. Middle School plays in the **GCMSAA**.
  https://www.si.com/high-school/north-carolina/a-closer-look-at-providence-day-s-2025-football-schedule-01jprqcq455s
  https://www.maxpreps.com/nc/charlotte/providence-day-chargers/football/schedule/
- Middle School splits larger sports into **"Blue" and "Red"** squads to widen participation.
  https://www.pdschargers.com/

## 5. Coaching

**Athletic Director: Nancy Beatty.** Sports Information Director: David Olliver.
https://www.pdschargers.com/more/about-pds-athletics/

### The pedigree hire — Chad Grier, Football (since 2020)

- **147–26 career record across 15 seasons**; **8 state titles — 3rd-most in NC history**;
  program cites the **highest win percentage in NC history** among coaches with 100+ games.
- At PD: 4 NCISAA titles in 5 years (2021, 2022, 2023, 2025); five straight D-I finals
  2021–2025; 13–0 in 2025.
- QB-developer reputation: coached son Will Grier (NFL), Sam Hartman (most TD passes in ACC
  history) and Jadyn Davis (Mr. Football). Every starting QB across 12+ years went on to play
  college football.
- Built Davidson Day from scratch (64–9, 4 titles in 6 years), then turned around Oceanside
  Collegiate (SC). Returned to Charlotte to raise his youngest son near family.

https://www.g7football.com/copy-of-mission-statement ·

### The continuity anchor — Ben Hovis, XC / Track (since 2002)

- PD alum, **Class of 1996**, and a former PD runner; took over from founding coach Gil
  Murdock in 2002 — **24 years and counting**.
- Member of the **NC Track & Field / Cross Country Hall of Fame**.
- 2023 Charlotte Observer Boys Track Coach of the Year, having won a 4th straight state title
  (9 of the prior 10). Boys XC won **20 consecutive CISAA titles, 2000–2020**.
