## Wells Fargo Cup history, as published by the NCISAA

> The Wells Fargo Cup, presented and sponsored by Wells Fargo Bank, N.A., recognizes overall
> excellence in high school athletics among NCISAA member institutions. Since 1985, the Wells
> Fargo Cup has been presented to the schools in each classification with the best overall
> interscholastic athletic programs. The Wells Fargo Cup is awarded to the school with the most
> combined points for each classification. The winning schools receive an engraved silver cup
> to serve as permanent recognition of that year's athletic excellence. As such, the Cup has
> become a valued athletic achievement award in North Carolina.

## Wells Fargo Cup scoring, as published by the NCISAA

> The winning schools are determined by point totals earned through competition in State
> Championships. Each school that finishes in the top eight of a State Championship tournament
> receives Wells Fargo Cup points. This is applicable for sports who compete by division as well
> as classification. Wells Fargo points are awarded as follows: 1st place = 50 points, 2nd place
> = 45 points, 3rd place = 40 points, 4th place = 35 points, 5th place = 30 points, 6th place =
> 25 points, 7th place = 20 points, 8th place = 15 points. In the event of a tie, the point total
> for that finish is divided evenly among the schools involved.
