# Charlotte Preparatory School — Sports — Sports Offered and Participation

### A middle school program, grades 5 through 8

Charlotte Prep is a PreK–8 school, so its interscholastic athletics are a middle school program. Teams are open from 5th grade, which the school presents as unusual among its peers:

> "Charlotte Prep is one of the few schools that allow 5th Graders the opportunity to be a part of our sports program. Our student-athletes go on to other schools prepared for the competition they face."
> — Athletics page, https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/athletics (retrieved 2026-10-19)

The Life at Prep page puts the same point as a mentoring benefit: "We are one of the few area schools to welcome 5th Graders onto our teams, allowing our younger students to develop technical skill and competitive spirit early and affording our older students valuable experience as mentors and leaders." (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/life-at-prep)

The program's stated priorities, verbatim from the athletics page: "Including others is sometimes more important than winning." and "our program teaches students to challenge themselves, cooperate as a team member, and compete with character."

The teams play as the **Tigers**. The school does not publish a team level structure (no A and B teams, no junior varsity): every team below is a single middle school team as far as anything published shows.

(https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/athletics, retrieved 2026-10-19)

The grade band 5–8 comes from the athletics page's heading "5th - 8th Grade Interscholastic" and the Fast Facts tile "Athletic teams, grades 5-8". The school does not publish which grades play on which team.

### How many teams: 10 or 11

The school's two pages count differently.

| Source | Count | Retrieved |
|---|---|---|
| Fast Facts, https://charlotteprep.org/about/fast-facts | "11 — Athletic teams, grades 5-8" | 2026-10-19 |
| Athletics page, https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/athletics | 9 teams named by season, plus Girls Cheerleading = 10 | 2026-10-19 |

An archived copy of Fast Facts from July 2023 read "10 — Athletic teams, grades 5-8" (https://web.archive.org/web/20230708225618/https://charlotteprep.org/about/fast-facts); by November 2024 it read 11 (https://web.archive.org/web/20241124043609/https://charlotteprep.org/about/fast-facts). The school does not say which team was added, and the athletics page still names only ten. Both figures are recorded here; neither is corrected to the other.

### Participation: two different figures from the school

### Fees: athletics included in tuition since 2024–25

From the school's Strategic Plan 2022–2027 progress report, verbatim: "We have included the fees for athletics, fine arts, and technology in tuition for the 2024-25 school year and will continue to do so moving forward. Other fees, which are mainly for optional activities, will still be billed separately." (https://charlotteprep.org/about/strategic-plan-2022-2027)

Consistent with that, the 2026–2027 Tuition & Fees page lists no athletics fee (https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/tuition-fees). Middle school tuition for 2026–2027 is listed there as "5th - 8th Grades - $30,200".

### Required forms

Verbatim from the athletics page: "All Middle School athletes are required to complete an athletic waiver, a concussion form, and a sports medical form. In addition, parents of all student-athletes must read and sign the Athletics Handbook." The Athletics Handbook itself is not published on the public site.

### Younger grades: After School Athletic Studios

There are no interscholastic teams below 5th grade. For younger students the athletics page points to the after-school studios, verbatim: "Various athletic studios are offered to students of all ages, from Pre-Primary to 8th Grade. Golf, Tennis, Martial Arts, Soccer, and Gymnastics are some of the Studios offered." (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/athletics). Studio prices and schedules are not published on the athletics page.

### Health support for athletes

The school publishes a full-time registered nurse, not an athletic trainer. Verbatim from the Health & Wellness page: "With a full-time registered nurse, a top-rated dining hall partner, and two student counselors, our resources revolve around our students' health and well-being." (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/health-wellness). The staff directory lists one School Nurse (M.S.N., Duke University; B.S.N., University of Tennessee) and no athletic trainer (https://charlotteprep.org/about/faculty-staff-directory, retrieved 2026-10-19).

### Facilities

**The athletic field.** The school's 2015 campus page, verbatim: "Our activity field, Founders Field, was developed in 2013 as part of our Healthy Living Campaign. The field is used by all age groups and sport teams." (https://web.archive.org/web/20150807133708/http://www.charlotteprep.org:80/about-us/campus.cfm). The school's 2014 announcement described it as "Charlotte Prep's New Activity Field, on Sardis Road", "made possible by the generosity of our school community", and set the dedication ceremony for March 25, 2014, followed by the girls' soccer team's "inaugural game on our home field" (https://web.archive.org/web/20150227033323/http://www.charlotteprep.org:80/fielddedication). A later update on the same page postponed it for weather and rescheduled it for "Thursday, March 27", beginning at 3:45 p.m. (The same page also prints "Tuesday, March 27, 2014" in its date line; March 27, 2014 was a Thursday.)

The name "Founders Field" does not appear anywhere on the current site; it survives only in archived pages, so it may no longer be in use.

**The field during construction, and its restoration.** Modular classrooms stood on the field while the new lower school building was built. The Head of School wrote on September 4, 2025: "These new classrooms will allow us to remove the modular classrooms and begin restoring our athletic field later in the fall." (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/message-from-head-of-school-coffee). The strategic plan's progress report now reads: "Modulars on our soccer field that were used during this period of construction have now been decommissioned." (https://charlotteprep.org/about/strategic-plan-2022-2027). The school has not published that the restoration is finished, and it does not give the field's current name.

**The gym.** The school has a gymnasium, which it does not name. It doubles as the school's theatre: the annual musical is staged "in our gym" (Willy Wonka Jr., https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/charlotte-prep-presents-willy-wonka-jr) and the fall 2025 play ran "in the Charlotte Prep gymnasium" (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/prep-players-present-alice).

**Tennis and other venues.** No tennis courts, track or golf venue are named anywhere on the school's site.

**Corrections to the indicative sweep**
- The sweep's cheer-season evidence (a Charlotte Country Day team page, athletics-team-id 167) is wrong: that page is Country Day's Girls' Tennis – Middle School schedule, not cheer. It does list a 2026–27 fixture against Charlotte Preparatory School on Thursday, September 17 at Bissell Tennis Courts (Country Day home). That confirms Charlotte Prep currently fields girls' tennis, and that it plays at least one non-conference fixture against a school that Country Day's own 2018–19 information sheet places in the Greater Charlotte Middle School Athletics Association (a different league from Charlotte Prep's). The score is published only by Country Day and its page layout makes the row pairing ambiguous, so no score is carried. The winter-cheer evidence instead comes from the school's own 2015 page.

- Founders Field: "developed in 2013" is confirmed (2015 campus page). The dedication was scheduled for March 25, 2014 and moved to March 27, 2014. Whether it actually took place on March 27 is not published.
