# Charlotte Preparatory School — Sports — Selected Results and Coaching

### The conference: the Queen City Conference

Charlotte Prep's teams compete in the **Queen City Conference (QCC)**, a middle school conference. The school's 2013 athletics portal said, verbatim: "Our student-athletes compete in the Queen City Conference with other independent private school in the greater Charlotte area." (https://web.archive.org/web/20130719131848/http://www.charlotteprep.org/athletics/portal/default.asp). Current news stories use the abbreviation QCC without spelling it out, and the current athletics page names no conference.

Earlier, in 2010, the school named a different league, verbatim: "Charlotte Prep's athletic teams consistently compete at the top of our conference, the Charlotte-Gaston Athletic Association." (https://web.archive.org/web/20101226175815/http://www.charlotteprep.org:80/athletics.html)

The QCC's member schools are not published by Charlotte Prep, and the conference has no website of its own.

**Girls' basketball, 2023–24.** From "Tigers Win QCC Championship!", February 14, 2024, written by the team's coaches, who are described as "three Charlotte Prep teachers" but not named (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/tigers-win-qcc-championship). Verbatim:

> "After our girls' basketball team won both their conference and the QCC Tournament Championship, their coaches (three Charlotte Prep teachers) reflect on the journey that awarded them this title for the first time in 14 years."

> "Our girls went on to another six-game win streak. They won the regular season conference for the first time in 10 years and the QCC Tournament Championship final for the first time in 14 years."

The season's shape, from the same story: the team opened with nine consecutive wins ("Winning nine consecutive games bolstered our confidence—but then it happened. We lost."), then won six more in a row. That is the only win-loss detail published; a full record is not. The roster mixed grades — the story quotes a 6th-grade player who was new to the sport, alongside the 8th graders who led the team. A separate first-person story by an eighth grader on the team, posted February 6, 2024, notes that she "helped take our girls' basketball team to the QCC Championship finals this year."

**Girls' volleyball, fall 2024.** From "Victory for Girls' Volleyball", October 24, 2024 (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/victory-for-girls-volleyball). Verbatim:

> "After our girls' volleyball team won the QCC tournament championship, their head coach … reflected on an unforgettable season."

> "This team of 12 student-athletes was every coach's dream."

> "And in 18 practices, we accomplished the ultimate goal: winning the conference championship!"

The story's summary line says "QCC tournament championship" and the coach's text says "conference championship"; it does not say whether the team also won the regular season.

**Earlier results on archived pages.** The school's 2010 athletics page listed three runner-up finishes in the Charlotte-Gaston Athletic Association for 2009–2010: boys soccer, boys cross-country and girls cross-country, each "2nd place in conference" (https://web.archive.org/web/20101226175815/http://www.charlotteprep.org:80/athletics.html).

### The "18 conference and tournament titles" figure is not current

The school's Fast Facts page shows the tile "18 — Conference and tournament titles" (https://charlotteprep.org/about/fast-facts, retrieved 2026-10-19). It should not be read as a current or recent count. The same figure appeared on Fast Facts in January 2013, with a time frame that the current tile has dropped, verbatim: "Our student-athletes have won eighteen conference or tournament championships in the last five years." (https://web.archive.org/web/20130130002213/http://www.charlotteprep.org:80/fastfacts). So "the last five years" meant roughly 2008 to 2012.

The figure has not changed since, through the July 2023, November 2024 and February 2026 captures of the same page, even though the school has published at least three titles since then (two in 2023–24, one in fall 2024). The school's own July 2013 athletics portal gave a different number in the same period: "Our student-athletes have won seventeen conference or tournament championships in the last five years." No itemized list of the titles has ever been published.

### Coaching

**Renee Suttles, Athletic Director and P.E. Teacher.** She is the only member of staff with an athletics title in the school's directory (https://charlotteprep.org/about/faculty-staff-directory?id=455452, retrieved 2026-10-19). Her current profile, verbatim:

> "Education: B.S., PK-12 Health and Physical Education, Kennesaw State University"

> "Renee Suttles brings a passionate love of sports and working with children to her role at Charlotte Prep. She previously served as a physical education teacher at Crowders' Creek Elementary School, and taught health and physical education for grades 6-8 at Moses Middle School, where she also coached softball and girls' basketball. At Charlotte Prep, Renee loves the small, close-knit community where everyone knows one another and has the opportunity to build strong relationships. Her career has allowed her to combine her two passions–athletics and education–while supporting students' physical well-being, teamwork, and confidence."

> "Charlotte Prep employee since 2018"

Her earlier profile, on the school's 2020 middle school staff page, adds detail the current one leaves out, verbatim: "Renee Suttles joined Charlotte Prep in 2018 as the Middle School Physical Education teacher and the Intramural Coordinator. Renee received her Health, Physical Education and Sport Science degree from Kennesaw State University. She has ten years of teaching experience. Renee started her career at Moses Middle School in GA. Renee was the Physical Education Department Head, taught health and physical education and coached multiple sports. After relocating to Charlotte, Renee spent five years teaching in the Clover School District in SC, where she was named Teacher of the Year in 2011." (https://web.archive.org/web/20200808013135/https://www.charlotteprep.org/about/staff-directory/middle-school-staff)

### Alumni giving back

From "Playing it Forward: Prep Grads Give Back", September 11, 2024 (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/playing-it-forward-prep-grads-give-back): two former Prep students, by then playing varsity soccer at Providence Day School, ran a summer soccer camp at Charlotte Prep for the second year in a row and gave the proceeds to the Soccer Foundation of Charlotte. Verbatim: "This year, they raised an impressive $4,150!" One of them, Prep class of '21, had committed to play soccer for NC State University in fall 2025.

**Conflicts and how they were recorded**
- Title count: Fast Facts "18" (unchanged since January 2013, when it was "in the last five years") vs the July 2013 portal's "seventeen". Both recorded; the figure is flagged as not current.
- Volleyball: the summary says "QCC tournament championship", the coach's text "conference championship". Recorded as the tournament title, with the wording difference noted.
- Suttles' biography: the current profile and the 2020 profile differ in what they include (the 2020 one carries Intramural Coordinator, Georgia, Clover School District and Teacher of the Year 2011; the current one carries Crowders' Creek Elementary and the PK-12 degree wording). They do not contradict each other; both quoted. Crowders' Creek Elementary is placed in the Clover School District by inference from the 2020 profile's "five years teaching in the Clover School District in SC"; the current profile does not name the district.

**Corrections to the indicative sweep**
- "Clover SD Teacher of the Year, 2011", "Intramural Coordinator" and "Moses Middle School, GA" are not in the current profile; they come from the 2020 archived profile and are cited to it.
- The current profile adds a prior role the sweep did not record: physical education teacher at Crowders' Creek Elementary School.

- A second current staff member with coaching history (Amber O'Connell, cross country) was not in the sweep.
