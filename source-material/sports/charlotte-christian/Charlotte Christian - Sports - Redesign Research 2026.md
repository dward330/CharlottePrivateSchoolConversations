## Corrections to the prior dossier

1. ❌ "Boys basketball reached the 2025-26 state final (runner-up), lost 59–78 to Wesleyan
   Christian." → **WRONG.** That was a **2nd-round** exit. The 4A final was Forsyth Country
   Day 69, Providence Day 59. Charlotte Christian finished **17–14**.
2. ❌ "Shonn Brown is the current boys basketball coach." → **OUTDATED.** He stepped down in
   **April 2025** after 25 seasons and 486 wins. **Luke Boythe** now leads the program.
3. ❌ "Chris James, head football coach since 2023." → **OUTDATED.** James left March 2026
   (22–14). **Jammie Deese** was hired **April 27, 2026**.
4. ❌ "No on-campus pool" and "Guy Gym and Lamb/Johnson are likely the same facility."
   → **BOTH RESOLVED.** The **CCS Swim Center** exists, and the two gyms are distinct venues.
5. ❌ "No named team physician or hospital partnership." → **RESOLVED.** The school
   **partners with Atrium Health**, which embeds AT Victor Jurkiewicz on campus.

Also new since that pass: the **2026 baseball state title (43rd overall)**, the **2026
wrestling runner-up finish (best ever)**, **Estep Field** naming, the **Saleh Athletic Center**
groundbreaking, and **two confirmed Gatorade POY winners**.
