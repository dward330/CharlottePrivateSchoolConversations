# Davidson Day School — Sports — College Commitments (2024–2026)

> **Provenance:** First compiled by Claude Code on 2026-07-04; extended 2026-11-08 (`/plan
> ddathletes`) from the school's *2025-2026 Athletics Recap*; **rebuilt 2026-11-08 by
> `/athlete-commits`** from a varsity-roster cross-reference (method, nulls and unchecked
> gaps in `Davidson Day - Sports - Varsity Roster Cross-Reference 2026.md`). This file holds
> the hard data behind the *Power 4 commits* and *Division I commits* Compare rows
> (`p4-commits-2426`, `d1-commits-2426` in `src/data/metricValues.ts`) and the College
> Pipeline card (`pipeline` in `src/data/sportsPrograms/davidson-day.ts`).
>
> **Standard applied:** every row is confirmed on the **college's own roster** (which also
> prints the athlete's high school), on a school-published list, or on two independent
> published sources. Recruiting databases and trackers are leads, never the confirmation.
> **Power 4 = SEC / Big Ten / ACC / Big 12** only. Walk-ons, Division II and Division III
> count; "scholarship" is never claimed.
>
> **Graduate test:** an athlete counts only if they finished high school at Davidson Day:
> on a Davidson Day roster in their senior season (MaxPreps), and listed by the college with
> Davidson Day as last school. Every row below passes both, except where noted.
>
> **What changed in the rebuild:** five athletes added — three in the Class of 2024, which the
> school never published a list for (Neace, Zulman, Perry), and two Class of 2025 athletes
> outside the school's November 2024 signing post (Jacobs, Laatsch); one row
> corrected (**Mason Smith signed with Furman, not Mercer**). Totals go from 16 / 11 D1 / 5 P4
> to **21 / 13 D1 / 5 P4**.
