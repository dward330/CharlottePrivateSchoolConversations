SCHOOL** with a **full varsity athletics schedule**. A search for "Trinity Episcopal
athletics" returns exactly the varsity teams, state championships and college commits a
K–8 school lacks — and it is the wrong school.

**Trinity Charlotte fields MIDDLE SCHOOL teams only.** The words `varsity` and `JV` appear
**nowhere** in its material. Teams are fielded **by grade band** (7th/8th, 8th, 6th/7th),
never by A/B or varsity/JV. Any varsity or state-championship athletics result attributed
to "Trinity Episcopal" belongs to Richmond until proven otherwise on `tescharlotte.org`.

## ⚠️⚠️ CRITICAL NEGATIVE — Trinity is NOT in the GCMSAA

**Trinity does not compete in the league the eight big Charlotte independents play in**,
and this is the most natural wrong assumption available.

**Independent corroboration**, from Charlotte Country Day's own
"2018-2019 Middle School Athletics Information Sheet" (PDF), verbatim:

> "**Greater Charlotte Middle School Athletics Association:** All teams participate in
> the GCMSAA, which is a conference that brings together schools with a like-minded
> approach to middle school athletics. **Schools include: Cannon, Charlotte Christian,
> Charlotte Country Day, Charlotte Latin, Covenant Day, Holy Trinity, Lake Norman
> Charter, and Providence Day.** Teams will occasionally compete against schools outside
> this conference grouping to ensure a more complete schedule."

**8 of 8 members named. Trinity Episcopal is absent.**

And the positive side of the same fact: **Trinity competes in the Queen City Conference
(QCC)**, named twice in its own blog.

> **Never describe Trinity as competing against the peer schools already in this app.**

Two caveats carried honestly: the Country Day sheet is **2018-19**, so it establishes the
GCMSAA roster historically rather than for 2026-27; and it notes teams "occasionally
compete against schools outside this conference grouping", so an isolated fixture against
a peer is not impossible. Neither weakens the standing rule.
