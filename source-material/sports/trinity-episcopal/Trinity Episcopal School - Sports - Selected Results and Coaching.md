## Fall 2023 — four sports

From "Congrats to Trinity's Fall Sports Teams", **November 03, 2023**, by Chris Miller.
All verbatim:

> "The **7th/8th Grade boys soccer team won the Queen City Conference title and tournament
> championship**."

> "The **8th Grade volleyball team tied for first** in the conference."

> "**Girls cross country was conference champion for the 4th year running, ranked first
> among independent schools in North Carolina, and had 7 runners named all-conference**."

> "**Boys cross country placed third** in the conference, had **3 runners in the top 15**
> in the championship, and had **1 runner named all-conference**."

> "We're also proud of our Wildcats on the **6th/7th Grade volleyball and soccer teams**,
> and the girls tennis team."

**The girls cross country line is the strongest single result Trinity publishes** — a
four-year conference title run plus a statewide independent-school ranking.

> ⚠️ Quote the school's own wording: "**ranked first among independent schools in North
> Carolina**", not "#1 among NC independent schools".

## Teams are fielded by GRADE BAND, not A/B or varsity/JV

Evidenced directly by the two posts: **7th/8th Grade** boys soccer, **8th Grade**
volleyball, **6th/7th Grade** volleyball and soccer, **8th Grade** boys basketball.

This is a structural fact about a K–8 athletics programme and should be stated — it is
what makes a record table inapplicable in the first place.

## Coaching — a twenty-year tenure

**David Martin**, Athletic Director **and head basketball coach**, verbatim:

> "**'For 20 daggum years, I have thought about this moment pretty much nonstop,' Martin
> said.**"

> "'I can't explain how much energy has gone into trying to win this tournament
> championship over the last 20 years,' said Coach and Athletic Director David Martin."

> "**Martin has helmed the basketball team since its beginning in 2004 when he joined
> Trinity. By his count, some 250 students have played on the 8th Grade team in that
> time** and there have been many close brushes with the championship win."

> ⚠️ **"By his count, some 250 students"** — the hedge is the school's own. Carry it;
> do not present ~250 as a counted figure.

**Assistant Coach Jason Martin**, "another longtime Trinity staculty member."

A detail worth keeping for texture: the team presented the two coaches with **a clay
statue of themselves, made by team members in art class**, after the victory.
