# Charlotte Latin School — Sports — College Commitments (2024–2026)

> **Provenance:** First compiled by Claude Code on 2026-07-04 from five clshawks.com
> signing-day posts; **rebuilt 2026-11-08** by the `/athlete-commits` graduate refresh
> (method, nulls and unchecked gaps in
> `Charlotte Latin - Sports - Varsity Roster Cross-Reference 2026.md`). This is the
> designated provenance file for the *Power 4 commits* and *Division I commits* Compare rows
> (`src/data/metricValues.ts`) and for the College Pipeline card
> (`src/data/sportsPrograms/charlotte-latin.ts`). Level classification is by hand:
> **Power 4 = SEC / Big Ten / ACC / Big 12** only. Counts are distinct athletes.
>
> **What the rebuild found.** Charlotte Latin holds **three** collegiate-commitment events
> each school year (November signing day, February, late April) and publishes a named post
> for each on clshawks.com. The first version of this file used five of the nine posts in
> the window; it had neither Class of 2024 bookend (November 2023, April 2024) nor either
> April event after that. With all nine posts the school's own class totals are
> **16 (2024) · 22 (2025) · 25 (2026)**, the last a school record ("the most of any
> graduating group since at least 2008"). The earlier version also printed "Not published"
> for ten sports that the posts do name.
>
> **Standard applied (same as Cannon, Davidson Day, Charlotte Country Day):** the card counts
> documented commitments to college athletics at any level. D3 counts, a walk-on counts.
> "Scholarship" is never claimed. College dance and cheer are counted **in the funnel total
> only** — not as roster rows and never in Division I / Power 4 — because neither is an
> NCAA-sponsored sport (the Charlotte Country Day Louisville-cheer precedent, user decision
> 2026-11-08).
>
> **Graduate test:** an athlete counts for Latin only if they finished high school there.
> Evidence: the school's own signing post (seniors only), the MaxPreps senior roster for the
> athlete's last season, and the college roster's "High School / Last School" line.
>
> **Confirmation rule:** every row is named in a school signing post. Every Division I and
> Power 4 row added by the rebuild is additionally confirmed on the college's own roster or
> signing release. Where a college's entry-year roster does not list a school-announced
> commit, the athlete is excluded (Massardo, below).
