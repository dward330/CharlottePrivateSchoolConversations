## 7. National Stage & NIL — card omitted, and why

Country Day has **one** national marker: MaxPreps ranked field hockey **#22 nationally** and
#2 in NC for 2025–26.
https://www.maxpreps.com/nc/charlotte/charlotte-country-day-school-buccaneers/field-hockey/25-26/

**But that figure is contested by the sport's own authority** — MAX Field Hockey's final
national top 25 contains **no North Carolina team at all**.
https://maxfh.longstreth.com/2025-final-national-top-25-rankings/

No other Country Day program holds a national ranking, the schedule is almost entirely
in-state (Savannah Country Day GA is the only confirmed out-of-state opponent), and **no
named Country Day NIL deal exists** in public sources. One contested data point cannot carry
a card, so **1g is omitted** and the field hockey ranking is noted on card 1b instead.
