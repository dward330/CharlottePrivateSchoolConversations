One portal. Everything runs through FACTS. Set up your FACTS admission account, then submit the application and the application fee from it. Trinity does not use Ravenna. If you have a Ravenna account for another Charlotte school, it does not carry over to this one.

Three different grade splits, and they do not line up. The deadlines break once, at Kindergarten to Grade 1. The test changes twice more — WPPSI-IV in K and Grade 1, the WISC-V in Grades 2 to 4, the ISEE in Grades 5 to 8. And the CAIS consortium administers the testing for K to 4 only; Grades 5 to 8 sit outside it entirely. Check all three against your child’s grade rather than assuming one split covers them.

If you are applying for financial support, contact the Admission Office before you submit. You will get a separate email with next steps. Kindergarten applies two weeks ahead of every other grade, and hears back six weeks earlier.

    Teacher recommendationby Feb 1

    Teacher recommendations are required for all K-8 applicants. The two-recommendation rule — an English teacher and a math teacher — applies to rising 6–8 applicants only, so a Kindergarten applicant is not covered by it. The school does not state how many are required at this age; ask when you apply. Transcripts are required for 1st–8th grade applicants, which does not reach Kindergarten.

  4. 4
    Book the WPPSI-IV — with one psychologist, and one onlyby Feb 1

    Kindergarten and Grade 1 applicants take the WPPSI-IV through the CAIS consortium — seven schools, one shared testing arrangement. Book with one psychologist only, at $300, paid to them rather than to Trinity. Booking opens the day after Labor Day, and payment by credit card or Zelle is due within 24 hours. Your child must be four or older at testing, which may be done only once in twelve months. Every cancellation costs a $100 nonrefundable fee, and canceling within 72 hours of the appointment gets no refund. CAIS also requires an interview with each child, usually November–February. Results go to you and to the schools you choose.

  5. 5
    A morning observation sessionfrom November

    Applicants are invited beginning in November. A rising Kindergartner attends a morning observation session rather than a full day. The full day on campus is for rising 1st–8th graders.

  6. 6
    Decision → the Follow-Up ConferenceFri Feb 26, 2027

    Decisions are emailed on Friday, February 26, 2027. Under the CAIS common reply date, no CAIS school requires a contract before Friday, March 5, 2027. Trinity invites every family who completes the application process, not only admitted ones, "to meet with the Admission team to share what we have learned about their child through the process, and help the family discern how Trinity Episcopal School can best partner with the family in support of their child."

Coaching or re-testing invalidates the application at EVERY CAIS school

"Psychologists will report to the schools any indication that a child has been coached, tutored, or re-tested. Any indication of the aforementioned will invalidate your application to any CAIS school." Not just Trinity — all seven: Charlotte Christian, Charlotte Country Day, Charlotte Latin, Charlotte Preparatory, Covenant Day, Providence Day and Trinity Episcopal. You sign a Test Validity Verification Form at the psychologist’s office.

Running in parallel: the financial-aid clock

Prospective families apply for financial support by February 1, 2027 — the same day Kindergarten supporting materials are due. If you plan to apply for aid, contact the Admission Office before you submit your application. The aid application runs online through Clarity and takes about 20 minutes. Note too that the $300 CAIS testing fee goes to the psychologist, not to Trinity, so it falls due before any aid decision.

The grade-by-grade service ladder

Kindergarten and grade 1 partner with Nourish Up. Kindergarten delivers Meals on Wheels weekly, and grade 1 visits the food pantry at First Presbyterian Church and leads the school food drive each November. Grade 2 works with InReach, whose customers visit Trinity monthly for gardening, art, music and self-advocacy. Grade 3 partners with Roof Above / the Urban Ministry Center. Grade 4 partners with the Children of the World Learning Center, learning how preschoolers develop and how bilingual education works. Grade 5 partners with Galilee Ministries of East Charlotte. Grade 6 — "Feeding Our Neighbors" — works with the 8th Street Friendship Garden, the Charlotte Community ToolBank and NourishUp. Grade 7 — "Understanding Our Neighbors" — works with Crisis Assistance Ministry, Hope Haven and the First Ward Child Development Center.
