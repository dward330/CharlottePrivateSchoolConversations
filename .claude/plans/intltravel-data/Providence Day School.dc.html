AVERAGE IS NOT MEDIAN
Every award figure the school publishes is an average grant. Aid awards carry a long upper tail, so an average usually sits above what a typical recipient receives; no median is published in any edition located, so a family cannot tell how far above.
A UNIT CHANGE THAT BLOCKS COMPARISON
The historical editions count students receiving aid (16%); the current page counts families (≈21%). Different units, different bases — the apparent 16% → 21% movement cannot be read as growth in reach. This report declines to reconcile them.
An internal discrepancy, reported not resolved
The 2012–13 Report states $3,105,191 as aid awarded on one page and $3,092,165 as the aid expense line a page later — a $13,026 difference. Likeliest explanation: different accounting bases; the school does not say, and inferring it would be a guess.
How aid is funded
Operating funds + endowment income. 2017–18: aid and discounts were 9.7% of expenses against a stated $40.6M budget; 5% of Annual Fund gifts designated to assistance. 2012–13: a 9.3% share (our arithmetic). Form 990 Schedules I & D not retrieved.
