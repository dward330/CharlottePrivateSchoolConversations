Where They Go

Sixteen documented trips abroad since 2022 — Round Square conferences, service builds, language homestays and science expeditions, filterable by division.

Trips run from one-week conferences to summer immersions, with groups of 2 to 12 students. Upper School offers "faculty-led spring break and summer travel options"; Middle School students "may take advantage of opportunities for world travel, including a Middle School conference with Round Square and a summer PDS trip."

The Global Studies Diploma

An optional second diploma for grades 9–12 — global coursework, four years of one language, travel or hosting, and a senior capstone in Global Leadership.

"An optional second diploma that recognizes students who pursue advanced global learning throughout their Upper School experience." Students "typically begin their GSD journey in 9th grade" and "can join later in Upper School under certain circumstances."

1e

Round Square & the Wider Network

A Round Square Global Member feeding international conferences, service Big Builds and virtual dialogues — taught by faculty who travel for a certificate of their own.

Round Square is a network of "280+ like-minded schools in 50 countries on six continents." In 2019 a Southern Association of Independent Schools accreditation committee called Providence Day "a national leader in the field of Global Education."

Among Charlotte private schools, Providence Day stands out for its credential (the GSD), its Round Square network and the breadth of its reciprocal exchanges.

A named second diploma, running since the mid-2000s, with clear published requirements
Eight exchange partners, with durations and reciprocity documented year by year
