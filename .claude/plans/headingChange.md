# Reword the home hero eyebrow and footer disclaimer to "independent research"

## Goal

Two pieces of site chrome describe the project as *parent* research. Both should describe
it as **independent** research instead.

1. The home page hero eyebrow — rendered uppercase as
   `CHARLOTTE PRIVATE SCHOOLS · PARENT RESEARCH` — becomes
   `CHARLOTTE PRIVATE SCHOOLS · INDEPENDENT RESEARCH`. The `·` separator stays.
2. The site footer disclaimer — `Parent-facing research on Charlotte-area private
   schools. …` — becomes `Independent research on Charlotte-area private schools. …`,
   with the rest of the sentence unchanged.

Done when both strings read the new way in English **and** in all nine other locale
catalogs, with the meaning shift (audience → provenance) carried through rather than
transliterated.

## Context

Both strings are **UI chrome**, not research prose — they live in the
`src/locales/*.json` catalogs and are reached with `useTranslation()`. Neither touches
the `src/data/**` overlay layer, so **no overlay, hash, or extraction work is involved.**

**String 1 — the hero eyebrow.** Key `home.eyebrow`, rendered at
[Home.tsx:38](src/pages/Home.tsx#L38):

```jsx
<p className="eyebrow">{t('home.eyebrow')}</p>
```

The English value is lowercase in the catalog —
`"Charlotte private schools · parent research"`. **The all-caps in the screenshot is
CSS, not data:** `.eyebrow { text-transform: uppercase; … }` at
[index.css:628](src/index.css#L628). Keep the catalog value in sentence case; do not
type it in caps.

The `·` (U+00B7 MIDDLE DOT) is a literal character inside the string in all ten
catalogs. Preserve it, with a space either side, exactly as today.

`.eyebrow` also has a script-specific override at
[index.css:4786](src/index.css#L4786) for `bn`, `te`, `fa`, `hi`, `ar` — untouched by
this change, but it is why the non-Latin catalogs must stay well-formed.

**String 2 — the footer disclaimer.** Key `nav.footerDisclaimer`, rendered at
[App.tsx:109](src/App.tsx#L109):

```jsx
<p>{t('nav.footerDisclaimer')}</p>
```

English value today:

> `Parent-facing research on Charlotte-area private schools. Notes are distilled,
> lightly-cleaned extracts of source documents — verify specifics with each school.`

Only the **first sentence's opening phrase** changes. The second sentence (`Notes are
distilled, …`) and the em-dash clause stay exactly as they are.

**Consumers — verified exhaustively.** `grep -rn "home.eyebrow\|footerDisclaimer"` over
`src/`, `scripts/` and `index.html` returns exactly the two JSX lines above plus the
catalog entries. In particular:

- **No SEO or meta consumer.** `src/lib/head.ts`, `scripts/seo_routes.mjs` and
  `scripts/prerender.mjs` contain no reference to either key or to the word "parent".
  Meta descriptions are composed from school and topic names, so the ≤160-char budget
  `check:seo` enforces is unaffected.
- The strings **do** appear in `dist/` (`dist/index.html`, the JS bundle) purely because
  the pre-render bakes the rendered home page. That is regenerated by `npm run build`;
  nothing needs hand-editing there.
- `src/lib/manifest.ts:96` has "Parent-facing" in a **code comment** only. Out of scope.

**Locale scope.** `TRANSLATED` in [i18n.ts:108](src/lib/i18n.ts#L108) is
`['en', 'es', 'bn', 'ht', 'te', 'fr', 'fa', 'it', 'hi', 'ar']` — **ten** catalogs, all
of which already carry both keys with real translations. Read that list at implement
time rather than trusting this count.

**The current non-English wording is genuinely audience-framed, not a neutral phrase.**
This is the crux of Phase 2 and the reason it is not find-and-replace. Today's values:

| Locale | `home.eyebrow` | `nav.footerDisclaimer` (opening) |
|---|---|---|
| `en` | `Charlotte private schools · parent research` | `Parent-facing research on …` |
| `es` | `Escuelas privadas de Charlotte · investigación para familias` | `Investigación dirigida a las familias sobre …` |
| `fr` | `Écoles privées de Charlotte · recherche pour les parents` | `Recherche destinée aux parents sur …` |
| `it` | `Scuole private di Charlotte · ricerca per le famiglie` | `Ricerca rivolta alle famiglie sulle …` |
| `ht` | `Lekòl prive Charlotte · rechèch pou paran` | `Rechèch pou paran sou …` |
| `bn` | `Charlotte-এর বেসরকারি স্কুল · অভিভাবকদের গবেষণা` | `শার্লট এলাকার বেসরকারি স্কুল নিয়ে অভিভাবকদের জন্য গবেষণা।` |
| `te` | `Charlotte ప్రైవేట్ పాఠశాలలు · తల్లిదండ్రుల పరిశోధన` | `… తల్లిదండ్రుల కోసం రూపొందించిన పరిశోధన.` |
| `hi` | `Charlotte के निजी स्कूल · अभिभावकों के लिए शोध` | `… अभिभावकों के लिए शोध।` |
| `fa` | `مدارس خصوصی شارلوت · پژوهش والدین` | `پژوهشی رو به والدین دربارهٔ …` |
| `ar` | `المدارس الخاصة في شارلوت · بحث لأولياء الأمور` | `بحث موجَّه لأولياء الأمور حول …` |

Every one says *for parents / aimed at families*. "Independent research" says something
different — it describes **who produced it and their lack of affiliation**, not who it is
for. A translator who swaps only the noun will produce nonsense (e.g. Spanish
`investigación independiente para familias` still says "for families"). Phase 2 must
**replace the whole audience phrase** with a provenance phrase.

**The word carries a live collision in this repo.** "Independent school" is the standard
US term for a private school, and this project's own source material uses it
(`CHARLOTTE-AREA INDEPENDENT SCHOOLS · FINANCIAL AID & TUITION`). In English the eyebrow
reads unambiguously because *independent* modifies *research*. In a language where the
adjective follows the noun, a careless rendering can attach it to *schools* — e.g. French
`écoles privées … recherche indépendante` is fine, but any construction that lets
*indépendante* drift next to *écoles* is wrong. Flag this explicitly to the translator.

**The nearest analogous prior work** is [shared-role-labels](shared-role-labels.md) — a
small, chrome-only, multi-locale wording change. Follow its shape: English first, one
commit, then the locales in a second commit on the same branch and PR.

## Decisions

- **Two phases, not one** — the change is user-facing text. Per CLAUDE.md this is
  standing behaviour: English lands and the user reviews the wording rendered before any
  other locale is touched.
- **Chrome layer only** (`src/locales/*.json`), not the overlay layer — both keys are
  `useTranslation()` chrome. `PROSE_TRANSLATED`, `i18n_build_overlay.mjs`, work files and
  content hashes are all untouched.
- **Keep the eyebrow value in sentence case** — the caps are `text-transform` in CSS.
  Typing it in caps would double-apply visually as no-op but break the pattern every
  other locale follows and look wrong anywhere the transform is overridden.
- **Keep `·` with its surrounding spaces** — the user explicitly asked for the dot; it is
  a literal U+00B7 in the string, not CSS.
- **Change only the opening phrase of the footer disclaimer** — the user quoted
  `"Parent-facing research on Charlotte-area…."` with an ellipsis, i.e. the rest of the
  sentence carries on unchanged. Do not restyle the `Notes are distilled…` clause.
- **Phase 2 replaces the audience phrase wholesale, per locale** — a noun swap leaves
  "for families" in place and inverts the meaning. See the table in Context.
- **No new check script** — `npm run check:chrome` already asserts every chrome key
  exists in all ten catalogs, which is exactly the failure mode here (a locale left
  behind). Nothing new to build.
- **Do not touch the `src/lib/manifest.ts:96` comment** — it is a code comment about
  metric labels, unrelated to this copy.

## Approvals needed

### Phase 2 — Every other locale

Only after that confirmation. Scope is the nine non-`en` files in `src/locales/` per
`TRANSLATED` in [i18n.ts:108](src/lib/i18n.ts#L108) — read the list from the file, do
not trust a hardcoded count. **This is chrome; the overlay layer and
`PROSE_TRANSLATED` are not involved.**

1. **Re-read the current values** for `home.eyebrow` and `nav.footerDisclaimer` in each
   of `es`, `bn`, `ht`, `te`, `fr`, `fa`, `it`, `hi`, `ar` before editing, so each new
   value keeps that locale's existing register and sentence shape. The Context table
   records what they were at planning time.

2. **Translate the meaning, not the noun.** In each locale, replace the
   *audience* phrase (`para familias`, `pour les parents`, `అభిభావకుల కోసం`, …) with a
   *provenance* phrase meaning **independent / unaffiliated research**. The eyebrow's
   second segment and the disclaimer's opening clause both change; the rest of each
   disclaimer sentence stays as-is.

3. **Guard the "independent school" collision.** In every locale, confirm the adjective
   attaches to *research*, not to *schools* — the term "independent school" is a live
   synonym for private school in this domain, and the eyebrow names both nouns. Prefer a
   construction where the two cannot be confused.

4. **Preserve per-locale mechanics.** Keep the `·` separator and its spacing in all nine
   eyebrows. Keep each locale's own terminal punctuation (`।` for `bn`/`hi`, `.` for the
   Latin scripts, Arabic-script punctuation for `fa`/`ar`). No figures, currency or
   numerals are involved in either string, so the figure/sepdrift/lakh-crore rules do not
   bite here — but the strings must remain single-line JSON with no stray bidi marks.
   For `fa` and `ar` (RTL), type the string RTL-natively; do not paste a
   left-to-right-assembled fragment.

5. **Run the Phase 2 verification below.**

6. **Commit** the locale pass as a second commit on the same branch, and open **one PR**
   covering both phases, per CLAUDE.md.

Mechanism reference if anything about the chrome layer is unclear:
[`prose-translation-architecture.md`](.claude/docs/prose-translation-architecture.md)

## Implementation notes

Shipped as PR #177 (both phases, one PR). Built as planned; three things worth recording.

**`check:spans` passed, contrary to the Verification section.** The plan told the
implementer to expect it red on pre-existing gaston-day findings and to confirm the
failures were the same ones. They are gone — fixed on `main` by the #174/#176 work that
landed between planning and implementation. The whole `npm run build` chain exits 0, so
there is no expected-red caveat left on this branch.

**Per-locale renderings, and how the "independent school" collision was guarded.** Step 3
asked for a construction where the adjective cannot attach to *schools*. In every case
grammatical agreement or word order settles it rather than word placement alone:

| Locale | eyebrow second segment | why it cannot attach to *schools* |
|---|---|---|
| `es` | `investigación independiente` | fem. sing.; `escuelas` is fem. **pl.** |
| `fr` | `recherche indépendante` | fem. sing.; `écoles` is fem. **pl.** |
| `it` | `ricerca indipendente` | sing.; `scuole` is pl. |
| `ht` | `rechèch endepandan` | adjective directly follows its noun |
| `bn` | `স্বতন্ত্র গবেষণা` | adjective precedes its noun |
| `hi` | `स्वतंत्र शोध` | adjective precedes its noun |
| `te` | `స్వతంత్ర పరిశోధన` | adjective precedes its noun |
| `fa` | `پژوهش مستقل` | ezāfe binds the adjective to `پژوهش` |
| `ar` | `بحث مستقل` | indef. masc. sing.; `المدارس` is def. fem. pl. |

Two footers needed more than a phrase swap because their opening clause was structurally
audience-framed rather than merely containing an audience noun: `te`
(`తల్లిదండ్రుల కోసం రూపొందించిన పరిశోధన`, "research designed *for parents*") became
`స్వతంత్రంగా చేసిన పరిశోధన` ("research carried out *independently*"), and `fa`
(`پژوهشی رو به والدین`, "research *directed at* parents") became `پژوهشی مستقل`. Both
second sentences are byte-identical to before.

**One cosmetic finding, deliberately left.** At 375px the **English** eyebrow now wraps to
two lines, breaking inside `INDEPENDENT RESEARCH` rather than at the `·`, because
"independent" is four characters longer than "parent". Phase 1's verification asked for
"no wrap mid-phrase at mobile width", so this is a miss against the letter of that check —
but it is a property of the English wording the user reviewed and approved at the gate, and
every other locale stays on one line at that width. Fixing it means a CSS change (a
`white-space` rule on the segments, or a smaller mobile `font-size`), which this plan puts
out of scope. Recorded here so a later pass can pick it up as a decision rather than
rediscover it as a bug.
