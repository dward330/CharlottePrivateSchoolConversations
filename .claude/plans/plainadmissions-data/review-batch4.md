repeat-group rules, Decisions and Style rulings. It made **5 findings, all fixed** (one is a
4-site repeat group):

1. `guide.bands[0].steps[1].detail`: the school's description became our own claim after the
   split. Fix: one sentence again, "The school describes TK as a bridge between preschool and
   kindergarten that gives a child time to build the basic skills kindergarten expects."
2. `guide.bands[0].steps[4].detail`: the referent was ambiguous. "it settles" could mean
   "qualifying". Fix: "Along with the birthday cutoffs, the assessment settles whether your
   child is placed in TK or K5."
3. `guide.bands[1].steps[2].detail`: a requirement was weakened. "The file also includes"
   reads as description, not a requirement. Fix: "The file also needs, **for fourth and
   fifth graders only, a Student Questionnaire**." The bold is on the same words.
4. `guide.bands[3].steps[3].detail`: who arranges the shadow day changed. The family
   requests it; the school arranges it. Fix: "You request it by completing a shadow request
   form."
5. The family-interview repeat group (`bands[0].steps[5]`, `bands[1].steps[4]`,
   `bands[2].steps[4]`, `bands[3].steps[5]`): "assess whether Hickory Grove is the right fit"
   had stopped being something the school says. Fix: "The school says it is also a chance to
   assess whether Hickory Grove is the right fit for your family." Applied identically at
   all 4 sites.
