## Independent review

A fresh `general-purpose` subagent read every row against the plan's "must survive" list,
Decisions and Style rulings. It made **2 findings, both fixed**:

1. `guide.bands[0].steps[1].detail`: the school's term was paraphrased. The school assesses
   "developmental readiness", and its Admissions FAQs list that as a criterion, but the
   rewrite had dropped "developmental". Fix: "It weighs the birthday alongside an individual
   assessment of your child's developmental readiness: social, emotional, physical and
   academic."
2. `guide.bands[0].steps[3].detail`: the records-release sentence was weaker than in bands 1
   and 2. "note that the release is broad" never said that entering the email grants the
   release. Fix: "If you enter a registrar's email to ask for records, you give permission
   for a broad release." Band 0 keeps its extra "so enter those carefully", which carries
   the school's "provide an accurate email address", found only in band 0's original.

It tried a third fix and reverted it. That fix would have restored "actively involved"
(the school's wording) in the `·` cell `comparison.rows[9]`, but both variants pushed the cell
over grade 10 (10.2 and 10.5). "a Christ-follower who is active in a local Christian church"
is accepted as equivalent, since the exact phrase stays in all three interview units.

Borderline cases it accepted:
- "late applicant" for "non-priority applicant", which matches the school's own definition.
- "a deadline for submitting scores" for "a submission deadline", since the bold rule just
  before it quotes the full requirement.
- "There is also one step you must complete first", since the bold sentence that follows
  names the step.

It confirmed:
- every condition is intact ("if space allows", "until priority decisions are complete",
  "unless space remains after April 9", "at least one parent", "should be four years old");
- the school's spellings (Weschler, WPPSi, WISC V) are unchanged;
- the three interview openings are identical, and the two ISEE units agree;
- the six-step chain is in order, and no bold appears outside the markdown fields.
