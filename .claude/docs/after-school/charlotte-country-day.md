Fees & Fine Print
• Program serves grades JK-8; drop-in requests are welcome.
• Contracted enrollment: $80 registration fee (due at enrollment; first month's service included in August billing); monthly or annual contracts for 1-5 days/week.
• Non-contracted drop-in care: $19/hour (one-hour minimum) plus a one-time $40 registration fee (distinct from the $80 contract fee).
• Late pick-up after 6:00 p.m.: $10 per 10-minute interval (or fraction thereof).
