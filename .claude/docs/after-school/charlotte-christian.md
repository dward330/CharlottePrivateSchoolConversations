Lower School Extended Day (JK-4)
Provides care beyond the regular school day for JK-grade 4 students. Through organized activities and
supervised free time, it is designed to enrich intellectual, spiritual, social, and physical development.
Classroom space, craft materials, games, and outdoor play facilities are available for homework, play,
and other activities.
Middle School Extended Day
To increase after-school accountability and safety, MS students remaining on campus after 3:15 p.m.
are expected to be in a supervised activity (athletic practice, musical rehearsal), working with a teacher,
or enrolled in extended day. Extended day offers a supervised space to complete homework and
engage with friends.
