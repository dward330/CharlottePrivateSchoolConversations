## Card — Verdict & Visit Checklist

Strengths
- Long, uniform coverage: 3:00–6:00 pm every regular day across the whole JrK–8 span, with
 no age-based cutoff and no split Lower/Middle program.
- Early-dismissal days are covered, 11:45 am–4:00 pm, which many peer programs do not do.
- True drop-in at $11.50/hour with no contract — unusually flexible. A family needing two
 afternoons a month pays roughly $100/year instead of committing to $1,904.
- A sharp price ladder: the 5-day rate ($3,784) is only about 3.7× the 1-day rate, so
 heavy users get a real discount. Five-day ASP is about 13% of JrK tuition.
- Deep and genuinely varied enrichment — 22 distinct classes in a single session spanning
 robotics, ballet, chess, pottery, golf, photography and an executive-function class for
 Middle School ("Let's Get Organized"), with real outside vendors.
- Beyond the Bell pricing is modest and transparent: $18–$30 per class, mostly $144–$300
 for a 10-week session.

Watch-outs
- Zero after-school coverage for grades 9–12.
- Pricing is effectively hidden. The rate card exists only on an orphaned backup URL,
 while the live page, the tuition page and the official fee PDF all omit it and price
 everything else. A prospective family doing normal research will not find it.
- The published rates are 2025-26; expect an increase for 2026-27.
- The $1/minute late fee is steep and applies to drop-in families too — 20 minutes late is
 $20, nearly double the hourly rate.
- Beyond the Bell and ASP are separate purchases. A 3:00–4:00 enrichment class does not
 cover you to 6:00, so costs stack.
- Two names for one program (ASP and ASC) across official documents suggests the program
 documentation is not tightly maintained.
- The Middle School 3:15 auto-enrolment rule can bill a family into paid care without an
 opt-in.
- No published daily rhythm and no staff-to-child ratios anywhere.
- Teacher workdays and holidays are unaddressed.
- No before-school care as a paid program — only free arrival from 7:30 am.

Visit checklist
- What are the 2026-27 ASP rates, and where are they published for prospective families?
- Is there ASP coverage on teacher workdays, conference days and school holidays, and at
 what rate?
- What is the actual daily rhythm — how much homework time, what is the staff-to-child
 ratio, and does it differ for JrK versus Grade 8?
- Is the $25 registration-fee waiver deadline repeating for 2026-27?
- How is ASP billed — monthly, per semester, or annually up front? Can we switch tiers
 mid-year?
- Are there sibling discounts, or any financial aid applicable to ASP or Beyond the Bell?
- If my child does a 3:00–4:00 Beyond the Bell class, am I charged ASP for 4:00–6:00, and
 does a BTB day count against my contracted ASP days?
- In Middle School, does the 3:15 auto-enrolment to ASC trigger a drop-in charge, and how
 are we notified?
- What happens for grades 9–12 — is there any supervised space, study hall, or activity
 bus after 3:00?
- On early-dismissal days, does ASP end firmly at 4:00 pm, and what is the late fee then?
