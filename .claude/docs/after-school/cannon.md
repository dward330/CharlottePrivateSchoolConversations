After School at Cannon School
Cannon School (JrK-12) - Concord, NC - Program Overview
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school after-school programs. Prepared July 2026. Hours, ages, fees, and
registration details change yearly - verify with the school before relying on them.
At a Glance
Cannon's After School Program (ASP) serves students in grades JrK-8, providing a safe, nurturing
environment after the academic day. A separate Beyond the Bell Enrichment Program adds
structured enrichment offerings, and Camp Cannon covers the summer.
The program is overseen within Cannon Life; Jordan Lauher serves as Enrichment Programs Assistant
Director. Registration and details are handled via the Family Portal (Veracross) or
afterschool@cannonschool.org.
What Students Do
(cid:127) Homework assistance in a supervised setting.
(cid:127) Sports and active play.
(cid:127) Art activities and cooking.
(cid:127) Nature exploration, including cultivating and observing the school garden.
Philosophy
Cannon frames ASP as an extension of its "adaptive experts" mission - partnering with families,
community, faculty, and staff so that experiences foster each child's individual, developmental, social,
and emotional needs.
