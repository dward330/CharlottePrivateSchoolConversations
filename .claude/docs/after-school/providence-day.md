Middle School Clubhouse
Clubhouse, the Middle School after-school program, is drop-in: Monday-Friday, 3:10-6:00 p.m., starting the first day of school. Cost is a $100 annual registration fee per student; no per-day or hourly rates are published.
Fees & Fine Print
• Extended Day annual registration fee: $80.
• Late pick-up: $1.00 per minute after 6:00 p.m., charged to the monthly bill.

• Semester enrichment classes carry a separate, unpublished fee (pro-rated only if a student withdraws after one class).
