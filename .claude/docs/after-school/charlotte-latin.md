After School at Charlotte Latin School
Charlotte Latin School (TK-12) - Charlotte, NC - Program Overview
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school after-school programs. Prepared July 2026. Hours, ages, fees, and
registration details change yearly - verify with the school before relying on them.
At a Glance
Charlotte Latin frames its after-school time around "more time for more curiosity," with three
combinable programs: Hawks' Club (daily after-school care), Enrichment Programs (session-based
classes), and the Talons youth recreational sports program.
The Three Programs
Hawks' Club
Daily after-school care for TK-Grade 5, Monday-Friday, 1:30-6:00 p.m. with flexible end times.
Structured and unstructured play, hands-on arts and crafts, and a range of after-school clubs (from
Math Olympiad to Sumo-Bots). Register via CampBrain.
Enrichment Programs
Session-based classes for TK-Grade 5 taught by professional instructors on specific days per session.
Register via CampBrain; can be combined with Hawks' Club.
Talons Sports
Youth recreational sports for Grades 3-6 on specific days per session. Register via TeamSnap; can be
combined with Hawks' Club.
Registration Notes
All three can be combined. 2026-27 Hawks' Club registration opens Thursday, May 14 at 8:00 p.m.;
Enrichment and Talons open per session. (Dates change yearly - verify with the school.)
