Pricing structure observations (analysis of published values, not published claims)
- Grades 1–5 and the TK/K 1:30–2:55 and 1:30–6:00 rows are perfectly linear — the N-day
 rate is exactly N × the 1-day rate. No volume discount.
- The TK/K 1:30–4:30 pm row is the sole exception. Linear would give 4-day $2,540 and
 5-day $3,175; published is 4-day $2,300 and 5-day $2,540. So the 5-day price equals
 what 4 days should cost. This is either a genuine volume discount on the most popular
 tier or a typo on Latin's page — a $635/semester question. The prior research pass
 captured the same two values, so the anomaly is stable across two collection dates,
 which favours "real discount" over "transient typo."
- The TK/K 1:30–2:55 tier is effectively paid coverage of the gap between TK/K dismissal
 and the Grades 1–5 dismissal: TK/K families pay $1,725/semester ($3,450/yr) just to
 reach the same 2:55 hour that Grade 1 families get free.

## Card — Verdict & Visit Checklist

Strengths
- A dedicated after-school building, not a repurposed cafeteria or gym — materially
 better for a child spending three-plus hours there daily.
- Two credentialed adults per room: "a dedicated lead teacher and assistant teacher" per
 classroom is a stated, structural commitment.
- Genuine tier flexibility with honest linear pricing — three real pickup times and 1–5
 day granularity, so a family needing exactly two days to 4:30 pays for exactly that.
- Grades 1–5 pricing is genuinely modest relative to Latin's tuition tier: $295/semester
 for one day a week to 4:30, and $2,950/yr for five-day coverage to 4:30.
- Full-day coverage on teacher workdays and conference days, 7:30 am–4:45 pm, with real
 field trips — this solves the days working parents struggle with most.
- An unusually deep 27-class enrichment catalog with credible outside vendors, spanning
 STEM (FabLab, Sumobots, Atomz Lab), arts (Clayworks, Ukulele, Jewelry) and 8+ sports.
- Talons is a real athletic on-ramp, explicitly designed as the feeder to Hawks varsity.
- Runs first day to last day of school — no gaps at the start or end of the year.
- Activity-bus integration at 4:30 (LS), 5:30 and 6:00 (MS/US), so after-school
 participation doesn't strictly require a car at pickup.
- Summer camp is open to any child ages 3.5–18, with extended care to 5:30 pm — a
 year-round wrapper and a way to try the school before applying.

Watch-outs
- Hard stop at Grade 5. There is no Middle School or Upper School after-school care —
 none. MS students are the most stranded: the LS/MS library closes at dismissal, and the
 only structured options are athletics or clubs. Upper School at least has library
 access to 5:30 pm.
- The TK/K cost cliff is severe. TK/K dismisses at 1:30, Grades 1–5 at 2:55, so TK/K
 families pay $1,725/semester ($3,450/yr) simply to cover the 85 minutes Grade 1
 families get free. Five-day coverage to 6:00 pm for a TK/K child is $9,300/year.
- No published daily schedule — for a program a child may spend three hours a day in,
 parents cannot see how those hours are structured before enrolling.
- Enrichment is a catalog of names with zero operational detail: 27 class names, but no
 day, no grade range, and no fee for any of them, and the page that once carried that
 grid now 404s.
- No drop-in option is published. The model appears to be pre-registered fixed days
 billed by semester, with no published way to change mid-semester.
- Fee opacity across the board: registration, late pickup, schedule change, cancellation,
 and the non-school-day rate are all unpublished. The summer camp publishes all of these
 equivalents, so the school clearly can be transparent — the school-year program isn't.
- Financial-aid applicability is unstated and the framing is discouraging: Extended Day
 is filed under "other optional costs" alongside transportation.
- Latin's own site contradicts itself on dismissal time (2:55 / 3:05 / 3:10 for Grades
 1–5; 3:20 / 3:25 for MS and US).
- The TK/K 1:30–4:30 row does not compute against the linear pattern every other row
 follows — a $635/semester question.
- Registration opened May 14, 2026 at 8:00 pm; an evening hard-open is the signature of a
 program where popular classes fill in minutes.
- Talons publishes only Fall sessions, so year-round athletic continuity is unconfirmed,
 and its fees are a $200–$350 range rather than a price.
- No published staff-to-child ratio or group size.

Visit checklist
- Is there a Hawks' Club registration fee, and is it per child or per family?
- What is the late-pickup fee after 6:00 pm, and how is it assessed?
- Can we change our days mid-semester, and is there a change fee?
