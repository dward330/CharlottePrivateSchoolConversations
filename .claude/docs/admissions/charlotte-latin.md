## ⚠️ The defining structural fact — NO decision or contract dates, any band

**Charlotte Latin publishes no admission decision date, no notification time, and no
enrollment contract due date — for any of its four bands.** Re-verified 2026-08-31 across
three official pages (Application Process, Tuition & Financial Assistance, Admissions
landing): the phrase does not appear, and every band's published timeline **ends at the
application or all-file-material deadline**.

This is a confirmed absence, not a research gap. Two consequences for the card:

1. **Every band ends one step earlier than Providence Day / Country Day / Charlotte
 Christian**, each of which closes with `Decision → contract + deposit`.
2. **The $2,500 deposit has no decision step to attach to.** It is published on the Tuition
 page as an enrollment condition, not as a dated admissions milestone.

A **prior-cycle** admissions-director blog post states, "Following our January 15 application
deadline, Charlotte Latin School enters rolling admissions." This is **not restated** on the
current Application Process page. Treat it as historical guidance, not confirmed 2026–27
policy — and do not ship it as a current rolling-admissions rule.

## Structure — four bands

The school "serves students in transitional kindergarten through 12th grade" and "naturally
expand[s] at TK and kindergarten and in 6th and 9th grades," but "welcome[s] and encourage[s]
applications for all grade levels," noting that "Applications for different grades have
slightly different processes."

The Application Process page is organized into **exactly four tabs**: TK/Kindergarten, Lower
School, Middle School, Upper School. All four follow the **same four-step spine** — apply
online → attend a Visit Day → complete a standardized assessment → meet the deadlines — and
differ in the assessment used, who schedules the Visit Day, whether an interview is included,
and the deadline.

**Verified 2026-08-31, tab by tab, against the live page.**

## One platform end-to-end

The school "use[s] an online management system for the entire admission process, including
event registrations, application submission, and final decisions." The portal is **Finalsite
Enrollment** (branded "Finalsite SchoolAdmin"), hosted at **charlottelatin.fsenrollment.com**.
Families "first submit an inquiry and then create a profile," after which the account "will
prompt you to RSVP for optional admissions events, submit materials, locate forms, and meet
deadlines."

*(A separate parent/enrolled-family system, Veracross "MyLatin," is used post-enrollment, not
for applying.)*

## BAND 1 — Transitional Kindergarten (TK) & Kindergarten

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Submit an inquiry | REQUIRED first step — "first submit an inquiry and then create a profile for your family" |
| Apply | Create a portal account and complete the application — REQUIRED. **Deadline: January 2, 2027** |
| Attend a Visit Day | REQUIRED; **the admissions office schedules it** for this band — "Attend Visit Day (our office will schedule)". "During a Visit Day, your child will experience the joy of a Latin classroom for a few hours while you meet with current parents and hear from a panel of Upper School students" |
| Standardized assessment | REQUIRED: applicants take the **WPPSI** (refer to the CAIS brochure); tests "need to be scheduled in advance" |
| Optional admissions events | OPTIONAL — open houses, campus tours, virtual sessions; RSVP through the portal. **TK/K Fly By Open House: October 10, 2026, 9:30–11:00 a.m.** |
| Financial aid | OPTIONAL, via **Clarity** — **due January 15, 2027** |
| Enrollment deposit | **$2,500 non-refundable** at enrollment (see the deposit note below) |

**No all-file-material deadline is published for this band** — the tab lists only the
application and financial-aid deadlines.

**Age eligibility / birthday cutoff: NOT PUBLISHED.** The site describes TK only
qualitatively — "Transitional Kindergarten, known as TK, is designed for children that are
newly five and ready for Kindergarten" — and elsewhere says the school works with "children
ages five to eighteen." **Re-verified 2026-08-31:** the Lower School academics page carries
no age requirement, no cutoff date and no readiness criteria. No calendar cutoff date for TK
or Kindergarten appears on any official public page.

**North Carolina's statutory public-school kindergarten cutoff is NOT stated by the school
and is deliberately not attributed to it here.**

**Recommendation forms, parent/student interviews, writing samples: NOT PUBLISHED** as
required for this band (portal-gated).

## (a) Cross-band comparison — exactly what changes

| Element | TK/K | Lower (1–5) | Middle (6–8) | Upper (9–12) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Application deadline | **January 2, 2027** | January 15, 2027 | January 15, 2027 | January 15, 2027 |
| Who schedules the Visit Day | **The admissions office** | The family | The family | The family |
| Standardized assessment | WPPSI | WPPSI (Gr 1) · WISC-V (Gr 2–4) · ISEE (Gr 5) | ISEE | ISEE (Gr 9–11; **Gr 12 not published**) |
| Interview in the Visit Day | Not described | Not described | **Yes** | **Yes** |
| Visit Day character | Child in a Latin classroom; parents meet current parents + an Upper School student panel | Classroom visit | Interview + assessment + class observation | Interview + Student Ambassador + day-in-the-life |
| Testing / all-file deadline | **None published** | **None published** | **February 26, 2027** | **February 26, 2027** |
| Financial aid (Clarity) | January 15, 2027 | January 15, 2027 | January 15, 2027 | January 15, 2027 |
| Decision / contract date | **None published** | **None published** | **None published** | **None published** |

**The TK/Kindergarten deadline is 13 days earlier than every other grade.**

**Assessment routing:** WPPSI/WISC testing is run through the **Charlotte Area Independent
Schools (CAIS)** consortium (families are referred to "the CAIS brochure"); the **ISEE is
developed and administered by the Educational Records Bureau (ERB)**, and families are
referred to the ERB/ISEE website to schedule.

*(CAIS consortium detail — the per-grade instrument, the $300 fee, the four-years-old and
once-per-twelve-months rules, and the coaching rule — is transcribed in
`source-material/admissions/charlotte-country-day/Charlotte Country Day - Admissions - CAIS
Testing Consortium.md`. Charlotte Latin is a listed member school.)*

## (d) Financial aid

- **Platform: Clarity** — create an account at Clarity's sign-up page
 (`app.clarityapp.com/sign-up`). "If you are applying for multiple children, you need only
 one application," and it "typically takes 30 minutes or less to complete."
- **Opens:** "The financial aid application process opens on September 15" (September 15,
 2026 for this cycle).
- **Deadline:** "To be considered for priority round decisions, all financial aid
 applications must be submitted by January 15" — i.e. **January 15, 2027**.
- **Aid application fee: NOT PUBLISHED** on the current Tuition & Financial Assistance page.
 *(A prior-cycle financial-aid presentation referenced a $60 nonrefundable fee tied to the
 former SSS/NAIS platform; the school has since moved to Clarity, and the current page
 states no fee. Do not ship the $60.)*
- **Enrollment deposit: $2,500 non-refundable** — "A non-refundable deposit of $2,500 is
 required at the time of initial enrollment or reenrollment. This amount will be deducted
 from the second tuition payment." Also: "No student can be enrolled or reenrolled without
 this deposit."

**Tuition (2026–27), for provenance only — NOT for the Admissions card:** TK and
Kindergarten $27,600; Grades 1–5 $32,600; Grades 6–8 $35,200; Grades 9–12 $36,500.
