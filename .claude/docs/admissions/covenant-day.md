## Structure — three bands, with Grade 6 a sub-step

Covenant Day is **"a JK-12 Christ-centered college preparatory school"** and **"a ministry of
Christ Covenant Church (PCA)"**, in Matthews, NC. Its own priority-deadline structure and
Admissions Steps split applicants into three bands:

| Band | Grades | Deadline | Screening | On-campus visit | Student interview | Decision | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| JK / K | JK, K | **Jan 2, 2027** | WPPSi | Little Lions Assessment | No | **Feb 26, 2027** 4 p.m. | **Mar 5, 2027** noon |
| Grades 1–5 | 1–5 | **Jan 15, 2027** | WPPSi (gr 1) · WISC V (gr 2–4) · ISEE (gr 5) | One-on-one academic assessment | No | **Apr 9, 2027** 4 p.m. | **Apr 16, 2027** noon |
| Grades 6–11 | 6–11 | **Jan 15, 2027** | ISEE | Shadow day (+ math/English assessment at gr 6) | **Yes** | **Apr 9, 2027** 4 p.m. | **Apr 16, 2027** noon |

**Two boundaries that do not coincide** — the same shape as Charlotte Christian:

- **The deadline breaks at K → Grade 1** (Jan 2 → Jan 15).
- **The screening instrument breaks at Grade 1 → Grade 2** (WPPSi → WISC V) and again at
 **Grade 4 → Grade 5** (WISC V → ISEE).

So the Grades 1–5 band contains **three different instruments**, and its Grade 5 shares the
ISEE with the 6–11 band while keeping the 1–5 on-campus assessment.

**The single biggest structural change is at Grade 5 → 6:** the test stays ISEE, but Grade 6
adds a shadow day and a student interview.

**⚠️ Grade 12 entry is NOT PUBLISHED.** Every published deadline, decision date and testing
reference addresses **"JK/K" and "grades 1-11" only**. The school is JK–12, but 12th-grade
entry is not offered as a published entry point.

## The six published Admissions Steps — VERBATIM

Rendered from `/admissions/process` on 2026-09-01. The accordion tabs are:
**VISIT · APPLY · SCREENING · RECOMMENDATIONS · INTERVIEW/SHADOW · DECISION**

### 1. VISIT — Arrange a Visit

> "We would love to show you our facilities and meet you face to face! To connect with us,
> schedule a personal campus visit below."

### 2. APPLY — Submit Your Application

> "Submit an online application with a non-refundable application fee of $100. Students
> should be four years old by March 1, 2027 to apply for JK. Students should be five years
> old by June 1, 2027 to apply for kindergarten. The Admissions Office considers a child's
> birthday and an individual assessment of developmental readiness when determining placement
> in JK or kindergarten."

### 3. SCREENING — Admissions Screening

> "A standardized admissions screening is one tool used to determine the best placement for
> each student. **These tests need to be scheduled in advance by the parent.**"
>
> - "Grades **JK-1** applicants take the **Weschler Preschool and Primary Scale of
> Intelligence (WPPSi)**. This assessment for younger children is administered by a
> qualified psychologist.*"
> - "Grades **2-4** applicants take the **Weschler Intelligence Scale for Children (WISC V)**.
> This assessment for elementary-age children is administered by a qualified psychologist.*"
> - "Grades **5-11** applicants take the **Independent School Entrance Exam (ISEE)**. This
> standardized assessment is administered to students in upper grades and measures verbal
> and quantitative ability, mathematics skills, reading comprehension, and writing skills.
> Please refer to the ISEE website for details and registration. **All Middle and High
> School testing needs to be completed and submitted by February 26, 2027.**"
>
> "*These tests need to be scheduled directly with the psychologist. Please refer to the CAIS
> brochure for all details and a list of psychologists."

**Note the school's own spellings** — it writes "Weschler" (not Wechsler), "WPPSi" (not
WPPSI-IV) and "WISC V" (not WISC-V). Reproduced verbatim above. *(The CAIS consortium brochure
itself uses WPPSI-IV and WISC-V; see the Country Day CAIS file.)*

### 4. RECOMMENDATIONS — Recommendation Forms

**Fully published, by grade** — a prior pass recorded this as NOT RETRIEVED:

> "To learn more about each student's unique strengths, interests, and challenges, we ask for
> a recommendation from the following people:
>
> - Preschool teacher recommendation (**JK/K**)
> - Lower school teacher recommendation (**grades 1-5**)
> - Math teacher recommendation (**grades 6-11**)
> - English teacher recommendation (**grades 6-11**)
> - Principal/school counselor recommendation (**grades 6-11**)
> - Pastor's recommendation (**grades JK-11**)"

> "Teachers, principals, and pastors may complete recommendation forms online. Please provide
> an accurate email address in the application so that we may send the forms. To request that
> transcripts be sent to Covenant Day School, please provide the registrar's email address at
> your child's current school in the application."

The transcript-release consent is broad and worth noting: entering the registrar's email
grants release of *"a complete transcript showing all former and current grades, IQ and
Achievement Test scores, psychological evaluations (if any), behavior records, attendance
records, health records, and other pertinent information."*

### 5. INTERVIEW/SHADOW — Family Interview / Student Shadow Day

> "The Admissions Office will provide a link to schedule your parent interview with the
> Admissions Director. **An essential component for admission is the requirement that one or
> both parents be professing Christians as evidenced by their confession of faith in Jesus
> Christ alone as Savior.** As such, the interview will be conducted to verify a Christian
> testimony from one or both parents and ascertain agreement with the school's statement of
> faith. We also want to get to know your student! Therefore, every applicant will schedule a
> time to join us on campus."
>
> - "**JK/K** applicants will participate in our **Little Lions Assessment** with a small
> group of JK/K friends."
> - "**Grades 1-5** applicants will work **one-on-one with an academic resource therapist** to
> measure their reading, math, and writing proficiency."
> - "**Grade 6** applicants will spend a morning **shadowing a 6th-grade student**, enjoy lunch
> with the middle school, meet with an admissions staff member, and **take a math and
> English assessment**."
> - "**Grade 7-11** applicants will spend a morning shadowing a student in the middle or high
> school, enjoy lunch with their peers, and meet with an admissions staff member."

### 6. DECISION — Admissions Decision

> "**We maintain rolling admissions.** After priority admission decisions have been
> communicated, applications are processed and decisions are provided as space is available."

And the priority footnote, verbatim:

> "*Applications for JK and K must be submitted by **January 2** to receive first-round
> notification on **February 26, 2027**. Applications for grades 1-11 must be submitted by
> **January 15** to receive first-round notification on **April 9, 2027**. All applications
> received after January 2 or January 15 will be considered, if space is available, after
> priority decisions have been communicated. **We will not conduct on-campus testing, shadow
> visits, or parent interviews for non-priority applications unless space is available after
> April 9**, when priority decisions are complete."

> - Mission appropriateness of both the student and the family

> - Observations made by faculty during a student's tour or shadow visit
> - The family's active involvement in a local church"

## Faith requirements — a real admissions gate

Unlike Charlotte Christian (where the research found **no** faith-based application
component), Covenant Day publishes an explicit requirement:

> "**An essential component for admission is the requirement that one or both parents be
> professing Christians** as evidenced by their confession of faith in Jesus Christ alone as
> Savior."

Verified at the parent interview, which also *"ascertain[s] agreement with the school's
statement of faith."* A **pastor's recommendation is required at every grade, JK–11**, and
the FAQs add:

> "it is very important to us that your family is part of a local congregation… If you are
> members of a large church and do not personally know your senior pastor, you may have the
> form completed by another pastor on staff, an elder, a deacon, or a ministry leader who
> knows your family well."

## Fees, platform and financial aid

- **Application fee: $100, non-refundable, per application.**
- **Enrollment deposit: $1,000, non-refundable, per student** — "due for each student at the
 time of enrollment and is applied towards the total tuition owed for the year."
- **Platform:** FACTS/RenWeb online application. Inquiry:
 `admissions-parent.renweb.com/en-us/inquire?districtCode=CDS-NC&memberId=13271`.
 **Member ID 13271.**
- **Financial aid:** need-based, through **FACTS**. "A new application is required each year."
 **"To be eligible for financial aid from Covenant Day, families must first provide proof of
 application for the NC Opportunity Scholarship."** Covenant Day "accepts the North Carolina
 Opportunity and ESA+ Scholarships, administered by the North Carolina State Education
 Assistance Authority (NCSEAA)." "Families should complete the financial aid application
 process concurrently with the admissions application process." A **Financial Aid
 Information Packet 2026-2027** is linked for key dates. Contact: Christen Marshall, Student
 Accounts Coordinator, 704-708-6102.

**Tuition (2026–2027, the only year published) — for provenance only, NOT for the Admissions
card:** JK $15,490 · K $15,490 · Grades 1–5 $17,790 · Grades 6–8 $20,490 · Grades 9–12
$22,790. Discounts: **$400** per student whose parents are members of Christ Covenant Church;
**$1,500** for the third and each additional student from the same household.
