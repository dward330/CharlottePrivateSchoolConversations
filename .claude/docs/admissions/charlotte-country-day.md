## Registration rules — VERBATIM

1. "Complete an application to the school(s) to which you are applying for admission. No
 school will consider an evaluation until an application for admission to that particular
 school has been received." — the application-before-testing sequencing rule.
2. "Appointments can be made the day after Labor Day. All appointments for testing are made
 by the parents with an individual psychologist-not by the schools."
3. "Since your child will be compared to other children with the same chronological age,
 late testing provides no advantages. However, for some ESL applicants a later test date
 may be beneficial to allow for greater language acquisition."
4. "Psychologists will not schedule appointments until after Labor Day. Allow up to 48 hours
 for a response. Ample slots are available for all applicants. Once confirmed, payment via
 credit card or Zelle is due within 24 hours."
5. **Age + retest rule:** "To ensure test reliability and validity, children must be at
 least four years of age at the time of testing. Testing can be administered only once in
 a twelve month period."
6. **Coaching rule:** "Psychologists will report to the schools any indication that a child
 has been coached, tutored, or re-tested. Any indication of the aforementioned will
 invalidate your application to any CAIS school. Parents will be asked to sign a Test
 Validity Verification Form at the psychologist's office."
7. Results go to parents and designated schools; an optional feedback meeting is billed at
 the psychologist's hourly rate.
8. **Interview:** "In addition to the assessment, CAIS schools require an interview with
 each child. The respective schools will contact you to make an appointment for the
 interview/observation sessions that are typically held November–February."
9. "Book an appointment with one psychologist ONLY."

## Cycle verification (year-label audit)

The CCDS Admissions Process page carries section headers reading **"2026-27 Important Dates
& Deadlines"** and **"2026-27 Admissions Calendar"**, but the content beneath those headers
is the **2027–28** cycle. Confirmed by three independent in-page signals:

1. "Applicants for junior kindergarten must turn four years old by December 31, 2026, to
 apply for the 2027–28 school year"
2. The explicit line "Application Deadlines for the 2027-28 School Year"
3. All listed dates falling in the 2026–2027 admission season (Oct 2026 open houses through
 April 2027 responses)

**The section header labels "2026-27" are a mislabel; the live, current content is the
2027–28 cycle.** A prior enrichment check flagged stale-cache 2026–27 dates (a Dec 31, 2025
deadline and a Feb 7, 2026 ISEE day); those are the **prior, closed** cycle and must not be
carried forward.

**Corroboration:** the CAIS brochure independently publishes the same JK/K and Grades 1–4
deadlines and notification dates for 2027–28.

## Structure — three bands, two process breaks

CCDS runs one shared six-step framework, but the process changes at two grade breaks,
producing three distinct plans:

- **K→1:** the deadline moves from Jan 2 to Jan 15, and the applicant shifts from the JK/K
 decision track (Feb 26) to the Grades 1–12 track (April 9). The JK/K teacher-rec deadline
 and bundled classroom-visit deadline drop away.
- **4→5:** the assessment changes from psychologist-administered CAIS screening to the ISEE,
 and the campus (for 5–8) moves to Bissell.

Application fee, portal, faculty meeting, and the 10% enrollment deposit are constant across
all bands.

## The six published steps — VERBATIM

> "Step 1: Start your journey. Step 2: Submit an application. Step 3: Complete an entrance
> test. Step 4: Meet with a faculty member. Step 5: Provide supporting materials. Step 6:
> Follow your progress."

The substantive differences between bands lie in Step 3 (entrance test), the deadline, and
the decision track.

## Assessment by band — VERBATIM

Grades JK–4: *"In the Charlotte region, a number of qualified psychologists offer
standardized screening assessments for younger applicants. These tests need to be scheduled
directly with the psychologist."*

Grades 5–12: *"Older applicants are asked to take the Independent School Entrance
Examination (ISEE)… The ISEE measures verbal and quantitative ability, mathematics skills,
reading comprehension, and writing skills."*

International: *"International applicants are asked to take the TOEFL test to measure the
ability to use and understand the English language."* Duolingo English Test is not
mentioned.

**Per-grade instrument** (from the CAIS brochure, retrieved separately): WPPSI-IV for
Pre-K/K/Grade 1; WISC-V for rising Grades 2–4. Fee $300 either instrument, paid to the
psychologist.

## All-applicant policies

**Application fee: $100** per applicant for 2027–28, VERBATIM: *"A $100 fee is required as
part of your child's application."* No separate international or late-application fee is
published.

**Application platform:** Veracross (inquiry, RSVP, and application "all in one place").
Families access it via the Inquire/Apply links on charlottecountryday.org. Portal:
`portals.veracross.com/charlottecountryday`; checklist login:
`accounts.veracross.com/charlottecountryday/portals/login`. The site is built on Finalsite;
the older `schooladminonline.com` portal referenced for the 2021–22 cycle is legacy and no
longer the active application system.

**Rolling vs. fixed notification:** CCDS publishes **fixed** notification dates (JK/K Feb 26,
2027; Grades 1–12 April 9, 2027) and fixed response dates — **not** an open rolling model.
Whether post-deadline applications are considered on a space-available basis is not stated.

**Financial aid:** CCDS partners with **Clarity** (`app.clarityapp.com/sign-up`). The Clarity
application carries a **$65 fee** paid at submission. Deadlines: **November 1** for current
families, **January 15** for prospective families. VERBATIM: *"The deadline for completing
the financial aid application including relevant supporting documents is November 1 for
current families and January 15 for prospective families."* Per the FAQ: *"admission
decisions are made separately from financial aid decisions,"* and CCDS offers *"only…
need-based awards and endowed scholarships, which are also based on financial need"* (no
merit scholarships). Both custodial and non-custodial parents must submit through Clarity.
Award decisions are communicated with enrollment contracts.

**Enrollment deposit:** 10% of tuition due at enrollment, credited to total tuition; families
in aid application pay a reduced deposit that is refundable if the aid award is insufficient.

**Tuition (2026–27, for context only):** JK $24,515; Kindergarten $25,725; Grades 1–4
$30,375; Grades 5–8 $32,700; Grades 9–12 $34,075. *(2027–28 tuition not yet published.)*

**Nondiscrimination, VERBATIM:** *"Country Day welcomes students in JK–12 without regard to
race, color, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national or ethnic
origin."*
