# Charlotte Preparatory School — Admissions — Application Process and Testing

Charlotte Prep runs two admissions tracks for fall 2027 entry: one for the Montessori Early School and Kindergarten, and one for Grades 1–8. Each track has its own application deadline, its own materials deadline, its own decision day and its own contract deadline. The test a child takes depends on the grade, and the testing split does not line up with the two tracks. Everything below comes from the school's own Application Process page (https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/application-process), read on October 19, 2026.

### Which cycle these dates belong to

The school's Important Dates list prints no year. The year comes from two places. The 2027–2028 CAIS testing brochure, which the school links from the same page, prints the same dates with 2027 attached. The school's open-house sign-up form asks for each child's "Rising Grade for 2027-28" (https://charlotteprep.org/admissions/admissions-open-house). So these are the dates for fall 2027 entry.

### The two tracks, side by side

| Step | Early School & Kindergarten | Grades 1–8 |
|---|---|---|
| Priority application deadline | January 2, 2027 | January 15, 2027 |
| Supporting materials due | February 1, 2027: "CAIS testing (kindergarten) and teacher recommendations" | February 26, 2027: "teacher recommendations, testing, and transcripts" |
| Admission decision notification | February 26, 2027 at 4:00 p.m., by email | April 9, 2027 at 4:00 p.m., by email |
| Enrollment contract return deadline | March 5, 2027 at 12:00 p.m. | April 16, 2027 at 12:00 p.m. |

The school calls the January dates "priority" deadlines. Its own wording elsewhere on the page also calls the first one the "Round 1 Deadline". Applications that arrive later are still possible: "Applications received after the Round 1 deadline will be scheduled for a visit as slots become available."

January 2, 2027 falls on a Saturday.

### Which test, by grade

The school's own list, word for word:

| Applying for | Test | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Montessori Kindergarten / Kindergarten | WPPSI-IV | February 1 |
| 1st Grade | WPPSI-IV | February 26 |
| Grades 2–4 | WISC-V | February 26 |
| Grades 5–8 | ISEE | February 26 |
| Pre-Primary, Primary and Transitional Kindergarten | No outside test | — |

The school says: "External testing is not required before Montessori Kindergarten."

So a first grader takes the same preschool-level test (WPPSI-IV) as a kindergartener, but on the Grades 1–8 deadline. Grades 2–4 take the WISC-V. Only Grades 5–8 take the ISEE.

The WPPSI-IV and WISC-V are given by psychologists approved by the Charlotte Area Independent Schools consortium (CAIS). The school says: "WPPSI/WISC testing should be administered by CAIS pyschologists" and "Appointments can be scheduled after Labor Day." The CAIS testing fee is $300 for either test, per the 2027–2028 CAIS brochure (see the companion CAIS Consortium file). The ISEE is registered separately at https://iseeonline.erblearn.org/. Charlotte Prep does not publish an ISEE fee.

The page says all Kindergarten through 8th grade applicants "will follow the Charlotte Association of Independent Schools (CAIS) applicant screening process". But the CAIS brochure covers testing only through Grade 4, and Grades 5–8 take the ISEE instead.

### Step by step

**Step 1: Application.** Apply online through the FACTS admissions portal (https://sis.factsmgt.com/admissions-parent/home?districtCode=cp-nc&memberId=1133). The application fee is $100 and is non-refundable. The school's wording: "Complete and submit an online application (including the $100 non-refundable application fee)."

**Step 2: Recommendations and transcripts.** On the application, families enter the names and emails of the child's teachers, plus the school registrar for grades 1–8. This sends the teachers and registrar an online request form automatically. The school asks families to tell teachers to watch for these emails.
- Montessori through Kindergarten: "Recommendations are due to Prep directly from teachers by February 1."
- Grades 1–8: "Teacher recommendations and transcripts for applicants to grades 1-8 are due February 26."
- The school also posts printable forms at https://charlotteprep.org/admissions-forms-and-documents. There are three teacher recommendation forms (Montessori applicant, Kindergarten applicant, Grade 1–8 applicant) and a Transcript Release Request.
- The school does not say how many recommendations each grade needs.

**Step 3: Assessment.** Testing by grade, as in the table above.

**Step 4: Campus visit.** This differs by grade.
- Early School Montessori applicants get a "brief Meet and Greet visit". A teacher works with the child and presents some Montessori lessons. The school says: "Visits usually last about 20-30 minutes."
- Kindergarten applicants who apply by the Round 1 deadline are invited to a Kindergarten Play Day.
- Grades 1–8 applicants "residing in or visiting the Charlotte area may be invited to an on-campus experience". The school adds: "Invitations may be subject to the results of academic assessments, to prevent unnecessary absences from the applicant's current school. Out of town applicants will be invited to visit virtually."

**Step 5: Decisions.** "Charlotte Prep will send admissions decisions via email. Details about how to enroll online will be provided at that time." The dates are in the table above.

### Age cutoffs for the early grades

The child must reach each age by August 1:
- Age 2 for Montessori Pre-Primary
- Age 3 for Montessori Primary
- Age 4 for Montessori Transitional Kindergarten
- Age 5 for Montessori Kindergarten or Traditional Kindergarten

The school publishes no age cutoff for Grade 1 or above.

### Two kindergartens

Charlotte Prep runs two kindergartens: Montessori Kindergarten in the Early School and a traditional Kindergarten in the Lower School. Both follow the Early School & Kindergarten track and the same testing rule. The visit differs: Montessori applicants get the Meet and Greet, and traditional Kindergarten applicants get the Play Day. Both cost $25,800 for 2026–27 (see the Tuition and Fees file).

# Charlotte Preparatory School — Admissions — CAIS Consortium 2027-28

Charlotte Prep is one of seven Charlotte private schools in the Charlotte Area Independent Schools consortium (CAIS). The members share one testing system for young applicants. A child applying to one or several of them is tested once, by one psychologist from a shared list, and the results go to every school the family names. Charlotte Prep links this year's brochure from its application page: the 2027–2028 edition (https://charlotteprep.org/uploads/files/Admissions/2027-2028-Forms/cais-2027-2028-upload.pdf).

### What the consortium covers

The brochure's title is "A Consortium for Applicant Assessment for Pre-Kindergarten–Grade 4". Its two tests are, word for word: "the Wechsler Preschool and Primary Scale of Intelligence–Fourth Edition (WPPSI-IV) for Pre-K, Kindergarten, and Grade 1 applicants; and the Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children–Fifth Edition (WISC-V) for rising Grades 2-4."

At Charlotte Prep that means:

| Applying for | Test | Given by |
|---|---|---|
| Montessori Kindergarten, Kindergarten, Grade 1 | WPPSI-IV | A CAIS psychologist |
| Grades 2–4 | WISC-V | A CAIS psychologist |
| Grades 5–8 | ISEE | Not CAIS. Registered separately. |

Charlotte Prep does not test children below Montessori Kindergarten, even though the consortium's test starts at Pre-K.

### The seven member schools

Charlotte Christian School, Charlotte Country Day School, Charlotte Latin School, **Charlotte Preparatory School**, Covenant Day School, Providence Day School and Trinity Episcopal School.

The brochure lists Charlotte Prep's contact as **Seher Khanna, Director of Enrollment**, 212 Boyce Road, Charlotte, NC 28211, 704-366-5994 ext. 1127, skhanna@charlotteprep.org.

### Shared 2027 dates

| | TK/JK/K | Grades 1–4 |
|---|---|---|
| Application deadline to CAIS schools | January 2, 2027 | January 15, 2027 |
| CAIS testing complete by | February 1, 2027 | February 26, 2027 |
| Notification date | February 26, 2027 | April 9, 2027 |
| Contracts returned by | March 5, 2027 | April 16, 2027 |

The rule that protects families, word for word: "No contract for a CAIS school will be required prior to this deadline." A family can hear back from every member school before committing to any of them.

Charlotte Prep's own Grades 1–8 track uses these same Grades 1–4 dates for every grade from 1 to 8. That includes Grades 5–8, whose testing (the ISEE) sits outside CAIS. The school's page also adds decision and contract times that the brochure does not give: 4:00 p.m. for decisions and 12:00 p.m. for contracts.

### Fees and booking

- **Testing fee:** "$300 for WPPSI-IV" and "$300 for WISC-V". The family pays the psychologist directly.
- **Booking:** "Appointments can be made the day after Labor Day." Parents book directly with a psychologist: "All appointments for testing are made by the parents with an individual psychologist-not by the schools."
- **Payment:** By credit card or Zelle within 24 hours of confirmation. Checks have a one-week grace period if arranged in advance. "Payments must be made in full for appointments made after January 1, 2027 and are non-refundable."
- **Cancellation:** "Once payment is made, a nonrefundable $100 fee applies to all cancellations." Cancellations within 72 hours of the appointment get no refund. For Monday appointments, cancel by the preceding Wednesday.
- **Book only once:** "Book an appointment with one psychologist ONLY."

### Rules that can end an application

- **Coaching or re-testing.** "Psychologists will report to the schools any indication that a child has been coached, tutored, or re-tested. Any indication of the aforementioned will invalidate your application to any CAIS school." Parents sign a Test Validity Verification Form at the psychologist's office. The penalty applies at every member school, not only one.
- **Age and frequency.** "children must be at least four years of age at the time of testing. Testing can be administered only once in a twelve month period."
- **The interview.** "In addition to the assessment, CAIS schools require an interview with each child." The schools arrange the interview or observation, "typically held November–February".

### Results and help for families

- Results go to parents and to the schools the family ticks on the registration form.
- A more detailed feedback meeting with the psychologist is optional and billed at that psychologist's hourly rate.
- For children learning English, the brochure says "an additional Nonverbal Index score will be provided", and families should pick a psychologist marked as offering ESL testing. Two psychologists on the 2027–28 list are marked "ESL Testing Available".
- It also says "for some ESL applicants a later test date may be beneficial to allow for greater language acquisition."
- The brochure lists 15 psychologists. Some give both tests and some give only the WPPSI-IV. The brochure marks which by shading.
