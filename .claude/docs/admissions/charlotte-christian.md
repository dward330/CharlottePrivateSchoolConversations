### Resolving the Jan 2 vs Jan 15 conflict — RESOLVED

The prior research pass flagged a `CONFLICT FLAG`: the Apply page widget showed **Jan 2**
for JK/K while the process PDFs showed a single **Jan 15**. **Both readings were partly
wrong.** The JK–Grade 1 checklist carries both dates in one sentence, verbatim:

> "Submit application for each child with a $100 non-refundable fee on or before
> **January 2, 2027 for JK/K and January 15, 2027 for Grade 1**.*"

And the Admissions Calendar independently confirms:

> "Saturday, Jan. 2, 2027 — Priority Applications Due - JK/K"
> "Friday, Jan. 15, 2027 — Priority Applications Due - Grades 1-12"

**There is no conflict.** JK/K is Jan 2; Grades 1–12 are Jan 15. The JK–Grade 1 checklist
spans a band boundary — it covers JK, K *and* Grade 1, and Grade 1 sits on the later
deadline. That is why a single-date reading of that PDF was wrong.

### A typo in the school's own calendar

but the date is out of sequence with every surrounding line (Jan 2 2027 → Feb 1 2026 → Feb 26
2027). The JK–Grade 1 checklist gives the correct year: *"CAIS testing is due by **February
1, 2027**."* Ship **February 1, 2027**.

## Structure — four bands

Charlotte Christian publishes three application-process documents, but they do not map 1:1
to bands: the JK–Grade 1 sheet spans a deadline boundary. The real structure is **four
bands**:

| Band | Grades | Deadline | Assessment | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| JK / K | JK, K | **Jan 2, 2027** | WPPSI-IV (CAIS) | **Feb 26, 2027** |
| Grade 1 | 1 | Jan 15, 2027 | WPPSI-IV (CAIS) | Apr 9, 2027 |
| Grades 2–4 | 2–4 | Jan 15, 2027 | WISC-V (CAIS) | Apr 9, 2027 |
| Grades 5–12 | 5–12 | Jan 15, 2027 | ISEE | Apr 9, 2027 |

**The instrument boundary is Grade 1 → Grade 2** (WPPSI-IV through Grade 1; WISC-V from
Grade 2), matching the CAIS consortium brochure retrieved for Country Day. **The deadline
boundary is K → Grade 1.** The two boundaries do not coincide — which is exactly why the
JK–Grade 1 document reads confusingly.

Siblings of JK–K applicants are decided on the **JK–K track (Feb 26, 2027)** regardless of
the grade applied for. Stated in all three checklists.

## Tuition & fees (2026–27) — VERBATIM

| Division | Tuition |
|---|---|

**Fees:**

instructional technology support, textbooks, language arts novels, spiritual retreat (middle
and upper school students) and caps and gowns for seniors." Middle school students are
assigned an iPad; upper school students a MacBook Air.

## Faith requirements — the finding

**No faith-based application component is published.** Across all three application-process
PDFs, the Admissions Calendar and the Apply page there is:

- **no pastoral or clergy reference**
- **no church-membership requirement**
- **no statement-of-faith affirmation for applicants or parents**
- **no faith-based question on the applicant questionnaire** (which is described only as
 completed "in the student's handwriting")

The school publishes a doctrinal Statement of Faith, but it governs the **institution**, not
applicants — nothing in the published admissions process requires a family to affirm it. The
family interview's published purpose is a conversation about fit and goals, not a faith
assessment.

*(The full Statement of Faith text is deliberately not transcribed here. The finding above
is the admissions-relevant fact; the Statement is available on the school's own site.)*
