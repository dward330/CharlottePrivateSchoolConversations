Year-over-year increase 2025-26 → 2026-27: $1,200 at every band (+5.6% at the
top band). **The app's `top-tuition` figure is $22,790 (grades 9-12, 2026-27)** —
the site's live table year, matching the year used for the other six schools.

### What tuition includes, and the fees beside it

- Tuition covers "use of on-campus computers/iPads, technology expenses, school
 retreats and required field trips, science lab and elective fees, and most
 athletic expenses."
- **$1,000 enrollment deposit** per student — non-refundable, applied to tuition.
 Halvable while a financial-aid application is pending.
- **$50/year FACTS fee** on the monthly plan; **3.05% card-processing fee** on
 debit/credit payments.
- Students in grades 9-12 must supply their own laptop.
- Extended Day and special-learning programs cost extra; tutoring/special testing
 beyond classroom help is billed.

### Payment plans (FACTS)

- **Annual** — full balance by May 1.
- **Monthly** — nine equal installments, May–January, autopay on the 5th or 20th;
 $50/year plan fee. Withdrawal must be official by the 10th to skip that month;
 refunds equal installments not yet due.

### Discounts

- **Multi-child:** $1,500 off the third and each additional student (applied to
 lowest grades).
- **Christ Covenant Church members:** $400 per student (membership affirmed at
 enrollment; February 1 cutoff for full-year eligibility).

### The aid engine

- **Need-based aid via FACTS** assessment; new application every year; decisions
 only on complete applications.
- **Mandatory prerequisite: proof of application for the NC Opportunity
 Scholarship** — "To be eligible for financial aid from Covenant Day, families
 must first provide proof of application for the NC Opportunity Scholarship."
- Accepts **NC Opportunity Scholarship and ESA+** (NCSEAA-administered).
- Contact: Christen Marshall, Student Accounts Coordinator, 704-708-6102.
- Only the party responsible for tuition may apply; aid is need-based only — no
 merit scholarships are published.

### Aid numbers — the structural dead end

- **~20% of students receive aid** — third-party only (PrivateSchoolReview,
 schooltuitions.org). The school's own pages state no percentage.
- **Total aid awarded and average award: NOT PUBLISHED, and not recoverable.**
 Covenant Day is a ministry of Christ Covenant Church (PCA); the IRS designates
 it a **religious organization not required to file Form 990** (tax-exempt since
 June 1989). ProPublica: "Nonprofit Explorer has no Form 990 data for Covenant
 Day School Inc." Every other school in this project is a 990 filer — this is a
 first for the roster. No filing exists to find; `aid-awarded` and `avg-award`
 are deliberate nulls.

### Force majeure and account terms

Standard force-majeure clause (tuition obligations survive closure);
30-days-past-due accounts can suspend re-enrollment, report cards, transcripts,
and class access.
