## Signature / flagship programmes

**iGEM — synthetic biology, GOLD MEDAL.** Gaston Day has fielded **International Genetically
Engineered Machine** high-school teams in **2012, 2016, 2018 and 2020** (S6, S7, S8), a
notable achievement for a school of this size — iGEM is a university-level competition with
a separate high-school track. Documented projects include a **kudzu phytotoxin** project
(2020, S6) and an ***E. coli* K-12 isobutanol biofuel** project. The team's **gold medal**
is recorded in the plan's assessment research; the iGEM team wikis **403 to WebFetch** in
this pass, so the medal level is carried as third-party-confirmed rather than re-verified
at source here.

**VEX Robotics / FIRST Robotics (S4, verbatim).** "After years of operating a joint-school
robotics team competing in FIRST Robotics where they placed **4th in state level
competition**, Gaston Day launched its own **VEX Robotics Club** and qualified for
**Technology Student Association VEX Nationals in 2023**, the first year of joining the
competition." Robotics (H) is also a listed Upper School elective.

**Sunship Earth and Peer Mentoring (S4).** Selected juniors and seniors accompany the
fourth-grade class to the mountains for several days each fall, leading activities on the
relationship between the environment and the human race. The relationships formed become a
year-long mentoring programme — each eleventh- and twelfth-grade student continues to meet
and work with their fourth-grade mentee throughout the school year.

**Capstone Project (S4).** All seniors complete an approved internship, research or
community-service project of **no less than 40 hours**, beginning in the second semester of
junior year and completing in the first semester of senior year, with documentation and
reporting throughout.

**Blutopia (S4, S10).** Student literary and art magazine, entirely student-produced.
Numerous **Scholastic Art and Writing Awards**; consistently recognised in the **North
Carolina Scholastic Media Association's Literary Magazine Contest**.
