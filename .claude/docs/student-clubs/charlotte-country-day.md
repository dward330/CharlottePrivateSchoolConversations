Honor Code and Honor Council
The Honor Code anchors Country Day's student-governance culture. Students sign the code each year and write the
Honor Pledge — “I will not lie, cheat, or steal, nor will I tolerate those who do” — on graded work. Violations such as
lying, cheating, and plagiarism trigger an Honor Council hearing in Middle and Upper School, with disciplinary and
academic consequences. The Honor Council is thus both a student-leadership body and the school's primary
integrity-enforcement mechanism. [Sources: charlottecountryday.org CD Education; charlottecountryday.org BucsNet
Quick Reference; Axios Charlotte sponsored feature]
Student Government and student leadership
Student Government appears consistently in the school's club listings and Upper School materials as a core leadership
organization, where students participate in governance, assist peers, and serve as role models. The Student Diversity
Leadership Corps (SDLC) provides a parallel, inclusion-focused leadership track. [Sources: charlottecountryday.org
School Profile; charlottecountryday.org Upper School page; charlottecountryday.org DEIB Our Program]

 affecting our school and the broader community. The group also supports Country Day's
 annual International Focus Topic."

 Affinity Groups."

- Spinoffs: **Diversity Awareness Lunch Forum** (est. 2014, "courageous conversations");
 **Alumni DAF Luncheon** (college-aged alumni return annually).

 https://www.charlottecountryday.org/about/deib/program

## 1b. Service & Civic Engagement

All three signature programs are **VERIFIED**. Primary source for all three:
https://www.charlottecountryday.org/cd-experience/beyond-the-classroom

### Program 1 — Big Brothers Big Sisters — scale: ~50% of juniors and seniors

> "Big Brothers Big Sisters serves as one of our flagship programs. Nearly half the members
> of the junior and senior class mentor and volunteer one lunch hour a week to Rama Road
> Elementary School students."

Sustained weekly mentoring — a real relationship rather than a one-off event. Corroborated
as a named student organization in the School Profile's leadership list. Partner: Rama Road
Elementary School.

### Program 2 — Special Olympics Spring Games — scale: hosted every April since 1984

> "Every April, the entire Upper School—students, faculty, and staff—takes a two-day break
> from academia to host the Special Olympics Mecklenburg County Spring Games. Students
> serve as buddies, timekeepers, and event assistants… which has taken place on Country
> Day's Cannon Campus since 1984."

The **1984** founding year is independently corroborated in the 2024-25 School Profile PDF.
Quote from Upper School Dean of Students Natalie Hutchinson: "This time-honored tradition
has become part of our identity shared by nearly four generations of Country Day families."
Source: https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1726775598/charlottecds/enzt08thdabvh3uxiz0i/2024-2025CCDSSchoolProfile.pdf

### Program 3 — Senior Externship Program — scale: 175+ employers, 20+ industries, since 2017

> "Launched in 2017, the Senior Externship Program has partnered with more than 175 local
> employers across 20+ industries."

Described as "a meaningful, one-day immersion into professional life," via "job shadowing
and mentorship."

**Internal inconsistency on the partner count** — three numbers across two pages:

- "more than **175** local employers across 20+ industries" — Beyond the Classroom, main
 section (**current; use this**)
- "Since 2017, we have partnered with **100+** employers in 20 different industries" —
 Beyond the Classroom, tile summary on the same page (stale)
- "connects them to **nearly 175** employers and organizations" — 2024-25 School Profile PDF

### Additional scale figure — VERIFIED

"**20+ service learning partnerships**, including Goodwill Industries, ourBridge, The
Learning Collaborative, Friendship Trays, and Loaves and Fishes." (Beyond the Classroom)

### "Individual activity, not a club" — VERIFIED

Real and documented, but **not standalone student clubs**:

- **Special Olympics Spring Games** — a whole-division, two-day event ("the entire Upper
 School… takes a two-day break"), not a membership club.
- **Senior Externship** — an individual one-day placement for each senior; a program.
- **Community Service Day** (US), **Middle School Learning Collaborative visit**, **Lower
 School cards for veterans**, **Veterans Day recognition**, **Parents' Association Run for
 Good** — schoolwide/divisional events; the Run for Good is a *parent* association activity.
- **IB Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS)** — a curricular requirement for IB students.
- **Big Brothers Big Sisters is the exception**: it does read as an organized student body
 and is listed among "clubs and activities" in the School Profile.

## 1c. Honor Societies

### The roster — VERIFIED (six)

Authoritative list under "SELECTED MEMBERSHIPS & ACADEMIC ORGANIZATIONS" at
https://www.charlottecountryday.org/about/fast-facts — identical list in the 2024-25 School
Profile PDF.

| Society | Division | What it recognizes | Feeds from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Cum Laude Society** | Upper School (seniors) | Top academic distinction; "one of the oldest and most prestigious academic honor societies in secondary education, founded in 1906 and modeled after Phi Beta Kappa" | The classroom |
| **National Honor Society** | Upper School | Scholarship, leadership, service, character (standard NHS pillars; the school publishes no local criteria) | Classroom + service |
| **National Junior Honor Society** | Middle School | The Middle School counterpart to NHS | Classroom (MS) |
| **Tri-M Music Honor Society** | Upper School | Music achievement and service | The Arts — music |
| **National Art Honor Society** | Upper School | Visual-arts achievement | The Arts — visual |
| **International Thespian Society** | Upper School | Theatre achievement | The Arts — drama |

**Note on the sixth entry:** International Thespian Society is **not** in the Fast Facts /
School Profile list but *is* confirmed active — the school groups it with Tri-M and NAHS as
one of "three Country Day art honor societies."

### Induction data — VERIFIED but thin

- **Arts, dated 2016:** https://www.charlottecountryday.org/cf_news/view.cfm?newsid=1056 —
 "On March 3, 45 Upper School students were inducted into three Country Day art honor
 societies: Tri-M Music Honor Society, National Art Honor Society, and International
 Thespian Society. In addition, 64 students were recognized for their continued membership
 and service." The page names every inductee by society. This is the **only** page with
 hard induction counts, and it is from **March 2016**.
- **Cum Laude, recent but count-free:**
 https://www.charlottecountryday.org/news-events/news-details-page/~board/school-news/post/cum-laude-society-induction-ceremony-a-celebration-of-the-whole-student
 (posted 3/11/26) — describes the March 10, 2026 ceremony, the 1906 / Phi Beta Kappa
 lineage, Head of Upper School Nidhi McVicar's per-inductee reflections, and speaker
 Dr. Jamie Fraser '02. It gives **no inductee count, no GPA threshold, no class-rank
 percentage**.

### GAP FLAG — 1c

1. **No induction criteria published anywhere** — no GPA cutoff, class-rank percentile, or
 service-hour minimum for any of the six societies. (Cum Laude nationally caps chapters at
 20% of the class; Country Day does not state its own figure.)
2. **No dedicated honor-societies page.** The roster exists only as a bullet list inside
 Fast Facts and the School Profile PDF — no per-society landing page, chapter number, or
 faculty advisor.
3. **No annual induction counts** for Cum Laude, NHS, or NJHS — ever. The only count found
 is the 2016 arts figure.
4. **No chapter activity descriptions** — what NHS or NJHS members actually do (service
 requirements, tutoring, projects) is unpublished.
5. **NJHS division is inferred, not stated** — its Middle School placement follows from the
 society's national scope; no Country Day page explicitly assigns it.

### NOT FOUND — explicitly searched

**No world-language honor societies** (no Société Honoraire de Français, Sociedad Honoraria
Hispánica, or National Latin Honor Society) despite French / Spanish / Latin / Chinese
offered through AP and IB. Also **no Mu Alpha Theta, no Science NHS, no Quill and Scroll**.
"Feeds from world languages" is therefore **absent** at this school, not merely
undocumented.
