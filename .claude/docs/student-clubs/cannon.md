Upper School Student Council
The Upper School Student Council leads community meetings and school-spirit events and reports activities to the
student body. Officers are President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Public Relations Director; the
Executive Board comprises elected officers, class representatives, and faculty advisors.[1]
Honor Council & Honor Code
Honor is positioned as “a way of life.” Students sign an Honor Code and Unity Pledge each year — pledging to tell
the truth, respect others’ property, do their own work, and treat others with kindness and respect. A student-elected
Honor Council hears and adjudicates honor matters and is described by the school as a model of community
governance.[1,2]
The House System
Using advisory groups as its foundation, the House System provides leadership opportunities, peer tutoring,
community outreach, and social activities across all four Upper School years, building relationships between
students and adult advisors.[1,2]
Middle & Lower School leadership
The Middle School runs both a Student Council (grades 5–8, peer-elected) and an Advisory Council that plans the
division’s annual theme and activities. The Lower School operates its own elected Student Council focused on core
values and community.[3,4]
Club discovery & funding (folded in)
Cannon’s clubs are student-led and faculty-supported, and the consistent public signal is a student-initiated
founding model: students may create new clubs when their interests are not already represented. This is the
school’s primary discovery mechanism.[1,5]
