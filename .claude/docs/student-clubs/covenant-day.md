# Covenant Day School — Student Clubs — Service and Community Engagement

> **Provenance:** Compiled by Claude Code on 2026-08-15 from the school's service
> page.

### Sources

- https://www.covenantday.org/campus-life/service
- https://www.covenantday.org/campus-life/discipleship (missions destinations)

### The division-laddered service programs

- **Goodness Gorillas** (Lower School, 4th grade) — "fourth grade students head out
 into the community to serve at **nearly twenty different sites**."
- **Doulos Day** (Middle School, **twice a year**) — middle schoolers serve "at
 various local nonprofits," then reflect together. ("Doulos" — servant.)
- **ContainIt** (High School) — students "use their different talents to transform
 used shipping containers" into housing for the **Pine Ridge Reservation, South
 Dakota**, aiming to "provide housing for **more than 30 teens**." A multi-year
 trades-and-design build.
- **Restore525** — the Restoration & Sustainability department's service program,
 which also appears as a credit-bearing course in the HS profile matrix.

### Partners named

Brookstone Schools, Jackson Park, Alexander Children's Home, Love INC., Matthews
Habitat for Humanity, Matthews HELP Center, Operation Christmas Child; missions
trips to Spain, Haiti, Belize, and Pine Ridge.

### Not published

**No service-hour requirement and no annual-hours total** is published — the
model is program-based ("students serve together, then reflect"), not
hours-logging. Confirmed absence.

# Covenant Day School — Student Clubs — Signature Traditions and Student Life

> **Provenance:** Compiled by Claude Code on 2026-08-15 from the school's
> student-life and discipleship pages.

### Sources

- https://www.covenantday.org/campus-life/student-life
- https://www.covenantday.org/campus-life/discipleship

### All-school traditions

- **Color War** — all-school team challenges.
- **Homecoming** — "a week of dress-up days and fun activities culminates with our
 homecoming game" (played at Warner Park).
- **Spirit Week** + all-school pep rally; **Spirit Fridays**; the **Covenant
 Crazies** student spirit section.
- **Field Day** — lower and middle school.

### Retreats

- **Windy Gap** — middle and high school students, "a three-day retreat filled
 with chapels, team-building."
- **Kanuga** — the fifth-grade overnight ("fifth graders enjoy their own overnight
 adventure at Kanuga").

### The discipleship structure (the school's grouping model)

Chapel weekly, **separated by age** ("praise songs, scripture reading, and a
message from guest speakers, principals, or teachers"); **HS Advisory Groups**
led by teachers, weekly, together all year; **daily Homeroom Devotions** with
shared prayer requests; **Missions** trips to Spain, Haiti, Belize, and the Pine
Ridge Reservation, with a Lower School Missions Week. This faith-and-structure
grouping model is the school's answer to belonging — it publishes **no
identity-based affinity groups** (see the app's omitted affinity card; confirmed
against /campus-life/student-life and /campus-life/discipleship).

### Signature academic events

- **Senior Capstone Project** — a named high-school milestone.
- **McKnight Oratory** — the school's signature speaking event.
- Educational field trips from JK up; "student clubs and extracurricular classes"
 are the stated vehicle for leadership, service, spirit, and new passions.
