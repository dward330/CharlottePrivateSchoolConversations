## Defensible Upper School organization count: 5

Standing HS student organizations: (1) National Honor Society, (2) National Beta Club,
(3) Spanish Honor Society, (4) Student Council, (5) The Timothy Project. **Recommend 5.**
Do NOT go below 4 or above 5. Do NOT inflate with: Ignite Praise Band (chapel worship),
Cornerstone (parent ministry), NJHS / MS SGA (Middle School), Chess/Broadcast Journalism
(Enrichment/elective classes), or the third-party "14 extracurriculars" (bundles sports +
arts + retreats).

## Clubs model (CONFIRMED, S1 p.74)

"If you are interested in creating a club you may ask for a form from the High School or
Middle School offices… Executing the application process does not guarantee the recognition
of your club. The administration reserves the right to review your intentions, and to
ultimately accept or deny your application." Clubs are **student-initiated by application** —
this fully explains the thin standing roster.

## Service (CONFIRMED, with correction)

There IS a published HS service requirement: **"A minimum of 20 hours are the required
service hours per grade level."** Beta Club requires 10 hrs/semester; J-Term service hours
can count (max 20 per J-Term course). No school-wide cumulative total is published. (The
brief's "no requirement" note was incorrect.)

## CONFIRMED ABSENT (deep pass, not a search failure)

- **Publications / media:** no yearbook, newspaper, or literary magazine in the handbook,
 live nav, or aggregators. Only a MS **Broadcast Journalism elective** (a class). → OMIT
 the `media` card.
- **Affinity / identity groups:** none in any source, as expected at a Christian school. →
 OMIT the `affinity` card.
- **Competition clubs** (robotics/FIRST, debate, Model UN, mock trial, quiz bowl, FCA,
 chess-as-club, DECA/FBLA): none as standing HS clubs. Chess exists only as an Extended-Day
 Enrichment Class; Geography/Spelling Bees are school-run gr 1-8 events, not HS clubs.
