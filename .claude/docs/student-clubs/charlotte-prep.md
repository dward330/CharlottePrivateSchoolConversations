# Charlotte Preparatory School — Student Clubs — Academic and Competitive Clubs

Charlotte Prep's competitive academic record rests on two things: a Middle School Model UN club, and a steady run of placements at the regional science and engineering fair. The school has no honor society of any kind.

### Model UN — CHARMUN delegates since 2023

**The club.** The Middle School Model UN club is sponsored by English teacher Ashley Shaefer and social studies teacher Griffin Kennard. "Mrs. Shaefer, who started the middle school Model UN program at Charlotte Prep and has helped it grow into a competitive team since 2020, usually describes Model UN as a 'blend of international diplomacy and theater.'" (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/model-un-club-awarded-at-conference, February 29, 2024)

**The conference.** CHARMUN is "a long-standing annual introductory Model UN conference for local independent school teams". It is a middle-school conference hosted at Providence Day School (https://www.charlottelatin.org/about/school-news/news-details/~board/news/post/2023-ms-mun).

| Year | Event | Result | Delegates |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | CHARMUN, Charlotte Prep's first year as a member school | First delegate awards: two club leaders | not published |
| 2024 | CHARMUN, second year as a member school | 2 awards: a vocal commendation, on a committee simulating the COP 28 climate conference, and Most-Improved Delegate, on an International Court of Justice simulation. Both went to 8th graders. | 20 |
| 2025-26 | "Model UN conferences" | not published | not published |

The 2025-26 line comes from an 8th grader's story of November 6, 2025, which lists "participating in fascinating Model UN conferences" among her Charlotte Prep experiences.

**What the club does between conferences** (February 29, 2024 story):

- research races, global current-events trivia, and mapping how cultures celebrate the end of the year
- in spring 2024, an in-house conference written by the students on the South China Sea dispute
- a planned community service project
- interest in "a middle school Model UN conference for the entire state of North Carolina"

No result from a statewide conference has been published.

Griffin Kennard describes the classroom side in "Sparking Debate to Grow Critical Thinking", which covers "history class and through the Model United Nations club" (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/sparking-debate-to-grow-critical-thinking).

### Science & Engineering Fair — a regional placement record

The school runs its own Science Fair each December (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/a-stage-set-for-success). Students then compete at the **UNC Charlotte Region 6 NC Science & Engineering Fair**, where top finishers advance to the NC State Science & Engineering Fair. The regional results lists published by UNC Charlotte's Center for STEM Education show a consistent Charlotte Prep presence. Counts only, no names:

| Year | Division | Placements that advanced to State | Honorable Mentions | Other |

| 2024 | Junior | 7: Biological Sciences B 1st and 2nd; Chemistry 2nd and 3rd; Earth/Environmental Science 3rd; Physics 1st and 3rd | 1 (Engineering) | — |
| 2025 | Junior | 5: Chemistry 1st; Earth/Environmental Science 2nd; Physics 1st and 3rd; Technology 3rd | 1 (Engineering) | 2 Thermo Fisher Scientific Junior Innovators Challenge nominations |
| 2026 | Elementary | 4: Biological Sciences 3rd and 4th; Chemistry 4th; Physics & Math 4th | — | — |
| 2026 | Junior | 3: Physics 1st; Technology 3rd and 4th | 2 (Biological Sciences B; Earth/Environmental Science) | 1 Thermo Fisher Scientific Junior Innovators Challenge nomination |

Charlotte Prep took **first place in Junior Physics at Region 6 in all three years, 2024, 2025 and 2026.**

**At the state fair, 2024.** "a Prep 8th grader was awarded at the state-level Science and Engineering Fair on Saturday in Raleigh! ... won 3rd place in the physics category for his project on golf drives." (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/prep-scholar-awarded-at-state-science-fair, March 28, 2024; video https://vimeo.com/928432491). The same project placed 3rd in Junior Physics at Region 6 that year. The state fair's own results list could not be retrieved, so the state placing rests on the school's report.

Science fair work is part of the academic program rather than a club. It is listed here because it is the school's most consistent external academic competition record.

### No honor society

Charlotte Prep has **no honor society**. "Honor society", "National Junior Honor Society", "NJHS" and "Beta Club" return zero hits across all 171 pages of the school's site, its admissions packet and its annual reports. The school's character framework is its **Honor Code** and **Four Pillars** (Respect, Integrity, Perseverance, Courage), which apply to every student. No body selects or inducts students. See the club catalog file.

# Charlotte Preparatory School — Student Clubs — Service and Civic Engagement

Charlotte Prep builds service into its leadership program. There are two annual service days and a parent-run holiday drive. Lower School students visit a nursing home, and the cross-age BUDEE buddy system carries much of the service work. The student council has led at least one major drive. No service-hours requirement is published.

### The school's five service goals

Verbatim from https://charlotteprep.org/community/service-learning:

> Community Service Goals:
> Nurture compassion and empathy for others by creating situations in which the student can begin to understand how their actions impact themselves and the lives of others. (Emotional Intelligence)
> Develop an awareness of the inequities that create vulnerabilities within our local Charlotte community and around the world. (Identification)
> Develop critical thinking skills to understand the causes and potential solutions to inequities in our society. (Causes and Solutions)
> Foster deep partnerships with local, national, and international organizations to organize tangible projects and advocacy to support their community. (Action)
> Develop a life-long commitment to service and our society. (Commitment)

The page adds: "The leadership curriculum expounds on the idea of servant leadership and the importance of civic engagement."

### The recurring programs

Verbatim, from the "Recent school-wide service projects" list on the same page:

| Program | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| Annual Family Day of Service | families | "In on and off-campus locations, Prep families do service projects for local organizations." |
| Annual Student Morning of Service | students | "students engage in service work that addresses childhood literacy, child homelessness, animal welfare, homelessness, or economic inequality." |
| Parents Association Holiday Drive, Preppy's Pantry | whole community, run by parents | "collects coats and non-perishable food items for a local service organization." |
| Community Engagement | Lower School | "lower school students routinely visit a local nursing home to read and play games with residents." |

The Family Day of Service is also listed among the Prep Fund's diversity and belonging outcomes for 2023-24 and 2024-25, as "Family community service day" (https://charlotteprep.org/support/the-prep-fund). None of these programs has a published size, such as participants, hours or pounds collected. Neither the nursing home nor the "local service organization" is named.

### BUDEE, the service backbone

BUDEE stands for "Be Understanding, Dedicated, Empathetic, and Engaged". It "matches TK students from the Early School with 3rd Graders and each of the other Lower School students with a Middle School buddy", and the pairs "meet for community service, character education, and teamwork exercises" (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/leadership). In 2023-24 the BUDEE groups ran programs for the International Day of Peace, Veterans Day, Hispanic Heritage Month, a Day of Service, Women's History Month, Earth Day, Diwali, Black History Month and Lunar New Year (Prep Fund impact, 2023-2024). As part of their capstone, "Many of our 8th graders ... have been taking time during their own study halls and lunches to tutor their 4th-grade BUDEEs" (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/how-our-middle-schoolers-become-leaders, February 8, 2023).

### Building it out

The school is formalizing its service program. Its strategic plan reports that "a service learning committee was formed to expand the school's community service program and to integrate service learning into the curriculum at each grade level during the 24-25 school year", and that a newer faculty "Community Service Committee" has "already made significant contributions" (https://charlotteprep.org/about/strategic-plan-2022-2027). One of the summer-2026 teacher grant projects is "The Bulb Community Service Project", which is not yet described (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/building-the-foundations-for-student-learning). The Model UN club said in 2024 that it planned to add a community service project.

### What is not published

- **No service-hours requirement.** None is stated anywhere for any grade.
- **No named standing partner organizations.** The goals speak of "deep partnerships with local, national, and international organizations", but the current page names none. The only organizations named in current stories are the YMCA (Helene relief, 2024), 7th Street Concerts and the Sphinx Virtuosi (the benefit concert, 2024), and Weddington Farm (the 3rd-grade field trip).
- No service awards and no participation figures.
