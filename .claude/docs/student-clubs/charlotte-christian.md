## 1a. Affinity & Identity Groups

**Headline finding: Charlotte Christian publishes no affinity/identity-group structure
in the conventional sense.** There is no enumerated roster of identity-based student
groups — nothing organized by race, ethnicity, gender, or orientation — and no affinity
umbrella council. What the school does publish is a *Diversity & Belonging* office and a
small set of **global/cultural-awareness** entities. The card for this school is therefore
built under the school's own global-awareness framing rather than an identity framing.

### The coordinating office — VERIFIED

- **Diversity & Belonging**, filed under *About*, not *Campus Life*.
- Director: **Jozi Walker**, Director of Diversity, Belonging and Global Awareness.
- Stated aim: to "foster a community that impacts the culture for Christ by actively
 reflecting His command to live in unity and reconciliation with one another," addressing
 "Biblical diversity and belonging from a developmental lens in each of our divisions."
- Source: https://www.charlottechristian.com/about/diversity

### The enumerated groups — VERIFIED (three total, school-wide)

| Group | Type | Division | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Global Knights** | Student group | Upper School (initiatives reach Lower School) | Global-awareness group; supports celebrating cultures from around the world, perspective-building, empathy, intercultural communication. Organizes the annual Multicultural Festival. |
| **Knights Nations Club** | Student club | Upper School | Student-founded and student-led; "fosters a friendly and welcoming environment for upper school students"; members drawn from all four upper school grade levels. |
| **Romans 12 Initiative** | Parent / community group | All-school | "a group made up of members of the CCS community that gather each month to engage in conversations around diversity, belonging, and Jesus' command to live in unity." |

- Global Knights, Romans 12 Initiative: https://www.charlottechristian.com/about/diversity
- Knights Nations Club: https://www.charlottechristian.com/post-details/~board/knights-headlines/post/knights-nations-club-fosters-a-friendly-and-welcoming-environment-for-upper-school-students

### Knights Nations Club detail — VERIFIED

Founded two years before the article by upper school students **Dailey Schenck** and
**Gabi Martin**; incoming leader **junior David Thomas Little**. Activities include visiting
international grocery stores, interactive games, service projects, and learning cultural
traditions (e.g. "the ways Easter is celebrated across the globe"). No faculty sponsor,
membership count, or countries-represented figure is published; the article carries no
visible date.

### The leadership arm — VERIFIED

**Prefect system — nine faculty-mentored student leaders:** Head, Academic, Admissions,
Athletic, **Global Awareness**, Fine Arts, **Service**, Spiritual Life, and Student Life
Prefect. The Global Awareness Prefect is the identity/global leadership arm; the Service
Prefect is the civic arm. Source: https://www.charlottechristian.com/academics/upper-school

### COUNT DISCREPANCY FLAG — VERIFIED

The school runs **two separate, overlapping global/cultural student groups and never
reconciles them on any page**:

- The **Diversity & Belonging page names only "Global Knights"** — no mention of Knights
 Nations Club. → https://www.charlottechristian.com/about/diversity
- The **Knights Headlines feature names only "Knights Nations Club"** (categorized
 "GLOBAL") — no mention of Global Knights. → https://www.charlottechristian.com/post-details/~board/knights-headlines/post/knights-nations-club-fosters-a-friendly-and-welcoming-environment-for-upper-school-students
- The **Upper School clubs list names neither** — its 13-club roster omits both. →
 https://www.charlottechristian.com/academics/upper-school

So the count of global/cultural student groups reads as **0, 1, or 2 depending on which
page you open**. No page publishes a total.

### Divisional programming — VERIFIED (programming, not groups)

- **Lower School:** multicultural awareness via literature, guest speakers, project-based
 lessons; each classroom is assigned a country for the Multicultural Festival.
- **Middle School:** cross-cultural development via curriculum, heritage month
 celebrations, and field trips (grade 6 Sudan Interior Missions; grade 7 Holocaust
 Museum; grade 8 Civil Rights activities).
- **Upper School:** mentorship through "difficult and/or culturally sensitive situations,"
 intercultural communication, international service projects.
- Source: https://www.charlottechristian.com/about/diversity

### Other parent groups — VERIFIED (not affinity-based)

**PTF (Parent Teacher Fellowship)** and **N.E.W. (Newcomers Embraced & Welcomed)** —
new-family assimilation rather than identity. Also **Haverim Ladies' Bible Study**.
Sources: https://charlottechristian.com/page.cfm?p=812 ·
https://www.charlottechristian.com/campus-life

### Gaps — 1a

- No affinity/identity groups by race, ethnicity, gender, religion, or orientation anywhere.
- No international-student club or program page (an international-student *admissions*
 policy exists — I-20 / non-permanent-resident — but no student group).
- No Middle or Lower School affinity/cultural groups; those divisions get programming only.
- No membership counts, meeting schedules, or faculty sponsors for any of the three groups.
- Global Knights has no page of its own — it exists only as a phrase on the diversity page
 and in festival coverage.

### SNIPPET-ONLY — the Multicultural Festival

The festival article (indexed at
https://www.charlottechristian.com/post-details/~board/ccs-news-20/post/celebrating-the-nations-at-the-multicultural-festival)

School Global Knights began planning the Multicultural Festival in the fall" working with

it… through its clothing and a musical performance"; guest speakers visited classrooms in
January and February sharing experiences "from Kenya to Japan to Peru"; Señor Fermin

## 1b. Service & Civic Engagement

The umbrella service brand is **Knights Serve**, anchored in Micah 6:8.
Source: https://www.charlottechristian.com/campus-life/knights-serve

### Program 1 — Winterim — VERIFIED (from the primary PDF)

- **Scale number: 1.0 credit required for graduation — 0.25 credit per year, every year
 enrolled in upper school.** Exact packet language: "Every upper school student is
 required to participate in a Winterim activity to earn .25 credit (per year) toward the
 total 1.0 graduation requirement." Graded pass/fail, excluded from GPA; "The title
 Winterim with a grade of pass and a .25 credit will appear on the report card each year."
- Week-long experiential program, **Mar. 9–13, 2026**, mixing mission trips, cultural and
 educational travel, internships, and on-campus options; every offering embeds a service
 component.
- **Critical carve-out, verbatim:** "This experience does not count toward additional
 requirements for conservatory or **service hours**." Winterim service is separate from and
 additional to the school's service-hours expectation — and it confirms a service-hours
 requirement exists even though no number is published.
- **19+ distinct 2026 offerings** across Local (A Walk in the Parks, Charlotte Cultural
 Experience, Food and Fun, Man Up!, Serve Charlotte), Regional Overnight (Ladies CLT &
 Mountain Getaway, Varsity Baseball CLT & CHS), Domestic (Camp Harrison, Disney World,
 Florida Fishing), International (Athens Greece, Beauty of Brazil, Birmingham England,
 Brazil Female Athlete Sports Ministry, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Panama, Scotland),
 plus the Alternative Winterim Experience and Vocational Shadowing (**at least 30 hours**
 shadowing a local professional, grades 11–12).
- Named partners embedded in trips: **SCORE International** (Dominican Republic),
 **Samaritan's Feet** (Camp Harrison), **Give Kids the World / Make-A-Wish** (Disney),
 **Surfrider Foundation** and **Save Your Hood** (Greece), **Seacoast Church** and
 **Beds 4 Kids** (Baseball), **Crossroads Christian Academy** (Panama), **Nourish Up,
 Crisis Assistance, Congregations for Kids** (Serve Charlotte).
- Source (primary): https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1754953602/charlotte/xirlf52kj6gxx2pteclb/2026WinterimPacketdocx.pdf
- Corroborating: https://www.charlottechristian.com/academics/curriculum (Winterim = 1 of
 26 units) · https://www.charlottechristian.com/academics/upper-school

### Program 2 — Knights Serve service hours — VERIFIED

- **Scale number: begins in grade 6.** "Beginning in sixth grade, students are encouraged
 to earn service hours each year."
- Framed as teaching students "the joy of selflessly serving others in the name of Jesus
 Christ." Note the verb is **"encouraged," not "required"** — yet the Winterim packet's
 carve-out language ("additional requirements for… service hours") implies a real target
 exists. **No numeric hour figure is published anywhere on the public site.**
- Sources: https://www.charlottechristian.com/campus-life/spiritual-life ·
 https://www.charlottechristian.com/campus-life/knights-serve

### Program 3 — Local service partnerships / Kingdom Impact Day — VERIFIED

- **Scale number: 5 named standing local partners** — **Samaritan's Purse** (Operation
 Christmas Child), **The Odd Sock** (socks for the homeless), **Bright Blessings**
 (baby/hygiene kits, snack bags), **Roof Above** (sandwich prep), **Harvest Center** (item
 collection). A sixth, **Congregations for Kids**, is named on the Knights Serve page.
- **Middle School Kingdom Impact Day** is the division-wide service day, tied to the Middle
 School "Kingdom" system (**six kingdoms**; students are assigned to one and meet routinely
 for team building and community engagement). The Upper School **Cross-Cultural
 Communications** class also serves Charlotte neighbors.
- Sources: https://www.charlottechristian.com/campus-life/spiritual-life (partner list) ·
 https://www.charlottechristian.com/campus-life/knights-serve (Kingdom Impact Day,
 Congregations for Kids, Cross-Cultural Communications) ·
 https://www.charlottechristian.com/academics/middle-school (six kingdoms)

### "Individual activity, not a club" — VERIFIED

The following are real and documented but are **not standalone clubs**, and must be labeled
as such rather than counted in any club roster:

- **Winterim trips** — a graduation requirement and travel program.
- **Kingdom Impact Day** — a division-wide service day.
- **Multicultural Festival** — an annual all-school event (organized by Global Knights).
- **Cross-Cultural Communications service** — a class doing service; explicitly curricular.
- **Vocational Shadowing / Alternative Winterim Experience** — individually arranged and
 family-brokered; explicitly individual.
- **Mission trips** ("local, regional and international… throughout the year") — trips.
- **Heritage month celebrations, S.L.A.M. days, Windy Gap / Carolina Point retreats** —
 programming.
- **Romans 12 Initiative** — a parent/community group, not a student club.

### Gaps — 1b

No total service-hours figure, no participation percentage, no student count or dollar
value of service, no founding year for Knights Serve or Winterim, and no annual service
report. The Knights Serve page advertises "2025-26 Service Projects" but publishes no
detail behind the heading.

### Historical only — 2014, current status UNCONFIRMED

A May 9, 2014 news post documents inductions and names **two societies absent from today's
Upper School page**:

- **National Junior Honor Society — Middle School — 54 students inducted** (grade 8: 6;
 grade 7: 48)
- **National Honor Society — Upper School — 60 students inducted**
- **National French Honor Society — Upper School — 5 students inducted**
- **National Spanish Honor Society — Upper School — 8 students inducted**
- Source: https://www.charlottechristian.com/cf_news/view.cfm?newsid=615

**NJHS and the French society appear only in this 12-year-old post.** NJHS is not on the
Middle School page; French NHS is not on the Upper School page. Neither is reported as
current. This post is also the **only** induction-count data that exists anywhere for this
school — a single 2014 snapshot.

### NOT FOUND — explicitly searched

**Mu Alpha Theta** (math), **Science National Honor Society**, **Quill & Scroll**
(journalism), **Rho Kappa** (social studies), **National English Honor Society**,
**National Latin Honor Society**, **French Honor Society** (current), **NJHS** (current).
Notably the school has a Latin Club and a Model UN but no corresponding Latin or
social-studies honor society, and **no Middle School honor society is published at all
today**.

### Adjacent recognition — VERIFIED (not honor societies; label accordingly)

- **Academic Conservatory Program** — graduation distinction in six tracks (I. Math &
 Science, II. Fine Arts, III. Bible/Philosophy & Religion, IV. English, V. Social Studies,
 VI. World Languages), noted on the transcript in senior year. **From the Class of 2024,
 14% of graduates received at least one conservatory distinction.**
- **Seal of Biliteracy** — implemented 2019; proficiency exam in listening, speaking,
 reading and writing in two languages. **4% of the Class of 2024 earned it.**
- Source: https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1725038322/charlotte/b12msqpqbmeqjqgszan1/2024_USprofile_admiss.pdf

### GAP FLAG — 1c

**Charlotte Christian publishes no honor-society detail beyond the five names.** Absent
site-wide: induction criteria (no GPA threshold for NHS anywhere), chapter activities or
service projects, faculty advisors, chapter officers, meeting information, current
membership counts, annual induction counts (last published 2014), and any dedicated
honor-societies page. The five societies exist solely as a bulleted list on the Upper
School page.
