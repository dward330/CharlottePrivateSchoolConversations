# Charlotte Preparatory School — High School Placement — Placement Outcomes

Charlotte Preparatory School (212 Boyce Road, Charlotte) ends at 8th grade, so every family goes through a second admissions process when their child applies to high school. This file collects what the school itself publishes about how that process turns out: its acceptance rates by class, the ten-year figure, the scholarships graduates have won, and the parent surveys.

### The headline figure: top-two acceptance, by class

The school states the same kind of figure for every class since 2023: the share of graduates accepted to one or both of their top two high school choices. The wording changes slightly from year to year, so each figure is quoted exactly as the school published it.

| Class | Rate as published | Exact wording | Where it was published |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | 97% | "This year, 97% of our graduating 8th graders were accepted into one or both of their top two choices for high school, and 15 students were accepted to every school they applied to!" | Class of 2026 High School Destinations, May 19, 2026, https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/class-of-2026-high-school-destinations |
| 2025 | 96% | "96% of our 2025 graduates were accepted into one or both of their top two choices for high school" | 2024-2025 Annual Report, page 3 (published on Issuu October 27, 2025), https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/2024-2025_annual_report |
| 2024 | 100% | "100% of our 2024 graduates were accepted into one or both of their top two choices for high school" | 2024-2025 Calendar and 2023-2024 Annual Report, page 2 (Issuu, September 23, 2024), https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/2024-2025_calendar_and_annual_report |
| 2024 | 100% | "100% of graduates admitted to one or both of their top two choice high schools in 2024" | Fall 2025 admissions packet, https://charlotteprep.org/uploads/files/Admissions/fall-2025-admissions-packet-web.pdf |
| 2023 | 95% | "95% of our graduates were accepted into their top choice high school in 2023" | 2023-2024 Academic Calendar and 2022-2023 Annual Report, page 6, "2022-2023 Charlotte Prep at a Glance" (Issuu, November 17, 2023), https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/cp03_-_calendar_2023-24_online_version |

**No class size is published alongside any of these rates.** The school never states how many students graduated in a given year, so none of these percentages comes with an official denominator.

The 2023 figure is worded differently from the others. It says "their top choice high school", singular, where every later year says "one or both of their top two choices". Read it as the school's own wording for that year rather than as the same measure.

### How many graduates each class had

The school publishes a named list of each class's high school destinations every May. Counting the names on those lists gives the following. These are our counts from the school's lists, not figures the school states.

| Class | Graduates named on the school's list | Rate the school published | Count consistent with that rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | 39 | 97% | 38 of 39 (97%) |
| 2025 | 27 | 96% | 26 of 27 (96%) |
| 2024 | 29 | 100% | 29 of 29 (100%) |
| 2023 | 20 | 95% (top choice) | 19 of 20 (95%) |

Each rate rounds exactly from the list size, and the alumni counter on the school's Fast Facts page rose by exactly 27 between November 2024 and February 2026, which matches the Class of 2025 list. That is strong evidence that the lists cover each whole class. It is not proof. The school never says "the class of N students", and the same counter rose by 59 between January 2023 and November 2024, while the 2023 and 2024 lists name only 49 graduates.

The 2026 announcement also says 15 students were accepted to every school they applied to. The school gives no denominator for that figure.

### The ten-year figure, and an older version of it

The school publishes a multi-year figure in two versions that do not match. Both are recorded here exactly as written. Neither has a published denominator.

- **96%, "over the past 10 years".** "Over the past 10 years, 96% of Prep graduates were accepted to one or both of their top two choices for high school." This is on the Middle School page, https://charlotteprep.org/academics/middle-school (live October 2026, but absent from the archived copy of that page dated November 24, 2024). The same sentence closes page 1 of the current high school placement brochure, revised in September 2026: "Over the past ten years, 96% of Prep graduates were accepted to one or both of their top two choices for high school." https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/cp-hsbrochure_for_web
- **94%, "since 2015".** "This, combined with the fact that since 2015, 94% of our graduates have received admission to one or both of their top two high school choices, points to the effectiveness of our PreK-8 model and the size of our school." From "The Goldilocks Effect: Choosing the Right Sized School" by Head of School Chris Marblo, February 27, 2024, https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/the-goldilocks-effect-choosing-the-right-sized-school

The two cover different windows and were written about two years apart, so both may have been accurate when written. The school does not publish the year-by-year series behind either one.

### Scholarships graduates have won

**Class of 2025: two merit scholarships.** From the Class of 2025 announcement, May 30, 2025 (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/class-of-2025-high-school-destinations): "Of particular note this year, two students from the class of 2025 have earned prestigious, merit-based scholarships as they head into high school".

- **Grant D. Williams Foundation Scholarship, Providence Day School.** One graduate. The school describes it as an "exclusive four-year scholarship [that] includes a financial award and a personal mentorship with Grant Williams himself, NBA player and Providence Day alum." The destination list labels the same award "Grant Williams Foundation Scholarship".
- **Michaels-Dickson Scholar, McCallie School.** One graduate. "Through a rigorous application and selection process, [the student] earned a merit scholarship that includes tuition support, mentorship, enrichment opportunities, a travel experience, and access to a network of top scholars." The destination list labels it "Michaels-Dickson Merit Scholarship".

**The McCallie record over 16 years.** From "Prep to McCallie—and Beyond", January 21, 2026 (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/prep-to-mccallie-and-beyond): "Over the past 16 years, Charlotte Prep has sent 11 graduates to McCallie, seven of whom earned the highly selective Michaels‑Dickson Merit Scholarship."

The school's own lists show four of those McCallie graduates by class: one each in 2023, 2024 and 2025 (the 2025 graduate being the Michaels-Dickson Scholar above). A parent's account from November 2023 adds that a Class of 2022 graduate also received a merit scholarship to McCallie (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/the-path-to-high-school).

No scholarship dollar amounts and no counts of other merit awards are published.

### How parents say graduates were prepared

- February 27, 2024: "We have been surveying parents of 9th graders for the past two years, and 95% of parents believe their children were extremely or very well prepared for high school." (Goldilocks story, URL above)
- September 3, 2026: "In a survey of alumni parents whose students started high school last year, over 90% said their Prep grad was either very well or extremely well prepared for ninth grade." Head of School Todd Ballaban, https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/a-campus-culture-where-everyone-matters

# Charlotte Preparatory School — High School Placement — The Placement Program

Charlotte Prep's high school search is run by a dedicated placement team. It starts before 8th grade and runs through the year. The school frames this second admissions process as the capstone of its PreK–8 model. Its brochure says "it is so much easier and more effective to choose the right high school as an 8th grader than guessing what it might be at a younger age."

### Who runs it

The school calls the people who run the process the "High School Placement Team". From the school's own pages:

- "Our 8th graders meet individually with our dedicated High School Placement Team to talk about their interests, their abilities, and how to find the right high school fit." (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/beyond-charlotte-prep)
- "Every student (and their family) meets with our dedicated team to prepare." (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/middle-school)

The 2022 edition of the school's placement brochure named the team's three roles: "The placement team, which consists of the head of middle school, the assistant director of placement, and the placement coordinator, guides parents and students through every step of the process." **The September 2026 revision of the same brochure dropped that sentence.** It now says only "The placement team guides parents and students through every step of the process."

What the current staff directory shows (https://charlotteprep.org/about/faculty-staff-directory, 100 people listed, October 2026):

- **Jessica Stargell, Head of Middle School.** The one role from the 2022 team list the directory still names. She also teaches the 8th-grade Leadership class, where students practise interviewing. A student describes "Mrs. Stargell's 8th Grade Leadership class" (November 2025), and her own April 30, 2026 post about the class's presentations says "I have adapted the format…".
- **Julia Walthall-Eisman, Head of Strategic Programs**, new in 2026. Head of School Todd Ballaban wrote on September 3, 2026 that she "is leading key initiatives in high school placement, PreK-8 STEM/Innovation, and artificial intelligence." (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/a-campus-culture-where-everyone-matters)
- **No one in the directory holds the title "assistant director of placement" or "placement coordinator"**, and no directory title contains the word "placement" at all. Whether those two roles still exist under other titles is not published.

### The 12-step path, from the school's brochure

The school's guide "Prep for the Next Chapter: A Guide to the High School Admission Process" sets out the process as twelve numbered steps under the heading "Your Path to High School" (https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/cp-hsbrochure_for_web). The text below is the brochure's own.

| Step | Title | What the school says |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | The Path Begins | Students at Charlotte Prep navigate an exciting path in a known community where they can explore their talents, challenge their minds, broaden their understanding, and develop confidence to prepare them for the next step into high school. |
| 02 | Leadership Development | From early school to middle school, leadership development is a cornerstone of the Charlotte Prep experience. Our four pillars of leadership – respect, integrity, perseverance, and courage – are woven into every aspect of the educational experience. |
| 03 | High School Fair | Each fall, our 8th-grade students and parents have the opportunity to meet with representatives from local and regional schools. Admissions counselors always enjoy meeting our Charlotte Prep scholars and prospective families. |
| 04 | Parent Meetings | Our high school placement team meets with parents throughout the process. Grade level meetings provide clarity about the process. Individual parent meetings highlight each family's unique hopes and dreams as we work together to find the best fit school. |
| 05 | Explore Schools | We encourage parents to visit area schools for open houses and tours. Our relationships with boarding schools offer additional choices for consideration. |
| 06 | Applications | While the application process is parent-driven, our team continues to guide parents to develop a final list of schools that are a good fit for their child. Our team manages all other aspects of the process to minimize stress and provide all necessary materials. |
| 07 | Practice Interviews | Our team provides students with multiple opportunities to practice interview styles, hone skills, and become comfortable with the process. Each practice provides more confidence and experience for our students to shine. |
| 08 | School Visits | Our 8th-grade students are well prepared to visit schools and interact with student peers, faculty, and administrators. These important day visits allow the student a deeper perspective while schools enjoy getting to know our students better. |
| 09 | Prepare for Admissions Testing | Students learn test-taking strategies and prepare for admissions testing to gain comfort and fine tune skills that benefit their preparedness for high school and college testing that follows. |
| 10 | Continued Communication | Our team is closely attuned to each family's path throughout the process whether it is independent, public, or boarding school. We continue to advise and support our families in their choices every step of the way. |

| 12 | Congratulations! | Our graduates are ready to write the next chapter in their educational journey. Prep graduates are poised, mature adolescents solidly prepared for the rigorous demands of high school and ready to explore new opportunities. |

The brochure's opening page adds: "We begin by considering the many high school choices that are available, including independent, boarding, public, and charter schools… We continue to assist and guide parents and students as they submit applications, weigh options, and choose the right high school."

### The placement program, from the school's website

The school's "Beyond Charlotte Prep" page lists the program in its own words (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/beyond-charlotte-prep):

- **Spring before 8th grade.** "Each spring, rising 8th-grade parents attend a Parent Coffee to learn about the admissions process, events, deadlines, and other tips and information to prepare for the year ahead."
- **September of 8th grade.** "In September, administrators from over 40 schools are invited to Charlotte Prep's High School Fair and Boarding School Fair to recruit our students… (The demand is so great for our students, that each year we have to limit the number of schools that we allow to attend!)"
- **One-on-one meetings.** The team meets individually with each 8th grader, and separately "with each 8th grader's parents to discuss their plans, their objectives for their child, and appropriate placement opportunities."
- **Interview preparation.** "In Leadership class, students learn interview etiquette and conduct. They learn about proper dress, eye contact, handshake, and what to look for as they seek the school that fits them best." And: "Teachers and administrators perform mock interviews with students." The brochure adds mock interviews "with our head of school".
- **Paperwork.** "Charlotte Prep electronically delivers our students' complete transcripts and teacher recommendation packets to the schools prior to deadlines."
- **Advocacy.** "In person and by phone, we work with these school officials throughout the school year to advocate for and make the best placements for our 8th-grade students."

The page also says: "Our administrators have relationships with their Admissions Directors, Upper School Heads, and Heads of Schools, and these schools are familiar with the quality of our program."

### The high school fairs

The fair has been described three ways over time. Each version is dated:

- December 7, 2022: "This fall, Prep hosted two high school fairs… Over 25 boarding schools attended our September fair, and our November fair included all of Charlotte's local day schools and several additional boarding schools." (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/top-high-schools-visit-recruit-prep-students)
- The current website (live October 2026, undated) describes one September event: a "High School Fair and Boarding School Fair" with "administrators from over 40 schools" invited.
- The brochure says "Each fall".

The boarding school fair includes a panel discussion. In May 2026, a family credited that panel with their decision to pursue boarding school: "the realization that boarding school was the right fit happened during a panel discussion at Prep's boarding school fair last fall." (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/prep-8th-grader-accepted-at-walnut-hill-school-for-the-arts)

### Admissions testing: the ISEE

The school names one admissions test, the ISEE (Independent School Entrance Exam). Its testing schedule, from "Testing at Charlotte Prep" by Director of Curriculum & Instruction Katherine Buhler, April 9, 2026 (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/testing-at-charlotte-prep):

| Grade | Test | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Grade 7 | Independent School Entrance Exam (ISEE) For Practice | ERB (Education Records Bureau) |
| Grade 8 | Independent School Entrance Exam (ISEE) For High School Placement | ERB (Education Records Bureau) |

**Test prep: "free" appears only in the older brochure.** The 2022 brochure said the school provides "free ISEE test preparation classes". The September 2026 revision says "providing ISEE test preparation", with no "free" and no "classes". Whether the prep is still free is not stated in any current source. A 2025 graduate described "in-depth preparation for the ISEE test and the admissions interviews" (school news story, May 7, 2025).

The SSAT and HSPT are not mentioned anywhere on the school's site.

### What families say about the process

- From a parent, quoted on the school's placement page and in a May 2025 story: "The placement team was an invaluable partner throughout the entire high school selection process. Our family is deeply appreciative of the support we received to make the process as smooth and painless as possible."
- From a parent of a Class of 2022 graduate (November 30, 2023): the team "brilliantly assisted us through every step of the applications themselves", and the "mock interviews with multiple members of the team… gave [our son] practice and valuable feedback." (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/the-path-to-high-school)

### Notes on the list itself

- **The list has 64 entries, but fewer distinct high schools.** The South Carolina Governor's School for the Arts appears twice, once under Boarding and once under Other cities.
