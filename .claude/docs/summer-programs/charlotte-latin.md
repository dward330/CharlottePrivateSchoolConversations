 minus a $50.00 cancellation fee per camp, per camper."

 minus a $100.00 cancellation fee per camp, per camper."

 waitlist."

 sickness."

 May 1, 2026. There is no fee to swap camps if a seat is available."

 not meet our required minimum number of campers. If a camp is canceled by Latin
 Camps due to low enrollment, families will be notified at least one week before the
 first day of camp and refunded in full."

the Latin Camps office will contact you. If you are contacted regarding an opening,
you will have 24 hours to confirm and complete payment."

Age policy: "When registering for camp, your camper's age is calculated as of August 1,
2026. For example, if your camper turns 5 years old in July, you'll be able to register
them for a camp in June for 5 - 7-year-olds, despite only being 4 years old."
