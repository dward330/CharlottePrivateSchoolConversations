### Season shape — Summer 2026

**8 weeks, June 1 – July 31**, breaking the week of June 29 (no Week between
June 22-26 and July 6-10): Week 1 June 1-5 · 2 June 8-12 · 3 June 15-19 ·
4 June 22-26 · 5 July 6-10 · 6 July 13-17 · 7 July 20-24 · 8 July 27-31.
**Early-bird discounts for registration on or before February 27.**

### The 2026 camp slate — 20 camps named on the page

The Great Covenant Day Bake Off · Girls Cheerleading · Art Discovery · College
Admissions · Boys Football · Basketball · Baseball · **Camp Lion** (traditional
day camp) · **Cub Camp** · Little Lion Camp · Co-Ed Soccer · Science, Engineering
& More · Pickleball · Nerf Camp · Middle School Musical Theater · Lady Lions

**Count reconciliation:** the school's marketing says "29 camps"; the public page
enumerates 20. The app uses the enumerable **20**, with the discrepancy noted —
per-week variants and portal-only camps likely account for the difference.

### Grades and care

Grades **JK/TK–12** across the slate (Little Lion/Cub Camp at the bottom, College
Admissions and Strength & Conditioning for HS at the top). Wrap-around care:

- **Before Care $50/week, 7:30–8:45 a.m.** (TK-8)
- **After Care $50/week, 4:00–5:30 p.m.** (TK-8)
- **Lunch Care $30/week, 12–1 p.m.**
- **Camp Lion (Full Day) / Cub Camp include Before & After Care free** (and Lunch
 Care; Musical Theater for MS includes Lunch Care).

Care span: **7:30 a.m. – 5:30 p.m.**

### Fees and policies (2023 brochure, most recent policy document)

- $20 exchange fee; cancellation: 100% less $35 processing before May 1; 50%
 refund after May 1 (≥14 days out); non-refundable inside 14 days.
- Sibling discount on Camp Lion/Cub Camp: **$50 off half-day / $75 off full-day**
 for each additional registration.
- Registration closes noon the Friday before each camp week.
- Camps are open to the community; grade = rising grade.

### Pricing

Current third-party range: **"$170–$325"** per week. The 2018 catalog (documented
sample, dated): e.g. Baseball $185/week half-day, Basketball $150 half / $250
full-day 4-day, Auto Survival 101 $85 (2-day) / $195 (5-day). Current per-camp
prices are portal-gated; the app's cost planner uses the third-party range marked
as an estimate plus the school-published care add-ons above.
