### ⚠️ The department count is 11, and the spread is real

The guide's table of contents lists **12 sections**, but one of them is **Dual Enrollment**
— two CPCC courses taken off-campus for college credit, not a CCHS teaching department.
The school's site navigation lists **10**, because it folds **Options Program/Pathways** in
with student services rather than listing it as an academic department. **This record ships
11**: the 12 guide sections minus Dual Enrollment. Each of the 11 is a department that
employs CCHS faculty and grants CCHS credit.

**Total core credits required: 20.5** · **Additional course credits required: 3.5** ·
**Total credits required to graduate: 24.0.** Students take **7 classes per day**; courses
meet every day. One-half credit is given for a semester course successfully completed.

Note the school profile states **28 credits are taken over 4 years** — that is credits
*earned* at 7 classes/year, against a *minimum* of 24 required to graduate. Both figures
are correct and are not in conflict.

## Placement and rigor rules

- Students recommended for Honors or AP courses should seek input from counselor and
 teachers; teacher placement recommendations are guided by departmental standards.
- A level change may not be available once the master schedule has been created.
- All courses listed will be taught **unless there is insufficient enrollment**.
