Boys Basketball Shonn Brown — 400+ wins; coached Stephen Curry; also Upper School
Principal (long-tenured continuity).
Baseball legacy The late Greg Simmons built a 17-title baseball power.
Athletic Department Leadership
Charlotte Christian's athletics are led by Athletic Director Andrew Ross, within a school whose Head of
School, Barry Giller, also serves as President of the NCISAA Board of Directors — placing a Charlotte
Christian leader at the head of the state's governing body for independent-school athletics. [Sources:
Charlotte Christian Athletics; Charlotte Christian Leadership page]
Football — Chris James (head coach since 2023)
James is a 2008 Charlotte Christian alumnus who played under his predecessor before playing college
football at the University of Richmond and earning an exercise-science degree from UNC Charlotte. He was
on the CCS staff from 2010–2016, left to be the founding head coach at Covenant Day School, served as
offensive coordinator at Ardrey Kell and Myers Park, then returned as CCS offensive coordinator before
taking the head job in 2023 — a homegrown-continuity hire. In his second season (2025) the Knights
reached the NCISAA Division I state final. [Sources: WCNC; The Charlotte Weekly]
He succeeded Jason Estep, one of the most accomplished coaches in school history: 138–42 across 16
seasons (2007–2022) with 8 NCISAA state championships, two-time National Christian School Athletic
Association Coach of the Year, and a former Kansas City Royals strength coach who played baseball at
Eastern Kentucky. Estep coached 96 future college athletes and two future NFL draftees. [Sources: WCNC;
CoachComm; The Charlotte Weekly]
Boys Basketball — Shonn Brown
Shonn Brown is a continuity anchor: a 400+ win head coach who also serves as the school's Upper School
Principal, and who famously coached Stephen Curry during Curry's Charlotte Christian career. His dual role
as principal and long-tenured head coach reflects unusual program stability. [Sources: Shonn Brown
professional profile; Charlotte Christian]

The NCISAA NIL Policy (Applies to Charlotte Christian)
In February 2024 the North Carolina Independent Schools Athletic Association — which governs roughly
94–100 private schools including Charlotte Christian — approved a policy allowing its student-athletes to
profit from their name, image, and likeness while retaining eligibility, effective the 2024–25 school year.
[Sources: HighSchoolOT; On3]
Key rules:
(cid:127) NIL activity must be kept SEPARATE from interscholastic participation; athletes may not use the school's
name/marks or appear in uniform in NIL deals.
(cid:127) Prohibited categories include cannabis, alcohol, gambling, adult entertainment, drugs, and tobacco.
(cid:127) All NIL agreements must be reported to and approved by the NCISAA state office.
(cid:127) School personnel may not use NIL to recruit/enroll students or act as an athlete's agent.
(cid:127) [Sources: On3; Lewis Brisbois sports-law analysis]
The Contrast: NC Public Schools
Public-school NIL took the opposite path: blocked by the legislature, banned by the State Board of

NCISAA schools like Charlotte Christian had an NIL window their public-school counterparts did not.
[Sources: HighSchoolOT; RallyFuel NC NIL guide]
