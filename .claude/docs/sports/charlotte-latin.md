The Broadway Era — a Continuity Benchmark
Jimmy Broadway, a Wake Forest graduate and four-year ACC basketball letterman, joined Charlotte Latin in
1986 as Physical Plant Director and became Athletic Director in 1993, serving 20 years until his 2012
retirement. Under his leadership, Charlotte Latin's teams won 8 consecutive Wells Fargo Cups (the
NCISAA's overall-excellence award). He served 15 years on the NCISAA Board of Managers, was inducted
into the NCISAA Hall of Fame, and received the NC Athletic Directors Association's Lifetime Achievement
Award. [Source: NCISAA Hall of Fame]
