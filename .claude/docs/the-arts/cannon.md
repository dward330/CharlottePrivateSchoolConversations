The Arts at Cannon School
Cannon School (JrK-12) - Concord, NC - Parent-Facing Deep Research Report
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school arts programs. Prepared July 2026. Draft for family review.
Executive Summary
Cannon School offers a comprehensive, philosophically coherent JrK-12 arts program built on student
choice and voice. Its signature is a fully choice-based (TAB) visual arts curriculum in every division,
complemented by a distinctive Creative Design & Engineering track that fuses art with digital
fabrication, a broad instrumental and vocal music program rooted in the Orff Approach, and a theater
program that treats technical craft as seriously as performance. Facilities are a genuine strength: the
2014 Cindric Performing Arts Center and Campbell Gallery give students professional-grade spaces.
For a family weighing arts opportunity, Cannon's strongest differentiators are (1) breadth of
upper-school electives across fine art, media, and design-engineering; (2) real-world/community
project work in Media Arts; and (3) purpose-built performance and exhibition venues.
Curricular Depth & Progression
The program is intentionally sequential. Choice-based art begins in junior kindergarten and matures
into portfolio-driven honors and AP work. Music scaffolds from Orff play-based learning to auditioned
jazz, chamber strings, and music-technology composition. Theater moves from storytelling and
improvisation to Stanislavski-based acting, directing, and festival competition. The three-part
upper-school visual arts structure (Studio, Media, CDE) is unusually broad for a school of Cannon's
size.
Signature Programs
(cid:127) Creative Design & Engineering (CAD, CNC, laser cutting, 3D printing, electronics) - art/STEM fusion.
(cid:127) Media Arts Senior Seminar - year-long non-profit client project with a public presentation of learning.
(cid:127) Cannon Theater Company - public productions and NC Theatre Conference festival participation.
(cid:127) Jazz Program - regular Charlotte-area gigs and regional festival appearances.
Facilities as a Differentiator
The Cindric Performing Arts Center (11,486 sq ft, 450 movable seats, thrust stage, student-run control
room) and the adjoining Campbell Gallery mean students perform, exhibit, and run production
technology in spaces designed for them - not repurposed multipurpose rooms.
Questions for a Campus Visit

outcomes?
(cid:127) What is the audition/entry path for Jazz Combo, Varsity ensembles, and Theater in Performance?
(cid:127) How frequently do Media Arts students secure non-profit partners, and what have recent projects
produced?
(cid:127) What are the annual production and concert calendars, and how are lead roles / chair placements
decided?
(cid:127) How is the CDE track staffed and equipped, and can non-artists enter it?
Caveats
This report synthesizes Cannon's public arts pages (2025-26) and the school's course catalog. Specific
course titles, prerequisites, and offerings change year to year; families should confirm current details
with the school and the latest course catalog (see Sources).

Cindric Performing Arts Center (CPAC)
Built in 2014 and fully funded by gifts from more than 400 families as part of the $7.4 million "Building
Bright Futures" campaign, the 11,486-square-foot Cindric Performing Arts Center serves as both a
performance space and a community gathering spot. It seats up to 450 in movable, reconfigurable
chairs and features a flat (rather than sloped) floor so it can host dances, dinners, and exhibits in
addition to shows.
The Leck Family Performing Arts Stage
(cid:127) A "thrust stage" extending into the audience on three sides for an intimate setting.
(cid:127) Raised to 42 inches for sightlines, with a front lift and rear ramp/backstage corridor for accessibility.
(cid:127) An overhead structural grid for changing sets and reconfiguring curtains, plus a catwalk for rigging
lighting.
(cid:127) Hardwood stage (painted black) that lets crews anchor multiple sets safely; boom speakers beneath
the stage for effects.
Campbell Gallery
The light-filled Campbell Gallery greets visitors entering CPAC. It hosts art exhibitions and events,
doubles as theater overflow (events can be broadcast from the theater to the foyer), and can be set for
formal seated functions.
Sound, Acoustics & Production Technology
(cid:127) Curved overhead "clouds" in two orientations distribute sound to the rear and sides of the house.
(cid:127) Angled wood wall panels aid sound distribution; fabric-wrapped acoustical panels trap echo.
(cid:127) A state-of-the-art AV system with a rear control room designed for students to run the technical side
of productions, plus projector and screen.
(cid:127) A dedicated media production room supports the Media Arts program.

Lower School: The Orff Approach
Lower school music uses the Orff Approach, where children "begin with what they do instinctively:
play." Imitation, experimentation, and personal expression build confident, life-long musicians and
creative problem solvers.
Middle School Ensembles
Students choose Chorus, Band, or Strings and build mastery through consistent rehearsal, studying
music across genres and performing in 2-3 concerts per year. Private lessons are recommended but
not required.
(cid:127) Band - 5th, 6th, and combined 7th-8th grade bands (woodwind, brass, percussion).
(cid:127) Chorus - 5th-6th and 7th-8th grade choruses; three-part singing and music theory by 7th-8th.
(cid:127) Orchestra (Strings) - 5th, 6th, and 7th-8th grade string orchestras with required formal performances.
Upper School Music Courses
(cid:127) Cannon School Jazz Combo (audition; modern jazz, heavy on theory and improvisation).
(cid:127) Concert Chorus (mixed-choir literature, vocal technique, several concerts per year).
(cid:127) Modern Ensemble (rock/R&B/rap; multi-track recording via Soundtrap).
(cid:127) Music Technology (composition and multi-track recording; open to novices) with a Music Technology
II option.
(cid:127) String Ensemble & String Ensemble II (chamber and honors-level orchestral repertoire).
(cid:127) Upper School Band (concert band, big band, and chamber ensembles).
Ensembles, Clubs & the Jazz Program
Beyond the schedule, students join Varsity Band (grades 6-12, meets before school), Varsity Chorus
(auditioned upper-school singers), and Varsity Orchestra (competitive, seat auditions). The Jazz
Program gigs regularly on campus and around Charlotte and has performed at local and regional jazz
festivals "to rave reviews."

The Arts at Cannon School
Cannon School (JrK-12) - Concord, NC - Program Overview
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school arts programs. Prepared July 2026. Draft for family review.
At a Glance
Cannon School delivers a JrK-12 arts program organized around three pillars: Visual Arts, Music, and
Theater, supported by dedicated Arts Facilities anchored by the Cindric Performing Arts Center. The
program is philosophically distinctive: the visual arts curriculum is fully choice-based across every
division, built on the nationally recognized Teaching for Artistic Behavior (TAB) model that Cannon
adopted school-wide in 2016-2017.
The arts are woven through the lower, middle, and upper schools with progressively more specialized
coursework. Younger students rotate through arts disciplines; by upper school, students can pursue AP
Studio Art, media production, jazz, chamber strings, and technical theater.
What Sets Cannon Apart
(cid:127) Choice-based (TAB) visual arts in all divisions - students select medium, subject, and direction,
producing portfolios of individual voice rather than uniform assignments.
(cid:127) Creative Design & Engineering track blending art with CAD, woodworking, electronics, 3D printing,
laser cutting, and CNC milling - an art-technology fusion many peer schools lack.
(cid:127) Media Arts & Productions with real-world client work: upper-level students produce content for
local non-profits as part of a senior seminar.
(cid:127) Purpose-built performing arts venue (Cindric Performing Arts Center, 2014) with a 450-seat
reconfigurable thrust-stage theater and the adjoining Campbell Gallery.
(cid:127) Music grounded in the Orff Approach in lower school, scaling to jazz combo, chamber strings,
modern/pop ensemble, and music technology in upper school.
How the Program Progresses
Lower School (JrK-4)
Choice-based visual art from junior kindergarten; Orff-based general music emphasizing play, imitation,
and improvisation; a parent-supported Art Appreciation Program adds exposure to masterworks.
Middle School (5-8)
Fifth and sixth graders rotate through Visual Art, General Music, Theater, and small-group instrumental
lessons. Band, Chorus, and Orchestra run as ensembles with 2-3 concerts per year. Seventh and
eighth grade visual art is a full-year TAB studio; eighth graders stage a scripted theatrical production.
Upper School (9-12)
Three visual arts programs (Studio Arts, Media Arts & Productions, Creative Design & Engineering), a
full music slate (Jazz Combo, String Ensemble, Concert Chorus, Band, Modern Ensemble, Music
Technology), and a theater sequence (Acting 1-2, Theater in Performance, Stagecraft 1-2) feeding the
Cannon Theater Company.

Cannon School - Theater
Cannon School (JrK-12) - Concord, NC - Theater
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school arts programs. Prepared July 2026. Draft for family review.
Program Philosophy
"Storytelling is at the core of Cannon's theater program." Students develop as performers while
learning to work in a creative team - and the program deliberately values the students who never set
foot on stage, spending months building sets, designing lighting and sound, and costuming actors.
Middle School Theater
Fifth and sixth graders (roughly a quarter-long rotation) learn group collaboration and storytelling
fundamentals. Seventh and eighth grade theater is full-year: improvisation and theatrical vocabulary in
seventh grade; character and text work in eighth, culminating in a scripted production.
Upper School Theater Courses
(cid:127) Acting 1 - fundamental actor skills through monologue and scene study.
(cid:127) Acting 2 - Stanislavski-based technique plus an introduction to directing (prereq Acting 1).
(cid:127) Theater in Performance - for advanced students; includes the NC Theatre Conference (NCTC) High
School Play Festival.
(cid:127) Stagecraft & Stagecraft 2 - lighting, sound, set/costume design, and stage management; student-led
production teams.
Cannon Theater Company (CTC)
CTC gives upper-school students the full theatrical experience - auditioning, rehearsing, acting, A/V
tech, set building, and directing - in productions that are open to the public. Technical-theater students
may run tech for CTC shows. The company competes in the NCTC High School Play Festival each fall
and mounts spring performances.

Cannon School - Visual Arts
Cannon School (JrK-12) - Concord, NC - Visual Arts
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school arts programs. Prepared July 2026. Draft for family review.
Philosophy: Teaching for Artistic Behavior (TAB)
Cannon's guiding premise is that "there is an inner artist within all of us." The curriculum uses Teaching
for Artistic Behavior (TAB), a nationally recognized, choice-based approach in which students make
authentic decisions about what to create, what to explore, and which medium to use. After a pilot in
2015-2016, all visual arts classes in all divisions became choice-based in 2016-2017. The school
reports that student engagement "skyrocketed" under the model.
Lower School Visual Art
From junior kindergarten, children explore the elements and principles of art in 2-D and 3-D through
verbal, visual, and tactile means, working in a choice-based model. Media include drawing, painting,
collage, paper sculpture, clay, and fiber art. A parent-run Art Appreciation Program adds exposure to
masterpieces.
Middle School Visual Art
Fifth and sixth grade art is quarter-long, rotating with other arts disciplines; seventh and eighth grade
art is full-year. Studios include drawing, painting, printmaking, ceramics, and sculpture.
(cid:127) 5th-6th Grade Visual Art - rotational studios; students select a "WOW" (Wonderfully Original Work)
with an artist statement.
(cid:127) 7th-8th Grade Visual Art - full-choice TAB; no two student pieces alike.
(cid:127) 7th-8th Photography & Tech Art Fusion - camera composition plus ThinkTank fabrication.
(cid:127) 7th-8th Creative Explorations - writing + visual art, curating a personal portfolio across genres.
(cid:127) Arts Magazine - a cross-media publication combining writing, visual art, music, and photography.
Upper School: Three Studio Programs
Studio Arts
Student-driven fine-art practice across drawing, painting, printmaking, sculpture, and digital
painting/illustration. Sequence: Visual Foundations, Studio Arts I, Studio Arts II Honors, Studio Arts III
Honors, and AP Studio Art (2-D Design, 3-D Design, or Drawing portfolios).
Media Arts & Productions
Commercial practice - graphic design, illustration, photo manipulation, and video. Courses: Media Arts I
& II, Media Productions, and a Media Arts Senior Seminar in which students partner with community
non-profits on a year-long media project culminating in a presentation of learning.
Creative Design & Engineering (CDE)
Art-technology fusion using CAD, woodworking, metalworking, electronics, physical computing,
textiles, 3D printing, coding, laser cutting, and CNC milling. A three-level sequence (CDE I-III); CDE III
emphasizes large-scale, service-oriented projects.
