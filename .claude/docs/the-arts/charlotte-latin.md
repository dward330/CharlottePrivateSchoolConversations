The Arts at Charlotte Latin School
Charlotte Latin School (TK-12, non-sectarian) - Charlotte, NC - Program Overview
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school arts programs. Prepared July 2026. Draft for family review.
At a Glance
Charlotte Latin - a non-sectarian, coeducational, college-prep day school serving 1,500+ students
TK-12 - treats the arts as "integral to our learning and life experiences." Over 80% of students
participate in the arts. The multidisciplinary program is organized into Visual Arts and Performing
Arts, progressing from foundational skills in Lower School to advanced performances and portfolio
work in Upper School.
What Stands Out
(cid:127) 80%+ participation - arts are a mainstream part of the Latin experience, not a niche.
(cid:127) The Horne Performing Arts Center, described as the "creative heartbeat" of campus.
(cid:127) A Fab Lab that arts students use for cross-disciplinary work (e.g., a student-designed, sewn, and
photographed fashion portfolio).
(cid:127) Arts Walk - a permanent collection of art installed across campus.
(cid:127) A dedicated Student Art Gallery and a full Arts Calendar of concerts, productions, and special
events.
(cid:127) Regional and national student recognition, including the Scholastic Art & Writing Awards and
All-State Honors ensembles.
Leadership
The program is led by faculty who are practicing artists, including Alicia Long (chair of performing arts)
and Richard Fletcher (chair of visual arts), fostering what the school describes as a dynamic, inclusive
environment where bold expression is celebrated.
Journey Across Divisions
Lower School builds foundational technique; Middle School emphasizes exploration of mediums and
self-directed projects; Upper School moves to advanced performance and portfolio development - a
continuous creative arc TK-12.

"Visual art is everywhere" at Latin - lining hallways and filling studios. Students move from foundational
techniques in Lower School to self-directed projects in Middle and Upper School, encouraged to

in the National Scholastic Art & Writing Awards - illustrating how studio art, design, and technology
intersect at Latin.
