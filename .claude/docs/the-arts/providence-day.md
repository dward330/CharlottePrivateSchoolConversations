The Arts at Providence Day School
Providence Day School (TK-12) - Charlotte, NC - Program Overview
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school arts programs. Prepared July 2026. Draft for family review.
At a Glance
Providence Day School (PD) runs a TK-12 arts program spanning Visual Arts and Performing Arts
(music and theater), with the Upper School program described by the school as "comprehensive and
award-winning." Offerings scale from lower-school foundations to Advanced Placement coursework -
AP Studio Art, AP Art History, and AP Music Theory - and a full slate of ensembles and productions.
Arts life is anchored by the McMahon Fine Arts complex (including the McMahon Theatre and Fine Arts
Foyer) and enriched by a monthly "Spotlight on the Arts" series during Upper School lunch, master
classes, and visiting artists. A parent-supported "Friends of the Arts" group backs the program.
What Stands Out
(cid:127) Award-winning performing arts with repeated recognition in the regional Blumey Awards
(Blumenthal Performing Arts).
(cid:127) AP pathways in three arts disciplines: AP Studio Art, AP Art History, and AP Music Theory.
(cid:127) A working darkroom for film photography alongside digital and unconventional-materials studio
work.
(cid:127) Active membership/participation in national and state bodies: NAfME, ACDA, and the NC Theatre
Conference (NCTC).
(cid:127) Tri-M Music Honor Society chapter; auditioned groups such as A Cappella and the ChargHers.
(cid:127) Monthly Spotlight on the Arts performances plus visiting-artist master classes.
Across the Divisions
Lower School
Foundational visual and performing arts, including a lower-school musical (recent season: "Jungle
Book Kids").
Middle School
Continued visual art and performing ensembles with a middle-school musical and honors opportunities
(e.g., NCAIS honors ensembles).
Upper School
Musical ensembles, AP Music Theory, a wide range of theater and technical theater classes, and
studio/AP visual arts, with extracurricular plays, musicals, and ensembles.

Program Emphasis
PD's Visual Arts courses emphasize fundamental processes while pushing students to be "original,
resourceful, and innovative." Students work across studio media - from traditional drawing and painting
to darkroom film photography and unconventional materials - developing the skills, discipline, and
self-confidence of practicing artists.
Advanced & AP Study
Upper School students can pursue AP Studio Art and AP Art History. The annual AP Art Show
showcases portfolio work, which the school frames around creativity, reflection, and connection. A
sequence of studio courses (e.g., Art I / Art II and beyond) builds toward these advanced offerings.
