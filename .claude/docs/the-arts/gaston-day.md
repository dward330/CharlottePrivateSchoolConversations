## Facilities (S1)

**Pamela Kimbrell Warlick Visual & Performing Arts Center** — **550 seats**, modern
lighting and sound systems, and a **professional dance studio**.

## Performing arts (S1)

**Theatre / Drama**

- "Theatre Arts engages students in the process of drama," developing communication and
 critical-thinking skills.
- **"Drama classes begin as early as the second grade."**
- **"Two major shows each year, including Gaston Day School's spring musical."**
- Students participate on stage, as stage managers, on crew, or in design roles.
- **Actors' Conservatory Theatre** operates as a dedicated programme.

**Music**

- Curriculum covers "singing, movement, improvisation, reading, writing, and performing."
- **Middle School:** instrumental ensemble — **woodwinds, brass, percussion, piano,
 guitar** — plus **Middle School Chorus**. Private lessons available.
- **Upper School: Vocal Performance Ensemble.**
- **Private lessons** are taught by contracted professional musicians (music lessons billed
 at **$35 per lesson** — see the financial-aid file).

**Performance travel (S1).** The programme has performed at **St Mary-le-Bow** and
**Canterbury Cathedral**, and given joint concerts with the **Westminster Youth Orchestra**.

## Visual arts (S1, S2)

- Upper School instruction covers **drawing, painting, ceramics, printmaking, and
 portfolio development**.
- Course sequence (S2): **Visual Art I, II, III** and **Visual Art IV (H)**.
- Student achievements: **Scholastic art awards**; wins at national, regional and local
 competitions.

## Recognition (S1, S2)

- **Chorus students earned "superior" ratings in regional adjudications over six years**
 (S1).
- Students have gained admission to the **North Carolina School of the Arts** and to
 **Governor's School** (S1). *(UNC School of the Arts also appears on the school's
 2019–2025 college acceptance list — see the college-support file.)*
- **Numerous Scholastic Art Awards** (S1).
- **Blutopia**, the student literary and art magazine — "entirely developed, created, and
 published by the students themselves," carrying short stories, essays, poetry,
 illustrations, paintings and photographs. Recognised repeatedly at **state and national**
 level, including **numerous Scholastic Art and Writing Awards**, and consistently strong
 in the **North Carolina Scholastic Media Association's Literary Magazine Contest** (S2,
 verbatim).

## By division

- **Early/Lower School (PS–4)** — music, art and drama lessons **coordinated with classroom
 units of study** (S4). Drama begins in **grade 2** (S1).
- **Middle School (5–8)** — students select electives from **art, drama, chorus and band**
 (S3); MS Instrumental Ensemble and MS Chorus (S1).
- **Upper School (9–12)** — Visual Art I–IV(H), Band, Concert Choir, Drama, Vocal
 Performance Ensemble; two major productions a year including the spring musical (S1, S2).
