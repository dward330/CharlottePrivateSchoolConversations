### Honest read on where CCD sits in the Blumey field

- CCD is a **consistent, credible Blumey contender rather than a current front-runner.** Across
 2023–2026 it converted a strong nomination haul into **zero** wins, and the trend line points
 down: 9ish nominations (2023) → 9ish (2024) → 5, all individual-performance (2025) → **0**
 (2026, in a 59-school field). **[VERIFIED]**
- Its high-water mark is genuinely strong: **two wins in 2022 including Best Direction**, the
 category that most reflects program quality rather than individual student talent.
- Its peer set is instructive: Charlotte Latin and Charlotte Christian appear in the same
 categories most years, and the perennial winners are large public magnets and arts schools
 (Central Academy of Technology & Arts, Northwest School of the Arts, Weddington,
 North Lincoln, Providence, Hough). CCD competes with those programs on nominations without
 usually beating them on trophies.
- The **individual-performance strength is real and repeatable** — Sabrina McGovern was
 nominated in three consecutive cycles (2023 Best Actress, 2024 Best Supporting Actress,
 2025 Best Actress). That is a signal about coaching of principals. **[VERIFIED]**

## 1e. Verdict

### Why the CCD arts program holds up (concrete, sourced)

1. **The facility is genuinely top-tier for a K–12 school, and it's verified, not marketing.**
 A 45,730 sq ft dedicated fine arts center with four studios, a two-story dance studio, a
 music suite with practice rooms, a Black Box Theater *and* a separate 400-seat proscenium
 house (Gorelick) means performance, rehearsal and studio work never compete for the same
 room. **[VERIFIED]**

2. **Two wet darkrooms.** Almost no peer K–12 program still runs film. Two darkrooms plus a
 ceramics/3-D studio with wheels and kilns plus sculpture in glass and metal means the
 "hands in the material" side of art hasn't been traded away for iPads. **[VERIFIED]**

3. **A real dual AP-and-IB arts ceiling.** Students can top out via AP (Sculpture, Music Theory)
 *or* IB (Theatre Arts, IB art courses). Most competitors offer one framework, not both — this
 matters for a student who wants portfolio-driven AP work or the IB's research-and-process
 emphasis. **[VERIFIED]**

4. **Externally validated theatre, at the highest level at least once.** Two Blumey wins in 2022
 including **Best Direction**, plus back-to-back NCTC state championships (*The Crane Wife*),
 in a state festival drawing 100+ schools. Nominated every year 2022–2025 in a 54–59 school
 Blumey field. **[VERIFIED]**

5. **Individual performer development is demonstrably repeatable** — the same student
 (Sabrina McGovern) earned lead-category Blumey nominations in three straight cycles, and CCD
 placed five different students in five performance categories in 2025. That reflects coaching
 depth, not one lucky cohort. **[VERIFIED]**

6. **Artist in Residence produces permanent work.** Bronze sculptures, murals, mosaics and
 commissioned music scores made *with* visiting professionals is a materially different
 experience from a guest-speaker assembly. **[VERIFIED as described by the school]**

7. **The strongest single credential is about the teaching, not the trophies: NCTC named Jenny
 Goodfellow North Carolina's 2022 K-12 Theatre Arts Educator.** Verified on NCTC's own awards
 page, not the school's. A statewide professional body picked CCD's theatre director out of
 every K-12 theatre teacher in the state. **[VERIFIED]**

8. **Three dedicated drama directors, one per division** (Maggart LS / Mize MS / Goodfellow US),
 with 7th graders staging *Henry V* and *Julius Caesar*. The JK–12 pipeline is staffed, not
 just asserted. **[VERIFIED]**

### Where a parent should press — sharp tour questions

1. **"Your site says 5 NCTC state championships. Which five years, and which plays? I can only
 find *The Crane Wife*."** The championship count is the program's biggest claim and the
 least documented. Ask which are recent versus 1990s-era — a 1998 title means something very
 different from a 2024 one.

2. **"CCD had zero Blumey nominations in 2026 for *Frozen*, after 5 in 2025 and about 9 each in
 2023 and 2024. What changed?"** Ask directly whether the creative team turned over, whether
 *Frozen* was chosen as an accessible/large-cast show on purpose, and what the theatre
 staffing looks like now. This is the single most revealing question in the file.

3. **"You've been nominated every year since 2022 but haven't won since 2022. Is the program
 aiming at competitive placement, or at participation breadth?"** Both are legitimate answers —
 with "100% theatre participation," breadth may genuinely be the strategy. Make them say so
 rather than implying trophies.

4. **"Which of the 8 ensembles require an audition, and can a beginner still join band or
 orchestra in 9th grade?"** The published pages never state audition requirements. Ask whether
 Honors Orchestra/Band/Choir are audition-gated and whether the non-honors tier is a real
 home for a late starter or a holding pen.

5. **"How many Scholastic Art & Writing Gold and Silver Keys did students earn last year, and how
 many went national?"** The school cites Scholastic recognition without numbers. Real programs
 can produce the count instantly.

6. **"Who chairs the arts department, and how many arts faculty are full-time versus shared
 across divisions?"** Only three arts faculty (Goodfellow, Rydel, Utley) are publicly named,
 and no chair is listed. Staffing depth is what makes a 45,730 sq ft building actually work.

7. **"What's this year's fall one-act and spring play, and did the one-act advance past NCTC
 regionals?"** The 2025–26 fall and spring titles aren't published anywhere findable — and
 whether the one-act advanced is the live measure of the NCTC pipeline.

Charlotte Country Day School (JK-12) - Charlotte, NC - Program Overview
Compiled research note - Charlotte private school arts programs. Prepared July 2026. Draft for family review.
At a Glance
At Country Day the arts are positioned as "at the heart" of a JK-12 education rather than an add-on,
spanning visual art, vocal and instrumental music, and drama across three campuses. The Upper
School offers an average of 39 fine arts courses per year - from Concert Choir and Symphonic Band to
AP Sculpture and IB Theatre Arts - reflecting the school's International Baccalaureate framework.
Every year students in grades JK-12 present roughly 70 performances, musicals, concerts, and
exhibits, and 100% of students participate in theater at every grade level.

(cid:127) ~70 play performances, musicals, concerts, and exhibits presented each year (JK-12).
(cid:127) 31+ Blumey Award nominations and 5 NC Theatre Conference State Championship wins (Upper
School Theater).
(cid:127) 20+ state and regional music competitions/events where students earn recognition.
(cid:127) 8 vocal and instrumental groups in Middle and Upper School (orchestra, band, choir, a cappella).
(cid:127) ~39 average Upper School fine arts courses offered per year.

Artist in Residence Program

Charlotte Country Day - Visual Arts

Program
The visual arts program develops students' talents at all levels, JK-12. Upper School offerings extend
to AP-level work (including AP Sculpture) and IB visual arts, supported by studio spaces for drawing,
painting, ceramics/3-D, photography (with darkrooms), and computer graphics.
Studios & Media
(cid:127) Four visual arts studios plus child-scaled studios in the Lower School.
(cid:127) A photography studio with two darkrooms for film work.
(cid:127) A ceramics/3-D studio for hand-building, wheel throwing, glazing, and firing.
(cid:127) A computer graphics classroom and a student publication center.
Exhibitions & Service
Student work is exhibited in the Hance Family Gallery and the Dalton Gallery, and featured in the
annual JK-12 All-School Art Show. The Art Honor Society has also led community-service projects (for
example, handmade welcome gifts for residents moving into permanent housing).
