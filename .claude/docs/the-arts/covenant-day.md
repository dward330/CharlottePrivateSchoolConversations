### Verdict-level synthesis

Covenant Day's arts program is a **JK/TK-12 ladder with a worship-music spine and a
theatre program with a real regional award history**. Its distinctive facts:

- A **Blumey ledger spanning 2013-2019** (two wins — Best Featured Performer 2013,
 Student Critic Award 2016 — plus six more nominations including Best Actress for
 *Curtains*, 2019) and a **2025 National Shakespeare Competition finalist**
 (Caroline Garbarino).
- **Worship bands at both MS and HS**, plus a credit-bearing Worship Leadership
 course — the clearest arts expression of the school's church-ministry identity,
 and a rung no other school in this project has.
- A clean visual path **Intro to Art → Art 2/3 → Honors Studio → AP Studio Art**
 with a public AP exhibit (Art Talk) and an all-school exhibit (ArtWalk).
- **Advanced coursework = AP Studio Art + Honors Studio Art** (plus Honors
 Auditioned Theatre on the performing side) — 2 advanced visual-arts courses, 3
 counting theatre.
- A 200-seat **Lions Theater** and a patron organization (Covenant Arts
 Association, $100-$1,000 tiers) that pre-sells its three annual productions.

### What is deliberately not claimed

- No arts participation percentage is published.
- No NCMEA/All-State music honors (confirmed absent from the music page).
- Production titles beyond *Curtains* are not published — the theater page lists
 categories (MS Musical, HS Musical, Spring Drama), and the calendar dates them
 without naming shows.
- No dance program exists; no orchestra/strings program (band + choir + worship
 only) — the ensemble count (~9-10) reflects that shape.

### Counts the app uses

- Program span: **JK-12** (art integrated JK-K, sequential from grade 1).
- Ensembles: **9 named ensembles** (Children's Choir 3-5, MS Band, MS Choir, MS
 Worship Band, Symphonic Band, HS Choir, HS Worship Band, HS A Cappella, plus
 grade-5 intro ensembles) + Tri-M Honor Society.
- Signature recognition: the Blumey ledger (2 wins, 8 total recognitions,
 2013-2019).
- Advanced coursework: AP Studio Art + Honors Studio Art.

# Covenant Day School — The Arts — Facilities

> **Provenance:** Compiled by Claude Code on 2026-08-15 from the school's own pages
> (theater/venue details surfaced via site search of covenantday.org).

### Sources

- https://www.covenantday.org/arts/theater
- https://www.covenantday.org/arts/arts-association (productions staged and
 ticketed per year)

### The venue

**Lions Theater** — the school's performance house: **200 seats**, LED lighting, a
costume room, and set storage. It hosts the Middle School Musical, High School
Musical, and Spring Drama (three ticketed productions a year with a staggered
pre-sale run by the Covenant Arts Association), plus the MS/HS Theater Nights and
the school's Shakespeare Competition.

### Scale note

At 200 seats for a ~1,050-student school, the theater is a black-box-scale house —
the pre-sale tiers (up to 10 tickets at the top patron level) exist because seats
are genuinely scarce for the musicals. Music ensembles perform beyond campus
(Biltmore Estates, sporting events, Christmas programs) rather than in a dedicated
concert hall; no separate gallery space is named — exhibits run as ArtWalk
(all-school) and Art Talk (AP) events.

Performances include Biltmore Estates, all-school events, Christmas programs,
sporting events, and spring concerts. **Director: Zach Thompson, Worship and Choir
Teacher.**

absence), and no marching band exists — the band program is concert/symphonic.

# Covenant Day School — The Arts — Program Overview

> **Provenance:** Compiled by Claude Code on 2026-08-15 from the school's arts pages
> and the High School Profile course matrix.

### Sources

- https://www.covenantday.org/arts
- https://www.covenantday.org/arts/visual-arts · /arts/music · /arts/theater
- https://www.covenantday.org/arts/arts-association
- 2026-2027 High School Profile (Fine Arts course matrix; 1 Fine Arts unit required
 to graduate):
 https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1758547252/covenant/x8i0qddxvctsp5jqbpmt/2025-26HSGuidanceProfile.pdf

### The program's shape

- **Span: JK/TK–12.** Art is integrated in JK-K and becomes sequential from grade 1;
 general music runs JK-4 with grade-5 introduction to choir and band; dedicated
 ensembles begin in grade 6.
- Organized as three areas — **Visual Arts, Music, Theater** — with a support
 organization, the **Covenant Arts Association** (four patron tiers, $100-$1,000,
 funding and volunteering across all arts areas; contact Sherie-Beth Wilson).
- **Fine Arts Director: Sherie-Beth Wilson** (also on the HS profile's
 administration block). One **Fine Arts graduation unit is required**; the school
 states arts are "a crucial part of the curriculum, through which students
 acknowledge God and take responsibility in the creative production, expression,
 understanding, evaluation, and response to art," built on four components:
 "foundations, creative expression, historical and cultural relevance, and
 critical evaluation and response."
- High school Fine Arts course matrix (2026-2027): CP — Choir, Symphonic Band,
 Theatre, Theatre Tech, Worship Leadership, Intro to Art, Art 2D/3D, Ceramics,
 Digital Photography, Graphic Design, Yearbook; Honors — Choir, Auditioned
 Theatre, Symphonic Band, Studio Art, Yearbook; AP — Studio Art.
- ~9-10 named ensembles across divisions (see the Music file for the roster).

### Calendar anchors

MS Musical (October), HS Musical (spring, e.g. March 8), Spring Drama (April),
plus ArtWalk (all-school exhibit) and Art Talk (AP exhibit) — see the Theater and
Visual Arts files.

### The course path — Intro to AP

High school sequence, per the school's own page: "Intro to Art, Art 2/3 (2D Art and
3D Art), Honors Studio Art, AP Studio Art, Digital Photography, Graphic Design,
Ceramics, Yearbook." The profile matrix confirms the weighting: Intro to Art / Art
2D/3D / Ceramics / Digital Photography / Graphic Design / Yearbook at CP, Studio
Art at Honors, **AP Studio Art** as the terminal course. Art is integrated JK-K and
sequential from grade 1.

### The exhibition circuit

- **ArtWalk** — the all-school art exhibit.
- **Art Talk** — the AP Art exhibit (AP students present their portfolios).
- **Pineville Art Show**, **Youth Art Month (YAM)**, **Mint Hill Arts
 Competition**, **Scholastic Art & Writing Awards**.

### Recognition

"Multiple Scholastic Art Award Winners"; **7 YAM honorees in 2023**; some students
"have even published their work."

### Faculty

**Katie Spata — High School Art Teacher.** The program aims to "cultivate personal
development and a sense of joy and confidence in students as they create and share
their artwork," integrating art history and design principles with "God-given
creativity."
