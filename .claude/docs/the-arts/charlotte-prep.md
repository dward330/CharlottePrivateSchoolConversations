# Charlotte Preparatory School — The Arts — Program Overview and Ensembles

Charlotte Preparatory School (PreK–8, 212 Boyce Road, Charlotte) teaches music, drama and visual art to every student from Kindergarten through 4th grade, then lets students narrow their arts choices in Middle School (grades 5–8). The school says students take part in "20+ Arts performances and events per year" (Fast Facts, https://charlotteprep.org/about/fast-facts, and the Fall 2025 Admissions Packet, which words it "20+ art performances and events per year"). No count of how those events break down is published.

### The arts ladder, grade by grade

| Grades | What the school publishes |
|---|---|
| Early School (age 2 to 5) | "All early school students participate in music classes. Montessori Kindergarten students have weekly art, P.E., Spanish, and Tech classes." (Fall 2025 Admissions Packet) |
| K–4 | "Students in Kindergarten through 4th Grade participate routinely in a 6-day rotation of specialized classes ... Students engage in Fine Arts classes (music, drama, and art), Technology and STEM, Spanish, and Physical Education." (https://charlotteprep.org/academics/lower-school) |
| K–2 | "Our youngest Lower School students (Kindergarten-2nd Grade) shine on stage annually in age-appropriate, curricular-themed costumed productions" (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/fine-arts) |
| 4–8 | Band. The Fine Arts page lists "recorders, Orff instruments and band for grades 4-8". The school's band teacher is titled "Middle School Band and 4th Grade Music Teacher". |
| 5–6 | "Students may choose between Chorus and Band as 5th graders, while they continue to take both Drama and Art." (Fall 2025 Admissions Packet) |
| 7–8 | "Once they are in 7th grade they may choose between Drama and Art." (Fall 2025 Admissions Packet) |
| 5–8 | "Middle School students further investigate dramatic arts in year-long electives, introducing students to every facet of theater including performance and stage production opportunities." (Fine Arts page) |
| 3–8 | "our after-school theater arts program features student performances where 3rd-8th Grade artists come together to produce and perform on the stage for the school community." (Fine Arts page) |

How often each art meets inside the 6-day rotation is not published. The Admissions Packet carries sample daily schedules for the Early School only, and no Lower School or Middle School timetable is posted anywhere on the site.

The packet does not say whether 7th and 8th graders keep a music ensemble alongside their Drama-or-Art choice. The school's own concert recordings show a Middle School chorus and band performing together (see below), and a 2022 story names "the middle school 7/8 choir", so a 7th–8th grade choir has existed.

- **Off campus**, in the school's own words: "Charlotte Prep's choral groups have performed at the Biltmore Estate, Charlotte Knights and Hornets games, and with Distinguished Concerts International New York at Carnegie Hall." (Fine Arts page). No dates are given for any of these.

### The Carnegie Hall claim is the school's own and is undated

The Carnegie Hall sentence has been on the Fine Arts page since at least December 2021, and the 2022 viewbook repeats it ("everywhere from local venues to Carnegie Hall", https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/char_intro_singles_fin). No year, concert or repertoire is published. No school news story describes the trip, and no Distinguished Concerts International New York (DCINY) page or program naming Charlotte Prep could be found. The 2015 music page does not mention it. Treat it as a school-reported performance with no published date.

A separate Carnegie Hall mention should not be confused with it. In October 2024 the school hosted a benefit concert by the Sphinx Virtuosi, a professional chamber orchestra, whose "next stop on their tour is Carnegie Hall". That was a visiting ensemble, not Charlotte Prep students.

### Arts spaces

- **The stage in the gymnasium.** Productions and showcases are staged in "the Charlotte Prep gymnasium". It has a raised proscenium stage with curtains. In the 2023-24 Prep Fund year the school paid for stage lighting costing $180,000, funded by "$65,000 Auction Proceeds 2024", a "$35,000 2021 Parent Fundraiser" and "$80,000 Prep Fund" (2024-2025 Calendar and Annual Report, https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/2024-2025_calendar_and_annual_report). The 2024-25 Prep Fund then bought "Brand new theater sets including 4 periaktos", "6 fabricated wall panels with removable top" and "Brand new digital lighting for our stage and proscenium". Separately, new LED gym lights "installed last year" were a gift from the parents of the Class of 2023 (https://vimeo.com/1026616876, November 5, 2024).
- **The Music Cottage** is where the before-school chorus studio meets (After School page). No description of it is published.
- **The Art Studio** is where the printmaking studio meets (After School page). No description of it is published.
- An earlier 2018-2019 Fine Arts Campaign paid for "the new sound system", "the band instruments", "art room upgrades and new stage lights" (2018-2019 Impact Report, https://issuu.com/charlottepreparatoryschool/docs/2018-2019_annual_report).

Since 2024-25, fine arts fees are included in tuition: "We have included the fees for athletics, fine arts, and technology in tuition for the 2024-25 school year and will continue to do so moving forward." (https://charlotteprep.org/about/strategic-plan-2022-2027)

Charlotte Prep runs theatre at three levels. There is a spring show for grades 3–8, a fall one-act play, and grade-level performances in the Lower School. All of them are staged on the proscenium stage in the school gymnasium. ### How a production is made

From the Willy Wonka Jr. story (April 11, 2024):

- "Our students have rehearsed more than 150 hours to perfect every aspect of this production."
- "the hardworking members of the set design club and stage crew have transformed our gymnasium into a world of pure imagination!" and "Students are involved in every detail; our set design club and stage crew are essential parts of the production."
- "Beloved director Ms. Davis and music director Ms. Moscati are leading the show." Erica Davis is the school's Drama Teacher, and Morgan Moscati is its Middle School Band and 4th Grade Music Teacher.
- "Wonka Bars" were sold at intermission, and one held a golden ticket for a photo shoot with the cast.

For Alice (November 10, 2025): "The cast is busy rehearsing, and our middle school set design club is crafting props and costumes."

The shows are performed under the "Prep Players" name ("Prep Players Present: Alice"). Ticket prices are not published.

### The stage

Performances are "in our gym" and "in the Charlotte Prep gymnasium". The school recordings show a raised proscenium stage with curtains at one end. The school has invested in it heavily. Stage lighting costing $180,000 was paid for in the 2023-24 Prep Fund year from 2024 auction proceeds, a 2021 parent fundraiser and the Prep Fund. The 2024-25 Prep Fund then bought "Brand new theater sets including 4 periaktos", "6 fabricated wall panels with removable top" and "Brand new digital lighting for our stage and proscenium" (https://charlotteprep.org/support/the-prep-fund). There is no separate theatre or black-box space.

### Recognition

Charlotte Prep's theatre record is about participation and what graduates go on to do, not external awards. The Blumey Awards, Blumenthal Performing Arts' regional theatre awards, are for high schools, so a K–8 school cannot enter them. No middle-school theatre competition or festival entry is published.

What the school does publish:

- **Arts high-school admissions.** A member of the Class of 2026 was accepted to Walnut Hill School for the Arts, a boarding arts high school, for musical theater, announced May 13, 2026 (same story as above). The student credits her start to a Charlotte Prep production.
- **A technical-theatre path.** A Class of 2023 graduate went on to the Northwest School of the Arts, majoring in Technical Theater (https://charlotteprep.org/community/news/stories/a-stage-set-for-success, January 17, 2024).
- **Faculty development.** The drama program's summer-2026 professional development was "iTheatrics Annual Musical Theater Professional Development", and earlier Prep Fund lists include a "Theater Education Conference".

### Visual-arts recognition: none published

No student art award, juried exhibition or Scholastic Art & Writing Award is published by the school. Charlotte Prep also appears in none of the Mid-Carolina Region Scholastic Art Awards winners lists (Gold Key, Silver Key and Honorable Mention) for 2020 through 2026. Those lists are published by UNC Charlotte's College of Arts + Architecture, the regional affiliate (https://cws.charlotte.edu/backup-coaa/?p=2201). The awards are open to students from grade 7, so 7th and 8th graders would be eligible.

On campus, student art is shown "Through performance and exhibits" (https://charlotteprep.org/student-life/life-at-prep), in the Winter and Spring Fine Arts Showcases, and on Grandparents & Special Friends' Day, when guests "view student work". A "Lower School Immersive Art Exhibition" was in development in summer 2026. Its form and date are not yet published.
