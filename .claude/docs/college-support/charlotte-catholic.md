## ⚠️ This is a MATRICULATION list, not an ACCEPTANCE list

The profile's own heading is **"COLLEGE MATRICULATION CLASSES OF 2023-2025"**, and its
prose reads "CCHS graduates have **matriculated to** 169 different colleges and
universities." Several schools already on this roster publish *acceptance* lists instead,
which are structurally larger (a student is accepted at many schools and matriculates at
exactly one). **The bucket counts below are therefore not like-for-like against an
acceptance-based school and must not be read as "harder to get into."** This caveat is
carried into the app prose and the per-cell provenance.

## Graduation requirements

Students take **7 classes per semester**; **28 credits over 4 years**; the academic year
is two 18-week semesters. All classes are college preparatory. All science classes are
designed and taught as laboratory classes. Transcripts reflect only classes completed at
Charlotte Catholic — transfer transcripts are not added.

## Curriculum and rigor

- Maximum **1 honors class in 9th grade**; **5 honors classes in 10th grade**.
- 9th-grade students may take a maximum of **3 advanced classes**.
- AP classes begin **junior year**. **"Most demanding" = 2 AP in junior year + 4 AP in
 senior year.** Students may exceed those numbers.
- **Admission to AP courses is competitive** — students submit an application for each
 course. Placement is determined by previous coursework and grades, standardized testing
 (PSAT) and teacher feedback.
- Placement in all core courses is determined by previous coursework and grades,
 standardized testing results and teacher feedback.

## Advanced Placement performance

2024-2025: **345 CCHS students in 11th–12th grade took 1,064 AP exams in 23 subjects.**
**94% of AP scores were 3 or above, and 72% scored a 4 or 5.**
**AP examinations are mandatory in each AP course** — so this is a whole-cohort figure,
not a self-selected subset of confident test-takers, which makes the 94% materially
stronger than the same number at a school with optional exams.

**AP Scholar tier counts are NOT PUBLISHED.** The profile gives exam-level performance
instead, which is the richer disclosure.

## Class of 2025 outcomes

- **95%** of graduates planned to immediately pursue post-secondary education.
- Of those: **81%** enrolled in public universities, **19%** in private universities.
- **38%** elected to remain in North Carolina.
- **2%** attending 2-year institutions.
- **Class of 2025 earned $17,242,184 in scholarships.**

## Institutional context from the profile

- **Athletics:** since 2000, **74 state championships** and **state runner-up 65 times**;
 **8 Wachovia/Wells Fargo Cups**; one of **four parochial schools** competing in the
 **NCHSAA**; currently **Division 6A Southern Carolina Split 6A/7A Conference**;
 **1000+** student-athletes have gone on to NCAA/NAIA/Junior College levels.
- **Fine Arts:** Fine Arts Center **dedicated in 2022**; band and choral programs have
 performed in New York and Chicago; **the band performs quadrennially in the St. Patrick's
 Day Parade in Dublin, Ireland**.
- **St. Augustine Scholars Program (SASP):** a **new program starting 2025-2026** with a
 9th-grade cohort — a four-year classical pathway (philosophy, logic, Latin, primary
 classical texts, Socratic seminars).
- **Honor societies named on the profile:** National Honor Society · Rho Kappa National
 Social Studies Honor Society · International Thespian Honor Society · Mu Alpha Theta Math
 Honor Society · Science National Honor Society · Spanish Honor Society · German Honor
 Society · French Honor Society · Latin Honor Society · National Art Honor Society.
- The profile states CCHS "offers over **50 club opportunities** each year." The clubs
 directory itself enumerates **76** (see the Student Clubs record) — the profile figure is
 a conservative round number, the directory is the countable one.
