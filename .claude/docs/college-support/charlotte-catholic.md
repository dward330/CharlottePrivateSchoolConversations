## ⚠️ This is a MATRICULATION list, not an ACCEPTANCE list

The profile's own heading is **"COLLEGE MATRICULATION CLASSES OF 2023-2025"**, and its
prose reads "CCHS graduates have **matriculated to** 169 different colleges and
universities." Several schools already on this roster publish *acceptance* lists instead,
which are structurally larger (a student is accepted at many schools and matriculates at
exactly one). **The bucket counts below are therefore not like-for-like against an
acceptance-based school and must not be read as "harder to get into."** This caveat is
carried into the app prose and the per-cell provenance.

Students take **7 classes per semester**; **28 credits over 4 years**; the academic year

## Curriculum and rigor

- Maximum **1 honors class in 9th grade**; **5 honors classes in 10th grade**.
- 9th-grade students may take a maximum of **3 advanced classes**.
- AP classes begin **junior year**. **"Most demanding" = 2 AP in junior year + 4 AP in
 senior year.** Students may exceed those numbers.
- **Admission to AP courses is competitive** — students submit an application for each
 course. Placement is determined by previous coursework and grades, standardized testing
 (PSAT) and teacher feedback.
- Placement in all core courses is determined by previous coursework and grades,
 standardized testing results and teacher feedback.

## Advanced Placement performance

2024-2025: **345 CCHS students in 11th–12th grade took 1,064 AP exams in 23 subjects.**
**94% of AP scores were 3 or above, and 72% scored a 4 or 5.**
**AP examinations are mandatory in each AP course** — so this is a whole-cohort figure,
not a self-selected subset of confident test-takers, which makes the 94% materially
stronger than the same number at a school with optional exams.

**AP Scholar tier counts are NOT PUBLISHED.** The profile gives exam-level performance
instead, which is the richer disclosure.

## Interpretation cautions

- **This is not the university's admit rate, and not the school's selectivity.** It is
 the rate at which that university admitted *this high school's* applicants — a joint
 property of the pair. Label it that way wherever it is surfaced.
- **Carry the denominator.** Several cells here are single-digit; a 100% rate off 2
 applicants is not comparable to one off 200, and a bare percentage from a
 single-digit base must not be published.
- **UNC-system campuses only.** This says nothing about private or out-of-state
 destinations, so it complements a matriculation list and never replaces one.

Admit Rate re-derived from that term's own Applied/Admitted to within rounding. The
scrape was smoke-tested first against the method file's reference table (Cannon School
